# Third-party code and assets

What in this repository comes from other projects, and the terms it comes under.

## Models and animation

All by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), who releases everything under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free for any use, no attribution required. We credit them anyway.

| Where | From | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/models/nature/` | Ultimate Nature Pack: a stump, a mossy log and grass | None to the files. The sandbox reads their shapes and applies its own colours. Its trees, rocks, bushes, plants and flowers were replaced on 2026-10-13 by grown plants and scanned rocks (D222). |
| `assets/models/character/mannequin.glb` | Universal Animation Library v3.0 (2026-06-16, Pro edition, 120 clips) and Universal Animation Library 2 v2.2 (2026-09-28, Source edition, 134 clips), both bought from [quaternius.itch.io](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library) on 2026-10-12: the mannequin, its skeleton and the clips the game plays | Only the clips listed in `art/animation/clips.txt` are kept, cut by `tools/characters/clips.py`, keyframes unchanged; the libraries themselves are not committed (`art/README.md`). |
| `assets/models/creatures/` | Ultimate Animated Animals pack: `Deer.glb`. Easy Enemies pack: `Spider.glb`. Both rigged and animated | None to the files. |
| `assets/models/tools/` | Survival pack: `Axe.glb`, `Knife.glb`. Medieval Weapons pack: hammer, spear, bow, arrow | None to the files. Made into the held looks in `models/held/` by `tools/characters/fit_held.py`: scaled, set at their grip, and stone and iron heads coloured. |

The individual model files were fetched from [quaternius-showcase](https://github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase), which republishes the packs one model to a file, and, until 2026-10-12, the animation library from [gltf-universal-animation-library](https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library), a copy of its 2025-06-10 release that missed three updates, one of them a fix for the very twist we were chasing; it is now from Quaternius directly. The mirror's models are its own FBX2glTF conversions of Quaternius's FBX files, not his exports; none of those packs has been updated since it copied them (checked 2026-10-12). Take a pack from Quaternius's own pages, at its current version, when it is next needed. The licence was checked against Quaternius's own [FAQ](https://quaternius.com/faq.html) on 2026-10-10. The creatures, tools and workbench were fetched from quaternius-showcase on 2026-10-11.

| `assets/sounds/hit_flesh_*`, `hit_flesh_heavy_*` | [37 hits/punches](https://opengameart.org/node/2558) by Independent.nu (`hit10` to `hit18` light, `hit01` to `hit13` heavy), with Kenney's `knifeSlice` | Layered: the body blow with the slice's start 8 to 9 dB down |

The fight's sounds were fetched from OpenGameArt on 2026-10-24, each pack's licence checked on its page (CC0 only; the Still North packs also quote their makers' terms: "use any of our sounds freely, with no royalties, and no credit needs to be given"). Every one is cut to a take, made mono at 48 kHz, faded out, its peak set to -2 dBFS and encoded as Ogg Vorbis (quality 4), with ffmpeg.

The rest of the game's sound, air, water, fire, birds and music, is made in code (`crates/terra_world/src/audio/`).
