# The forest's fight route (iso-forest-demo, v6 from j13): `cargo iso --route forest` plays it
# and films it (f-N is frame N, as the logs number it, at FILM_FPS 30); add `--shot target/job/film/f.jpg` for the
# frames' folder, or `--frames 1230 --film 0 --shot target/job/g/end.jpg` to play it through
# for its gates alone (demo/tally.rs: every foe falls; demo/occlude.rs: nothing hides him).
# A route is the flags `cargo iso` takes, whitespace apart, `#` to the line's end a note
# (main.rs, `--route`). Its walks between packs are `--goto` points (hero/abilities.rs), so a
# timing change before one moves only the frames he waits there, not where he stands. Its
# parry and dodge answer the blow's tell (`--on-tell`, hero/react.rs), not a frame.
--biome forest --play --frames 60 --film 1170 --zoom 10
# The packs its run must clear, each mob woken and fallen by the last frame, or it fails
# (demo/tally.rs `cleared`): `expect all` (also when no line says), `expect packs 0 1`, or
# `expect nothing` for a hero film that fights none.
expect all

# Pack 0, the camp: walk up, three lights and a heavy, a block, four lights.
--press w@60-95
--do light@130 --do light@148 --do light@166 --do heavy@195
--do block@230-255
--do light@260 --do light@280 --do light@298 --do light@316

--goto -4.9,7.5@350-469
