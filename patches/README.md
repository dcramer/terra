# Patched crates

Bevy crates we carry a small change to, as D184 allows when extending Bevy can't do it: each
is the crates.io release we pin, copied whole (its licence files kept), with the change marked
`Terra's patch (Dnnn)` in its source, and taken in by the workspace's `[patch.crates-io]`.

## Carrying one to a new Bevy release

1. Look for the fix in the new release's source and changelog first; if it is there, drop the
   crate and its `[patch.crates-io]` line.
2. Otherwise copy the new release from `~/.cargo/registry/src/*/<crate>-<version>/` over the
   old one (leave out `.cargo_vcs_info.json`, `.cargo-ok`, `Cargo.lock` and `Cargo.toml.orig`),
   and make the change again, found by grepping for `Terra's patch`.
3. The tests that hold it (`crates/terra_world/src/mesh_upload_tests.rs` for `bevy_mesh`;
   `terra_walker::skinning`'s glTF of eight influences and `terra_world`'s shader test with
   `SKINNED_EIGHT` for `bevy_gltf`, `bevy_mesh` and `bevy_pbr`'s eight influences (D601); the indoor light still, docs/dungeons.md, the sky light's stills, D492, and `fire::fog`'s glow-mark test and the fire sheet, D496, and D537's bench, and `daylight`'s air-sun test, D212, and its own `atmosphere::kept_luts` tests, D614, and `atmosphere::spread_aerial` tests, D615, and `prepass::previous_view_tests`, run from a copy as below with the workspace's `Cargo.lock` and `[patch.crates-io]` entries for the patched `bevy_render` and `bevy_mesh`, for `bevy_pbr`)
   must pass. `bevy_render`'s are its own `render_phase::tests::render_multidrawable_batch_set`
   and `view::visibility::range::tests` (D587), `view::cascade_lod_tests` (D612) and
   `batching::merged_unpacking` (D613, with the stills of D613's Why for its shaders) and `batching::draw_priority_tests` (D618);
   a patched crate is not a workspace member, so run it from a copy whose `Cargo.toml` gains
   an empty `[workspace]` table: `cargo test --manifest-path <copy>/Cargo.toml --lib -- render_phase::tests view::visibility::range view::cascade_lod_tests batching::merged_unpacking batching::draw_priority_tests`.
