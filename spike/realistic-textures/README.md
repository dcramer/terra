# Realistic textures

Question (2026-10-12): do building pieces look real enough, and less cartoony, when the concept is drawn realistically and Tripo's own detailed textures are kept, rather than drawn flat and painted one palette colour a face (D14)? The developers asked for more realism and said it may change the art direction.

Tried on the log cabin kit's log: the same log three ways, side by side in the workbench in the game's light, close and at a distance:

1. Flat palette roles, as the style guide has it (`assets/models/kits/Log.glb`).
2. Tripo's textures kept, from the flat concept (`art/props/kit-log/model.glb`).
3. Tripo's textures kept, from a realistic concept, modelled with h3.1 and textured at 4K with its PBR maps.

Scratch output is in `out/`, which is ignored. Findings go to a dated note in `research/`, then this folder is deleted.
