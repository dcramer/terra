Four ways Terra could look

The same wolf, villager, workbench, tree and outpost, drawn in four directions by an image model. These are concepts, not finished models: each one is the first step of the pipeline, and the chosen direction becomes the brief every model is made from. Click any image to see it large.

C

The mood board: four worlds in C's light

The target, as concept art: two cities and two landscapes across settings, at life size (D110), in direction C's painted look and rich light. To make C work with many realms, colour stays a palette role on each part and the painted look moves into shared, tintable materials and the lighting, so the same models can be recoloured per realm.

D

D, tested: real renders and other worlds

Not concepts: our own six models from the pipeline, placed in a scene in Blender and rendered. D is made after the render, with the light on each pixel cut into three crisp bands and the shadow band tinted cool, then haze by distance, which is what a game shader would do. Each realm is only a palette and a light: every model is the same file in all six.

Beyond fantasy

Concepts in D for science fiction and tech, and for very different realms. These four subjects are being made into models next.

What these are

gpt-image-2 concepts made through Tripo's API, one per subject and direction, 10 credits each. Same subject text every time; only the style paragraph changes.

Textures

Only C uses painted textures. Choosing it reopens D14 (flat colour, no textures) and changes the pipeline: models keep their generated textures instead of being coloured from the palette.

Mixing

A direction can be mixed: A's facets with D's bolder colour blocks, say. Tell me what you like in each and the next round of concepts can combine them.
