# Materials

Where each material came from. Made by `cargo xtask textures` ([tools/textures](../../tools/textures/README.md)).

- `bark_brown_02`: Poly Haven [Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02), CC0, 2k. 1.00 m across. Its height map, inverted and at 1k, is `depth.jpg` for relief mapping, as the lush forest's oaks and spruces wear it. `trunk_depth.jpg` is that drawn out to its full range (ImageMagick `-contrast-stretch 1.5%x1.5%`), which they are relief-mapped by (D309).
- `birch_bark`: made from a generated image (art/textures/birch-bark.png) by `textures.ts material`. 1.00 m across.
- `clay_floor_001`: Poly Haven [Clay Floor 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/clay_floor_001), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across.
- `flora`: a stack, in this order, 1024 pixels each, of `pine_bark` (Poly Haven [Pine Bark](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_bark), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across); `bark_brown_02` (Poly Haven [Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02), CC0, 2k. 1.00 m across); `leaves` (made from a generated image (art/textures/leaves.png) by `textures.ts material`); `mossy_rock` (Poly Haven [Mossy Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_rock), CC0, 2k. 3.00 m across); `lichen_rock` (Poly Haven [Lichen Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/lichen_rock), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across); `rock_boulder_dry` (Poly Haven [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), CC0, 2k. 1.80 m across).
- `foliage`: cards, 1024 pixels each: oak (LeafSet016), hazel (LeafSet024), berry (LeafSet001), oakbranch (LeafSet016), spruce (drawn needle by needle), bramble (LeafSet014) composed by `textures.ts cards` from leaves scanned by ambientCG, [LeafSet016](https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet016), [LeafSet024](https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet024), [LeafSet001](https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet001), [LeafSet014](https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet014), CC0, each with its normal map; fern, herb, daisy, buttercup, campion, holly each cut out of a generated picture on a blue background (art/textures/cards); birch, pine kept from the atlas as it stood.
- `grey_roof_tiles_02`: Poly Haven [Grey Roof Tiles 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/grey_roof_tiles_02), CC0, 2k. 1.50 m across.
- `grey_stone_path`: Poly Haven [Grey Stone Path](https://polyhaven.com/a/grey_stone_path), CC0, 2k. 1.80 m across.
- `ground`: a stack, in this order, 1024 pixels each, of `brown_mud_02` (Poly Haven [Brown Mud 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_02), CC0, 2k. 1.30 m across); `leafy_grass` (Poly Haven [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across); `rock_face_03` (Poly Haven [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03), CC0, 2k. 2.70 m across); `sand_01` (Poly Haven [Sand 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01), CC0, 2k. 1.50 m across); `dry_decay_leaves` (Poly Haven [Dry Decay Leaves](https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_decay_leaves), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across); `forest_leaves_04` (Poly Haven [Forest Leaves 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_leaves_04), CC0, 2k. 1.50 m across); `roots` (Poly Haven [Roots](https://polyhaven.com/a/roots), CC0, 2k. 1.67 m across); `moss_004` (ambientCG [Moss 004](https://ambientcg.com/a/Moss004), CC0, 2k); and, for the mountains (D322), `gray_rocks` for scree (Poly Haven [Gray Rocks](https://polyhaven.com/a/gray_rocks), CC0, 2k. 1.80 m across), `rocks_ground_01` for gravel (Poly Haven [Rocks Ground 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocks_ground_01), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across) and `snow_02` (Poly Haven [Snow 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across), stacked alike and appended below the first eight.
- `litter`: cards, 512 pixels each: oakfall (LeafSet012), oakmould (LeafSet012), beechfall (LeafSet011), leafneedle (LeafSet012), needles (LeafSet012), needlecone (LeafSet012), cones (LeafSet012), twigs (LeafSet012), needledark (LeafSet012), ivy (LeafSet017), ivydark (LeafSet017) composed by `textures.ts cards` from leaves scanned by ambientCG, [LeafSet012](https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet012), [LeafSet011](https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet011), [LeafSet017](https://ambientcg.com/a/LeafSet017), CC0, each with its normal map.
- `log_end`: made from a generated image (art/textures/log-end.png) by `textures.ts material`.
- `log_ends`: ambientCG [TreeEnd002](https://ambientcg.com/a/TreeEnd002), [TreeEnd003](https://ambientcg.com/a/TreeEnd003), [TreeEnd004](https://ambientcg.com/a/TreeEnd004), [TreeEnd005](https://ambientcg.com/a/TreeEnd005), CC0, each cut out and drawn round in a cell of 2 by 2, greyed 15%, 5 checks cut in each by `textures.ts ends`.
- `log_wood`: `pine_bark` (Poly Haven [Pine Bark](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_bark), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across) at 1 m lying in patches over `rough_wood` (Poly Haven [Rough Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood), CC0, 2k. 0.50 m across) at 1 m, 0% covered, its colour 35% saturated and the wood's 112% as light and 0.8 rough, by `textures.ts blend`. 1.00 m round by 2.00 m along.
- `logs_pieces`: the logs kit's modelled pieces (Tripo models of concepts pending the developers' approval, welded and cut to budget: the whole modelled log in art/kits/logs/log), unwrapped afresh into one atlas and baked from each model's full detail, colour and tangent-space normal, by `tools/kits/pieces.py`; its occlusion, roughness and metal plain.
- `medieval_wall_01`: Poly Haven [Medieval Wall 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_wall_01), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across.
- `oak_bark`: Poly Haven [Bark Willow 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_willow_02), CC0, 2k. 2.15 m across. Furrowed as an old oak's bark is; the lush forest's trunks. Its height map, inverted and at 1k, is `depth.jpg` for relief mapping; maps recompressed. `trunk_depth.jpg` is that drawn out to its full range (ImageMagick `-contrast-stretch 1.5%x1.5%`), for the trunks (D309).
- `packed_earth`: ambientCG [Ground 104](https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground104), CC0, 2k.
- `patterned_clay_plaster`: Poly Haven [Patterned Clay Plaster](https://polyhaven.com/a/patterned_clay_plaster), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across.
- `pine_bark`: Poly Haven [Pine Bark](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_bark), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across. `trunk_depth.jpg`, its depth drawn out to its full range (ImageMagick `-contrast-stretch 1.5%x1.5%`), is what the lush forest's trunks are relief-mapped by (D309).
- `pine_log`: baked round by `tools/kits/bake.py` from the reference model `art/kits/logs/log/model.glb` (Tripo), laid 0.3 m thick and 3 m long, 2 m along. 1.00 m round by 2.00 m along.

- `rock_boulder_dry`: Poly Haven [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), CC0, 2k. 1.80 m across.
