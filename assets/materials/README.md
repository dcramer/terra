- `grey_roof_tiles_02`: Poly Haven [Grey Roof Tiles 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/grey_roof_tiles_02), CC0, 2k. 1.50 m across.
- `grey_stone_path`: Poly Haven [Grey Stone Path](https://polyhaven.com/a/grey_stone_path), CC0, 2k. 1.80 m across.
- `ground`: a stack, in this order, 1024 pixels each, of `forest_ground_04` (Poly Haven [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04), CC0, 2k. 3.15 m across); `leafy_grass` (Poly Haven [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across); `rock_face_03` (Poly Haven [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03), CC0, 2k. 2.70 m across); `sand_01` (Poly Haven [Sand 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01), CC0, 2k. 1.50 m across); `forest_leaves_02` (Poly Haven [Forest Leaves 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_leaves_02), CC0, 2k. 3.00 m across).
