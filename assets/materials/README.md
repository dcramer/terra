# Materials

Where each material came from. Made by `cargo xtask textures` ([tools/textures](../../tools/textures/README.md)).

- `clay_plaster`: Poly Haven [Clay Plaster](https://polyhaven.com/a/clay_plaster), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across.
- `ground`: a stack, in this order, 1024 pixels each, of `forest_ground_04` (Poly Haven [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04), CC0, 2k. 3.15 m across); `leafy_grass` (Poly Haven [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across); `rock_face_03` (Poly Haven [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03), CC0, 2k. 2.70 m across); `sand_01` (Poly Haven [Sand 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01), CC0, 2k. 1.50 m across).
- `log_end`: made from a generated image (art/textures/log-end.png) by `textures.ts material`.
- `old_wood_floor`: Poly Haven [Old Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/old_wood_floor), CC0, 2k. 3.00 m across.
- `rough_wood`: Poly Haven [Rough Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood), CC0, 2k. 0.50 m across.
- `rustic_stone_wall`: Poly Haven [Rustic Stone Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/rustic_stone_wall), CC0, 2k. 1.52 m across. Its height.jpg is for parallax.
- `shakes`: made from a generated image (art/textures/shakes.png) by `textures.ts material`.
- `weathered_planks`: Poly Haven [Weathered Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_planks), CC0, 2k. 2.00 m across.
