# Making a good biome scene

A biome is a data file of the iso crate (`crates/terra_iso/`), `crates/terra_iso/biomes/<name>.ron`, drawn by the shared kit and run by `cargo iso --biome NAME` (D634, [kit.md](kit.md); `--scene ruin|forest|marsh` reads the same file). What its file cannot say yet is its layout's modules. The ruin at night (`crates/terra_iso/src/ruin/`) is the worked example: copy its split, not its numbers, for a new biome. Build it in steps, a still each, measured and landed on its own: camera and ground; walls and props; light; the fight; effects; ambient; the film.

## Modules (one each, numbers to tune at its top)

| Module | Holds |
|---|---|
| `mod.rs` | the scene's plugin, adding the rest |
| `camera.rs` | the ARPG view (below) |
| `ground.rs`, `blocks.rs` | the floor; the box-mapped block mesh builder |
| `walls.rs`, `beyond.rs`, `dress.rs`, `grass.rs` | standing structure; what fills to the frame's edges; props; ground cover |
| `light.rs`, `fire.rs` | key and fill; local sources with their flames |
| `fight.rs` | the cast posed at a clip and time |
| `effects/` | `kit.rs` (clock, camera, fires found, sparks, noise, glow materials), `flame.rs`, `spell.rs`, `hit.rs` |
| `ambient/` | `fog.rs`, `dust.rs`, `grass.rs` + `wind.wesl`, `banner.rs` |

## Camera

Perspective, FOV 30 vertical, pitch 55, yaw 20, about 19 m from a focus between the fighters at chest height (`camera.rs`), so a 1.85 m hero is 0.10 to 0.13 of the frame (the refs' 0.07 to 0.20, median 0.12; at 23.5 m the knight read 0.07 and too small). EV100 8, TAA with contrast-adaptive sharpening 0.6 (TAA's blur was the glade's biggest gap), SSAO, TonyMcMapface, bloom. Fixed in the experiment: a still is judged from the game's pose. Which camera the game has is D629 (Open).

## Ground and walls from the scanned materials

Use the repository's Poly Haven scans in `assets/materials/` at their true size (each one's metres across is in assets/materials/README.md), box-mapped in world space (the dominant normal axis), never a texture stretched per block.
- Floor: one mesh of `large_grey_tiles` at its 3 m, cut along a noise contour into holes and a ragged edge over `packed_earth`, plus loose and displaced slabs where it breaks. Separate slabs of one size read as a checkerboard (judge r1); a hue tint per slab reads pink, green, grey; vary value only.
- Walls: one continuous skin of `castle_wall_varriation` at its 2 m, so courses run unbroken; the top broken to a jagged line and a ridge, faces a little proud or back, darker at the foot, mossed on top, fallen stones heaped below. Stacked boxes step like voxels.
- Weather on the flags per pixel (the kit's laid floor, `kit/laid.wesl`, weights in the paving's second UV): grime in the joints (the scan's own AO under 0.5 to 0.75 marks them), moss out of them (the ground stack's Moss 004 at 1.1 m), Voronoi hairline cracks in patches, `detail_stone` every 0.5 m at 0.6. It lifted fine detail only 0.086 to 0.094 (the lower corners 0.03 and 0.05, a third of the full still's): at night those corners are near black (luma p50 0.008), so the floor's texture cannot show; light or scatter there moves them, the material alone does not. Moss hardly shows under the moon.
- Lawns by day (`wood/lawn.rs`, iso-art j39): never the ground's grass layer under sparse blades ("a flat, lurid yellow-green carpet", judge 2026-11-06); masses of card clumps (broad-leaf rosettes, fern fronds, tufts, 7 tries a m², 0.3 to 0.55 m) whose leaves are dark at the foot (x0.2) over dark leaves lying flat, blue-green under the oaks; off the trodden ground and 2.2 m from the cast. Lawn crop p50 0.147 to 0.080, still p50 0.076 to 0.070 (le-river's ferns 0.023, a quarter near black: ours none yet).
- A dithered fade (the trees' hole about the cast): interleaved gradient noise turned each frame for TAA to average (`foliage.wesl`), never a fixed Bayer matrix, which showed as a checker at 1:1.
- Fill the frame to its edges (`beyond.rs`): the refs have no margin of bare ground. Rocks: the scanned `assets/models/nature/forest/mossy_rock_*` set (its own maps); never the bare `Rock_1..10` nor the untextured `Rock_Moss_*` copies, which render near white at night.
- Dress at the edges and about the fight: the iso props, the game's own (`assets/models/props/`), bones, debris, ground cover in drifts. A detail texture (D362, `assets/materials/detail_stone`) is the cheap way to the refs' sharpness: fine detail is the measure the ruin is furthest from (0.06 to 0.10 against 0.13 to 0.23).

## Light against the refs' numbers

Measure every still (judging.md) and tune to the refs of the same kind of frame. Night (vr-lamplit-street, poe2-fire-spiral, d4-dungeon-fight): luma p5 0.0002 to 0.0007, p50 0.0077 to 0.0145, p95 0.043 to 0.138, bright share 0.0003 to 0.015, saturation 0.42 to 0.75. Day (le-desert-fight, d4-fire-breath, poe2-shock-nova): p5 0.007 to 0.036, p50 0.09 to 0.15, p95 0.31 to 0.44.
- The sky fill sets p5: the ruin's is 1.5 nits (5 left the blacks 3 to 10 times too bright). A moon of 190 lux, bluish, low from the NW (`light.rs`).
- Pools set p95: one short-range shadowed point light a fire and no far fill light; the ruin's brazier 140k lm cut off at 8 m, torches 25k lm to 6 m (`fire.rs`). A wide wash of fill flattens the frame into one even light (judge r1).
- Stage the fight in the pools: the hero lit from before, the creature from behind, within about 2 m of a source.
- A brazier's bowl casting shadow blacks out its own pool: make it `NotShadowCaster`. Coals: a bed of lumps heaped to a yellow heart, dull red at the rim, over an additive glow.
- By day (the forest, `wood/mod.rs`): EV 7.5, sun 4,200 lux, sky fill 300 nits, midtone saturation 1.25 over post 1.3 took forest-r4's dusk (p50 0.044, p95 0.216, sat 0.23) to p50 0.082, p95 0.355, sat 0.32.
- Stage a day fight in a sun break, as the ARPGs do: cast the trees whose shade falls on it out of the shadow map, trunk and boughs as well as the crown (`wood/clearing.rs`'s `shades_the_fight`: a point h up throws its shade h/tan(elevation) down the sun). With the crowns alone out, the boughs' long shadow still lay over the knight.
- Rim: a light behind a figure spills everywhere (Bevy lights every mesh in reach) and barely reads from 55 degrees; use a fresnel emissive material (`crates/terra_iso/src/rim.rs`'s pattern).

## The fight

`fight.rs` poses each of the cast at a clip and time: when the GLB's AnimationPlayer appears, a one-clip graph, `seek_to(at)`, speed 0 under `--shot` (real time would make a still random), playing in a film. Models face +Z: yaw = atan2(dx, dz). Choose the moment by a sheet of times (traps.md): the knight's chop reads at 1.3 s of his 2 s attack; 1.0 reads as arms raised. Hold the blow's moment in `hit.rs` to match.

## Effects (`effects/`, a fixed pool of entities, nothing spawned at run time)

- Fire: a 32-frame flipbook made at startup from rising noise (white-hot root, yellow, orange, ragged red edge) on 3 upright cards a fire with a white-hot core card; embers as velocity streaks; smoke puffs, unlit blend, warm at the bottom greying as they widen. At 70 px a metre make them bigger than feels right: flames 1.3x, embers 0.035 m, smoke alpha 0.6.
- Spell: a bolt is a core sphere, a glow card, a ribbon trail rebuilt each frame and its own light; the impact a swelling flash, sparks and a light dying in 0.09 s. Judge r3 wanted a hard white core, a rough trail, a pool of its colour on the ground.
- Hit: sparks, a flash card about 0.6 m, a light for 0.06 s, the creature's emissive (emissive texture = its albedo, strength under 1: a flat emissive over 1 turns it into a pink silhouette) white then red then off over 0.17 s, a flinch about its feet, and hit-stop: both clips at speed 0 for 80 ms.
- Slash (`slash.rs`): Unreal's two-socket weapon trail and Diablo IV's slash mesh, a strip between 0.3 and 1.12 of the blade over the last 0.2 s, hot edge outside, orange body, a light on the edge. A still has no past, so the blade's path through the swing (1.0 to 1.42 s) is baked from the clip at startup: the knight seeked a sixtieth a frame and the sword's `GlobalTransform` read the next frame, then the clip put back.
- The hits' and the spell's flashes, sparks, smoke and lights and the slash's look are the biome kit's presets since iso-kit j16 (kit.md's effects); what follows is their history.
- Slash look (j40, Niagara ribbon tutorials' sword trail, now effects.wesl's trail mode): white-hot head, orange-red body, deep red tail, noise streaks along V, a dissolve against age; premultiplied, so the body hides the floor and stays saturated by day (additive washed to peach). Marks and shells: a radial noise splat windowed to zero well inside the card; a shell's rim wandering and broken into arcs, each bolt's turned. The ruin's lit square right of the brute is the flags' puddle (`kit/laid.wesl`), not a mark.
- Impacts (j32): a star over about 0.6 m hides what it hits; a small glint gone in 0.1 s, a ring shell swelling to 1.7 m, thicker sparks (2.6 to 3.2 cm), a light 0.5 m over the floor under it and a `mark.rs` decal (glowing rim, cooling to residue) carry the hit. The ruin's p95 went 0.13 to 0.21 with them: the pools light a lot of floor.
- Every effect has a light, a bright core over bloom, and fades rather than pops.
- Marks on the ground (scorch, frost): Bevy 0.20's `ForwardDecal` works at the ARPG camera unchanged (its SSAO already needs the `DepthPrepass` a decal reads), on uneven ground too.
- The fighters' rim: every body `ruin::fight` poses takes `rim::Fighter` in every scene (a new scene adds `rim::plugin` to its branch in main.rs); its biome file's `bodies` (by model path) or its roster entry's `tone` sets its albedo and rim share (`fight::cast`). The rim is a thin cold band on the outline (`fighter_rim`, `lift`), not a wash; 1:1 crops of each fighter judge it (traps.md).

## Ambient and living nature (`ambient/`)

- A wisp (marsh/wisps.rs): a 0.07 m core at 6x its colour, a 0.5 m glow card (a 1.3 m one reads as 70 px of lens bokeh), 0.5 m over a water's margin at 4,000 lm so its pool falls on the bank as well as glinting.
- A lamp (marsh/dress.rs's lantern): no glow card (a 0.7 m one read as "a bloom ball with no readable post", "a sprite"); a crisp flame (a sphere scaled 0.025 x 0.06, emissive 12, 6, 1.8) just before an emissive box (0.15 x 0.2 x 0.15, 2.2, 1.0, 0.3) inside Lantern.glb's glass, hung 0.6 m under the end of a crossarm on a round pole (`Cylinder`, `with_generated_tangents` for `scene::material`'s rough_wood): at 960 the frame and pole read as a lantern, not a ball of light.
- Fog: lit alpha-blended horizontal cards low over the ground with tattered noise, drifting on the scene's `WIND` and fading in and out, alpha at most about 0.34. It lifted the mids into lavender smudges at r3: keep it low and in the pools.
- Dust motes in each fire's light, fireflies blinking by the trees and grassy edges: soft additive dots facing the camera. Under 0.07 m they do not show at 960 px.
- Wind: a `StandardMaterial` extended with a vertex shader (and its prepass) bending blades by height in rolling gusts; cloth (a banner) on the same material, by how far down. Only a film shows it.
- Creatures beside the fight (ruin/life/, iso-art j37): the repository's animals (`assets/models/creatures`, not Tripo; a GLB's clips: `node -e` over its JSON chunk). `Blows` counts the knight's blows (his attack passing 1.25 s); each creature runs a keyed script (`along`, `heading`) from the first blow after its rest, `Gait` switching its moving and resting clips (the walk paced to its speed), held in a still at a set moment. Spiders (0.1 scale, 0.58 m, ~25 px at 960) cling to stone faces by a torch (`Stone::Face`, `Pillar`); pigeons (1.2) perch on the back wall's `ridge` and circle once (lift 1.8 m keeps them in frame; 3 m left it). A test keeps each clear of the fighters as 3.4 m columns. No crow, rat or bat is in the repository.

## Water (history: pool.rs and pool.wesl, deleted in iso-kit j38)

Every biome's water is the kit's (`kit/water.rs`, kit.md's water section): a planar mirror, not this page's faked sky; the notes below are what the kit's water was ported from (git history has pool.rs), its names (`Look`, `Edge`, `Sheen`, `Body`) no longer in the code.

At 55 degrees real water reflects 2% (Fresnel), so it is near black; what reads as water is what it mirrors. `pool.rs` draws it so: the lit face is dark with no specular (a glossy one under a point light is one flat lit disc), and the shader adds the mirror as emissive.
- Mesh: `surface(lo, hi, step, level, ground)`, a grid at the level with each vertex's depth (level minus ground) in its second uv, triangles only where wet. `material(look)`, or `with_edge(look, edge)` for a margin; `plugin`; `Glint` on each point light to mirror (the brightest 6).
- `Look`: `deep`/`shallow` the water's own colour and clearness; `sky` (nits, a: the lift over Fresnel) and `horizon` (nits, a: how deep the bank's dark reflection reaches); `wave` (wind xy, ripple steepness z: 0.05 shows at 960 px, 0.03 is flat); `look` (y the glint lobe, 600 about 3 degrees; z gain; w drip rings a m²). The marsh's dusk: sky (6, 10, 30) x 25, horizon (40, 18, 9), EV 7.5; at a lift of 6 water reads as mud.
- `Edge` (all zero is the plain 6 cm margin): `fade` x the depth the water fades in over (0.3: a soft bank onto wet mud, not a road's cut edge), y how much the mirrored sky breaks into blots (0.8), z their size (6 m); `weed` duckweed's cover, reach (m deep), patch size, and w lily pads a m² of open shallows (0.06 to 0.45 m deep); `green` their albedo and a pad's radius (0.2 m: at 960 a pad is 6 to 10 px).
- `Sheen` (`with_sheen(look, edge, sheen)`; zero elsewhere): a moon's broad lobe, `toward` its direction and exponent (8), `colour` nits and glitter share; `lane` the moon's lane and every `Glint`'s streak (anisotropic Beckmann: x the slope spread toward the eye, y across, z the glitter's facet cell, w its share; zero keeps the round Phong glints), `moon` its lux (rgb) and gain. Bevy 0.20's SSR is deferred and opaque only, so the alpha-blended water fakes its mirror so. The lane needs the moon's mirror in frame: at 34 degrees up it fell over the frame's top and the water read flat (blind-marsh6); the marsh's moon is 46 degrees up behind the fight (`light::MOON`, its test puts the lane's head on the far channel). Lane (0.16, 0.045) glittered the whole frame; (0.08, 0.03) at 4 cm cells, 0.6 glitter and gain 1 read as "blown white speckles, like snow" behind the caster (judge 2026-11-06). `soft` softens it: x a peak (nits, max channel, x / (1 + x / peak)), y the share of the ripples' tilt taken away, z the glints drawn as dashes z times long across the lane. The marsh's: lane (0.1, 0.025, 0.09, 0.3), gain 0.4, soft (160, 0.5, 3), the moon at (-0.492, 0.670, -0.556) so its head sits on the far channel's left reach, 0.4 of a half-frame or more from every fighter (`light::tests`); a soft light-blue patch with stacked dashes, no white (marsh-r10/s1). Keep the lane's head off the fight: its glare eats the bolts' contrast.
- Colour by the ref's pixels, not by eye: vr-pond-slash's water is sRGB 1, 41, 86 deep and 57, 113, 155 in the sheen, no red; a sky with red in it (6, 10, 30) reads as lilac paint. The marsh's: sky (0.3, 5, 26) x 25. Grey fog cards over water grey it: give a scene its own `FogLook`, cold and thin.
- By day the sun's glitter is many small sharp glints over clear water (le-river-beam, a 600x400 crop of its river: body sRGB 85, 91, 63, glints at luma 200 3,200 a megapixel, median 2 px, mean 5.8, 1.8% cover). `with_body(look, edge, sheen, Body)` (zero elsewhere): `ripple` fine ripples, x height (m), y wavelength (m), z cat's-paw patch size (m), w how calm the calm ones are, two scales a golden ratio apart and turned, so nothing tiles; `clear` x the deep's opacity, the mirror added over the bed undimmed. The forest's pond: ripple (0.0094, 0.106, 3, 0.7), clear 0.78, broad `wave` z 0.03, a greenish canopy mirror sky (170, 190, 110) x 1.8, the glint light 400 m up the mirrored ray (at 40 m the glitter was one patch). Its glints 3,700/Mpx, median 3 px, mean 8, 3% cover; body 78, 84, 67 (forest-r9). Smooth broad ripples (`wave` z 0.07) under a near light drew big white blobs in a repeating patch on milky blue (r8, body 80, 90, 100, median 20 px); 600 over 0.04 one round dappled disc on a grey slab (judge forest-r4).
- Puddles: on earth, sink the land a few cm under the level (the marsh's `land::puddle` in the path's ruts); the pool's face covers them and the lights glint in them. On paving, no film: the floor's own material darkens (x0.38), smooths (roughness 0.09) and flattens its normal under a sharp ragged water line (the kit's `laid.wesl`, Lagarde's wet surfaces); a pool film over flags mirrored the sky as violet ovals (judge ruin-r8).
- The margin is half the ground's: a band of wet black mud at the water line in the land's `cover` (the mud layer's `wets: 1.0` darkens and glosses it, kit/ground.wesl), reeds standing in the shallows (plants.rs grows them from 0.25 m deep), a mud shelf at the line (`land::shelf` presses heights within 12 cm of -3 cm to half their slope, monotone, unchanged outside) and the lips (`marsh/lips.rs`): stones on the lip 1 to 14 cm deep, 0.5 m apart, each scaled so its top stands 6 to 18 cm out of the water (3 to 10 cm read as nothing at 1080), pebbles in the mud beside them, and roots from `Log.glb` drawn thin (origin at its foot's middle, 1.7 m long) dipping into the water from the bank.
- Glints: a light's mirror image lands about 0.7 x its height toward the camera from its foot, so a glinting light stands on the water's lip or in it; `node ~/.cache/terra-review/iso-art/proj.mjs --focus x,y,z x,y,z...` projects world points to the 960x540 frame at the ARPG camera, so a light, a wisp or a prop is placed in frame before a render (the lantern sat on the bottom edge).

## Shooting and the film (on the render worker, embargoed machines build nothing)

- Every biome at once, measured against its refs and its kept still, with its judges' briefs: `node crates/terra_iso/loop.ts shoot [--biomes a,b] [--film] [--dirty]` ([kit.md](kit.md)). A still runs on the film's fixed step, so unchanged code gives the same still (to 0.0001 of a measure; in real time the fires moved p95 by 0.0025).
- One still by hand, from the repository root, `--dirty` for uncommitted code: `cargo xtask remote-render --gpu --dirty --minutes 40 --collect target/job/amb -- sh -c "mkdir -p target/job/amb; cargo test --release -p terra_iso > target/job/amb/test.txt 2>&1; cargo iso --biome ruin --shot target/job/amb/s.png > target/job/amb/run.txt 2>&1; true"`. Files land in `~/.cache/terra-review/remote/<job>/target/job/`; block with `cargo xtask remote-render --wait <job>`; grep `test result|panicked|ERROR` in its `job.log` and the run file.
- Film: `shoot --film` adds each biome's 16 s film (`--frames 300 --film 480 --shot dir/f.jpg`: 480 frames at 1920x1080, a fixed 30 a game second from frame 300) and encodes it for the desk (crf 28, 1600 wide, under 15 MB) into the round and `~/.cache/terra-review/iso-kit/films/NAME.mp4`. About 3 minutes for all four. The round is filed like any; a film-only round needs no verdict.
- Stills and films go to `~/.cache/terra-review/iso-art/<round>/`, each with its stats line, for the judge and the desk.
