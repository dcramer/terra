# Controller: where each row is measured

The course's files and the game's side of each group of rows, from the [controller skill](../SKILL.md). The open rows: [tasks.md](tasks.md).

The game's walk drives the mover (landed in unit U4d on a fixed-eye A/B, D53; handoff `controller-stage2.md` "Unit U4" to "Unit U4d"): `terra_walker::movement` and its sources in `terra_walker::mover_world` (props, flora, a base by `terra_build_layout::solids`, others, water); the shown figure's step smoothing in `stepping.rs`. A change to the mover that the game feels is checked in the game too: `cargo xtask cabin` (door, stone steps, deck stairs), `cargo xtask collide` (its step read from movers.ron, 0 failures), and `build.contacts` when the player stops where they should not.

Far from the origin (`far.grounded`, `far.float`, `far.queries`, criterion 22, D482) is `course/far.rs`: level ground, 20 deg up and down and 0.3 m steps walked at 2 km and at 30 km (`Course::on`), tick by tick against each other; reverting D482's frames fails all three.

Flights and a doorway's sill are `course/stairs.rs`: the ground's steps 0.1-0.5 m and a base's whole, half and stair cubes, up and down at the kind's jog (`stair.up`, `stair.down`, `stair.hop`), and a floor 0-0.1 m over the ground through a doorway (`door.hop`).

The kind-data rows (`accel.data`, `turn.data`, `air.control`, `slope.speed`, `landing.speed`, `data.kinds`) are `course/data.rs`, run for a man and a test-only second kind; a mover reports facing and landings through `Mover::facing` and `Mover::landed`.

Falls (`fall.harmless`, `fall.speeds`, `fall.rise`, `fall.normal`, `fall.water`, D476) are `course/fall.rs`, for a man and the second kind: the mover reports a landing's harm (`Mover::fall`, `Body::fall`, by `Kind::fall_harm` of the speed into the floor along its normal), checked against `sqrt(2gh)` and the kind's `fall_onset`/`fall_fatal`. The game takes it as `terra_walker::movement::Fell` -> `terra_creatures::combat::take_falls`.

Pushing (`push.*`, D479) is `course/pushes.rs` with `runs.rs`'s share and fixed cases: a step meets another body only by its core (`Kind::core_m`), and `mover/push.rs` eases two soft kinds apart by `soft_s` and parts any pair with a hard kind at once by mass, handing what a wall stops to the other; the course's monster is its own (no monster walks the mover in the game yet). In the game players ease apart from each other and from recruits (`terra_walker::bodies::keep_apart`, a person met by a man's core in `mover_world::others`).

Bodies closing fast (`tunnel.closing`, criterion 1) are `course/closing.rs`: two men closing at 10-60 m/s head on, at a glance and one falling onto another, a meeting failing where their cores came within both cores over a tick's straight motion. Jacobi steps meet the others where they began, so after the steps `mover/swept.rs` casts a long step's core (over 0.75 of radius less core, Jolt's CCD threshold) relative to each other's and puts both of a pair back to their time of impact (handoff "Unit S"). Not covered: a third body crossing where a pair was put back.

What stops a body besides the ground (`step.building`, `step.prop`, `step.rock`, `tunnel.door`) is `course/sources.rs`: a fixture's `obstacles` (`mover::shapes`: turned boxes for a base's cubes, a door's leaf and a prop; `terra_core::Solid` pieces for a rock), answered apart from its ground field. A step is as high as named at its face, where it is climbed: a box laid level there, a rock's crest lowered by the world's curve at its middle.

Water (`swim.shore`, `swim.wade`, `swim.float`, criterion 17) is `course/water.rs`: a lake with smooth shores to 12 and 25 deg walked into, waded, swum across and out, straight and 25 and 45 deg off, and a body floated to rest; water is a medium the mover reads at the feet (`World.water`), never a contact. Wading (`wade.speed`, `wade.step`, D523) is `course/wade.rs`: two kinds jogged on level ground under water 0.025-1.375 of their swim depth, the speed against the kind's `wade` share of a jog at swim depth; `wade.step` is held at 0.62 m/s, a man's 2.58 there against his 2.0 swim (a question for the developers). Diving and breath (`swim.dive`, `swim.rise`, `swim.surface`, `swim.breath`, `swim.bottom`, `swim.exit`, D478) are `course/dive.rs`, for two kinds in a pool 11.8 m deep and the lake: the mover's `mover::swim` (strokes along `Intent::pitch` past `dive_from_deg`, `Intent::rise` for jump held, breath on `Body::breath`, drowning a pulse a second on `Body::drown`); the game passes the view's pitch while forward is held and writes `movement::Drowned`. Climbing out (`swim.ledge`, `swim.ledge.high`, D523) is `course/ledge.rs`: banks 0.35-1.5 of the kind's `climb_out` over deep water swum into straight and 30 deg off, two kinds; the mover's `swim::climb_out` (Unreal's `CheckWaterJump`).

The swim pose (`swim.pose`, D514) is the filmed course's lake run (`yard.ts`'s `lake`: the play world's mountain lake swum out into, dived and surfaced, `lake.mp4` beside `yard.mp4`), judged by `swim.ts` from the record's `swimming`, `shows`, `torso`, `going`; the figure's side is `terra_walker::swim`, its clips made in the water on Uthana (`made.json`, strokes lying and `afloat`; `tools/characters/swim_clips.test.ts` holds them so).

The in-game filmed course (`step.smooth`, `feet.sliding`, `feet.glide`, `turn.foot`, `feet.ground`, `feet.sinking`, `feet.aloft`, `pelvis.drop`, `pelvis.drop_worst`, `film.yard`; stage 1b, unit 2a) is one command, `cargo xtask gpu film -- cargo xtask controller --film` (started in the background, a few minutes; a continuous check, `controller film`): the sandbox's `controller-yard` experiment digs and builds the course's features east of the start (a trench of digs side by side stepping 0.1-0.5 m down to 1.5 m and up, a lump, a base's stairs, doorway, 2 m passage and 1.5 m drop, then a mound past the slope limit), its route jogged and sprinted, filmed through the game's camera, and `tools/controller/yard.ts` measures the rows from each run's walk record (`--fight-log`, the player as a walker: body, `below`, balls, camera; per foot its heel and ball over the world's ground sampled down from them, `terra_creatures::melee::sole`, never the IK's probe, beside its clip's own height over its flat ground, `clip`, and the leg's shortfall from its goal and the lock's alone, `short`, `short_lock`), not by eye; after the route the body turns in place twice (turn.foot's plants), and the route is done once its last mark is reached, so that wait is not stuck; `node tools/controller/yard.ts --check ~/.cache/terra-review/controller-yard` re-judges the records; a run that did not finish leaves only its own rows not measured (`--out DIR` films elsewhere). Film and checks in `~/.cache/terra-review/controller-yard/`. A change to the walker's figure, camera or stepping runs it.
