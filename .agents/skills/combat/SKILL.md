# Combat

Read [docs/movement.md](../../../docs/movement.md) ("The goal", the pipeline) before changing a move, and D450 (clips by Kimodo keyed to the guard), D447 (the move check), D449 (stamina), D451 (the camera), D452 (this ratchet). Design calls (how a move should feel, what a tired fighter can do) are the developers': recommend, put it on the review desk, don't build on it.

## The scorecard and the ratchet (D452)

- `cargo xtask combat`: every cheap criterion of `tools/combat/criteria.json`, a line each, about a minute. `✓` within its bar, `·` below it but held (a task), `✗` worse than `tools/combat/baseline.tsv` (fails), `?` not measurable yet, `-` heavy. `--film` adds the duel film's checks (the GPU: `cargo xtask gpu film -- cargo xtask combat --film`, started in the background, D319); `--out DIR` films into DIR instead of `~/.cache/terra-review/duel/`, which two films at once would share (`cargo xtask gpu --status` shows what runs). A run that did not finish (silent 8 min, stopped) leaves only its own rows not measured, the line naming its log; only a whole film is kept for nightly.
- A number may never get worse. If a change must make one worse (a trade the job chose), raise it in the same commit: `cargo xtask combat --raise "<why>"`, the why naming the gain it pays for. Never hand-edit the baseline, never lower it: a gain is banked by `cargo xtask nightly` (D414).
- `clip.ts --into` runs the scorecard itself and puts every file back when the set is worse.
- A new criterion is a row of `criteria.json` (what a player notices, `noticed` 1-5, bar, source, check, cost, measurable or what it `needs`); its first number goes in with `--raise "<why>"`.

## The loop, one job

1. **Pick** the top failing criterion of the list below (or the one the brief names). One criterion, or one move's criteria together when one clip fixes them.
2. **Change** the least that moves it: a key in `art/animation/sets/fight.json` and `clip.ts`, a number in `assets/data/moves/sword.shielded.ron`, a rule in `crates/terra_motion`.
3. **Score**: `cargo xtask combat`. Nothing `✗`; the criterion's number lower.
4. **Bank**: land on main (AGENTS.md); nightly lowers the baseline. Two jobs without a gain on a criterion: stop, step back (AGENTS.md), and hand it off with what was tried.
5. **Film** when a move changed: `cargo xtask gpu film -- cargo xtask combat --film` (the duel's film and its checks; the film in `~/.cache/terra-review/duel/`). On a clean checkout of main its checks are kept by commit, so nightly banks the film's rows (`--filmed`, D452); a film of uncommitted work is never banked.
6. **Feel** goes to the review desk: a film for the developer's eye in a `FOR REVIEW` line (`review-desk` skill), never a claim in chat that it feels right.

**What the developer saw in play comes first.** Each complaint from play becomes a `film.pose.*` row on the game's composed pose (D530) that fails on main before anything is fixed, and goes to the top of the list. A clip row (moves.ts) never proves a play complaint fixed: on 2026-10-26 the scorecard had gone from 50 to 155 passing while play still showed the head turned 45°, the shield up at rest and the arms in the body, none of which a clip row measured. A job that changes a move, a hold or a strap puts a still from the game's own camera in its `FOR REVIEW` line.

## The task list (failing criteria, 2026-10-25, after job 25)

Ordered by what a player notices most (`noticed`), then by how far out. Re-read it from `cargo xtask combat --json` when it moves; the scorecard is the truth, this list its order. The light chain (slash, backhand, finisher, the partner's slash) and the heavy and partner's heavy are keyed takes (Keyed_*, D450) played as `Open_Slash`, `Open_Backhand` and `Open_Heavy`: the shield arm held low at the left side throughout (upper arm down and out, forearm forward, face out; hold.py `aim`/`roll`, job 20), where the game's guard rests it (`Low_Guard`, the set's `guard`: Keyed_Guard with that arm, D531, job 32), so a blow starts and ends with the shield at the hip and the chain carries nothing (`move.chain.shield_round_trip` 0.00 m; no `chained`/`carry` in the set, D454); the strafes lay the same arm with the forearm a little further out (`Low_Strafe`), clear of the stepping thigh; the roll (Fight_Roll, the library's with the arms held out of the tuck, D454) passes every row; the block (`Clear_Block`: Keyed_Block, the shield raised from the low rest across the face and back down to it, an `out` before an `in`, its raise measured by moves.ts's `raised` bars, with the sword arm eased out to the right behind the board, D454, job 23) passes every row. A landed blow holds 0.03-0.05 s and shows by a burst where the blade met the body (a glow 3 frames and 20 sparks along the blade, `burst`) and a punch of the view, the struck body keeping its own colours (D456, D487, the set's `landed`; `hit.*` rows, measured by the tests that hold them, `hit.flash.frames` the struck body unlit at 0).

The sword through its own shield and the shield through the sword arm are a fault of every move and drawn clip moves.ts checks, not only the blows (D481, job 29): every one clears the heater, so a re-posed hold, a re-hung board or a new take must too, and clip.ts refuses a clashing take.

## The pipeline's commands

| Step | Command |
|---|---|
| The move check, every body template | `node tools/characters/moves.ts` (`--json rows.json` for the numbers, `--set`, `--clips`, `--spec`) |
| A clip from its spec (Kimodo, D450) | `node tools/characters/clip.ts <Clip>` into a scratch set; `--measure` rescores; `--into assets/data/moves/sword.shielded.ron` keeps it, refused if the scorecard is worse |
| A clip's keys, before its takes (D565 step 3): judged, searched, between two | `node tools/characters/keys.ts check <Clip>` (a key past the wrist's bar or too soon after the guard refused); `search <Clip> <at>` (`--edge-toward`, `--swing`, `--twist`, `--dev`, `--grip`, `--yaw=-15`); `between <Clip> <a> guard` for recovery keys. Check every key before `clip.ts` spends a take |
| A shield pose's board place against the chest, on every template, from any commit's assets (D554) | `node tools/characters/shield_place.ts` (`--at <commit>`, `--capture <Pose>` into `art/animation/shield_targets.json`, `--json`) |
| The duel film and its checks | `cargo xtask gpu film -- cargo xtask duel`; `cargo xtask duel --check <fight.jsonl>…` on records already made |
| The fight's rules | `cargo test -p terra_motion` (the duel test among them) |
| The Enshrouded reference | `tools/combat/refs/enshrouded.json` (`targets`, `unmeasured`); how it was read: `research/2026-10-24-enshrouded-combat-measured.md`; its pictures under `~/.cache/terra-review/refs/enshrouded-combat/` |
| The scorecard | `cargo xtask combat` (`--film`, `--json`, `--raise "<why>"`) |
