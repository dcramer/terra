# Realm two: the far realm

Status: **a first slice built, 2026-11-08; its theme is the developers' (D642, Leaning)**: the realm is a row of `assets/data/realms.ron` (`terra_world::realm`): its light, sky, ground colours, the share of plants that grow (one in ten) and the turn of their hue (toward ochre), and five built parts scattered by the seed, standing round the player as they walk (`terra_game::play::realm_parts`); the first realm's gate leads to it. Only the animals its row names live in it (foxes and hares, a stand-in; nothing keeps its den). Its sign shines teal and lights the ground round it by night (a `Light` part and a catalogue `Lamp`). Its ground has its own layers (`assets/materials/ground_industrial`: gravel, asphalt and cracked concrete for the first realm's grass and leaf litter). Its small life is its own (`data/drift.ron`'s `realms`): glass wings by day, lantern moths and glow spores by night, none of the first realm's butterflies, bees or fireflies. Its animals are its own (`animals` in its row; a kind a realm names lives in no other): the Glimmerhorn, the Glimmerdoe and the Moonhare, the red deer's and the brown hare's bodies, skeletons and clips in coats of slate violet with turquoise spots, antlers and ears (`art/creatures/glimmer_*`, `lilac_hare`; 36 credits). It sounds of itself (`birdsong`, `chimes` in its row): none of the first realm's birdsong or calls, and its air rings, soft glassy notes over a low breath (`terra_world::audio::synth::far`). Its monster for now is the Drillhog (D250, placed here by D650, Open). Not built: lights on its other parts; no still has been shown to the developers. The realm beyond the first world's gate (D641): the proof that a realm is data ([v0-tracks.md](v0-tracks.md), Realm two; [art-style.md](art-style.md), "What every realm shares"). It needs enough of each part to show a stranger another world of the same game, not a full realm's worth.

## What it is

**A fantasy and science-fiction world, where the first realm is real and medieval** (D642, the developers, 2026-11-08: "great, and different than the first world"). Another world at a glance: a violet sky and a cool rose sun, a forest of teal leaves over rose and plum grass, plants of its own that glow at night, a land of its own shape, and the standing remains of an older, stranger making. Seen on 2026-11-08 it was the first realm's forest in another colour: its trees, its land's shapes and its grass were the first realm's. That is what is to change, in this order: its look by data (built: sky, sun, leaves, grass, soil), its own plants (grown kinds with their own habits, bark and leaf cards, D222), its night light, its land recipe, its parts (crystal, stone, machines: models, which cost credits), its small life, monsters and sound.

## What grows in it (proposed, 2026-11-08; the developers: "we might not want regular looking trees", the trees' way of making being how to get whatever is chosen detailed)

Not the first realm's trees in another colour. What stands where trees would is its own, each grown by rule as the forest's trees are (a skeleton by its kind, cards on it, a scanned or made surface, levels of detail and a far form: `terra_sandbox::experiments::lush`), so it is as detailed near and as cheap far:

| Kind | What it is | Made as |
|---|---|---|
| Cap trees | Great fungi 8 to 25 m tall: a ribbed stalk, one or several wide caps in tiers, gills that glow at night | A grown skeleton (stalk and cap ribs) with cap and gill surfaces; the modelled FarGiantFungus is its small form. Built: `Habit::Cap` (`grown/caps.rs`), each cap shaped by its age (a bell, a dome, then flat and dished with a waved, lifted, split rim; `caps::Cap`), on a fluted stalk with a skirt ring, all in a skin of their own and no bark (`cap_skin.wesl`, which a point's texture coordinates tell what it is: gills, top, stalk or ring; a top darker at its middle and paler and lined at its rim, streaked along its radius, wet, with raised pale warts thick at its crown and shed in patches, gills under it in their own colour that shine by night, a fibrous scaled stalk, a pleated ring, all its row's: `cap: (tint, gills, glow, stalk)`; research/2026-11-09-great-fungi.md) |
| Frond spires | A tall bare stem with whorls of long drooping fronds, lit at their tips | The conifer's skeleton with its whorls kept and its needles replaced by frond cards |
| Fan trees | A low, wide, forking tree whose branches end in flat translucent fans | The oak's forking skeleton with fan cards at its tips |
| Undergrowth | Lantern bulbs, spore pods, coral fans, coil tendrils, knee to head high, among plum scrub | Models (`art/props/far_*`, `assets/models/nature/far`), rows of the realm's `undergrowth`, laid in drifts and clumps by the floor (built, below) |

**Each is data and each has its levels of detail** (the developers, 2026-11-08: "everything needs somewhat data driven and things will need LOD"; D136, D127): a kind is a row (its habit's numbers, its surface, its cards, the realm it grows in, from how far it is drawn), never a name in code, and it is drawn whole near, simpler at the middle distance and as a far form, as the forest's trees are. The forest's three species fixed in code are to become rows the same way.

Which of these, and how strange, is the developers' to say; the first to make is the cap tree, as it is the least like a tree.

**What it costs** (a reading, not a bench: the high preset on the Radeon 8060S, a full-size world, standing where the player arrives): the first realm at its start 41 frames a second; this realm 57, its forest its own: cap trees 900 a square kilometre drawn to 450 m, frond spires 500 to 320 m and fan trees 300 to 280 m, rows of `flora.ron` that grow `Stood` (the two last the existing grower's habits in the realm's fern and rush cards, data alone), one part a 20 m cell and one in sixteen of the first realm's trees (`trees`, a share of its own beside `plants`). The cap forest first ran at 16: each tree spawned a crown, far crown and shadow stand-ins of empty meshes, six parts in nine; a leafless tree is now its wood alone (`grown::drawn`), and drawn only to 150 m it ran no faster. Measured (`cargo xtask bench far-walk` and `far-walk-first`, the same walk on the same land in each realm; 2026-11-09, the same card, release, 1920x1080, three runs each): the far realm a median 12.5 ms a frame, a 99th percentile of 16.8 and no hitch; the first realm 17.1 ms, 23.7 and twelve hitches: the far realm is the lighter by 4.6 ms, its cap forest standing for most of the first realm's trees. Of its own dressing, its parts are 0.4 ms (12.07 without them), its drifting life 0.1 and its animals 0.3; a part is stood only within its own sight (963 for 1,510). An earlier reading here had it 2.8 ms the heavier: that was against `play-walk`, a small default world, not this land.

**Denser, and none of the first realm's trees** (2026-11-09; the developers: "its not lush", "mostly just a shitty version of a scifi realm right now with mostly normal realm stuff"): seen beside the first realm it was a meadow, its own trees a tenth as close as the first realm's, one in sixteen of the first realm's spruces standing among them, its undergrowth halved, and its grass blades teal on a land drawn magenta past them. Now cap trees 2,600 a square kilometre, frond spires 1,500, fan trees 1,200, great fungi 300 and crystal 120 (a part a 10 m cell, 10,000 at most), none of the first realm's trees (`trees: 0`), all its undergrowth (`plants: 1`), and its grass turned further than its leaves (`grass_hue`, `Tweaks::grass_hue`) to the rose and plum its ground is. `cargo xtask bench far-walk`, one run each on a busy machine (an upper bound): a median 12.8 ms a frame before, 14.1 after (with one in sixteen of the first realm's trees still standing), the GPU 7.4 to 8.2. Still the first realm's: the grass's shapes; the caps are smooth discs with ruled gills.

**Its floor is its own** (far-flora j1, 2026-11-09; the developers: "the scifi realm shoudl be exotic too"). Two things were wrong. Its floor was bare: the woods' floor is laid only among the trees on the floor's map, which are the first realm's, and this realm grows none (`trees: 0`), so a row of pieces the first realm lays 2,071 plants on laid none; a realm with none of the first realm's trees now lays its floor on all its dry ground (`woods_floor::among_its_trees`). And what would have grown was the first realm's. Now the realm's row names its undergrowth (`undergrowth` in `realms.ron`, `terra_world::realm::Undergrowth`, D136): four kinds of its own, each a row (its models, clumps tried a hectare, clump size and spread, how grown, how far drawn, where its far level takes over, its drifts' breadth, how it likes dry ground and damp), and the first realm's kinds it keeps (`keeps`: the scrub and bramble its leaf atlas turns plum; bracken, ferns, herbs, hazel, holly, saplings, young spruce and the rest no longer grow there).

| Kind | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern bulb | A rosette of dark indigo leaves, five pale teal bulbs on stalks that shine at night, 0.6 m | The damp hollows, in carpets |
| Spore pods | A cluster of ribbed teal pods on a mound of roots, the tallest split open, 0.8 m | The damp hollows |
| Coral fan | A bone-pale branching fan on a dark foot, 1.3 m | Dry ground |
| Coil tendril | Four fleshy stalks ending in fiddlehead coils with pale nodules, 1.7 m | Dry ground most, the damp too |

Each is one Tripo model (50 credits each; `art/props/far_*`), drawn in its own textures, grown to 0.6 to 1.4 of its size and turned by the dice: **four kinds, told apart by shape and height, each of one model** (D182: a second and third model of each is the next thing they need; no two plants of a kind differ but in size and turn). Each has a far level (`<file>_far.glb`, 400 to 600 triangles for the near level's 1,500 to 2,400; `tools/props`' new `far`) from 14 to 22 m, and is drawn to 55 to 90 m. Laid within 220 m of seed 3's start: 43,058 plants of undergrowth in the first realm; in this one (before the bulbs were thinned by a third) 65,647. They cast no shadow, do not sway and take no snow: they are models in a standard material, not the grown plants' foliage. They are not placed by the realm's own trees' shade: its cap trees are not on the floor's map, so its floor reads every place as open.

## Order of work

1. The realm as data: `realms.ron`, read on entering a world of its kind; the light, sky and ground colours from it (replacing the placeholder realm's code). Check: a scripted crossing reads the realm's name and its sun and ground colours from the game.
2. The first built parts: four or five props, concept then model, each checked against the style guide and the model contract.
3. Their scattering by the seed, clear of the gate and the start, with their solids.
4. Fewer plants. Stills beside the first realm for the developers.
