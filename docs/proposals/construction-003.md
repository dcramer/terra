# Construction 003: Space Engineers 2's ground and construction loop

Status: **Open** (D491). A candidate, for the developers to adopt in part or whole; nothing here is built on until they do. The research is [research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md](../../research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md).

## What it answers

The developers, 2026-10-25: "space engineers 2 is actually more the kind of voxel terrain system that we want. its SLIGHTLY better than enshrouded but its fully procedural thats the main diff. that means things like mining behave differently than they currently do, and we MIGHT focus on just making the core building system block-based (with the ability to carve it out) so the buiding system can behave more like space engineers where you deliver resources (which is kind of what we're doing already) and less like enshrouded".

Our world is already procedural (D8) on a 3D density field (D9), and our construction loop is already SE's in outline: free plans, built part by part from what is delivered, taken back whole. What would change is mining, and how a block is built and taken down. The look is a separate question (below).

## The ground: mining as Space Engineers 2's

| | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Digging | A 0.7 m scoop every 0.9 s of holding | Held, the hole growing continuously ahead of the tool while held, as SE's drill; the pickaxe's swings kept as the motion, the ground going a little with each |
| How fast | One rate, by the tool's material for ore (crafting.md) | The tool's tier times the ground's hardness: soil and sand go fast, stone slow, ore slower; a tool too weak for a material does not cut it (crafting.md, unchanged) |
| What it gives | Stone, scree and ore; soil and sand nothing | Every ground material something, by volume: soil (earth for the earth kit, D236), sand (glass, crafting.md), stone, ore |
| Ore | Veins of one ore through all stone, everywhere | Deposits: bodies of ore of several kinds, deeper and richer by region, uneven across a world (SE2's sectors), so a mine is somewhere worth an outpost (tribe.md) |
| Finding it | Ore rocks stained on the surface (D383) | The same stain where a deposit reaches the surface, in its rock and the scree below it, so you find ore by looking (D93); a finer way (a dowser's tool, a skill) later, not a detector |
| Hardness in the world | None | Later, not now: soft ground giving under a fall or a heavy step, as SE2's |

Technical, and the agent's once the design is chosen: continuous digging sends a swept shape per stretch of holding as one operation, not one per frame, so D21's operations stay few; deposits are generated by the world's data (D136), each realm its own kinds.

## Construction: blocks built and taken down as Space Engineers 2's

The cube stays the truth (D181, D192): half-metre cubes on each base's grid, drawn by their kit. What would change is the loop round each one:

- **Everything placed is a plan first**, brushes included (D226): the brush lays ghosts of its box, as SE's projections, and the whole base can be laid out free before anything is gathered (D139, unchanged).
- **A block is built by working it**: hold the hammer at a plan and the cubes aimed at go in, one after another, each through stages drawn as it fills (D358's drawing, staged), the material taken as it goes, from the pack, then storage in the claim (D141), then later carts and recruits bringing it (D90). Logs and other big materials still carried and set (D140) or not is a question below.
- **An area at once**: the brush's size works a box of plan at a time, as SE2's area welder, so a 25 cm grid's tedium does not come to a half-metre one.
- **Taking down is working it backwards**: Q and hold, a block's stages coming out in reverse and everything given back; damage lowers a block's stages the same way, and repair works it up again (D305).
- **Carving**: cutting any box out of what is built, an opening or a room, as D233 does now; taken down as above.

This lifts the pause on costs and storage in D333's baseline: building would spend what is delivered, not infinite pretend resources.

## What stays

- The terrain's representation and its edits (D9, D21): SE2's ground is voxels as ours is; only how we dig changes.
- The grid: half-metre cubes (D181), not SE2's 25 cm with blocks of many sizes; a kit already draws many cubes as one piece, a log or a beam (D146).
- Plans as ghosts walked through, and nothing floats (D192).
- The look, unless the developers say otherwise: D211's bar, Enshrouded's blending of cubes by what they touch, judged on our kits. SE2's blocks are modelled each, with bevels at their edges; for log and stone buildings the blending is what makes them read as one building.

## Questions for the developers

On the desk, each with a recommendation:

1. **What "block-based" changes**: the construction loop only, keeping D211's look (recommended), or also the look, blocks drawn as their own models with bevels as SE2's.
2. **What carving means**: cutting a box out of what is built, as now (recommended); a block cut to a finer shape, below half a metre; or carving rooms out of the ground (D143).
3. **Mining**: held and continuous, by tool tier and hardness, every material giving something (recommended); or the scoops as now with SE2's yields only.
4. **Ore**: deposits uneven by region and depth, found by their surface signs (recommended); or veins everywhere, as now.

Left open in this proposal, to answer once those are: whether players can put ground back (SE2: no; Enshrouded: yes); whether big materials are still carried to a plan (D140, D296) or every material comes from the pack and storage, as SE's.
