# Construction 003: Space Engineers 2's ground and construction loop

Status: **Open** (D491). A candidate, for the developers to adopt in part or whole; nothing here is built on until they do. The research is [research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md](../../research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md).

## What it answers

The developers, 2026-10-25: "space engineers 2 is actually more the kind of voxel terrain system that we want. its SLIGHTLY better than enshrouded but its fully procedural thats the main diff. that means things like mining behave differently than they currently do, and we MIGHT focus on just making the core building system block-based (with the ability to carve it out) so the buiding system can behave more like space engineers where you deliver resources (which is kind of what we're doing already) and less like enshrouded".

Our world is already procedural (D8) on a 3D density field (D9), and our construction loop is already SE's in outline: free plans, built part by part from what is delivered, taken back whole. What would change is mining, and how a block is built and taken down. The look is a separate question (below).

## The ground: mining as Space Engineers 2's

| | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Digging | A 0.7 m scoop every 0.9 s of holding | Held, the hole growing continuously ahead of the tool while held, as SE's drill; the pickaxe's swings kept as the motion, the ground going a little with each |
| How fast | One rate, by the tool's material for ore (crafting.md) | The tool's tier times the ground's hardness: soil and sand go fast, stone slow, ore slower; a tool too weak for a material does not cut it (crafting.md, unchanged) |
| What it gives | Stone, scree and ore; soil and sand nothing | Every ground material something, by volume: soil (earth for the earth kit, D236), sand (glass, crafting.md), stone, ore |
| Ore | Veins of one ore through all stone, everywhere | Deposits: bodies of ore of several kinds, deeper and richer by region, uneven across a world (SE2's sectors), so a mine is somewhere worth an outpost (tribe.md) |
| Finding it | Ore rocks stained on the surface (D383) | The same stain where a deposit reaches the surface, in its rock and the scree below it, so you find ore by looking (D93); a finer way (a dowser's tool, a skill) later, not a detector |
| Hardness in the world | None | Later, not now: soft ground giving under a fall or a heavy step, as SE2's |

Technical, and the agent's once the design is chosen: continuous digging sends a swept shape per stretch of holding as one operation, not one per frame, so D21's operations stay few; deposits are generated by the world's data (D136), each realm its own kinds.

## Construction: prefab parts, built and taken down as Space Engineers 2's

The developers, 2026-10-25: "block based mostly means the build loop - so like, instead of it being place each voxel and what not, maybe we'll focus on more prefab parts? but i think walls/etc you could still carve out? maybe its not all that diff but space engineers 2 is really well done here".

SE2 builds from a catalogue of parts by purpose (armour cubes, slopes, corners and halves; trusses, catwalks, windows; working blocks), each named by its size, snapping to what it is set against by mount points, every part placed a hologram that joins a build queue. Its parts are models and cannot be carved; Valheim's and Rust's are models too. Proposed: the same catalogue and loop, each part a **stamp of half-metre cubes** in its kit, so the cube stays the truth (D181, D192) and carving, rooms (D261), damage (D305), fire (D377) and D211's look all work on parts as they do on cubes now. What differs from Enshrouded is that you place parts, not paint voxels.

- **The parts**, the same in every kit, as data (D136): walls 1 and 2 m wide, a storey or half one high; a post; a beam; floors 1 and 2 m square; a stair a storey up; roof slopes, ridges, hips, valleys and eaves at the kit's pitch; a doorway wall and a window wall (a wall with its hole, D225's doors and shutters fitted); and cube boxes of 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 m for footings, terraces and fill. Hold R for a part's sizes and variants, as now.
- **Snapping**: a part snaps to the edge of the part aimed at, as SE2's mount points do (a wall to a floor's edge or a wall's end, a floor to a wall's top, a roof slope to a wall's top or the slope below), and to the base's grid otherwise. Drawing a wall as a line or a room as a rectangle (D152, D154) lays a run of parts.
- **Everything placed is a plan first**, as SE's projections: free, ghosts walked through (D139, unchanged). The plans in a claim add up to one list of what they need and how much is in reach, SE2's build queue.
- **A part is built by working it**: hold the hammer at it and it goes in, in its kit's physical order (a log wall course by course, a stone wall stone by stone, D139), drawn as far as it is filled (D358), the material taken as it goes, from the pack, then storage in the claim (D141), later carts and recruits (D90). The brush's size works a box of plans at once, SE2's area welder. Whether logs are still carried and set (D140) is a question below.
- **Taking down is working it backwards**: Q and hold, a part coming out in reverse and everything given back; damage takes a part's cubes the same way, and repair works it up again (D305).
- **Carving**: any box cut out of a part, planned or built, as D233 does now: a doorway or a window anywhere in a wall, its cubes given back, the rest of the part standing.

Amends D226 and D253: the hammer leads with parts, not brushes painting cubes; roofs are still built by hand, of roof parts, to any shape (D241, D343). Lifts the pause on costs and storage in D333's baseline: building would spend what is delivered, not infinite pretend resources.

## What stays

- The terrain's representation and its edits (D9, D21): SE2's ground is voxels as ours is; only how we dig changes.
- The grid: half-metre cubes (D181), not SE2's 25 cm; a part is a stamp of them, and a kit already draws many cubes as one piece, a log or a beam (D146).
- Plans as ghosts walked through, and nothing floats (D192).
- The look: D211's bar, Enshrouded's blending of cubes by what they touch, judged on our kits ("block based mostly means the build loop").

## Questions for the developers

Answered in chat, 2026-10-25:

1. **What "block-based" changes**: the build loop, with prefab parts in place of placing each voxel; not the look.
2. **What carving means**: walls and the rest can still be carved out.

On the desk, each with a recommendation:

3. **Mining**: held and continuous, by tool tier and hardness, every material giving something (recommended); or the scoops as now with SE2's yields only.
4. **Ore**: deposits uneven by region and depth, found by their surface signs (recommended); or veins everywhere, as now.
5. **What a part is**: a stamp of cubes, carved and drawn as cubes are (recommended); or its own model, as SE2's, Valheim's and Rust's, with openings only as fixed variants and D211's blending lost on it.
6. **Placing a single cube**: kept, as the smallest part in the catalogue (recommended); or parts only, carving the one act on a single cube.

Left open, to answer once those are: whether players can put ground back (SE2: no; Enshrouded: yes); whether big materials are still carried to a plan (D140, D296) or every material comes from the pack and storage, as SE's.
