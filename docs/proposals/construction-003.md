# Construction 003: Space Engineers 2's ground and construction loop

Status: **Decided** (D491 for construction, D495 for mining, D494 for the modes, D296 Leaning for carrying), 2026-10-25. The research is [research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md](../../research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md).

## What it answers

The developers, 2026-10-25: "space engineers 2 is actually more the kind of voxel terrain system that we want. its SLIGHTLY better than enshrouded but its fully procedural thats the main diff. that means things like mining behave differently than they currently do, and we MIGHT focus on just making the core building system block-based (with the ability to carve it out) so the buiding system can behave more like space engineers where you deliver resources (which is kind of what we're doing already) and less like enshrouded".

Our world is already procedural (D8) on a 3D density field (D9), and our construction loop is already SE's in outline: free plans, built part by part from what is delivered, taken back whole. What would change is mining, and how a block is built and taken down. The look is a separate question (below).

## The ground: mining by hits, ore in deposits

Decided as D495: "mining should be like, not drill, but you hit it like a pickaxe or whatever gear you have (it might vary.. maybe drills come later). for the most part hitting it 1-3 times should chip away a chunk and collect the resources"; "ore will be deposits".

| | Now | Decided (D495) |
|---|---|---|
| Digging | A 0.7 m scoop every 0.9 s of holding | Hits of the tool in hand: one to three chip a chunk away, collected into the pack; other gear later, perhaps drills |
| How many hits | One rate, by the tool's material for ore (crafting.md) | By the tool and how hard the ground is; a tool too weak for a material does not cut it |
| What it gives | Stone, scree and ore by volume; soil and sand nothing | By volume, as now (`ground.ron`); soil and sand once something uses them (the earth kit, D236; glass, crafting.md) |
| Ore | One ore in veins at the same share through all stone, everywhere | Deposits of several kinds, uneven by region and depth, most ground with none |
| Finding it | Ore rocks stained on the surface (D383) | The same stain where a deposit meets the surface, in its rock and the scree below (D93) |

Technical, and the agent's: a chunk a few hits is one of D21's operations, as a scoop is now; deposits are generated by the world's data (D136), each realm its own kinds.

## Construction: prefab parts, built and taken down as Space Engineers 2's

The developers, 2026-10-25: "block based mostly means the build loop - so like, instead of it being place each voxel and what not, maybe we'll focus on more prefab parts? but i think walls/etc you could still carve out? maybe its not all that diff but space engineers 2 is really well done here".

SE2 builds from a catalogue of parts by purpose (armour cubes, slopes, corners and halves; trusses, catwalks, windows; working blocks), each named by its size, snapping to what it is set against by mount points, every part placed a hologram that joins a build queue. Its parts are models and cannot be carved; Valheim's and Rust's are models too. Proposed, and the developers agree on the cubes: the same catalogue and loop, each part a **stamp of half-metre cubes** in its kit, so the cube stays the truth (D181, D192) and carving, rooms (D261), damage (D305), fire (D377) and D211's look all work on parts as they do on cubes now. What differs from Enshrouded is that you place parts, not paint voxels.

- **The parts**, the same in every kit, as data (D136): walls 1 and 2 m wide, a storey or half one high; a post; a beam; floors 1 and 2 m square; a stair a storey up; roof slopes, ridges, hips, valleys and eaves at the kit's pitch; a doorway wall and a window wall (a wall with its hole, D225's doors and shutters fitted); and cube boxes of 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 m for footings, terraces and fill. Hold R for a part's sizes and variants, as now.
- **Snapping**: a part snaps to the edge of the part aimed at, as SE2's mount points do (a wall to a floor's edge or a wall's end, a floor to a wall's top, a roof slope to a wall's top or the slope below), and to the base's grid otherwise. Drawing a wall as a line or a room as a rectangle (D152, D154) lays a run of parts.
- **Everything placed is a plan first**, as SE's projections: free, ghosts walked through (D139, unchanged). The hammer does this already: every brush box, shape, wall and room lays plans (`terra_building::tool::aim`, `terra_build::plans`), and holding the hammer at one fills it (`Op::Fill`); only the costs are pretend (D333). The plans in a claim add up to one list of what they need and how much is in reach, SE2's build queue.
- **A part is built by working it**: hold the hammer at it and it goes in, in its kit's physical order (a log wall course by course, a stone wall stone by stone, D139), drawn as far as it is filled (D358), the material taken as it goes, from the pack, then storage in the claim (D141), later carts and recruits (D90). The brush's size works a box of plans at once, SE2's area welder. Logs and other big materials are still carried and set (D140).
- **One thing at a time, or a whole structure**: the developers, 2026-10-25: "i think the main difference is we should be able to build one thing at a time vs building the entire structure from ghosts?" Then: "big parts will not place at once only individual cubes". So a part goes down as a ghost, one or a whole structure of them, and is built after: the hammer, or the tool its material takes, builds the ghost aimed at, in any order, once what it rests on stands (nothing floats, D192, D193), not only the plan's next part as now. Only a single cube is placed and built at once.
- **Taking down is working it backwards**: Q and hold, a part coming out in reverse and everything given back; damage takes a part's cubes the same way, and repair works it up again (D305).
- **Carving**: any box cut out of a part, planned or built, as D233 does now: a doorway or a window anywhere in a wall, its cubes given back, the rest of the part standing.
- **A single cube is placed at once**: "you'd have to have the materials in your inventory and it'd immediately place as long as it followed structural rules (e.g. not floating, connected to something)": built where it goes from what you carry, no ghost, if it rests on or joins something built or the ground (D193).
- **Big materials are carried by hand** (D140), on the shoulder or in the hands with no weapon out, varying by material, piled up at the build, perhaps on a resource pile, and built in from there (D296, Leaning).
- **What is destroyed comes back as a ghost**: a part lost to an attack, fire or collapse, not taken down with the hammer, is left as its ghost in place to build again (D355, decided and deferred; "we'll auto replace a destroyed part w/ a ghost i think"), as SE2 marks what is missing against the blueprint.

So the loop is ours already; what is new is the catalogue of parts and their snapping, the claim's build list, and delivery made real. Amends D226 and D253: the hammer leads with parts, not brushes painting cubes; roofs are still built by hand, of roof parts, to any shape (D241, D343). Costs come with the modes (D494): creative has infinite resources and is what the developers test with; survival spends what is delivered, which lifts D333's pause there.

## What stays

- The terrain's representation and its edits (D9, D21): SE2's ground is voxels as ours is; only how we dig changes.
- The grid: half-metre cubes (D181), not SE2's 25 cm; a part is a stamp of them, and a kit already draws many cubes as one piece, a log or a beam (D146).
- Plans as ghosts walked through, and nothing floats (D192).
- The look: D211's bar, Enshrouded's blending of cubes by what they touch, judged on our kits ("block based mostly means the build loop").

## Questions for the developers

Answered in chat, 2026-10-25:

- **1. What "block-based" changes**: the build loop, with prefab parts in place of placing each voxel; not the look.
- **2. What carving means**: walls and the rest can still be carved out.
- **5. What a part is**: made of cubes, as now: "so things still need to made of cubes like they are now. i think that part of our system can stay? but each block would need to be placed as a ghost first, then constructed by delivering the parts".
- **3. Mining** and **4. Ore**: hits, not a drill; ore in deposits (D495).
- **6. Placing a single cube**: yes, at once from what you carry, if it is not floating.
- **Carrying**: big materials are still carried by hand (D140).
- **7. One thing at a time**: "big parts will not place at once only individual cubes": a part is a ghost first, the one aimed at built.
- **Putting ground back**: "ground can be filled back in with the right tools+materials (we'll figure out later)" (D495).
- **Modes**: "we should have a mode, creative vs survival, and creative has infinite resources, which is what we'll use to test a lot" (D494, decided); "for now creative will only give infinite resources".

Nothing is left open on the desk.
