# Essences, abilities and ranks

Part of [game design 001](README.md). A proposal for the developers to react to; nothing here is decided unless it cites a decision.

## What the developers have said

- Many skills, gained by doing and improved by doing more (D88).
- *He Who Fights With Monsters* as a core basis for mechanics and how the world works (D89).
- Some on-screen display and character stats (D93).

## Based on

**He Who Fights With Monsters.** What I could confirm: a person absorbs essences, each giving abilities themed on it; three essences together produce a fourth, the confluence, from their combination. As I recall, unchecked: abilities and people climb ranks (iron, bronze, silver, gold, diamond), and each step changes what an ability does, not only its strength.

From the developers, 2026-10-11 (D98): use the books' system as the baseline and a guide, and tweak it where the game needs.

## Three layers

From the developers on 2026-10-11 (D124): in the books, not everything comes from essences; some magic, such as astral magic, is a technique you learn and study, and so are combat styles, learned from books and practice. So there are three ways to grow, not two. As with everything here, the developers will invent their own, drawing on literature and games (D125).

| | Skills | Studied techniques | Essences |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | What you can do with your hands: swim, climb, chop, smith, a weapon | Disciplines anyone can learn: magic such as astral magic, and combat styles | Powers: what makes you more than a person |
| How you get one | By doing it (D88) | By study: from a teacher, a text or a ruin that shows it (D92), then practice | By finding and absorbing an essence |
| How many | Many, small | A few disciplines, each deep | A few, large |
| How it grows | By doing it more | By studying and working it, through ranks | Abilities by use, through ranks |
| What it says about you | What you have practised | What you have learned | Who you are |

Proposed for studied techniques, Open:

- **Studied magic** is slow and deliberate, done in places and with materials rather than in the heat of a fight: wards and circles, binding, opening and closing ways (D72), reading magic.
- **A combat style** is a way to use a kind of weapon: its stances, combinations and timing. You learn its first form from a book or a teacher and the rest by practice, so a style shapes how a weapon skill (D88) plays rather than replacing it, and a style learned with one sword carries to another.
- **Both are the clearest home for the realms' learned lore** ([settings.md](settings.md)): one realm's people may teach a discipline or style no one else knows. A book is something found, kept and carried, so learning fits finding (D68, D92). Recruits who know one can practise or teach it in your claims (D90, D123).

## Skills on the rank ladder

Asked by the developers on 2026-10-11: can skills rank up iron, bronze and so on, after the plan? Proposed, Open: **yes, on the same ladder as everything else**, so monsters, places, portals, materials, essences and skills are all read one way.

- **Each skill climbs iron, bronze, silver, gold, diamond**, with many small steps inside a rank that come often, and a rank-up now and then as a milestone. That is the feel of the grinding games with many skills: always a step coming, sometimes a big one.
- **A rank-up opens something**, not only bigger numbers: a technique, a kind of recipe, a move. As in the books, where a rank changes what an ability does.
- **A skill's rank decides what rank of thing you can work.** An iron-rank smith cannot work silver-rank metal; an iron-rank miner chips at a bronze-rank vein slowly, if at all. Climbing a skill then has a place in the world to go to, the way level requirements pull a player along in RuneScape.
- **Separate ladders.** Essences make your rank as a person, which decides what you can survive (D98); skills decide what you can make and do. A gold-rank crafter can be an iron-rank fighter.
- **Recommended: skills may run ahead of your own rank**, so a crafter or gatherer has a whole game of their own. Open: the books might say a person's rank caps what their hands can do.
- **Grinding, but never a chore** (D122): every step should feel like progress. Repetition for its own sake is where the grind games lose people.

## How essences would work

- **Essences are found**, mostly from monsters of their kind and from strong pooling places: fire from fire things, wing from fliers. What you can become depends on where you hunt, which is a reason to explore (D91).
- **A few slots**, as in the books: three essences, and a confluence formed by the three together. The combination is the player's identity, chosen by what they absorb, with no class picked at the start.
- **Each essence grants abilities**, which open over time and improve by use, keeping D88's rule.
- **Ranks are the spine.** Your rank rises as your abilities do. Monsters, places and portals have ranks too ([arc.md](arc.md)).
- **Read on the character screen** (D93): your essences, abilities, ranks and skills. Others' ranks are judged by looking.

## Mana

The books' fighters spend mana on abilities. From the developers (D102): **there is mana as well as stamina**, a pool of its own beside health and stamina (D61). Shown with them, in blue, as the developers said on 2026-10-11. Proposed: filled by rest and food ([survival.md](survival.md)).

## Questions

1. Answered: **the books' system is the baseline**, as a guide we may tweak (D98).
2. **Can an essence be removed** or replaced? Maybe; the developers expect it to be complex and are not sure yet.
3. Answered: **mana and stamina** are both pools (D102).
4. **What rank does a player start at**, and how long is a rank?
5. **Skills and essences together**: does a high skill change what an ability does (a skilled archer's fire arrow)?
6. **Skills on the rank ladder**, as proposed above, and **may a skill run ahead of your own rank**?
7. **Which grinding games are the reference** for many skills: RuneScape, Ultima Online, Wurm Online, Star Wars Galaxies, the Elder Scrolls games, or others?
8. **Studied techniques**: which disciplines and styles, how they are learned, and whether they rank on the same ladder?
