# Your character: body, movement and what is on screen

Part of [game design 001](README.md). A proposal for the developers to react to; nothing here is decided unless it cites a decision.

## What the developers have said

- A player model, walking around with tools (D50); the character swims (D74); gear is yours wherever you go (D86).
- Rich systems, little on screen, somewhat realistic, with some on-screen display and character stats (D93). No hand-holding (D92).
- Equipment slots (D64), a backpack (D65, D84) and an action bar (D66).

## Who you are

- **No class at the start.** What you become comes from what you practise (D88) and what essences you take ([essences.md](essences.md)).
- **Creation is the look**: body, face, hair, voice.
- **Where you come from** is open (premise question 8 in the [README](README.md#questions-for-the-developers)): a newcomer to the frontier, or someone born there.

## Getting around

Worlds are round planets large enough that walking round is ridiculous (D4, D13), so how you cross them matters.

- **On foot**: walk, sprint, jump, climb, swim, all on stamina. Climbing is a skill by doing (D88).
- **No glider or grappling hook** for now; they can be added later if wanted.
- **Faster**: fast travel between your claims, and portals and waystones you have found or built (D72); you may carry materials (D72).
- **Roads** worn by travellers between settlements ([people.md](people.md)).
- **Mounts**: probably, by taming (D100, [creatures.md](creatures.md)). Boats: open.

## What is on screen

Within D93: what your body would feel, and a few compromises for play.

| Shown | Not shown |
|---|---|
| Health, stamina, mana ([essences.md](essences.md)), breath under water | A minimap, quest markers, objective arrows |
| The action bar, what is in hand | Names and levels floating over people and monsters |
| A character screen: essences, abilities, ranks, skills, equipment, backpack | A journal (D92) |
| The map, only where you have been, with what you mark, and where you are (D76) | What you have not explored |

**Sound carries information**: a monster's call, a settlement's bell, rain coming, a predator behind you. Music sparse, and from the world where it can be.

## Questions

1. Answered: **third person**, first person perhaps optional later (D97).
2. Answered: **no glider or grappling hook** for now.
3. Answered for now: **the map shows where you are**, and **only where you have been**: the rest is hidden until explored (D76). Whether to stop showing your position, as Outward does, is for later.
4. **A compass**: on screen, as an item, or none?
5. **Names over heads**: none at all, or other players' names only?
