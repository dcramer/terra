# The arc: the start, the long game, and playing together

Part of [game design 001](README.md). A proposal for the developers to react to; nothing here is decided unless it cites a decision.

## What the developers have said

- Premises: not "you came from elsewhere"; the frontier (A) and a spreading threat (B) are interesting (game design 001).
- Realms with no fixed number, reached by portals (D17, D72). A persistent world for many players (D52), with no PvP (D2) and no fail state (D18).
- No hand-holding (D92).

## The fiction

Open, and the developers' to write. What it has to answer: what the realms are and why portals join them; what magic is and why it pools; whether there are gods, as there are in the books; and, for premise B, what is spreading and why. My suggestion: say little and let players find it, in ruins, in what people believe, and in what the portals lead to.

## The first hour

Without tutorials or markers (D92), the start has to teach by its shape.

- **You begin with little**, at the frontier, in a gentle place: low-rank land, mild weather, a settlement within sight of smoke.
- **The first needs teach the first tools**: shelter, fire, water, food, a stone axe ([survival.md](survival.md), [crafting.md](crafting.md)).
- **The first people teach the rest**: the nearby settlement's requests lead to the first claim, the first monster, the first rescue ([people.md](people.md)).
- **The first claim** is where it becomes yours.

## The long game

- **Ranks are the ladder** ([essences.md](essences.md)): iron-rank lands and portals, then bronze, and up. A world, or a region of one, has a rank; portals lead to higher ones.
- **Each rank brings** new materials, new monsters, new essences, new peoples.
- **The spread** (premise B) is the long threat: something growing across realms, pushed back by held ground and by striking at its sources, as Enshrouded's Shroud is. It gives the long game a direction without a fixed story.
- **Holds grow**: from a camp, to an outpost, to a settlement of recruits, to a stretch of the wild kept safe.
- **There is no end**, in a persistent sandbox; there are summits: the highest ranks, the hardest realms, the greatest holds.

## Playing together

- **A few friends** share claims, quests and kills; the small case (D51).
- **Groups** hold claims together and share stations, recruits and travel ([construction.md](../../construction.md#claims)).
- **Many players at once** is where this world earns a thousand: a surge on a city, a frontier pushed back by everyone near it, a great monster that needs a crowd. What the server can carry decides how many (D52).

## Questions

1. **The fiction**: who writes it, and how much is said?
2. **Premise B**: what spreads?
3. **Where you start**: one starting world for everyone, or each their own?
4. **Rank of worlds**: whole worlds of one rank, or ranks by region within a world?
5. **Server-wide events**: run by the world's systems, or by us?
