# Decisions

The source of truth for what is settled. Other docs must agree with this one.

**Who decides:** design decisions (gameplay, art, scope, what the game is) belong to the developers. Technical decisions (engine, architecture, tooling, workflow) are made by the agent, recorded here with the reason, and can be overruled. The Owner column says which kind a decision is.

**Who made it:** the By column says who made the call. **Developers**: they said it, chose it or agreed to it; only they change it. **Agent**: an AI agent chose it, a technical question or a design question the developers left to it; it is a soft decision. An agent may change a soft decision when it has a reason, recording the reason and a log line, without asking; anything the developers say overrules it; and when they confirm one, it becomes theirs. If the developers asked for something and the agent chose how, the how is the agent's. Open, superseded and rejected items have no one (—).

**Everything here is provisional.** This is pre-production: the whole record is our current thinking, written down so we can keep working from it, and any of it may change. The statuses say how firm an item is relative to the others, not that anything is locked.

**Decided**: our current answer; don't reopen without a reason. **Leaning**: working assumption, untested or unconfirmed. **Open**: not chosen. **Rejected**: considered and dropped. **Superseded**: replaced by a later decision. Rejected and superseded items are kept in their own section below the main table, so their numbers still resolve.

A new decision goes in its area's section with the next free number. **By area:** [The game as a whole](#the-game-as-a-whole) · [Worlds and realms](#worlds-and-realms) · [The player and survival](#the-player-and-survival) · [Items and crafting](#items-and-crafting) · [Lore, magic, combat and growth](#lore-magic-combat-and-growth) · [People and the tribe](#people-and-the-tribe) · [Building](#building) · [Art, characters and sound](#art-characters-and-sound) · [Engine and code](#engine-and-code) · [Tools and workflow](#tools-and-workflow) · [Superseded and rejected](#superseded-and-rejected) · [Log](#log)
