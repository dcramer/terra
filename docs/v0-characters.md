# v0 characters: log and next steps

The people of v0: the player's body, what it wears, how it stands and moves, as [v0-goals.md](v0-goals.md) asks of them (the creator of a few choices so two players tell each other apart, D111; equipping gear; the boss's mark shown on the arm, D590; locomotion and fight sets earning the developers' yes). How characters are made is [characters.md](characters.md); this page is where the work stands and what comes next, kept short, newest last. Pick-up point for whoever continues it.

## Decided

- **D599**: Terra's people are built on Quaternius's base bodies and modular outfits, in our own paint. The developers, 2026-11-02: "the quarternius stuff is clearly good, and its way better than our stuff. so you're going to stick with it. but we might have an issue w/ clothing/etc".
- **Their parts stay as made**: meshes, UVs, normals, weights, the split into pieces and the bones are never changed by our tools (no clipping pushes, stretch, scale, retopology or decimation). We add paint (new maps on their UVs) and new pieces they lack; a new piece that clips is fixed, never theirs. A check proves every imported piece matches its source vertex for vertex. The developers: "just dont fuck up their parts/etc".
- **D588**: moves no source does well are keyed from a generated pose sheet ([keyed-moves.md](keyed-moves.md)). The idle in the game today is one, on the old body.
- **No twist bones** (D599): their 65 joints and weights stay as made; a forearm wringing in a sword swing goes back to the developers with the film.
- **The swap** (D600): an empty slot shows a bare piece cut exactly from a copy of their body.
- **The starting kit's clothes are the Ranger outfit** (D602): coat = its body and arms, its leggings, its boots.
- **Their elbows and wrists hold without twist bones**: shown their man through our clips, the developer: "looks good".
- **One game copy a machine** (D319): the game refuses a second copy, naming the one running and what to do; `--multirun` allows exactly one more for a two-player test; the filming tools film in turn, the render box keeps its three slots. Built (`terra_world::single_instance`, one-copy j2).
- **The paint direction holds**: shown the repainted Ranger beside theirs, the developer: "looks the same tbqh so i guess thats good?" At play distance our paint reads as theirs does, so job 4 refines, it does not push.

- **2026-11-02, the intact-parts check** (`tools/characters/intact.ts`, its test beside it). Every piece of theirs in an output is compared with its source: each corner's position, normal, UVs and weights by bone name within float noise, every triangle and its winding, each bone's bind; the order of vertices and how they are grouped are free, since any export reshuffles them. A mesh of ours is listed; one that is mostly their vertices under another name fails. A Blender import and export of their pieces passes, with one catch it found: Blender keeps four weights a vertex unless told to keep all, and the woman's Ranger body and legs use up to eight (up to 7.5% of a vertex's weight beyond the fourth). The game's loader reads only four too.
- **2026-11-02, do pieces of two outfits mix?** Measured on the man with `tools/characters/layers.ts` through the 34 clips, at the one new seam, the waist ([research](../research/2026-11-02-character-base.md#clothing-on-the-quaternius-bodies)). The Peasant tunic over the Ranger leggings is as clean as either outfit as made. The Ranger tunic over the Peasant trousers is not: the fuller trousers' waistband shows 1–2 cm through the tunic's hips in the sprint, the sword stance and jumps. So v0's leather coat (their Ranger body and arms) goes with the Ranger leggings. Every pairing, theirs included, pokes 4–8 cm in the roll, crouch and sitting, where the tunic folds into the thighs.
- **2026-11-02, what bare pieces they ship** (D600). Both zips listed: besides the full body, each body comes cut by Quaternius to the head alone and to the head with bare shoulders, arms and hands (`UpperBody`, every triangle, vertex and weight the full body's). No bare torso, legs or feet, and no underclothes piece: the underwear is painted on the body's skin texture, and the outfit pack's "Regular" textures are that skin, for the hands its arms pieces carry. The man's upper body is vendored as shipped for the trial.
- **2026-11-02, their man through our pipeline** (char-switch j3). `finish_character.sh` given their full body skips the conversion: no twist bones, no bones re-pointed, no stretch. Our clips are carried onto his skeleton by `retarget.py`, which is needed: his joints are the mannequin's by name and parent but sit up to 4.6 cm elsewhere, with rest turns up to 17° apart (neck), and the game sets each joint's whole position from the clip, so the clips are re-sampled on him (bones turned up to 11°). He passes the rig check through all 34 clips: no pinched elbow, wrist or knee past 4% (the limit is 20%) with no twist bones; the worst crushed skin is Hit_Chest at 96% of its limit; he sinks 13 mm deeper than the mannequin in the swim. His six sockets pass the attachment table. The export keeps his eyes and brows and all his weights, and `intact.ts` passes on it. Three checks assumed our old rig and were changed, never his parts: the rest-pose test is judged only on a rig with twist bones (his clavicles point 12° off their children as made); the proportion check reports his fingertips (0.754 m, the 99th percentile 0.732 m) as made rather than failing (D599); the export keeps every skinned mesh and every weight. `rig_render.py` frames all his meshes, and joins `head+piece+...` files onto one skeleton. The 4-weight limit stays, since the game reads four: the man has four at most, the woman's eight are item 3.
- **2026-11-02, the first stills of the mix** (char-switch j3): their head-only man in the Ranger tunic over the Peasant trousers, and in the Peasant tunic over the Ranger leggings, through Sword_Idle and Sprint_Loop (`rig_render.py HEAD+PIECE+...`), with his bare sheet, handed to the desk.
