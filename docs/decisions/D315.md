# D315: Climate's crate, clock, weather and cells

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`terra_climate`, free of Bevy (D28). The world's clock is days (`WorldTime`, a 64-bit count, the day its whole part), all the server sends of the climate (D52); a world's climate is a data file (`assets/data/climate.ron` for the start). The year's slow terms (the sun's path, the season's warmth and wetness) move on once a day, at midnight. Weather is two layers of `terra_gen` noise drifting at different speeds, fronts a theme's size across passing in a theme's spell of days; something falls where they pass a threshold set by the share of the time it should, snow below 0 °C, rain above 2, mixed between. The past is folded per 32 m cell at eight samples a day, from the first midsummer at least half a year back (0.5 to 1.5 years): warmth (degree-days above 5 °C since midwinter) and chill (below 10 °C since midsummer) are exact from their resets, snow and ice are none at midsummer, and the ground's water forgets its start within the fold; each cell is kept as of the last day asked. The clock starts in late August, the sun 10° north as the art was lit, and the sun's path follows the date.

## Why

Standard models, so the numbers mean what they do in the field: snow melts by Hock's enhanced temperature index (1999), ice grows by Stefan's law, the ground dries by Hamon's evapotranspiration, the mean falls toward the poles as in Held and Suarez's idealised Earth. Late August rather than mid-April, the other date of that sun: the summer's leaves and warmth the forest was tuned under, not spring's bare oaks. Measured: 1.8 µs a cell a day, 0.65 ms a cell folded afresh.
