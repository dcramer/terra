# D424: A creature's clip plays within 0.8-1.2 of its own rate

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Every creature on foot plays its gait's clip at its ground speed over the clip's own pace (`paces` in creatures.ron, the speed its stride covers at its own rate), and only from 0.8 to 1.2 of that rate; below its walk's band it slows its walk to stop or set off (`play::creatures::gait`, `keeps_to` and `on_foot`). A speed it would go that no gait covers within its band becomes the nearer band's edge (the slower on a tie), past its fastest gait's top that top: it changes gait or goes slower, never quickens its legs. Wandering, returning, fleeing at every pace, chasing and running to a trunk all go so. A trot or canter is one more clip and pace. A kind with no paces measured goes as asked, its clip at its own rate.

Each runner's run clip is made for the speed its kind runs (`tools/creatures/animal.py`'s gaits): a gait's footfalls and duty factor (walk, trot, rotary gallop, the hares' half-bound, the squirrels' bound; Muybridge, Hildebrand 1977, Gambaryan 1974, Alexander & Jayes 1983), its speed, and from these and the hips' height a real animal's stride and cadence (Alexander's relative stride, 1976), a suspension where no foot is down. So `run_mps` is its clip's own pace and no kind is held below it (a test holds every paced kind to it): squirrel 5 m/s bounding (6.7 strides a second of 0.75 m), brown hare 11 (5.5 of 2.0 m), mountain hare 10.5, red and arctic fox 11 and 10.5 galloping (4.6 of 2.4 m), stag and hind 12 (2.5 of 4.8 m), calf 10, boar 9. Foxes, deer and boar also trot (fox 3 m/s, stag 3.5, hind 3.2, calf 2.8, boar 2.5), and a kind that trots trots off wary (`Pace::Wary`) where one that does not walks. The calm stag crossing a player's way walks at 1.49 m/s, its walk clip at 1.2; wary, trots at 3.5; startled, gallops at 9.6, 0.8 of its run clip. The hopping and wading birds and the ptarmigan on foot go as their clips allow (0.24-0.27 and 0.88 m/s), their `run_mps` set so.

The generated clips keep their feet as the check measures them (D442): a swinging foot leaves and lands at the ground's speed (a Hermite swing whose end tangents are the stance's carry, at most 1.5 sweeps) and lifts at once; a planted foot turns about its sole's edge or whatever skin is lowest, the toe as the heel peels, the heel as it lands, and in the air about its tip; no skin of a foot goes under where it stands, and a lifted one clears the ground with its tip, by a twentieth of the hip's height at most; a kind standing on laid hind feet is raised onto their soles. A knee or elbow and an ankle or wrist bend without turning about their bone, and fold no tighter than 30 degrees. A bird's arm rolls at most 25 degrees about itself, folded or beating; its forearm and hand only swing. Grazing stops where any skin meets the ground.

## Why

Developer, on the morning forest walk: "the stag animation is a little bit wonky, as in it moves at the right speed, but the legs are moving too quickly vs. more majestically slower movement of limbs." The calm crossing quickened the stag's walk clip (a 0.83 s cycle, 1.04 m a stride) to 1.6 of its rate, 1.9 strides a second; a red deer walks about one. Shipped games keep a clip's rate near its own and switch gait past it (Unreal's speed-matched blend spaces and stride warping, Red Dead Redemption 2's animals). The test `no_creature_quickens_a_clip_past_its_rate_and_its_feet_keep_to_the_ground` holds every kind of the bestiary to it.

The first clips, made by eye, covered too little ground for the speed asked (a fox's run 2.35 m/s against its 5.5), so the rule held the small runners to a crawl; the coordinator, after it: "the small runners' run clips cover too little ground, so they flee far too slowly", with "a fleeing hare bounds at 10+ m/s and a fox gallops at 13 m/s". Making a clip from a gait's measured footfalls and a stride of the length a real animal takes at that speed fixes it at the source, as data for every kind, as Spore and Overgrowth make their clips from a body plan. `every_runner_runs_flat_out_as_fast_as_its_kind_goes` holds it.
