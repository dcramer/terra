# D567: Six kinds' holds: a one-hander blocks alone, the pickaxe is two-handed, the spear upright, the torch put out at the hip, the bow low in the left hand, the basket in the off hand

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The six questions the kinds table left to the developers (D566, [assembled-items.md](../assembled-items.md#the-kinds-in-plain-words)), answered as recommended:

1. **A one-hander may block alone**, weaker than a shield (D461's second question): the axe and the sword guard on the weapon in the Single stance.
2. **The pickaxe is two-handed** (Two, the main hand leading), with a second grip on its haft, and is stowed across the back.
3. **The spear is carried upright** at the side in the lead hand, point up, and stowed across the back.
4. **A torch put away is put out** and hung at the hip.
5. **The bow is carried low in the left hand** (its lead hand, the off hand), its top tipped forward.
6. **The basket is carried by its handle in the off hand** and put away into the pack, not shown; it has no lid or cover.

The kinds' files in `assets/data/kinds/` carry the answers; how each is built (the block clips, the two-handed takes, the carry tilts, the stowed turns) is technical work on them.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-28, on the review desk, answering the six "your call" questions of the kinds table: "Approve all six". Each closes a situation the assembler and the kind check need filled: hands and the second grip decide a kind's slots and points, carrying and stowing its mount.
