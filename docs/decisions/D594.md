# D594: The volumetric fog's ray direction is turned from view space, not taken as two world positions' difference

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Bevy 0.20's `volumetric_fog.wesl` took each pixel's ray direction as `normalize(position_ndc_to_world(Rd_ndc) - view.world_position)`: the near plane's point less the camera's. It now turns the view-space direction it already has into world space with no translation, `normalize(direction_view_to_world(Rd_view))`. A patch of `bevy_pbr` 0.20.0 (`patches/bevy_pbr`, D184), marked `Terra's patch (D594)`. Bevy's main after 0.20 has the same line; its atmosphere's `uv_to_ray_direction` does the same, and Unreal's Large World Coordinates take every ray camera-relative (its "translated world" space) for the same reason.

Upstream: fixed on Bevy's main; drop the patch when we move past 0.20.

## Why

Our worlds are planets of about 30 km radius, so the camera and the near plane are tens of kilometres from the origin, where an f32 is about 2 mm apart. Their difference is about 0.1 m long, so the direction snapped to a grid, an error of up to about 2%; a step of the near-plane point is about 20 pixels. Where the march samples the haze's density texture, which depends on the direction, the error laid a lattice of diamonds over the tundra's sky, strongest with TAA off (tundra rounds 16 to 18 ruled out the sun's shadow lookup, the jitter and the step count). With the patch the lattice is gone in the tundra's band crops at 11 h, TAA off and on, with only the shader changed, and in its looks views at 13 h (tundra round 19, against main 8b7028133). Six fresh judges a build on the tundra's five views at 13 h: main 4.30, the patch 4.52 (+0.22, 95% interval +0.04 to +0.40, p 0.024), every view higher; main's views drew 11 faults naming a crosshatch or dither over the sky and the patch's none. The looks tables of the tundra, lush, lake and town hold as main's: every number within 0.01 but town's brightness, which two runs of main put at 0.388 and 0.406 and the patch at 0.405.
