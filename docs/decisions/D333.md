# D333: The building baseline: wood and stone, built and destroyed

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Construction work is held to a minimal baseline until it is flawless and fast: wood (logs, hewn timber, shakes) and stone (stone, slate), built by hand and destroyed, with infinite pretend resources. Earth, lime, wattle and daub and thatch are frozen: kept building and passing checks, not polished, and out of the settlement, the QA sets and the fault ratchet. Costs, storage, carrying and recruits (building-plan step 7) are paused. No new materials or props unless asked.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14 and 15: "we're only doing the core construction"; "we're not implementing the carry resources/etc systems just the core 'can we construct it with infinite pretend resources' stuff"; "keep this minimal set of scopes, the wood, the rocks, the construction, the destroy, and make sure everything works flawlessly and performantly because thats our baseline"; "dont scope creep".
