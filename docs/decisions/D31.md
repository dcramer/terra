# D31: Bevy version policy

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Pinned, now to 0.20. Upgrade once the crates we depend on support the new release, or sooner when the developers ask; a dependency that has not caught up is then pinned to an exact commit of its port, or switched off, and returned to a release as soon as one supports the new Bevy.

## Why

Bevy changes its API every few months. Moved to 0.20 on 2026-10-12 at the developers' request, a few days after its release, with `bevy-inspector-egui` on the commit of its open port (see `Cargo.toml`); what it took and what it changed is in [research/bevy-0-20-upgrade.md](../../research/bevy-0-20-upgrade.md).
