# D246: The lush forest's floor follows the light

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

What grows on the lush forest's floor is worked out from the canopy over it, as real floors are: each tree's crown, where its clumps of leaves are (how far it reaches and which way it leans), hides a share of the sky by its kind (an oak's most of it, a Scots pine's much less, a spruce's nearly all, a young tree's less), thinned in gaps by noise, with light down the paths' corridors. Grass grows by that light: thick, with tall patches, in clearings, gaps and along paths; under a closed canopy only in tufts where a fleck falls, thin grass in clumps rather than a stubble, the floor there fallen leaves under broadleaves, needles under conifers (Poly Haven's Forest Ground 03, a sixth layer of the ground) and moss in damp hollows; thinner and drier on needles. Grass is pushed back from every trunk, log and rock by a ring of duff and roots (Poly Haven's Roots, a seventh layer), and a path's edge wanders and frays into dirt, roots and leaves. The cover map gains a second picture, of needles, duff and how much canopy stands over, which dims the ground's sky light in place of the leaf litter (D216). An eye-height layer of ten kinds, six variants each in tints of their own and scaled at random: bracken (fronds rising and arching out), ferns (the scan and grown fronds), herbs, hazel, bilberry under the pines, oak scrub, saplings, dead branches and flowers; placed as a Thomas process, drift centres where the light, the trunks, the logs and each kind's own noise like it and a Gaussian clump round each. They are walked through. The floor runs on 70 m past the forest's edge as meadow, so the forest grades into the open. Fallen logs taper, lumpy and burred, broken off at the crown's end in splinters, sawn with weathered end grain or broken at the foot, with stubs of branches, dark and wet below, of pine bark among the conifers; stumps are generated with spreading roots, sawn or broken; rocks are clustered round boulders, sunk a fifth to two fifths of their height once their bounds are known, and drawn with moss over what faces up (`mossy.wesl`). The middle layer, tussocks of thin grass and the forest's soil are D268; the ground's layers, needles and litter D275; grass under a closed canopy D293.

## Why

The developers wanted "a good demo" as The Forest is, and earlier "more grass/denser grass, more shrubbery"; a review against Sons of the Forest found the floor a mown lawn of one height even in deep shade, nothing at eye height but one lime bush in even dots, trunks meeting grass in a straight cut, logs as cylinders and the stump a pale post. On the RTX 3080 the walk bench is 11.9 ms median and 13.4 ms at the 99th percentile, within budget, with the canopy of D247 (another agent's bench on the same GPU doubles it, so measure alone); bare ground at the vantage views 6.8% at the floor view, all of it path, and at the forest's edge 37% to none; lodcheck (worst 0.07% a frame) and collide pass.
