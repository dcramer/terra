# D196: When Bevy falls short

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Find the engine or public tool that does it well and reimplement its version on Bevy: ported from its source where the licence lets an MIT project take the code (MIT, BSD, Apache, zlib: Godot, Voxel Tools, O3DE, Jolt), and from its documentation, papers and talks where it does not (Unreal, anything GPL). Not an invention of our own, and not a change of engine. How deep into Bevy the change goes is D184.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12, after asking whether Haxe and Heaps would spare us the character and terrain problems: "if bevy isnt good at something, our best solution is to find a version of it in an engine that is, and reimplement it, ideally from source if we can". Those problems were mostly not Bevy's (stale clips, a rig whose twist bones moved no skin, a crate's seam bug), and a smaller engine has the same gaps. A shipped implementation's source carries the edge cases a description leaves out.
