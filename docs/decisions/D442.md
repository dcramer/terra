# D442: The creature check gates a creature by measured deformation and gait

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

`tools/creatures/creature_check.py <kind>` gates a creature as the rig check gates a body: the rig check's measures that apply to any skeleton, then on its plan's pose sheet (each joint to its limits, the gaits' extremes, grazing, lying) volume loss per bone's region, ring squeeze, flatten and candy-wrapper twist, edge stretch per zone, folds and skin through skin, weight creases, foot slide, feet against the ground and each clip's rate band (D424); bars set by measuring known-good rigs (Quaternius's deer and spider); a score, and a sheet of each failing measure's worst moment ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#5-the-creature-check-d442)).

Built first from the exported model alone (`creature_check.py OUT.json MODEL.glb@SCALE`, any rig judged the same): belly weight on leg bones, trunk stretch at the 95th percentile, skin lag at joints, lowest skin by clip kind and the loop's wrap, with kinks and the seam's velocity jump reported beside them. Calibrated 2026-10-24 on the deer, which passes. Foot slide is the skin touching the ground: a step is the median move of a foot's vertices within the contact tolerance at both frames, less the clip's pace, so a heel peeling about its toe holds (the foot's mean read a peel as a 23 cm slide). It is a bar for clips we generate (`animal.py` marks its armature `terra_rig`): 1 cm mid-stance, 3 cm over a whole stance. The deer is the reference for deformation only, its slide reported: it skates (its walk carries the hind hooves at 1.2 m/s and the fore at 0.8). Skin lag takes a bone's turn without its scale (a bird's folding humerus, drawn smaller, read as 80 degrees).

Amended 2026-10-24 (round 8): the seam where a limb's skin meets the trunk's (skin 10-90% on limb bones, a wing's too) is measured, reported per clip beside the gates: `seam_p95`, its edges' stretch at the 95th percentile (slivers left out), and `crease_deg`, the 99th percentile of how much further its hinged triangle pairs fold than at rest. The deer reads 46% and 60 degrees in its gaits; the zoo v3 rigs 58-590% and 130-170; the round's rigs 55-200%. Reported, not yet a bar: the crease reads 140-165 degrees on rigs the review calls clean as well as creased, so it does not yet tell them apart; the seam stretch does, and gets a bar once the review and the numbers agree on one.

## Why

The animals were fixed one developer report at a time (the stag's knee, the fox's chest, the hare's seat, the stag's belly) because nothing measured them; the people's pipeline stopped that with `rig_check.py`, `deform_check.py` and `gait_check.py` (D115). Rigging studios run a range-of-motion pass on every rig; we measure it so an agent can judge it (D69).
