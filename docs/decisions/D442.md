# D442: The creature check gates a creature by measured deformation and gait

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

`tools/creatures/creature_check.py <kind>` gates a creature as the rig check gates a body: the rig check's measures that apply to any skeleton, then on its plan's pose sheet (each joint to its limits, the gaits' extremes, grazing, lying) volume loss per bone's region, ring squeeze, flatten and candy-wrapper twist, edge stretch per zone, folds and skin through skin, weight creases, foot slide, feet against the ground and each clip's rate band (D424); bars set by measuring known-good rigs (Quaternius's deer and spider); a score, and a sheet of each failing measure's worst moment ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#5-the-creature-check-d442)).

Built first from the exported model alone (`creature_check.py OUT.json MODEL.glb@SCALE`, any rig judged the same): belly weight on leg bones, trunk stretch at the 95th percentile, skin lag at joints, lowest skin by clip kind and the loop's wrap, with foot slide, kinks and the seam's velocity jump reported beside them. Calibrated 2026-10-24 on the deer, which passes; foot slide is not a bar, since the deer itself skates (its walk carries the hind hooves at 1.2 m/s and the fore at 0.8) and so cannot set one.

## Why

The animals were fixed one developer report at a time (the stag's knee, the fox's chest, the hare's seat, the stag's belly) because nothing measured them; the people's pipeline stopped that with `rig_check.py`, `deform_check.py` and `gait_check.py` (D115). Rigging studios run a range-of-motion pass on every rig; we measure it so an agent can judge it (D69).
