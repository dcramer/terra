# D520: The moon's cycle

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The moon has real phases over a month (29.5 days on the start; a world's month its data) and its own path, up by day about half its time, with earthshine on a crescent; the night's light follows its phase (a quarter moon about a tenth of a full one), and starlight and airglow keep a moonless night darker but never black, so play stays readable. It amends D310, whose moon stands high across the sky from the sun at every phase with a full moon's light. The other options were real phases but always high by night (D310 today), and the full moon every night. See [sky.md](../sky.md#sun-moon-and-stars).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "Real phases, own path". This settles the question in the recommendation's favour.

The developer, 2026-10-26, named "sun, moon" among what the sky should show. Real phases make nights differ in darkness, which changes play (D122, realistic but fun).
