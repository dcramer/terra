# D520: The moon's cycle

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: the moon has real phases over a month (29.5 days on the start; a world's month its data), its own path, up by day half its time, earthshine on a crescent, and the night's light by its phase (a quarter moon about a tenth of a full one), with starlight and airglow so a moonless night is darker but never black and play stays readable. Options: real phases and path, as recommended; real phases but always high by night (D310 today, a full moon's light at every phase); or the full moon every night. See [sky.md](../sky.md#sun-moon-and-stars).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-26, named "sun, moon" among what the sky should show. D310 (Agent) lights every night as a high full moon; real phases make nights differ in darkness, which changes play (D122, realistic but fun), so it is the developers' to choose.
