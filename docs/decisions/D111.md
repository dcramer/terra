# D111: Character creator

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A creator of a few simple choices: sex (Quaternius's man or woman, D599), skin tone, eye colour, hairstyle and hair colour. No height, build or proportion sliders for now, so every outfit is made once a body and fits every player (the build morphs and height wait, past v0). Hairstyle is not tied to the body: it can be changed in the game later, as Enshrouded's barber does. Equipment is visible when worn.

Before 2026-11-02 the creator was sliders for build (heavier, thinner, stronger) and a widely varying height as well, for other races and humanoid types later; that waits.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11: "we don't need a ton of sliders.. diff hair, skin colors, slightly diff body templates", after Enshrouded. On 2026-10-12 they settled sliders only, rather than templates or both; height "varied ... otherwise we won't be able to have varied races/humanoid types"; proportions beyond height not yet. Each slider is a weight of the base body's morphs, a uniform scale or a skin tint (D112), so every item fits every setting; the named templates stay as presets for people and tests.

The developers, 2026-11-02, once the characters moved onto Quaternius's bodies: "tbqh for character creator we could just do skin tone/eye color/simple things vs height/weight", "and gender"; asked whether that was settled: "Settled".
