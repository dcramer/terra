# D111: Character creator

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A creator of a few sliders, not templates: sex, build (heavier, thinner, stronger), height, and skin colour; hairstyles and hair colour. Height varies widely, enough for other races and humanoid types later; other proportions (limb length, shoulders, head size) not yet. Hairstyle is not tied to the body: it can be changed in the game later, as Enshrouded's barber does. Equipment is visible when worn.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11: "we don't need a ton of sliders.. diff hair, skin colors, slightly diff body templates", after Enshrouded. On 2026-10-12 they settled sliders only, rather than templates or both; height "varied ... otherwise we won't be able to have varied races/humanoid types"; proportions beyond height not yet. Each slider is a weight of the base body's morphs, a uniform scale or a skin tint (D112), so every item fits every setting; the named templates stay as presets for people and tests.
