# D523: At the water's edge: wading slows, a swimmer climbs out, breath is shown

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Wading slows the walk linearly with depth to 60% of a jog at swimming depth (no step from a jog to a swim). A swimmer climbs out onto a ledge up to about 1 m over the water when swimming into it, as Unreal's water jump does. A small breath bar shows only while under water or refilling. Numbers are placeholders to tune in a playtest.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "All three recommended". Today a body jogs at full speed until 1.26 m deep then swims at 2 m/s, cannot leave the water onto a bank higher than about 0.5 m, and learns of its breath only from the red edge of drowning.
