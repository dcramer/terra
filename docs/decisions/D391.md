# D391: Cheaper models for mechanical subagent work

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Two subagents in `.claude/agents/`: `finder` (Haiku; Read, Grep, Glob) finds code, docs and decisions and returns paths, lines and quotes; `runner` (Sonnet; Bash, Read, Grep, Glob) runs a given check, test, QA script or benchmark and reports its numbers and failures, its log kept in a file. Neither edits, decides or reviews: judgement, design, debugging and fixes stay on the default model, and their descriptions say so.

## Why

At list price Haiku 5.5 is a fortieth of Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 half (`worker/src/prices.js`). Finding and running take many turns and need no judgement. A general-purpose subagent's first turn wrote a 72 to 77k prefix on 2026-10-16; fewer tools should shrink it, which `context.mjs` will show.
