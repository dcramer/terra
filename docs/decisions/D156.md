# D156: Gods

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

There are gods, because they enrich the world. A god is a superior user of magic, one who went far past the highest rank a person reaches, and belongs to one realm, its nature following that realm's magic; no god is everywhere. In play, kept simple for now: blessings at shrines and thematic touches (shrines, priests, holy places); more later.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "let's keep gods as they just enrich the world. They're just like 'superior magic users' I think, and they're realm specific". See [lore.md](../lore.md#gods).
