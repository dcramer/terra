# D316: Locomotion clips made, not taken

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The idle, walk, jog and sprint the game plays are made with NVIDIA's Kimodo (2026, open weights under a licence that allows commercial use and claims no output), run on this machine from a prompt held to a straight path at the clip's speed, rather than taken from Quaternius's libraries; the best looping stride of the take kept is carried onto our skeleton by world rotation from the T-pose, run in place, the upper limbs' roll handed to the forearm and shin for our skinning (`tools/characters/made_clips.py`, `motion.ts`, `art/animation/made.json`). Other clips stay Quaternius's until one of ours measures better.

## Why

The library's jog leans 28 degrees and its sprint 41, two to four times a real runner's, and reads as a hunched charge on our bodies; Kimodo's walk and jog lean 0.4 and 7.8 degrees with the arms close (research/2026-10-14-animation.md, `gait_check.py`). The developers: "get the character dressed and running not so fucking weird."
