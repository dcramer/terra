# D366: The woods' conifers are the lush forest's, asked for by the world and given by the layer above

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D335. `terra_world::woods` places the conifers and asks for their models through a small type the layer above fills in before `woods::Planting` (`Conifers`, a `ConiferModels`: draw this kind this tall here, and the far crowns' colours), as a placement system is handed its trees' models, the way `CoverSource` (D246) is answered; with none, the flora's generic pine as before. The sandbox answers with the lush forest's own Scots pines and Norway spruces (`lush/conifers.rs`): its `KINDS` grown from its seed in its materials (`Looks`, `grow`, `draw`, moved out of `plant` so both draw the same trees), each tree its kind's variant nearest its height, a tree under 9 m a young spruce, no ivy, moss bands as their kind's. They are drawn out to where their crowns' cards end, the lush forest's own trees' distances (about 150 to 280 m by size); past that each tree's far card (`far_forest.wesl`) comes in where its grown tree goes, told in the card's colour, drawn as the kind its tree was drawn as, squat as it is, coloured from the lush crowns' leaves as the lush far forest is, and gone only within 25 to 45 m of the eye where no grown tree stands for it. From the lake's shore: 2,064 of 16,469 grown trees drawn, about 17 million triangles a frame in the main pass (the flora's pine about 8 million), 4.4 million shadow casters.

## Why

The developers on the mountain lake's film: "those pines do have a flat effect too". The flora's pine had flat leaf cards, its far lumps a lime that read as a tree farm, and its far cards came in at a fixed 450 to 540 m, leaving holes behind any tree whose levels ended sooner. Moving the lush forest's tree grower into terra_world would move 3,000 lines another agent is changing; the lush forest's trees drawn to their lumps' 420 m-for-20 m were about 37 million triangles, where a game's last level of a tree is its billboard.
