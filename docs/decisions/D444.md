# D444: A horn stands in a massif of lesser tops, kept off the valley pins

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Each horn (D425) sends ridges out along two or three of its arêtes to lesser tops of their own faces, at half to three quarters of its height, about twice its reach out (`terra_voxel` `mountains/massif.rs`). They rise out of the ground the horns and cirques leave by a smooth maximum, so the faces find no crease to step into a lip, and they stand as walls too.

- **Kept off the valley pins:** round a kept spot out of the ranges (the market town's), the neighbours are off within 4.5 km and whole from 8 km, past the 7 km skyline a town's site is chosen by. A kept spot in a range (the lake's) keeps them from the usual 0.7-1.8 km.
- **Cost:** the massifs round a point are worked out once per quarter of a horn cell, per thread, and only the ones that can reach it are kept. The ground sampled costs 1.32x main at the lake and 1.05x at its peak. Most of the rest is the faces' broad slope over the new walls.
- **Teeth (round 29):** a ridge's crest breaks into teeth about a fifth of the horn's reach apart, sharp-tipped, notched up to 22% of its height unevenly, faded at the horn and the neighbour (`teeth`); the neighbours stand 0.5-0.78 of the horn (was 0.55-0.85). Round 28's right-hand ridge at the lake read "pale, smeared and flat-topped"; in a blind review the lake views rose 5.4 to 6.0 and 5.5 to 6.0, the others unchanged.
- **Spurs (round 32):** every arête without a neighbour sends out a spur, a ridge with no top of its own (`Neighbour { spur: true }`): from 0.3 of the horn's reach at 0.75 of its height (`SPUR_HIGH`), down to 0.12-0.25 of it (`SPUR_END`) at 1.3-1.7 reach (`SPUR_OUT`), strayed ±0.15 rad, toothed as other ridges and kept off the town as the neighbours are. The lake horn's bare arête had capped flat with the range's crest and then fallen about 1000 m in 330 m into the cirque cut, a cap, a cliff and a V; along its spurs it now steps down with benches. A test holds it: no 300 m stretch falls more than 0.7 of the height, no dip deeper than 0.15. In a blind review (round 33) the lake views rose 4.8 to 5.1 and 4.9 to 5.2; the settlement and valley were unchanged.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-22, on the lake's hero peak: "theres notinh around it thats not usually how mountains work". Real horns stand in ranges, like the Matterhorn between the Dent d'Hérens and the Breithorn.

With the neighbours standing round the town too, the settlement's walls stood up to 10° higher 2-4 km out, and the town search moved 360 m to a site whose forest edge fails its check. Re-finding the sites (D425) would have moved the town and its view, and D425 ties that one move to the drainage bake, so the neighbours stay off the town instead. With the ring, the town's skyline is main's to 0.01°.

The blind review (mountains round 28) scored the lake 5.5 against main's 4 and the settlement and valley the same as main.
