# D418: The weather at the eye drives the sky, the light, the air and what falls

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

One `Weather` resource holds the climate's cloud, what is falling and how much of it is snow, and the wind where the camera is, eased over seconds (`terra_world::weather`), and every drawn part reads it as everything reads the one wind (D255). Cloud dims the sun's beam and keeps the sky's light by Kasten and Czeplak's fit (a full deck leaves a quarter of a clear day's light, none of it direct; rain thickens it); the eye takes back half the stops lost; a deck covers the sky as one grey layer as bright as the light it gives the ground, and the haze takes its colour; what falls shortens visibility as a METAR grades it, snow far more than rain. Rain and snow are drift kinds (D379) brought by the weather, thinned by their cards with the rate. Named weathers (`assets/data/weather.ron`) are queries only: `--weather <name>` moves a run's clock on from its date to the first day whose hour has that weather at the start, so the ground, the snow lying and the light all agree with what falls. See [weather.md](../weather.md).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-18: "spin up a new session working explicitly on weather and season conditions (i.e. storms, and winter)". The climate already made the weather, but nothing drawn followed it: a full deck lit the forest with a clear day's sun. Reading the quantities, not a weather flag, keeps climate.md's rule ("fundamentals not hardcoded simplistic concepts"); finding a moment rather than forcing one keeps every machine's climate the same (D312).
