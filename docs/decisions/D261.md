# D261: Rooms, doorways and door steps from the cubes alone

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Rooms (D155) are the space the built cubes close in all round, floor and roof too, found by flooding in three dimensions the cubes not built that have something built over and under them in their column, as Minecraft's and Enshrouded's room checks do; a flood that reaches the sky or the open ground is the outside. Only what is built closes a room in; an opening is part of its wall. So a room is any height and shape (two storeys and a mezzanine are one room), a knee-high ring of wall is none, and every room is indoors; they are worked out when asked after a change (`terra_build::rooms`), from the last rooms found where there are some: only the rooms beside a cube whose closing in changed are let go and flooded again, the rest kept, so a castle edit costs its rooms near it, not the castle (D308). A doorway is any opening at a floor: an open cube with something not open under it, two cubes clear, a floor on one side at the threshold's layer leading, through the rest of the opening, into a room and not into the wall (a window in a thick wall is none), and none on the other (`Base::doorways`), however and on whatever storey it was made. Door steps are cubes (amends D206): from each doorway out, a stair a column rising to the threshold, a quarter of a metre a riser, the first a half cube where one riser is all the drop needs, the last a whole cube where the ground rises over a stair's lower tread (on a slope falling from the door, a stair there showed a thin wedge over the ground), each on a footing to the cube the ground passes through, of the footings' kind, as footings take the ground; none where the ground is within 20 cm, none for a door more than four metres over it (`Base::door_steps`). The hammer and the remote plan them as a part of their own when an opening or a carving makes a doorway; the shortcuts lay them built with their footings. Nothing outside the cubes collides, and the planet is no longer read for them at run time.

## Why

Mine, from the goals review's findings 4 and 5 on 2026-10-13 ([goals-review-2026-10-13.md](../../research/goals-review-2026-10-13.md)): door steps collided outside any cube and took their height from the planet at run time, against D192's "the cube is the truth"; rooms were found per six-cube storey in 2D, so walls built off a storey's layer or storeys over a roof were misread, and doorways needed a door-shaped wall tile on the ground storey. Stairs and halves exist since D220 and D231, as D206 waited for.
