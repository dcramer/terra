# D34: Networking library

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`renet` behind a transport seam of two lanes, one reliable and ordered, one unreliable, with our own messages and replication; no replication framework. `terra_net` talks only to the seam: an in-memory transport with seeded latency and loss for solo play and tests, `renet` 2 with `renet_netcode` over UDP for friends by direct address, `renet_steam` for Steam's relay later. The design is [netcode.md](../netcode.md).

## Why

Surveyed on 2026-11-06 ([research/2026-11-06-netcode.md](../../research/2026-11-06-netcode.md)): `renet` 2.0.1 (2026-10-30) is a plain crate with no Bevy in it, current, with netcode and Steam transports, so it fits D28. `lightyear` 0.31 and `bevy_replicon` 0.45 support Bevy 0.20 but replicate ECS components and need Bevy in every peer; our authority's state is mostly plain structs outside the ECS (the mover's bodies), and headless bot clients for load tests should cost no engine. The seam keeps the choice cheap to change.
