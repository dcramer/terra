# D635: The iso experiment is one game with the main game, under an iso camera, modular enough not to collide

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The 2.5D top-down experiment (`crates/terra_iso`, once `spike/iso`) becomes **one game with an iso camera** (option A of three, [iso.md](../iso.md)). Iso is a camera, its controls and a look over the game's own world, with one binary, one world and one server. Its biomes become realms' themes and sites.

- **Modular enough not to collide** (the developers, 2026-11-08). Iso's camera, controls, look kit and figures are their own plugins and modules beside the third-person game's, talking through components, events and small shared types (AGENTS.md, Systems apart; D198's layers). Neither camera's work breaks or forks the other's: a system that assumes a horizon or a third-person eye takes the camera as an input, and a change for one camera keeps the other's checks passing.
- The rejected options: **B**, a sibling game on shared crates with a world layer and server of its own, and **C**, iso kept apart as an art and look lab.

The steps of iso.md follow: input to `Intent` on the 64 Hz tick, the `terra_motion` mover, the walker split into movement and camera, the hero as a `Player`, the fight's rules, then creatures, people and animation, then the world. Which camera is D629's question, and which budgets is D630's.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08, on the desk (q-iso-one-game), chose "A. One game, iso camera": "one game, just make sure its modularized enough to not collide".

Earlier, on making iso part of the repository's root: "make sure that they are majorly using the same systems, same networking, same ecs etc, ik lots of things will be different like models etc." Today iso shares only Bevy, the patches, the assets and `terra_core::cooked` with the game. Its hero is a point stepped each frame with no fixed tick, and its fight is a cone and a count of blows. The server decides everything (D51). Under B there would be a second world layer and server to keep in step, and C did not answer the ask.
