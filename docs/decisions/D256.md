# D256: Dusk's shade lit by the sky above

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The sky light (D190) takes its hue half from the drawn sky and half from the shade's cool colour; while the sun is below about 22°, the shade's share rises to nearly nine tenths at 4°, since the drawn horizon is then orange and pink and the shade under trees is lit by the sky overhead. The warmth stays in the sky and the low sun.

## Why

The second review found 18:30 "a flat pink wash". The rest of the warmth is the sun reddened by the air, as at a real dusk, and the bare dirt's own salmon colour, which the floor's work takes on.
