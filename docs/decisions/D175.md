# D175: Sizes of structural parts

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Walls, floors, storeys, doors and windows are D181's. Proposed for the rest, in [building.md](../building.md#sizes): real buildings' sizes, rounded to the half-metre grid, scaled to a person 1.8 m tall; roofs pitched by their covering, with eaves overhanging 0.3 to 0.5 m.

## Why

Asked for by the developers on 2026-10-12: "we should document the expectation for sizes on all these parts", after saying the walls were "super thick". The sizes D181 fixed were proposed here first.
