# D155: What a building is

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The game knows its buildings: the walls, floors and roofs that touch one another are one building, and the spaces they enclose are its rooms. A room closed all round, walls and roof, is indoors, sheltered from rain and cold; one missing a roof or a wall is not. Stations belong to their building, and their expansions count anywhere in it (D151). How weather and warmth act indoors is D78's and D153's.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "lets make them count anywhere in the building … buildings will need to know if they're 'complete' (aka walls/roof) anyways because otherwise you have natural elements like rain/cold that should have effect". How it is worked out is mine: from the grid, when a part is built or taken away, flood-filled into buildings and rooms. See [building.md](../building.md#planned-then-constructed).
