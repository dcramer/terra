# D96: Tools

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

No science-fiction toolset, and no single tool that harvests everything: each job has its own tool. Whether items wear and need repair is open.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11. Replaces the excavator, harvester and scanner proposed earlier. A proposed set (axe, pickaxe, shovel, knife, hammer), and a recommendation on wear and repair, are in [crafting.md](../crafting.md#tools). The game has axes, pickaxes, a knife and a hammer.
