# D578: A play benchmark measures the load and the freezes of a walk

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`cargo xtask bench` gets a scene timed from the program's start, `play-walk`: a new game's load (the wall clock to the first playable frame, and its longest steps), then a scripted 60 s walk out from the start through ground not yet streamed, every frame timed, with freezes (frames of 100 ms or more) counted beside the hitches. It runs warm, cooked textures present (D188), and fails if the cook cache was empty. Its load and freezes are recorded in `research/benchmarks.csv` and compared with the last result like the medians, a freeze failing the scene; baselines are lowered by nightly alone (D414). `--build debug` runs it in the debug build for an A/B, never ratcheted. [launcher.md](../launcher.md#the-play-benchmark-d578).

## Why

Every scene settles 240 frames before timing (D185), so neither the load nor the first minute's streaming, where the developer saw 25 to 30 s loads and 1 to 3 s freezes (perf round 1, 2026-10-29), is measured, and nothing holds a fix to them.
