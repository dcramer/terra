# D225: Doors and shutters as props; windows open

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Doors and windows' shutters are props fitted into a hole that fits them (D192, D201), made again in the style (D14) after the first ones were taken out as not good enough: three doors (plank, ledged and braced, studded), one leaf in a doorway 1 m wide and a pair in one 1.5 or 2 m wide, and two shutters (board, louvred), a pair over a window wider than half a metre; each a kind in `builds.ron` with what a leaf costs, a pair two. Each comes with its own frame of squared timber sized to its hole, seated on the wall's own face as its kit's numbers give it (a log wall's crests, a stone wall's cubes), in place of the kit's frame round that hole: a door hangs inside its frame and swings a quarter turn into the room (D207); shutters hang centred on their frame's outer face and fold back flat against it; a leaf stops a walker where it stands, shut, open or swinging. The hammer fits them once the walls round the hole are built, shows them as a ghost first, and takes them out for what went in; the interact key opens and shuts them; the village has them again. Windows have no glass: a window's cubes are open, as a doorway's are, and collide as open cubes do; a window prop is shutters and the kit's frame, never glass.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "we have some of these ugly ass door and window props we can probably just remove for now", "until better props are made"; "also remove the glass windows which i think still exist (unless im wrong). just leave windows as a hole in the wall". Before that: "windows could also be props if we need them to be more than voxels … so they can actually look nice and be interactive", "same w/ doors - so you'd just need a hole that fits them. same as enshrouded". On bringing them back, 2026-10-14: "Like get it done", approval delegated, nothing to ship that reads cheap.
