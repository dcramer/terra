# D491: Space Engineers 2's ground and construction loop

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Whether how blocks are built and taken down follows Space Engineers 2, as [construction-003.md](../proposals/construction-003.md) proposes (its mining is D495, decided): building from a catalogue of prefab parts, each a stamp of half-metre cubes in its kit, snapped to the part it is set against, placed as a plan first, built up in its kit's order by working it with what is delivered, an area at a time, taken down the same way backwards, and carved as cubes are. A single cube is placed and built at once from what is carried, if it is not floating; big materials are still carried (D140). A part can be built one at a time, its ghost laid and built in one gesture, or a structure laid as ghosts first; what is destroyed is left as its ghost (D355). Would amend D48, D139, D226 and D253, costs following the mode (D494); D211's look stays. Nothing is built on it until they choose.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25: "space engineers 2 is actually more the kind of voxel terrain system that we want. its SLIGHTLY better than enshrouded but its fully procedural thats the main diff. that means things like mining behave differently than they currently do, and we MIGHT focus on just making the core building system block-based (with the ability to carve it out) so the buiding system can behave more like space engineers where you deliver resources (which is kind of what we're doing already) and less like enshrouded". Then: "block based mostly means the build loop - so like, instead of it being place each voxel and what not, maybe we'll focus on more prefab parts? but i think walls/etc you could still carve out? maybe its not all that diff but space engineers 2 is really well done here": the build loop, not the look, and walls still carved. Then: "so things still need to made of cubes like they are now. i think that part of our system can stay? but each block would need to be placed as a ghost first, then constructed by delivering the parts": parts are made of cubes, as now. Every cube the hammer lays is a plan first already (D139, D201), filled by holding the hammer, the costs pretend (D333); what is new is the catalogue of parts, the build list and real delivery. Then: "i think the main difference is we should be able to build one thing at a time vs building the entire structure from ghosts?" and "we'll auto replace a destroyed part w/ a ghost i think" (D355 already says so); the modes are D494. Then, answering the desk: mining by hits and ore in deposits (D495); "single cubes: yeah you can place them i guess? you'd have to have the materials in your inventory and it'd immediately place as long as it followed structural rules (e.g. not floating, connected to something)"; "big materials would still be carried by hand". Open while one question remains on the developer's desk (how a part goes from ghost to built). Research: [research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md](../../research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md).
