# D491: Space Engineers 2's ground and construction loop

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Whether mining and how blocks are built and taken down follow Space Engineers 2, as [construction-003.md](../proposals/construction-003.md) proposes: digging held and continuous by the tool's tier and the ground's hardness, every ground material giving something, ore in deposits uneven by region and found by their surface signs; every block placed as a plan first, built up through stages by working it with what is delivered, an area at a time, and taken down the same way backwards. Would amend D48, D139, D226 and D333's pause on costs and storage; D211's look stays unless the developers change it. Nothing is built on it until they choose.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25: "space engineers 2 is actually more the kind of voxel terrain system that we want. its SLIGHTLY better than enshrouded but its fully procedural thats the main diff. that means things like mining behave differently than they currently do, and we MIGHT focus on just making the core building system block-based (with the ability to carve it out) so the buiding system can behave more like space engineers where you deliver resources (which is kind of what we're doing already) and less like enshrouded". Thinking out loud, so Open; its four questions are on the developer's desk. Research: [research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md](../../research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md).
