# D491: Space Engineers 2's ground and construction loop

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Whether mining and how blocks are built and taken down follow Space Engineers 2, as [construction-003.md](../proposals/construction-003.md) proposes: digging held and continuous by the tool's tier and the ground's hardness, every ground material giving something, ore in deposits uneven by region and found by their surface signs; building from a catalogue of prefab parts, each a stamp of half-metre cubes in its kit, snapped to the part it is set against, placed as a plan first, built up in its kit's order by working it with what is delivered, an area at a time, taken down the same way backwards, and carved as cubes are. Would amend D48, D139, D226, D253 and D333's pause on costs and storage; D211's look stays. Nothing is built on it until they choose.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25: "space engineers 2 is actually more the kind of voxel terrain system that we want. its SLIGHTLY better than enshrouded but its fully procedural thats the main diff. that means things like mining behave differently than they currently do, and we MIGHT focus on just making the core building system block-based (with the ability to carve it out) so the buiding system can behave more like space engineers where you deliver resources (which is kind of what we're doing already) and less like enshrouded". Then: "block based mostly means the build loop - so like, instead of it being place each voxel and what not, maybe we'll focus on more prefab parts? but i think walls/etc you could still carve out? maybe its not all that diff but space engineers 2 is really well done here": the build loop, not the look, and walls still carved. Then: "so things still need to made of cubes like they are now. i think that part of our system can stay? but each block would need to be placed as a ghost first, then constructed by delivering the parts": parts are made of cubes, as now. Every cube the hammer lays is a plan first already (D139, D201), filled by holding the hammer, the costs pretend (D333); what is new is the catalogue of parts, the build list and real delivery. Thinking out loud, so Open; three questions remain on the developer's desk. Research: [research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md](../../research/2026-10-25-space-engineers-2.md).
