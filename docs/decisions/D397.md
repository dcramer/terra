# D397: Subagents keep their prompt cache an hour

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Developers · Status: Decided

`.claude/settings.json` sets `subagentPromptCacheTtl` to `"1h"`, so subagents, like sessions, write the one-hour cache rather than the five-minute one. A subagent that waits on a render, a build, a check or a push longer than five minutes then reads its context back on its next turn instead of writing all of it again.

## Why

A token audit of every session and subagent from 2026-10-13 to 10-17 found that 91% of what subagents wrote to the cache was a whole context written again after a wait longer than five minutes, about half of all spending. Replayed at a one-hour TTL, the subagents' cost falls by about half; a one-hour write costs 2x input against 1.25x, and gaps under an hour cover nearly all of the waits. The developer: "do fix the first holy shit what a mess".
