# D585: A gait's clip is shown within a tenth of as made at its speed; the game's speeds are fitted to the clips

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Every gait (walk, jog, sprint) is played at the speed its clip was made at, within a tenth, as docs/animation.md rule 1 says.
- **The clamp.** At the speed a gait moves a body, its clip plays at most 10% faster or slower than made, and its stride is warped at most 10% longer (Unreal's stride warping keeps to this range). Faster play, up to 1.6, is left only for speeds between gaits: a start, a stop, a change of gait (`terra_walker::pace::gait`).
- **A test fails past it.** It names the clip and the speed, and says what to do (`pace::matched`): the player's jog and sprint (`terra_walker` pace tests), the town's and people's walk and jog (`terra_folk` people tests). Never a silent stretch, never a wider clamp.
- **The speed is fitted to the clip** where the clip can't reach it, as project-whiskeyjack did. Otherwise a clip is made at the game's speed.
- **A clip plays at its take's own frame rate** (`tools/characters/clip_rate.test.ts`). made_clips.py keyed Kimodo's 30 fps strides one take frame to one frame of Blender's 24 fps scene, so the walk, jog, sprint and the fight's strafes played at 0.8 of the cadence they were made at, and every pace below read a fifth low until 2026-10-29.
- **Speeds now** (2026-10-29), each from the clip's pace on the base body (`pace.py`), all in `terra_motion::gaits`, which the player (`movement`, `movers.ron`) and the town (`terra_town`) read:
  - The player's jog is 4.0 m/s: its clip, Uthana's locomotion take since 2026-10-30 (pace 3.442), played 1.1 times as fast and its stride warped 1.06. Uthana's locomotion makes no clean jog faster on our body (asked for 5.67 m/s, its feet skated), so the speed is fitted to the clip (4.535 for the Kimodo jog before it, 3.7 from the slowed clip, 4.3 before D585).
  - The sprint is 6.0 m/s (was 4.6): its clip (5.10) played 1.1 times as fast and its stride warped 1.07.
  - The townsfolk walk at the walk clip's 1.375 m/s and jog at its 3.442, each person up to 8% either way; one following the player jogs at 1.03 times the player's jog.
  - Sprinting starts halfway between the jog and the sprint (`gaits::SPRINT_FROM`, 5.0), for the figure and for stamina alike.
- **One stride clock.** Every switch between the walk, jog and sprint keeps the planted foot's phase (`Markers::synced`, tested for every pair).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-30: "they proved its doable, so we need to figure out how to make our shit work". The sprint clip was made at 3.9 m/s and the game ran it at 7.4: played 1.6 times as fast and its stride stretched 1.19, which bent the legs. Others fit the speed to the clip (research/2026-10-30-agent-driven-animation-deep.md).

No source has a sprint at 7.4 m/s that stands upright. All measured 2026-10-30, on our body unless noted:

| Source | Asked | What came out |
|---|---|---|
| Uthana's locomotion API | 7.4 and 6.5 m/s | 4.67 and 4.60 on its own Manny, so it tops out near 4.6. It is trained on 100STYLE, which has no sprints. |
| Kimodo, path held | 6.5 m/s | The legs stride at 5.2 m/s on its own body while the root is dragged at 6.5, so the feet skate. 3.9 on ours: the clip shipped. |
| Kimodo, path held | 7.4, 8.5 and 10 m/s | Takes that leave the ground, the hips rising to 2-3 m. |
| Uthana text-to-motion 3.0, "a sprinter at top speed" | (no speed) | About 4 m/s, leaning 27°. |
| Quaternius UAL1 Sprint_Loop | (stock) | 8.5 m/s, but a hunched charge, leaning 39°, which D316 turned down. |

So the sprint is set to what its clip supports, until a sprint clip is made at a sprinter's speed. Which speeds the game wants is the developers' call. The film of each choice is on the desk.
