# D295: Storage, and building from it

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

As step 7 of [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md) builds D141 on D84's storage: storage is an item set down from the build menu with the cells it `holds` (`builds.ron`; a chest of pine and iron, 8 by 6, for six Wood), its grid a `Storage` component on it, saved with it (`play::storage`). Whatever is built in a claim, a plan's parts and the items set down, is paid from the builder's pack first and then from the storage standing in that claim, nearest the builder first (`Stores`, through `build::act::Building`); what taking apart gives back still goes to the pack. F opens a chest beside the backpack, a click moving a thing across and Shift and a click every one of its kind (`storage_screen`). Storage holding anything is neither taken apart nor moved. Personal containers (D161) wait for other players and recruits. Driven by script with `storage.place`, `storage.put` and `storage.state`, and checked by `cargo xtask cabin`.

## Why

Mine, building step 7 on 2026-10-14. "Nearby" read as the claim, as Enshrouded's base storage serves its crafting: one rule a player can reason about (D122), with the claim already the bound of building (D94). The pack first, so what you brought is what you spend. Refusing to take apart a full chest keeps D193's rule that nothing goes while something rests on it, as Valheim refuses.
