# D416: The ground shades and mirrors what the camera does not see from a horizon map of the land, not from what is drawn

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

What lies off screen already casts: Bevy 0.20 culls a shadow caster against each cascade's own frustum (`update_directional_light_frusta`, `check_dir_light_mesh_visibility`), which has no plane on the sun's side (`ViewFrustum::from_clip_from_world` leaves the cascade's near half-space open) and draws what lies past it pancaked (`unclipped_depth`), and `terrain` chooses its chunks all round the viewer, never by the view; no terrain chunk is `NotShadowCaster` or ranged. What is missing is past what the shadow map and the screen hold: the sun's cascades end at 300 m (`Quality::shadows`), so a mountain's evening shadow across a valley a kilometre off is not drawn at all, and the lake's reflection, a march through the screen's depth, falls back off screen to the skyline's flat colours (D322). The fix is the far shadow D322 named and never built, as Unreal's far shadow cascades and distance-field heightfield shadows do for landscape past the dynamic shadow distance and The Witcher 3's terrain shadows do from the heightmap: a horizon map of the land round the camera, baked on a worker in `terra_land` (`Round`, beside its skyline march, no Bevy, D28) as the elevation of the horizon in 16 ways over a grid of about 24 m out to 6 km, the planet's curve taken off as the skyline's march takes it, about 4 MB as a texture. The ground's shader takes the sun's light off where the sun stands under that horizon, softened over the sun's half degree and faded in over the last cascade's last fifth; the far forest's cards and the lake read the same texture, so a valley's trees and water go into the mountain's shadow with its ground. It is baked again when the camera has moved a kilometre from its middle, the sun's moving needing nothing. The lake's off-screen fallback takes the same map's shade on its skyline, so a peak mirrored off screen is lit as the one on screen is; a reflection probe captured from the lake is the next step if that seam still shows.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-18: "the terrain must be rendered even if its not fully visible because reflections/ray tracing get fucked, same with shadows". Read in Bevy 0.20's source, culling was not it: the casters behind and beside the camera are in the shadow map already; the shadows and reflections past it were never drawn. A fifth cascade is not possible (Bevy's `MAX_CASCADES_PER_LIGHT` is 4) and stretching the last over kilometres redraws every chunk's shadow each frame and coarsens the near ones; a horizon map costs a lookup a pixel and nothing a frame. Leaning until the before and after vantages (~/.cache/terra-review/terrain-casters/) and the bench hold it.
