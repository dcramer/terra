# D382: Fliers, and the dragon

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Creatures that fly are a body plan of their own (`play::fliers`), each kind a file entry (`assets/data/fliers.ron`: its model, its clips by what they are for, its cruising and hover speeds, how far and fast it banks and turns, its mouth's socket and its breath), flown as shipped games and films fly a big flier (research/2026-10-15-dragons-and-fire.md, research/2026-10-17-large-flier-motion.md): along a course planned when it is told it (`play::fliers::plan`), a clamped cubic B-spline over the points that cuts each corner, flown no faster than its most bank lets it turn there (tan φ = v²κ/g), slower climbing and faster diving, gathering and shedding speed no harder than its kind's `accel_mps2` and `brake_mps2`, into a short hover facing a point; banked by the turn a third of a second ahead, eased as a heavy body rolls; nose up as it brakes (its flare); its neck turned into each turn and climb ahead of its body and its tail trailing behind it, laid over its clips; its clips weighed rather than switched, beating its wings to climb, gather speed or stay up slow and gliding as it comes down, brakes or cruises, with a bout of beats every few seconds in a cruise, its body lifted on each downstroke; rearing back for its kind's `windup_s` before its breath comes; trailer scenes work the same plan out in TypeScript (`tools/sandbox/trailer/flight.ts`) to aim their cameras, and `tools/sandbox/flightplot.ts` plots a scene's course before it is filmed; breathing fire in a strafing pass or hovering, aimed at a point within a turn of its head. Its breath is `build::fire::BreathesFire` on its mouth, a cone cast into the base as rays that heat what they meet, and drawn as a jet of flame (`terra_world::fire`). Until monsters think, it is told what to do over the remote (`flight.spawn`, `flight.path`, `flight.hover`, `flight.breathe`, `flight.roar`), as an AI will tell it. The first kind is a dragon, wyvern-built as Skyrim's, 13 m long and 18 m across its wings (Tripo, rigged and hand-keyed in Blender, `tools/creatures/dragon.py`); whether dragons are in the game, and where, is the developers' (Open).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15 asked for a demo of "a dragon fucking setting it [a castle] on fire". The flight model is the one every shipped dragon uses: a path, bank from the turn, hover as a short cheat, breath as a cone test; data so other fliers (birds, wyverns) are entries, not code.
