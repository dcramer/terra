# D382: Fliers, and the dragon

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Creatures that fly are a body plan of their own (`play::fliers`), each kind a file entry (`assets/data/fliers.ron`: its model, its clips by what they are for, its cruising and hover speeds, how far and fast it banks and turns, its mouth's socket and its breath), flown as shipped games fly their dragons (research/2026-10-15-dragons-and-fire.md): along a flight path, a Catmull-Rom spline through points, at its cruising speed, banked into each turn by tan φ = v²κ/g within its most, its heading turning no faster than it turns; climbing on beating wings and gliding as it comes down or banks fast; slowing over its last 40 m into a short hover facing a point; breathing fire in a strafing pass or hovering, aimed at a point within a turn of its head. Its breath is `build::fire::BreathesFire` on its mouth, a cone cast into the base as rays that heat what they meet, and drawn as a jet of flame (`terra_world::fire`). Until monsters think, it is told what to do over the remote (`flight.spawn`, `flight.path`, `flight.hover`, `flight.breathe`, `flight.roar`), as an AI will tell it. The first kind is a dragon, wyvern-built as Skyrim's, 13 m long and 18 m across its wings (Tripo, rigged and hand-keyed in Blender, `tools/creatures/dragon.py`); whether dragons are in the game, and where, is the developers' (Open).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15 asked for a demo of "a dragon fucking setting it [a castle] on fire". The flight model is the one every shipped dragon uses: a path, bank from the turn, hover as a short cheat, breath as a cone test; data so other fliers (birds, wyverns) are entries, not code.
