# D653: The one-handed tool axe is a hatchet, its own kind; the long axe's envelope is kept as the axe kind

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The bar's one-handed axe is a hatchet: 0.40 to 0.45 m overall, its head 0.6 to 0.8 kg in iron. It is the kind `hatchet` (`assets/data/kinds/hatchet.json`, D566), whose reference is the hatchet head on a 0.40 m hickory haft as the assembler weighs it (0.419 m, 0.93 kg, the head 0.70 kg, inertia 0.0741 kg m² about the grip), with D459's envelope round it: length 0.377 to 0.461 m, head 0.53 to 0.97 kg. Its hafts are code-made at their own length (`short_hickory_haft`, `short_ash_haft`), drawn in the long hafts' skins. The `axe` kind keeps its 0.61 to 0.75 m envelope and its parts for a later felling or battle axe; today only the iron axe's look is one.

## Why

The developers, of the tools in play: "the tools are wack, scaled wrong". The stone and iron axes took their size, 0.68 m, from the free pack's model, and D565's first envelope was copied from it; carried at the side, a tool that long held near its butt reads as dangling to the shin, though every measure of the carry passes. A one-handed tool that fells and trims is a hatchet (a camp hatchet's 16 to 18 in handle and 1.5 lb head, recalled, not looked up). A second kind and not a changed one, because a kind is a move-set class with one reference (D566): a hatchet's inertia, 0.074, is a quarter of the long axe's 0.32, far outside D459's tempo clamp, so the two cannot share clips; and the long axe's parts and envelope stay checked, not deleted. Decided by the agent (the coordinator's technical call, held-assembled j2); what the pickaxe is, one- or two-handed, stays the developers'.
