# D234: Shadows that hand over without popping, and a guard on Bevy's range limit

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Bevy 0.20's shadow passes never dither a crossfade (`specialize_shadows` does not set `VISIBILITY_RANGE_DITHER`, unlike the main pass and the depth prepass), so a shadow caster draws in full until its range ends and then is gone at once: the sun flecks flipped as a crown's shadow handed over from cards to lumps. Shadow stand-ins instead shrink in the vertex shader, each card or lump into its middle, carried in its colour since only the sun sees it and Bevy's shadow passes get no tangents: cards shrink away and lumps grow in between 14 and 26 m, for every tree alike as Horizon Zero Dawn keeps one shadow detail a cascade, and lumps shrink away toward 100 m; all follow `DetailScale`. Every detail range is kept to a 0.25 m grid, so however many things are placed few distinct ranges are in use (a test: 10,000 trees of their own sizes share under 6,000), and the world reports an error past 20,000, a third of Bevy's 65,535. `cargo xtask lodcheck` runs three views in parallel in about 40 s, and `cargo xtask bench` with every scene runs it. Patching Bevy's shadow pipeline to dither is the alternative (D184), kept for if the shrinking does not hold.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "the shades are clipping in", "for the 65k limit, is this something we can defend for it", and the check to run with performance testing, under a minute. lodcheck: floor mean 0.026% to 0.004% a frame and worst 1.93% to 0.33%; path worst 0.68% to 0.01%; clearing 0.42% to 0.09%; it now passes. The walk bench held the budget at 11.8 ms before another run took the GPU.
