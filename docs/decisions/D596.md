# D596: A world is made from its seed alone

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A world's seed, a `u64`, is the only input its generation takes. It is kept in its `world.ron` (`seed`), chosen when the world is made and never changed; a world kept from before worlds had seeds was made from seed 1 and reads as it. `--seed <n>` gives the seed of a run that keeps no world and of a world made (`--fresh`, a new `--world=`); a kept world's own seed always wins. The world's home realm takes the world's seed as its own (`WorldSpec::seed`, `planet::entering::first_world`); another realm's seed derives from it through `terra_gen::hash` (a portal's from the realm left and its place). Every system's random stream is its realm's seed derived for its purpose (`Seed::derive` by name, `at` by place, `index` by number), never a seed written into the code, the clock or the operating system's randomness.
- **Held by**: the world-seed lint in the push hook (`xtask/src/world_seed.rs`): in the crates' own code, outside tests, benches and programs, no `Seed(<number>)` and no system randomness, with a short list of a sound's dice, which are heard and not the world. And `terra_land::fingerprint`'s test: the same seed makes the same ground, stands, trees and weather bit for bit on other threads, and another seed makes a world that differs in every part.
- **The start is searched for, not made** (`terra_land::spawn`): the land is never reshaped for it. As shipped survival games pick a spawn (Minecraft walks out from the origin to a biome it allows; Valheim starts in its Meadows near the middle), the search walks out from the planet's own start, nearest first, every 250 m to 6 km, to the first place where the start's base plot lies level (`Plot::START` and 3 m round it rising at most 2.5 m, as `terra_world::start` asks), the ground is not walled in (25 km² reached on foot over slopes of at most 40°), and a walk reaches three biomes: a square kilometre of closed forest, a shore, and a tenth of one of tundra or bare rock past the trees. The walk is followed 8 km east, west, north and south: a day's walk out and back, where a survival game puts its first biomes, a twelfth of the way round the 30 km world. The planet's frame, latitudes and sky stay turned for the planet's own start (`Planet::origin`); the player, the camera and everything laid round them stand at the found one (`Planet::start`). Seed 1 keeps the planet's own start, which its demos and baselines stand on (D393), as does a world too small for latitudes.
- **Held by the sweep** (`cargo xtask worlds`): each seed's 30 km world, its start searched for and surveyed by the same rules, a row a seed, and its fingerprints' and starts' digests, which `--against` an earlier table compares bit for bit.
- **New world rolls a seed** on the title screen (Enter rolls again, digits type one's own); playing a world of another seed than the land behind the title crosses to its land.

## Why

v0's second seam (D589, docs/v0-goals.md): "The planet is `Seed(1)` and no option changes it. A world's seed becomes the only input; each realm's seed derives from it (`terra_gen::hash`)." The home realm keeps the world's seed rather than one derived from it so that seed 1's world, on which every pinned demo place (D393), look baseline and scorecard stands, stays as it is; a derivation would have moved all of them for no gain. A lint rather than review because a seed written in is easy to add and invisible in a picture; the fingerprint because only a run shows what a lint cannot see (an unordered map's order, the clock, a stream shared between two parts).

The start is searched for rather than the generator reshaped round it (the coordinator's call, seeds j3): the land's rules stay the same on every seed, and the first sweep, of 128 seeds, found only 43 valid with every world starting at the planet's own start, failing on a level plot (41), a start walled in by steep ground (23) and tundra out of reach (67), faults of where the start was, not of the land. Reshaping the land for a start would make the one place a player sees first unlike the rest of the world.
