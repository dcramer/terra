# D282: A calmer wind: gusts to one and a half times the steady wind, leaf clusters that rustle as one

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D255. Gusts run from under a half to one and a half times the old steady wind, about the same on average, not a fifth to two and a half, at which a gust swung an oak's crown a metre. A leaf card's flutter takes its phase from where it is at a branch's scale (a third of a radian a metre, not two), so a card's corners move together, and shakes 1.5 cm, not 5: a card is a cluster of leaves. Trees, the world's flora and grass share the gusts; grass keeps its own flutter.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "very bubble trees, like the shake effect was a bit crazy". Measured on a still view of crowns at 15:00: the picture changed 2.2% between frames a tenth of a second apart (0.07% with the trees' wind off), three quarters of it from the cards' flutter; now 1.2%.
