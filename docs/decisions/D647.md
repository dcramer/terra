# D647: v0's worlds have small wildlife about for ambience: insects, small birds and the like

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A world of v0 is not empty at a few metres: small life is about in it for ambience, as a part of the world's design, beside the animals a player meets: insects (butterflies, bees, midges, dragonflies by water, fireflies after dark), small birds near and overhead, and creatures of that size, each where and when it would be: by place, season, warmth and hour. They are for the eye and ear, not for play: nothing is gained from them and they need not cross the network. Each realm has its own. **How many**: dozens about at once, not overwhelming and never rare. **How they are made**: the art is uniform; what is the size of a fly may be a speck drawn by a shader, but a bird is clearly a model, as every creature is. **The bar**: a world that feels as alive as Crimson Desert's. Water has its fish. The proposal for all of it is [ambient-life.md](../ambient-life.md).

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08: "we should make sure we've also included various wildlife (small, like insects/birds/similar creatures) for ambience in our worlds. That should be in v0 for world design"; "i want like. dozens of small creatures/bugs/birds in the atmosphere. not overwhelming but it shouldn't be rare"; "make sure you've generated the right visuals for all these. i want art to be uniform. things like a fly might just be a spec with shaders or something, but a bird is clearly a model"; "our goal is an environment and world that feels similarly alive to crimson desert"; "similarly we'll want fish in water".
