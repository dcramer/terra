# D647: v0's worlds have small wildlife about for ambience: insects, small birds and the like

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A world of v0 is not empty at a few metres: small life is about in it for ambience, as a part of the world's design, beside the animals a player meets: insects (butterflies, bees, midges, dragonflies by water, fireflies after dark), small birds near and overhead, and creatures of that size, each where and when it would be: by place, season, warmth and hour. They are for the eye and ear, not for play: nothing is gained from them and they need not cross the network. Each realm has its own.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08: "we should make sure we've also included various wildlife (small, like insects/birds/similar creatures) for ambience in our worlds. That should be in v0 for world design".

## Log

- **2026-11-08**: D647 decided (by the developers, as quoted). The first realm had its small birds and animals as creatures and midges, motes and far birds as drift (D379); butterflies, bees, dragonflies and fireflies are added to the drift's data, with a butterfly's look and a light of its own for what is seen in the dark, and `drift.state` says what is about. The industrial realm's own small life is not chosen (D642).
