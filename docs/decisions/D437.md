# D437: What a generated creature promises, and who gets hand work

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

There is no hand-finished tier: every creature, monsters and the ones that matter most included, is the pipeline's best estimate, and it is good enough when it passes the creature check (D442) and a harsh adversarial review rates it 7/10 or more. Important monsters are worked out further in dialogue between the developers and the agents, not finished by hand ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#how-good-it-would-get-honestly)).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-23: "bar is the most important, adversary reviewer is still important. we do not 'hand-finished' because not enough people work on this game, you need to do best estimation and for important monsters we work them out further via dialogue between us". Earlier (2026-10-22) he asked for creatures that "will generally be good" from a predictable pipeline. The recommendation had hero creatures finished by hand; with too few people for that, the bar and the review carry every creature.
