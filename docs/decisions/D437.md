# D437: What a generated creature promises, and who gets hand work

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: a generated creature is good enough when it passes the creature check (D442) and a harsh review rates its gait film 7/10 or more beside reference footage, with no hand work; hero creatures (the dragon, the troll, bosses) are finished by hand on top: signature moves, faces, corrective shapes ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#how-good-it-would-get-honestly)). Open: the bar, and which creatures are heroes.

## Why

The developer asked for creatures that "will generally be good" from a predictable pipeline (2026-10-22). Shipped games hand-finish their headline creatures and let rules carry the rest; where the line falls sets how much a template body and a check are worth.
