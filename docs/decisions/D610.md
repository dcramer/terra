# D610: The play scenes are benched at each landing, a failure bisected unattended, and an A/B reads main's side from it

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A continuous row `perf` (`tools/continuous/checks.json`, D398) benches a short set of play scenes (lake-play, town-dusk, forest-run, castle; one run each) at each landing that touches what they read (`xtask/src/scope.rs`), landings that come while it runs taken together, and keeps each scene's numbers by commit. A miss after a pass bisects its range itself, as the nightly does (D411), and names the commit. A job's A/B reads main's side from that record at its commit and benches only its own, its variants probed in one load (D609). It runs within D319 (a bench has the GPU alone) and D445's planner. As Chromium's perf bots chart every change and Pinpoint bisects a regression, and Mozilla's Perfherder alerts on a sustained change. [research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md](../../research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md#4-proposals-ranked).

## Why

On 2026-11-02 town-dusk regressed at 14:44 and was seen only at ~18:12, by a job benching four scenes for another reason; its last +11% was bisected by hand over 41 commits and never confirmed. The two perf rows are lake-only and rest 6 and 12 hours. Meanwhile every job benched both sides of its own A/B, so benches held the worker's queue: three back to back starved it for 1.5 hours, j9's three hours went mostly to the queue, j13's bench was cancelled while waiting. Leaning until the row and its bisect are built and its cost on the worker's queue measured.
