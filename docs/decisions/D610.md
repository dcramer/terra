# D610: The play scenes are benched at each landing, a failure bisected unattended, and an A/B reads main's side from it

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The perf row ([tools/perf](../../tools/perf/README.md)) benches lake-play, forest-run and town-dusk, one run each, once at each landing batch on main that touches what they read (`tools/perf/row.json`): the landings that come while it runs are taken together at the next tip, as a busy Buildbot builder collapses its pending requests. It runs in the render worker's daemon, the one always-on GPU box, as a bench job of its own queue, so D319 (a bench has the box alone) and D445's planner order it, with a rest of 20 minutes after each of its benches so it never holds the box with benches back to back. Each scene's median, p99, hitches, load and card are kept by commit (`~/terra-render/perf/history.csv`). A metric past its noise band, the window of its 12 points before (the median, and the wider of a floor and three robust deviations), is benched again at the same commit, and when that holds too an alert names the scene, the metric, before and after and the range; a regression is bisected by halving the range's commits touching the row, a step bad when nearer the bad end, at most five benches, and the alert names the first bad commit or what is left. An A/B reads main's side from the history at its base (`node tools/perf/perf.ts ab`), naming the commits between and whether they touch what the row reads, and benches only its own; its variants go through the probe (D609). As Chromium's perf bots chart every change and Pinpoint bisects a regression, Mozilla's Perfherder alerts on a sustained change past a threshold from its back window and its sheriffs retrigger a suspect to confirm it, and Unreal's Gauntlet runs a fixed set per changelist. [research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md](../../research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md#4-proposals-ranked).

## Why

On 2026-11-02 town-dusk regressed at 14:44 and was seen only at ~18:12, by a job benching four scenes for another reason; its last +11% was bisected by hand over 41 commits and never confirmed. The two perf rows are lake-only and rest 6 and 12 hours. Meanwhile every job benched both sides of its own A/B, so benches held the worker's queue: three back to back starved it for 1.5 hours, j9's three hours went mostly to the queue, j13's bench was cancelled while waiting.

Built in the worker rather than as a row of `tools/continuous/checks.json`: under the embargo no machine runs continuous with a GPU, and the worker already owns the queue and the bench's turn on the card; the daemon reads main by `git ls-remote` of the public repository and the landing's LFS objects are on the box from its push check. Castle is left out of the row for now: three scenes keep a landing's bench to a few minutes. One run a landing is noisy for hitches, so a single point never alerts: the retrigger is the second run, as a Perfherder sheriff's. The developers: "these should all be based on what are the best practices on the gaming industry, not what i want".
