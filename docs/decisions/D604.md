# D604: The volumetric fog takes the air's dimming of the sun at a ray's two ends, not at every lit step

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Bevy 0.20's `volumetric_fog.wesl`, with an atmosphere on the camera, works out at every step of a directional light's march that the shadow map finds lit how much of the sun reaches that point through the air: a transmittance table lookup and the share of the sun's disk over the horizon. Over one ray through the haze (D273, at most a box of a few hundred metres) that changes by next to nothing, so it is now taken at the ray's first and last steps and drawn linearly between them. A patch of `bevy_pbr` 0.20.0 (`patches/bevy_pbr`, D184), marked `Terra's patch (D604)`.

Upstream: not yet offered.

## Why

The lit steps were where the haze's time went: at the lake in play (route view, 1901x1550, worker 4090) `volumetric_lighting` fell from 0.73 to 0.40 ms when the low sun went behind the ridge (play-lake-fps j11, the clock running), the march's shadow lookups unchanged. With the clock held at 17 h, two runs a side in one job (play-lake-fps j12): `volumetric_lighting` 0.73/0.73 to 0.51/0.51 ms, the frame's GPU total 7.92/7.95 to 7.68/7.71 ms. The sun's colour through the air differs along a ray by its height and direction's change across the haze box, a few hundred metres on a planet tens of kilometres round, which a straight line between the ends follows. The lake, range and tundra stills match the unpatched build within noise.
