# D153: Body temperature

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A survival attribute of its own, for players and recruits alike (D169). It drifts toward what the surroundings push it to (air, wind, wet, sun, shelter, fire), slowed by clothing and raised by work. Out of a comfortable band it costs you: cold or heat raises stamina costs, and slows a recruit's work (D169). Proposed: cold also raises hunger and heat thirst, and far out of the band health falls; shown on the body first (D93).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "body temperature is probably useful, and basically if you're too cold it could increase stamina usage (same with too hot) or increase thirst or something else"; settled with D169 the same day: recruits "need to deal w/ temperature as well as itll slow down their work", and "npcs generally should be pretty similar to a player". See [climate.md](../climate.md#body-temperature).
