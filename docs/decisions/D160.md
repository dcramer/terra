# D160: How structures work in code

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

What stands is kept by `terra_build` as half-metre cubes of a kind of construction on the base's grid (D181), saved and sent as operations; each cube is planned or built to a fill level, which is the unit of progress, cost and collision; a part is how the interface groups cubes (since 2026-10-12). Pieces, what is wall and what floor (D277), buildings, rooms in three dimensions (D261) and collision are worked out from the cubes by pure functions, for the building that changed. Each kind is drawn by its kit (patterns in code, kits as data, D177, D258), each exposed edge by its role (D215), and always follows the current kit, so a kit tuned later redraws what is built (since 2026-10-12; was frozen once a piece was in). A roof is cubes of a roof kind, drawn from their neighbours (D241, D258); it was a height field with The Sims' sections for gables. Built pieces are merged into meshes per fixed section of cubes and material, rebuilt where cubes changed, with a simplified stand-in per section far off. A base is saved and sent as a snapshot and the operations since; ground heights under a plan are sampled into it. Saves of any older format are refused, not converted (D353). See [building-tech.md](../building-tech.md).

## Why

Mine, for D139, D146, D152, D154 and D155, after [research/procedural-buildings.md](../../research/procedural-buildings.md). Rule tables over wave function collapse, which can fail. The height field because walls run in four directions, so a hip roof needs no skeleton solver, and none in Rust fits our licence; checked by a test on L, T, U and courtyard plans rather than a separate spike. Progress per cube, not per piece, so a piece's identity can change with its kit and neighbours without losing what was built ([construction-001.md](../proposals/construction-001.md#gaps-and-how-to-close-them)).
