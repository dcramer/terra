# D568: The backhand is a falling diagonal cut

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The sword-and-shield set's backhand (the light chain's second blow, `Keyed_Backhand`, played as `Open_Backhand`) is a falling diagonal cut, not a flat one: from the sword cocked over the left shoulder, the blade falls across the body to the low right, its working edge (-Z, D565 step 3) leading down and to the right, then returns to the guard. It still sweeps left to right and crosses ahead at its fastest, so the slash, backhand and finisher keep their alternating sides.

## Why

A flat backhand with the blade ahead at contact needs its edge facing right, and no person's wrist turns the hand there: the reach solver (kimodo_keys.py, on wrist.ts's limits) puts it 38° past a pronated wrist, while edges leading down and to the right are in reach (D565 step 3, job 5). The developers on 2026-10-28, asked whether to keep it flat or cut it on a diagonal: "Diagonal cut".
