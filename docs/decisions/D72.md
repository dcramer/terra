# D72: Getting between worlds

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

Settled on 2026-10-12: there is no fast travel between claims. Quick travel comes from other systems, such as magic (astral magic), portals players build and mounts, and what you carry goes with you. Leaning on the rest: probably portals, or a similar mechanism. The first is likely a portal you find in the world; more ways of crossing come over time, perhaps portals players build, as in Valheim. Not flight: there is no space (D4). No traditional loading screen when crossing; the crossing itself, a portal effect, may cover loading and unloading if it has to.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10. Was flight through space, seamless and without a loading screen, Decided earlier the same day. Whether crossing has a loading screen, and how the server hands a player between worlds, are open; see [worlds.md](../worlds.md#realms-and-portals).
