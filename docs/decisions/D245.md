# D245: How closed the canopy is, measured

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The vantage sheet also looks straight up from eye height, 1.7 m over the ground, at twelve places on a spiral 40 to 170 m from the start (`--vantage-ground` sets a fixed camera's heights over the ground under it, and stands it out of any trunk), and reports the share of each picture that is sky, told by its colour and smoothness (bluer than red and green, not dark, and even over a few pixels, or white cloud; bark and needles in the sky's light are as blue but darker, greyer or textured), each view's mask kept beside it, and their mean and median. A forest that feels like being inside one shows little sky from under it: under a closed canopy 7 to 9% of full sun reaches eye height ([research/forest-lod-and-mood.md](../../research/forest-lod-and-mood.md)); the lush forest's aim is a mean under 15%.

## Why

An adversarial review against Sons of the Forest found the canopy open, about 70% sky looking up. Measured this way on 2026-10-13 at 15:00 it was 17.5% (median 16.5%), and 5.1% (median 2.4%) after D247.
