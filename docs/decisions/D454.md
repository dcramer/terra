# D454: The fight's roll and light slash hold the arms by a layered blend per bone

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The sword-and-shield set's roll plays `Fight_Roll`: the library's forward roll with both arms held as the guard (`Keyed_Guard`, D450) holds them, each turned 90° out about the body's up axis at the shoulder, so the strapped shield and the sword stay beside the body through the tuck. It is a layered blend per bone, as Unreal's Layered Blend per Bone and Unity's avatar masks lay an arm's pose over a whole-body clip, baked into the clip at export (`tools/characters/hold.py`, a set's list entry with `"of"` and `"hold"`), so the game and the move check play it as any other clip. It plays at 2.0, 0.6 s from the stand to standing and to the next roll, Enshrouded's cadence (0.5-0.53 s to standing, 0.6-0.64 s roll to roll); its drive is scaled with it, so it still covers 3.3 m, and its i-frames, in the clip's own seconds, shorten with it (0.46 to 0.33 s).

A held part may be eased in and out (`"in"`, `"out"`, clip seconds): Unreal's Layered Blend per Bone with its weight driven by a curve, a Unity layer's weight, each held bone slerped in its own parent frame from the `of` clip's pose to the held one by a smoothstep weight, keyed every frame. The light blows and the heavy hold the shield arm low at the left side throughout, where the guard rests it (D531): `Open_Slash` (the slash, the finisher and the partner's slash), `Open_Backhand` and `Open_Heavy` are the keyed takes with the shield arm held so (round 20: the guard's pose with the upper arm aimed down and about 45° out and a little back, the forearm forward and down, rolled so the board's face looks out to the left and a little forward, its middle at the hip about 0.4 m out; hold.py's `aim` and `roll`, research/2026-10-25-shield-prior-art.md), as Enshrouded's shield goes out left through a swing (research/2026-10-25-shield-arm-in-a-swing.md). Until combat round 32 the guard carried it at the chest and each blow eased it out over its wind-up and back in its recovery. The right arm and the rest of the body are the take's, so its timings and spec are unchanged. The backhand (`Open_Backhand`, in 0-0.25 s before the sword reaches the left shoulder at 0.4, out 1.25-1.65) and the heavy and the partner's heavy (`Open_Heavy`, in 0.3-0.6 s, the wind-up, out 1.9-2.3) drop it the same way. The block plays `Clear_Block`, `Keyed_Block` with the sword arm held out of the raised shield's way (clavicle_r and below: the guard's upper arm a little further out, the forearm less across the body and rolled 45°, so the blade stands up at the right side leaning out, still behind the board's plane), eased in over 0-0.25 s with the raise and back over 0.95-1.35 s as the shield comes down; its shield arm is held at the low rest at both ends and plays the take's raise between (an `out`, 0-0.2 s, before an `in`, 0.95-1.35 s), so the board rises from the hip and goes back to it; the rest of the body is the take's, so the raise and its timings are unchanged.

A chained blow keeps the shield where the blow it cuts short has it. With the shield held low through every blow and the guard, the blows carry it nowhere, so the set names no held takes; a set whose blows ease a part out and back names, for each chained blow, a held take with no `in` (`chained`) and how the blow it cuts short carries the part (`carry`), and the move runner (`Runner::takes`, terra_motion) mixes the held take into the blow's clip by how far out that part still is at the press (`figure::mix`, terra_walker), as a layered blend per bone keeps its own weight when the base pose changes (Unreal's Layered Blend per Bone with a curve-driven weight; an inertialized transition, Bollo, GDC 2018, would still play the part going in and out again).

A reel (a flinch, a stagger, a broken guard) plays its library clip with the guard's arms laid over it by the hold layer (`terra_walker::layer`, the set's `guard`), as a hit reaction plays under a weapon stance's upper body (Unreal's montage in an upper-body slot through a Layered Blend per Bone): the body reels, the shield and sword stay at the guard, out of the body, and are where the guard wants them when the reel ends. A stagger blends in over 0.3 s (a flinch, a broken guard and going down 0.08 s), as a hit reaction's montage blends in (Unreal's default 0.25 s), so the shield goes from where the parried blow carried it to the guard without a snap. The fight record carries each fighter's shield (`shield`: the board's middle and its face), and the duel's `stagger shield jump` check scores the farthest the board moves in the fighter's own frame within 0.1 s through a stagger (`film.stagger.shield_jump`, bar 0.15 m).

The guard these holds start and end at carries the shield low at the left hip with the block not held, and only the block raises it (D531, the developers, 2026-10-26): `Low_Guard`, `Keyed_Guard` with the blows' low shield arm held throughout (the set's `guard`, combat round 32). The strafes lay the same arm over their own with the forearm aimed a little further out (`Low_Strafe`, [0.15, -0.5, 0.85]), so the stepping thigh clears the board.

## Why

Combat round 29 (2026-10-26): research/2026-10-25-shield-prior-art.md's fault 4, measured from the duel's record. The parried heavy's stagger played the unarmed `Hit_Head`, whose left arm hangs, so the shield dropped from the heavy's carry at the left to the hip in 5 frames (0.3 m) and, 1.2 s later, swung back up to the guard 0.6 m in 10 frames: 0.47 m in 0.1 s at worst. The bare `Hit_Head` and `Hit_Chest` also put the sword 11 cm and the shield 12 cm into the thighs; under the guard's hold both pass every move-check row. Under the guard with the 0.08 s blend, the shield would still cross the 0.4 m from the carry to the guard in 5 frames; blended over 0.3 s the worst is 0.21 m in 0.1 s (the reel's torso turns the guard's shoulder as it blends) and the stagger's end shows no jump. A longer blend would soften the reel itself; inertialization (Bollo, GDC 2018) is the next step if the shield must go below the bar.

Combat round 23 (2026-10-25): in the guard the sword stands upright just behind the shield's board, 3 cm clear; `Keyed_Block` raises the board across the face and tilts its top back, into the blade's upper half (19.1 cm through at 0.47 s). Holding the sword low and forward at the right hip (Dark Souls' and Elden Ring's shield-up stance) cleared the held block but went 12-18 cm through the board's front on the way back to the guard, the blade having to come round the rim to get behind it again, and into a wider-hipped template's thigh; leaning the blade out to the right while it stays behind the board's plane clears it the whole way (-5.5 cm, every template) with no other row of the move check changed.

Combat round 21 (2026-10-25): each chained blow eased its shield in from the guard (`in`) while the blow it cut short still carried it out, so between blows it swung in toward the body and out again: 0.30 m backhand into finisher at the cancel, 0.32 m at the worst press of the window (`moves.ts` now measures every press from the cancel to the end of the blow, by what the runner picks there, and counts a round trip as the shield straying from the straight way between where it was and where it is 0.3 s on). Round 20 found that simply dropping `in` would put the late press's blow 0.4 m from the guard. With the held take mixed in by the outgoing's carry: 0.08 m at the worst press (backhand into finisher at 1.39 s, the shield 72% out), 0.02 m at the cancel; the blows' starts from the guard and into the next unchanged (0.03, 0 m); one of two takes picked by the nearer pose left 0.17 m at the ease's middle.

Combat round 8 (2026-10-24). The library's roll was made unarmed: its arms fold into the chest, so the shield on the forearm went 22.8 cm through the spine and the left hand 2.6 cm into it (`moves.ts`, D447). A re-take by the clip step (D450) does not fit a roll: Kimodo's keys are places in the body's frame at the guard, which a tumbling body leaves, and the step scores blows. Holding the guard's arms unturned put the shield 10 cm into the thighs, which the tuck brings to the chest; turned out 45 to 75° it was 4.9 to 8.2 cm, the sword into the right thigh once the shield cleared; turned 90° both ways, the shield and sword clear the body by 4.7 cm and the hands by 3.3 cm on every body template (3.2 cm sampled at 30 fps). The move runner ends a move at `done` and starts a waiting press only once its cancel opens, so a cancel past `done` would drop a second roll: the roll's length and its roll to roll are one number, 0.6 s, where both of Enshrouded's ranges meet. Whether a roll with the arms out reads well is for the developers' eye on the duel film.

Combat round 16 (2026-10-25): `Keyed_Slash` held its shield across the chest, so its own sword went 25.1 cm through the board on the way back to the guard and the board 11.2 cm into the sword hand in the follow-through (D481). Round 15 re-keyed the left hand for Kimodo: the shield cleared in all 32 takes but Kimodo moved the right arm off its path in every one. Holding only the shield arm over the kept take leaves the right arm exactly as it passed: the first try (90°, in 0.1-0.3, out 1.35-1.75) cleared both, sword -3.2 cm and arm -8.0 cm on every body template, the rest of those moves' rows still passing (hands -1.4 to -2.2 cm); constant holds (`Fight_Roll`, `Fight_Walk`) export to the same numbers. The same turn and eased windows cleared the backhand (sword 16.5 to -4.8 cm, arm 7.5 to -8.9) and the heavy (27.5 to -7.0, 15.2 to -10.6) on their first try, every other row passing.
