# D241: Roofs as cubes, built by hand

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

As D226 asks, in place of D209's roof sections: a roof is cubes of a roof kind, a kit of `pattern: Roof` numbered from 2048 (`Kind::is_roof`; shakes, slate, thatch, each a file), laid like any cube by hand, with the material picker, brushes and shapes (D231 to D233), or by "add a roof with this material" (`terra_build::roofer`), which lays a plan of them over the space walls close in or a rectangle drawn, a gable (each arm of an L, T or U its own ridge) or a hip (the chessboard distance from the footprint's edge) or a lean-to, slopes, outer and inner corners and, where a span is odd, a ridge's whole cubes, and the walls under its gable ends carried up to it in their own kind. A roof's cubes are no wall tile, floor or room; a room is indoors under them. They hold as any cube does (D193), and a slope also joins the cube a layer below and over whose top its ramp runs on from (`support::ramp`), so a roof climbs a cube a cube with nothing under its slopes. Each goes up in four steps of its fill: frame, battens, covering, trims; a walker stands on its slopes and passes under its underside. Its look is its place among its neighbours (`layout::roofs`): every roof cube with nothing over it is sampled every half cube in four triangles a square, a square carrying on into the next where their corners meet, so roofs at different heights stay apart; creases are ridges, hips and valleys, edges eaves and verges, or flashing where a wall or a higher roof stands beyond; past an open edge the covering is carried on by the kit's `eaves`, drawn only, on the planes of the cube it runs from, meeting the next round a corner as a hip or in a valley; a ridge a cube wide is one cap. D209's stages are kept; since D258 its covering is the cubes' surface in the covering's material and its edges and frame the kit's hand-made modules, not generated courses, caps and boards. A wall under a roof cube is carried up into it, cut at the roof's deck, the plane its covering lies on and its boards under it, eaves and verges are drawn on, so a gable meets its roof in one straight line with no gap: a block kind's surface cut before it is rounded and textured where it ends up, sawn square at the cut, a timber kind's courses with their ends cut to the slope, a log the roof falls across fitting whole under the deck or not at all; a roof cube standing on a wall is that wall to its roles, so no cap, quoin or corner is dressed at the steps a gable's cubes climb in. The roof's frame stops inside the roof (`roofs::framed`): battens and a ridge's beam only over the roof's own cubes and never in a wall or a gable, no rafter along a gable's face or out under a verge, so nothing of it ends in the air past a gable or shows its end through one (2026-10-14). Saves holding D209's roof sections are refused with every older format (D353). Amended by D343: a roof cube with anything over it but a roof is still the roof, its covering running on under what stands on it, which is carried down to its deck; the covering tucked into and flashed against what stands beside it.

## Why

Mine, on 2026-10-13, building D226: the developers want to "customize the roof, or add more stories, which means you gotta be able to manually build the roof"; roof cubes on D231's shapes are Enshrouded's roof blocks, any shape and storeys over or beside them, and the convenience D226 keeps ("auto add a roof with [x material]") is a plan of the same cubes. Roof kinds by number keep the 32-bit cube and let support, rooms and walking know a roof without the kits.
