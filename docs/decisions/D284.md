# D284: A block surface's triangles only where its shape bends

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

How D197's surface is meshed, as terrain and voxel renderers mesh theirs: a cube's face has points only where its shape bends, across the rounding of each edge open to the air (three steps near, two a few metres off, one far), and is otherwise one flat square; earth's packing, real relief in its shape, keeps a point every quarter cube near. The stone's relief is no longer displaced vertices but its maps': a normal map and parallax at the texture's own depth (`depth` in each kit's textures: stone 0.025, brick 0.015, wattle's daub 0.02, earth and lime-wash as they were), flat at the low preset (D239). A cube cut to a roof or a shape has points also where its cut crosses its top or foot or bends, all a straight cut needs (earth's, packed, keeps its even steps); one with a lintel or sill bedded within reach has one every 10 cm near, for the wall's lap over the piece. Where faces of different detail meet, each side's line takes the points of every cube round it (`kin`: the cubes drawn into the same surface, in this call or another), so the surface stays closed with no T-junctions; a cell given points by a neighbour is fanned from its middle. Normals are the rounding's own where the surface is no more than rounded. A round log's bark likewise: its relief is its material's parallax at the timber's `relief` (`Kit::depth`), not its points, and its rings come only where its shape bends, every 25 cm near (`timber::SHAPE_RING_M`) and three across each knot, a metre apart a few metres off with no knots, every 25 cm still where a log is cut to a roof's slope. The budget near (`surface::NEAR_TRIANGLES_A_SQUARE_METRE`, `sections::NEAR_TRIANGLES_A_CUBE` and `NEAR_MIB_A_BASE`): 64 triangles a square metre of surface open to the air, 120 a cube with its dress and 4 MiB a building, held by tests on a wall and a stone house in `cargo xtask check` and on the village by `cargo xtask sections`.

## Why

Asked for by the lead on 2026-10-14: "Fix the block surface's density the way shipped voxel and terrain renderers do", "Fine stone relief from the normal and height maps ... not from displaced vertices", "It must look the same or better at 2, 5 and 10 m". A face was ten by ten squares, 200 triangles, at every distance under 25 m; the village's near detail fell from 14.2 to 10.8 million triangles, the block kinds' surface and dress to 109 a cube, a stone house's surface from 800 triangles a square metre (200 a face) to under 64; stone, earth and brick side by side at 2, 5 and 10 m look the same ([research/building-scale.md](../../research/building-scale.md)). Amends how D197 draws its relief, not what it looks like.
