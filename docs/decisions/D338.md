# D338: The mountain lake's open air: the eye opened, the air's sky in the water, trees off the shore

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

On a world whose views reach kilometres (D334), by day out in the open (`OpenOverhead` over 0.55), the camera is set up to 1.5 stops brighter than the hour's key; in a forest, on the lush forest's world and by night, the key. Lakes mirror the air's own sky (`AirPicture`, exposed as the sky is), and the slopes round them in its horizon's light, not the painted sky's two colours; their ripples are half as wide and calmer. A tree's foot stands 0.4 m over the surface of any lake within two of its 10 m cells, and a lake's shore is bare of trees to 1 m over its water, wooded fully from 2.5 m, the band its high water and ice keep scoured, strewn with its granite's stones and blocks thickest at the waterline; the shore walk (`terra_land::site::shore_walk`) keeps 0.5 m over the water on that band. Where the ground's vertices carry the snow (D335), the climate map's snow fades out before its edge. No cost to frame time. The shore's reflection on screen is marched along a calmer ray than the sky's, the waves' slope bent by 0.3 (`REFLECTION_BUMP`, as Unreal's and CryEngine's water distort reflections by a scaled normal), since one ray bent by a facet's full slope swung twice that off a far shore seen at a fraction of a degree and mirrored the far woods a fifth of their height (lake film r13); where the water thins to nothing over the ground, a film can darken that ground (`WET_FILM`), wet ground being darker than dry, off until the shore's submerged strips stop reading as hard bands (lake film r14). Swimmers trail a wake (`terra_world::wakes`): two arms at Kelvin's 19.47 degrees, laid on the water's slopes as fish rises are.

## Why

The adversarial review scored the lake 4/10, its first gap the noon sky reading as dusk. Swept one thing at a time on the shore view at 13:00 on 10 June: the air's scale, its dust and its dust's height changed the sky's colour but not its brightness (dust 3 km deep turned it grey-lilac); 1.5 stops brighter took the sky from 0.17 to 0.53 (the references' 0.73), the frame from 0.24 to 0.41 (0.39) and the far land under the sky from 0 to 0.44 (0.45). The exposures were tuned under Sons of the Forest's canopy. The water's lilac was the painted sky's horizon at a fixed brightness where the sky drawn is the air's; the trees in a metre of water stood in dry cells whose ground lay under the lake's level; the far ridges' white smear was the climate map's edge, which answers for everything past it.
