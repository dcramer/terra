# D3: Tone

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Challenging, with goals to prepare for rather than punishment: far gentler than Vintage Story, and never bleak. Threats are answered by fighting, building and holding ground with recruits (D90).

## Why

Planet Crafter, the first half's old yardstick, belonged to the terraforming pitch and was dropped on 2026-10-12 ("planet crafter is dead yeah"). The "challenging, with goals" wording is the developers' own, of the starter world (D62). The second half, "Factorio-style: threats are answered with automation", was replaced by D90 on 2026-10-11; the developers did not recognise it as theirs.
