# D300: Leaves lit through mostly toward the sun

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D247. A leaf passes the sun on mostly looking toward it (the forward scatter), and a twentieth, not a fifth, from any angle on its far side; the light through it from the sky above (D272) is kept, so crowns out of the sun still glow green from below.

## Why

Review of 2026-10-14: "the top of the frame is a lime crown". Measured (`tools/sandbox/look.ts`, which now gives the lit green share of the top third): four forest views at 13:00 from 13.7% to 9.7% (Sons of the Forest 2%), the films 11% and 9% before; the rest is low crowns in the sun, which is how the forest is laid out. Halving all the light through leaves, or the sky's, did no better and darkened the forest.
