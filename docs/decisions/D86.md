# D86: Across realms

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

One art style across the whole game (D14); cultures differ in their models and details, made by generation and theme. Your gear is your gear in every realm. Settings and cultures per realm, and recruiting from them, are D116 and D161.

## Why

Said plainly by the developers on 2026-10-10, among ideas marked "might", "maybe" and "idk"; those ideas (more than one breathable realm, settings such as fantasy or futuristic, cultures that unlock things) are carried by D116, D161 and [settings.md](../settings.md).
