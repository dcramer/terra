# D419: Winter strips the plants; falling snow grades cool; trails are shared and fade

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

In winter the land is in its winter state. Broadleaf trees and bushes stand bare; only evergreens (the conifers, holly, ivy) keep their needles and leaves. Herbs, flowers and bramble have a leaf year too and die back, so none is green or in flower in January; every place and date asks the season the same way. While snow falls the grade is cool, as data on the named weather (`assets/data/weather.ron`: snowfall's temperature -0.05, tint +0.05, saturation 0.9, eased in as the weather is); every other weather and season keeps the neutral grade (D370), and snow lying under a clear or grey sky with nothing falling is graded neutral too. Footprints and trails are shared, every player sees them, and fresh snowfall fills them over minutes; they wait for a design doc (D49). In a snowy spell snow sticks to the windward side of each stem, in streaks. Every winter look (snow on stems, boughs and ground, its light) is gated by the snow lying or the season, so spring, summer and autumn look as they did.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-19, answering the design questions of [research/2026-10-19-winter-aaa.md](../../research/2026-10-19-winter-aaa.md): on bare broadleaves, "Bare"; on flowers and green herbs in January, "No"; on the winter grade, "i would experiment and compare", then, on side-by-side stills of falling snow neutral and cool (round 7's A/B, cool B over R +31 against the references' +3 to +30), "Cool"; Bevy's temperature alone went cyan, so the tint goes with it; on trails, "Shared, fade"; on dark trunks in a snowy forest, "Snow on the windward side", "and some other techniques, but we cant keep affecting the summer version of it". Every reference with broadleaves shows them bare in winter, and Assassin's Creed Shadows strips bushes and grass; a green crown in January made the frame read as spring with snow.
