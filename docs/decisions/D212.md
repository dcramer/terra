# D212: How the air, the exposure and plants' materials are drawn

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The sky's colour, the sun's colour through the air and the haze over distance come from Bevy's physical atmosphere (Hillaire 2020, as Unreal's sky atmosphere), with Earth's air as Hillaire gives it and a little more dust, centred below the camera and seen with every distance through it 18 times the world's, since the stand-in planet is 2 km round (`terra_world::air`). Our sky dome draws the clouds, lit by the sun through the air, the stars and the moon over it, and the dusk's and night's own colours once the sun is down, as the atmosphere goes dark at sunset. The camera is exposed for sunlit ground, EV 10.8 with a low sun and 11.8 with a high one, opening to 9 at night through the dusk, keyed by the sun's height with the rest of the light (`daylight`); AgX tone mapping; screen-space ambient occlusion over 1.2 m, with D204's contact shadows; a faint bloom (0.03, since an energy-conserving bloom blends that much of a wide blur into every pixel and at 0.1 washed the middle distance flat). The sun's light is given as it is above the air and the atmosphere dims and reddens it. Trees, bushes and rocks are drawn in real materials, a texture stack of bark, leaves and lichen-covered rock (`materials/flora`) named per part in `flora.ron`, projected triplanar as the ground is, tinted by the kind's colour, with the free models' flat-shaded normals smoothed across curves and split at creases.

## Why

The developers: "the graphics/lighting still dont look like our art goals fwiw (closer to realism)" and "you can just fix the core textures if you need to (and the lighting) to make our test scene match up" (2026-10-13). The scene was over-exposed and washed out, ACES turned greens saturated and flat, the sky was a painted wash with distance fog of one colour, and plants were flat colours. A physical atmosphere is how shipped engines draw sky and aerial perspective, and Bevy ships one; a fixed exposure keyed by the time of day is how games with a day cycle hold a look, cheaper and steadier than eye adaptation; AgX is the film-like response Blender moved to from Filmic. Triplanar materials texture models with no UVs without remodelling them.
