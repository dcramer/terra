# D456: A landed blow flashes and kicks the camera, it does not freeze the fight

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A blow that lands holds the fighters for at most 0.05 s (hit-stop), as Enshrouded does (measured: 0 frames on a landed hit, tools/combat/refs/enshrouded.json); the hit is shown instead by a short flash on what was struck (3 to 4 frames) and a small kick of the camera, so chains keep flowing. It is the combat scorecard's bar (D452).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-24, on the review desk, of three options (Enshrouded's way, a short freeze of 0.05-0.08 s, today's 0.10-0.20 s): "Like Enshrouded". Enshrouded is combat's baseline (D180).
