# D343: Walls on roofs, the roof whole round them

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Walls, chimneys and anything else may stand on a roof's cubes, pass up through them and stand beside them, and the roof stays modular round them with no gap, in every arrangement: built as [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md#where-roofs-and-walls-meet-d343) designs it (R1 to R7), as real roofs are, with the cases of [building-voxels.md](../building-voxels.md)'s matrix. The roof is drawn the same whatever stands on it: its covering, boards and frame run on under a wall standing on it, the frame carrying it, its creases' caps stopping at it (R1). What stands on a roof cube is carried down into it to the roof's deck in its own kind: a block kind's surface cut at the deck (`Piece::Gable`, carried down), a timber kind's foot a curb of its boards cut to the deck (`Piece::Curb`), in with the cube it carries (R2). The covering is tucked 15 cm into whatever stands beside it at its height, under its own boards, so a log wall or a post thinner than its cube leaves no slot (R3). Every line where the covering meets something standing is flashed by a part of the roof pattern's shared pieces in data (`patterns/roof.ron`): `Flashing` where the roof rises to it (an apron), `Step` up a sloping line, `Gutter` where it falls to it (a back gutter); butted in an inside corner, narrowed where the roof folds away under it (R4). The boards lie a few millimetres under the deck (R5). The hull draws a cut roof cube something stands on whole (R6). Nothing is refused for meeting a roof; what holds is D199's and D193's (R7). A hole in a roof, a cube or two across, is flashed round as something standing beside it, with no eaves run into it; an opening up to a roof's cube takes a head board cut to the deck; a roof's cube is cut only to the shapes a roof has (whole, half, slopes, corners), else refused, "A roof is cut only to slopes, corners and halves." Proved by `tests/rules.rs` (`a_wall_stands_on_a_roof_and_is_held_by_what_holds_the_roof`, `a_storey_goes_up_over_a_roof_left_on`, `a_roof_is_cut_only_to_the_shapes_a_roof_has`), `layout::roofs`' tests, and the *roofs and walls* family of the mesh checker (`roof_walls`, every case in logs, hewn and stone under shakes and slate at every detail, ratcheted at zero), each a row of the kit sheet. Amends D226 and D241. First decided (2026-10-15) as a refusal, "nothing but a roof on a roof": a wall on a roof's cube refused, "Take the roof off to build above it", which the developers then replaced with this.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15, watching the build film: "youve also got some weird stuff in here where you try to add the second story with the roof still built and theres some issues (you wouldnt really wanna do that and we might wanna just not allow you to build non-roof voxels on the roof voxels?)"; then "its possible roof tiles could go under it but they need to be modular then so theres no gaps/etc", and "nah we should implement it correct, but the issue is we had gaps, because we didnt think this through".
