# D204: How sun shadows are kept steady and let through

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The sun's shadow direction moves in steps of a twentieth of a degree rather than every frame, since a light turning by a sliver each frame redraws every shadow edge differently and they crawl; Bevy's cascades already keep their size and snap to whole texels, so the camera moving does not. Contact shadows (Bevy's, marched 0.4 m through the depth buffer) on the camera and the sun, where feet, roots and blades meet the ground. A crown's solid shadow lumps are 0.6 of its size, so a low sun looking through many crowns still lands in shafts and patches.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "the shades are working weird"; at 17:00 the lumps put the whole lush forest floor in shade. Contact shadows cost nothing measurable on the RTX 3080 (walk 5.8 ms).
