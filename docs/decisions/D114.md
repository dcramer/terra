# D114: What the game sounds like, for now

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Sound carries information; music is sparse and follows the place: phrases with long silences, warmer near a settlement, low and uneasy near danger, made in code. Ambience follows where you stand: wind, birds where it is safe, water near the shore, fires, the hum where magic pools, a settlement's bell. Clips are CC0 packs as a start.

## Why

From [player.md](../player.md) ("sound carries information... music sparse, and from the world where it can be"), built so it can be heard. Questions for the developers in [audio.md](../audio.md#questions-for-the-developers).
