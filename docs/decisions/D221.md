# D221: Trees and grass that do not pop while moving

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A crown's levels of detail are one set of cards: the simpler levels keep six, then two, of each tip's cards in the same places, enlarged to cover as much, as SpeedTree's do, so a crossfade shows one crown thinning rather than two different crowns; full detail to 45 m for a 20 m tree, then 100, 200 and 420 m. Crown shadows come from the crown's own first two cards to 30 m and solid lumps to 100 m, on their own ranges (card shadows farther cost 4 ms a frame). Grass keeps 60% of its blades in its second ring and 35% in its third, with rings ending at 22 and 42 m, reaching 64 m and fading from 42 m. Bark is darkened to about sRGB 78. Measured by rendering one view with every switch distance of a kind moved 25% (what changes is what popping would show): tree changes went from 13% of the picture to 1.3% in the clearing and 7.4% to 0.8% on the forest floor; and by comparing a moving walk with one at full grass detail, frame by frame, the grass's difference went from 1.4% to 0.4%.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "when you run the shit still comes in and out into existence", "same problems happen for grass", and "the trees are too bright right now, bark wise ... look at witcher 3 or sons of the forest". The walk bench on the RTX 3080 is 10.1 ms, within budget.
