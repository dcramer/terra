# D329: What the player wears

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A player starts in a leather coat with long sleeves, cloth trousers and plain leather boots, with the small pack, and bald; no cap. Trousers are worn in a Legs slot and boots in a Feet slot, two of D64's standard slots; the boots are the townsfolk's (D365), made from the smith's dressed model, lying close on the skin where the ankle creases. Both are made, as all clothing is, from one design of the base person wearing them (`art/gear/dressed-male-outfit`: a dark, matte, plain-fronted leather jacket and plain dark cloth trousers), cut from the base body and baked, leather graded to a dark muted brown and every material kept matte (`skin_piece.py`). The player's body is the base with most of the thin morph (`templates.json`), not a bodybuilder's; its feet are a person's width (6% of the height) and its hands a person's length (10.8%), which every morph keeps.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-15: "Fix the player character. This one is just too goofy, we may need to fix it up, at least it has too large feet if you look at it. The cap is goofy, we can eliminate it. We only need pants and jacket, a bald guy is fine otherwise." Later the same day, on the barefoot player: "Boots, stay bald".
