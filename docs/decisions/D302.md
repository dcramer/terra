# D302: Night graded as moonlight

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The day's warm grade goes with the sun, and by night the grade is cooler and keeps less than half the day's saturation, as an eye sees by moonlight and as games draw it. Amended by D370 (2026-10-15): the grade neutral at every hour, so the night keeps only the loss of colour, its blue the moon's light. Amended 2026-10-18: the dim loses its colour first, as an eye's rods take over in dim light (mesopic vision): from the sun 3° up to 3° under, the grade's shadows go to 0.3 of their saturation while the bright sky and a fire keep theirs, and the whole picture's fades only from 4° to 12° under. Faded all at once from 4° under, a dusk under a pink sky was either noon's green land dimmed or a grey sky (lake film r7: grass 41,53,19 under a peach horizon). Amended 2026-10-18 (lake film r8): through the dusk the grade also lifts every section by 0.02, and the dim keep 0.2 of their saturation: Khronos PBR Neutral's toe takes a dark colour's least channel to 6.25 times its square, so grass faded to 0.2 before it came out at 0.62, lawn green, and the shaded hill at luma 3. Amended 2026-10-18 (lake film r12): Bevy 0.20 crossfades its grade's shadows into its highlights, not its midtones (`bevy_core_pipeline/src/tonemapping.wesl`, still so on its main branch), so a colour at the shadows' edge stepped from 0.2 of its saturation to all of it, a crisp grey disc round the moon in the dusk sky; the library is patched as it loads (`terra_world::grading`) with Blender's crossfade, the shadows' weight what the midtones' leaves. Amended 2026-10-19 (fire sheet, round 6): by night only the dim loses its colour, the night's fade on the grade's shadows and not the whole picture, so a fire keeps its colour on what it lights, as an eye's cones see by firelight while its rods see the moonlit land; faded over the whole picture, the ground a campfire lit kept 7% of its colour and stood grey. Amended 2026-10-19 (fire round 7): by night the grade's midtones start at 0.12, not Bevy's 0.2, so the ground a campfire lights (level 0.08 to 0.2) is graded as midtones and keeps its colour, while the moonlit land (0.02) stays in the faded shadows: from 0.2 the firelit yard kept 9% of its colour (R/B 1.1 at 1.5 m; 3.6 at 0.12), and from 0.05 the moonlit grass went green and the sky blue. Deep night's moon and ambient are 350 and 150 lx (fire rounds 10 and 11, 2026-10-19), half of before, so a night campfire's view is near RDR2's and Sons of the Forest's (median sRGB 34 -> 18; at a quarter, 10, the town's street went black, median 4, and the lake's land to silhouettes).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14 asked to see the forest by night. At 22:30 two forest views measured red 0.047, green 0.043, blue 0.013, a brown dusk: the day's warm grade on the moon's blue light; without it green and murky (0.013, 0.050, 0.021), the leaves' bounce; graded, a cool grey-green (0.047, 0.072, 0.063).
