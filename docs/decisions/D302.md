# D302: Night graded as moonlight

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The day's warm grade goes with the sun, and by night the grade is cooler and keeps less than half the day's saturation, as an eye sees by moonlight and as games draw it. Amended by D370 (2026-10-15): the grade neutral at every hour, so the night keeps only the loss of colour, its blue the moon's light. Amended 2026-10-18: the dim loses its colour first, as an eye's rods take over in dim light (mesopic vision): from the sun 3° up to 3° under, the grade's shadows go to 0.3 of their saturation while the bright sky and a fire keep theirs, and the whole picture's fades only from 4° to 12° under. Faded all at once from 4° under, a dusk under a pink sky was either noon's green land dimmed or a grey sky (lake film r7: grass 41,53,19 under a peach horizon). Amended 2026-10-18 (lake film r8): through the dusk the grade also lifts every section by 0.02, and the dim keep 0.2 of their saturation: Khronos PBR Neutral's toe takes a dark colour's least channel to 6.25 times its square, so grass faded to 0.2 before it came out at 0.62, lawn green, and the shaded hill at luma 3.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14 asked to see the forest by night. At 22:30 two forest views measured red 0.047, green 0.043, blue 0.013, a brown dusk: the day's warm grade on the moon's blue light; without it green and murky (0.013, 0.050, 0.021), the leaves' bounce; graded, a cool grey-green (0.047, 0.072, 0.063).
