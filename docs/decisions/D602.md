# D602: The starting kit's clothes are Quaternius's Ranger outfit

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The v0 starting kit's clothes are Quaternius's Ranger outfit, worn whole: its body and arms pieces as the leather coat, its leggings as the trousers, its boots as the boots (D599). The Peasant trousers are not worn under the coat: measured through the clips, their fuller waistband shows 1–2 cm through the coat's hips in the sprint, the sword stance and jumps, where the Ranger leggings stay clean. The pack, sword and shield are ours, fitted over it.

## Why

Asked on the desk which trousers go under the leather coat, with stills of both mixes, the developers, 2026-11-02: "Ranger leggings".
