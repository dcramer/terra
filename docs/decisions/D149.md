# D149: Seasons

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Worlds have seasons, and they change play, not only the look. One season covers a whole world: no opposite hemispheres. Seasons are fundamentals, not hardcoded concepts: a slow swing in temperature, daylight and precipitation, with weather on top, and bodies, ground, plants and creatures respond to those quantities, so cold is cold and hot is hot on any world, and winter is not snow all the time. They never change how fast anyone moves: the walk keeps one speed on any ground, deep snow and mud included (amended 2026-10-25; snow and mud had slowed a walker, to a third at the most). A year's length is per world and changeable; it starts at 365 days, about 7.6 real days. See [climate.md](../climate.md).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "yes we will have seasons"; "hemispheres having opposite seasons seems complex and not totally useful"; "seasons SHOULD affect play"; "cold is cold, hot is hot, but remember, procedural multiple realms so it's about fundamentals not about hardcoded simplistic concepts"; the year "we should be able to change/configure, and you can pick something that fits the rest of the design", and "winter isn't snow 100% of the time". Sons of the Forest has seasons (D85).

Amended 2026-10-25: asked on the review desk whether winter slowing walking to a third was what they wanted, the developers said "i dont want to fuck with speed of movement, we disable all of that".
