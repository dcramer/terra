# D503: The forest floor's litter is mixed, green in the brown

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D293. Under the canopy the grass stays as D293 grows it, and the litter between it is never brown alone: umber leaves and needles mixed with leaves gone green-brown with decay, small green leaves and moss. The ground's litter layer (`litter_tone` in `ground.wesl`) lays its umber over patches a metre or two across of an olive decay no lighter than it, with flecks of green through them; a share of the litter cards, 15% and up to half where their own decay drifts lie, are tinted green-brown (`DECAYED_SHARE` in `cover/litter.rs`), and ivy's drifts are a little wider; the lush floor's moss grows in cushions among the litter, not only in the dampest hollows. The autumn's fresh fall stays warm brown over all of it (D327), and lit litter stays umber, never orange (the art guide's rule 5).

## Why

The developers, on the desk (q-forest-shade-and-tools), asked whether some grass under the canopy could give way to brown litter, Sons of the Forest's shade being 44% brown pixels against our 4%: "generally speaking yeah you can, i noticed in witcher that theres reasonably lot of grass, BUT the litter is also greenish, not brownish only (like mixtured texture? idk) but it makes for more convincing effect." A real forest floor is so too: leaves a season down go olive with decay and the algae and moss on them, seedlings and moss come up through them. Sons of the Forest's lower third by hue (research/2026-10-14-sotf-forest-look.md) is olive more than either: green 11%, olive 35%, orange 23%.

Measured on the worker (RTX 4090), the looks check's four forest views (floor, clearing, path, grove) at 13:00, the lower third by those hue classes, main d4b9b15b against this: orange 14.7% to 12.9%, olive 29.8% to 31.5%, green 55.1% to 55.3%; the saturated lit blades unchanged (16.9%); at 18:30 the low sun turns the floor orange whatever it is made of (55.3% both). The looks check holds the same rows as main (13 of 22, the same 9 out): lit orange below 10.2% both, shade red over green 0.915 to 0.911, hard shade edges 34.5% to 34.3%. A small step, and a harsh review of the stills sees no difference yet (4.5 of 10 both): in these views most of the brown is the paths' soil, a soft warm orange-brown, and most of the green the grass by them. Gaps between the grass clumps under the crowns were tried with it and not kept: they bared that soil as plugs of grass in dirt (forest round floormix, ~/.cache/terra-review/final/forest-models/HANDOFF.md).
