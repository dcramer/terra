# D603: The start's far view streams in after the loading screen

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The loading screen waits for what is near the start, not for what is kilometres off: the ground's first-view chunks of level 7 and coarser (2 km and wider, from some kilometres out) and the woods' coarse cards from 1.6 to 4.5 km are worked out after it lifts, on a pool of their own of a quarter of the cores, each thread working its task alone (`terra_gen::par::keep_to_this_thread`), so the game's main and render threads keep their cores while it works (`crates/terra_world/src/streaming.rs`), and drawn as each is made. A scripted run is still held until they are in (`loading::hold`, D135), so its shots and films see the same world on any machine; a bench is not, so the play benchmark (D578) times the walk while they stream. Open worlds do the same with their distant cells (Unreal's World Partition and HLODs, Skyrim's distant LOD). [terrain.md](../terrain.md#a2-on-screen).

## Why

The developers hold the mountain lake's load under a minute (D580, 2026-11-02, from 350 s on the worker). Of what was left at 105 s, the first view's levels 7 to 9 were 39% of the ground's meshing (420 of 1080 thread-seconds) and the coarse cards 60% of the woods' laying; neither is near enough to the player to be needed on the first frame. On the worker the ground's first view went from 36.5 s to 26.6 s, the far ground following in 14 s after the screen lifted, with the same picture once in (load-time j3).

A quarter of the cores, not all but two (perf-regress j1, 2026-11-02): with 14 of the bench's 16 cores streaming, lake-play's main thread took 15 to 22 ms a frame while they worked, not 6.5 ms (60% of frames over budget, 800 hitches; town-dusk 325 hitches), and at nice 19 it was no better, as the threads shared the game's caches and hyperthreads, not only its time. The woods' far cards, one task, fanned out over every core through `terra_gen::par::map` and alone held 286 frames over twice the budget. With 4 threads that keep their maps to themselves lake-play's walk is 9.3 ms, p99 11.8 ms, 0.17% over and no hitch, its load unchanged at 73 s; the far ground takes about four times as long to come in (some 110 s on the worker, past the bench's walk), the trade an open world makes for its frame rate.
