# D159: Rank names

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The ranks are the books' own, low to high: iron, bronze, silver, gold, diamond, with normal rank below them for those without magic. The one place our names follow *He Who Fights With Monsters* exactly (D125). A rank is always said with "rank", so the metal alone means the material.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "I want the exact same rank names as from the books", replacing naming.md's proposal of Stream to Ocean.
