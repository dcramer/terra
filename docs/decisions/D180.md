# D180: Combat's baseline

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Enshrouded is the baseline for combat: fast-paced, fun, with good animations and smooth play. An action RPG, where a fight is weapon play, timing and footwork; not mashing a button to perform an ability. Abilities (D66) and combat styles (D124) add to that rather than replacing it.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "our baseline is enshrouded: its pretty fast paced, its fun, its got good animations and smooth gameplay. its not just like 'mash a button to perform an ability' ... so action-rpg esque". Firms up D85's liking for its combat. What Enshrouded does is in [research/enshrouded-combat.md](../../research/enshrouded-combat.md); how ours would follow it is proposed in [combat.md](../combat.md).
