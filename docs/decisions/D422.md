# D422: The review desk, one per developer

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Each developer runs their own review desk: `tools/review-desk/index.html` published as a private claude.ai artifact by their coordinator, its URL kept in the uncommitted `~/.config/terra/desk.json`. The coordinator adds questions and reviews to the page's database and uploads every still and film (under 15 MB, films re-encoded) as the artifact's assets; a subagent hands items over in a `FOR REVIEW` line of its handoff. Nothing is centralised and no server of ours is involved. The desk is personal triage; an answer that settles a design question becomes a decision file (By: Developers, the answer quoted), which is the shared record. The playbook is the `review-desk` skill.

## Why

The developers' rule, 2026-10-19: questions and reviews must never be buried in chat; they review from another machine, so every file is on the desk, never a local path; stale items are removed. A page every developer publishes for themselves needs no hosting, accounts or sharing of ours, and the repository holds only the page, the skill and the decisions.
