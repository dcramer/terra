# D463: Left-handed bodies

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Whether a body's main hand may be the left ([holding.md](../holding.md)). Options:

- (a) Everyone right-handed.
- (b) Chosen at character creation for the player; an NPC's from its seed, about one in ten.
- (c) Switchable at any time.

Recommended: (b). It is realistic (D122) and adds variety among people, for one mirror baked at export and the move check run on both sides (D459). Switching at any time adds nothing a player needs.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-25: "sometimes one in a left arm sometimes one in the right arm (it varies right)". No source was found for how a shipped action game handles a left-handed player body (research/2026-10-25-loadouts-and-animation-layers.md).
