# D257: Auditing building, and the holes it closed

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`cargo xtask rules` audits building end to end ([research/building-audit.md](../../research/building-audit.md)): `terra_build`'s rules tests (`tests/rules.rs`; a rule known to fail is `#[ignore]`d with its reason, so `cargo xtask check` gates what holds and the audit reports the rest), the random operations over 64 seeds, a drawing audit of every sort of village building in every kit with its dress (`examples/drawing.rs`: laid twice the same, coplanar overlaps that would z-fight, what one edit sets waiting and merges, an operation's cost), and the rules checked live (`tools/sandbox/rules.ts`), with `Build: change on screen` logged when a change's sections are all on screen and `sandbox.frames` giving a stretch of frames' spread. What it found and closed: a walker is stopped where an underside overhead comes lower within a column than at its edge, as an inverted slope's does, not only at the column's edge (`Base::walk` sweeps the move); a door is not shut on someone standing in it; something set down (a workbench, the claim stone) is in the way of a cube built through it, as a body is (construction-001, gap 17); and the pickaxe does not dig within a scoop of any column a build stands on or has planned (D193).

## Why

Mine, on 2026-10-13, asked by the developers to "make sure we're still on track for making the voxels work correctly and efficiently, and look good. and all of our physics rules/etc are working"; checks only a picture would show become a tool (D71).
