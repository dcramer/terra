# D229: The village laid out from its seed

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The MVP's settlement (D108) is a street village made by rules in `terra_town::village`, not placed by hand: a street east and west with a green and the well on it, houses along both sides a few metres apart with their doors to the street, a woodshed, a lookout deck on posts with its stair toward the street, and gardens walled in stone behind two houses, each length of wall its own build so it steps with the ground. A building is a plan (rectangle, L, T, U, two storeys with a stair up the outside of a gable end to a landing on a post, shed, deck, low wall) in a scheme of the kits' kinds (logs; stone; hewn timber; logs on a stone plinth a metre high; a stone ground floor under hewn timber; log walls with stone gable ends; cob; wattle and daub; a stone ground floor under wattle and daub, its floor and stair of hewn timber) under a roof (gable, hip, lean-to; an L, a T or a U under gables or hips, never a lean-to, its wings' ridges dying into the main roof), at a width; its windows, pitch, a lean of a few degrees and a step along the street come from the village's seed. That is about three hundred buildings told apart by plan, scheme, roof and width (`village::variants`); the village has twelve houses, a shed, a deck and eight lengths of wall, every plan and scheme at least once. The village is set down, 75 to 115 m from the start and well away from the lair, where its street and every building's door, back and corners stand on the most level dry ground, its street turned to suit. Each is put up by the shortcuts in its own cubes, its door's middle at its origin, stood where it goes and lifted onto its footings; it gives the world its footprint, so trees, bushes and the forest floor keep out of it and a margin round it, grown before or after it, and the street is cleared and trodden bare. It logs where to stand for its views, so `cargo xtask demo` takes the same pictures of it every time. A roof the shortcuts put up is of the kind at the top of the walls under it. Whatever stands on the ground storey's foot, a post, a flight, a low wall, stands on footings like a wall. It is test content: procedural villages are designed later.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "complete the construction tech implementation fully for our demo, with the two core materials (log-based and stone-based), and the varying builds of it", on par with Enshrouded (D211); trees grew up through the village's four flat-roofed log houses. Then, on 2026-10-13: "village layouts will just be some random stuff for now for testing, we'll design procedural villages later".
