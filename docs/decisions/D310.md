# D310: Night lit by a high moon, its sky a glow

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D212 and D302. By night most light is the moon's: 700 lux to the sky's 300 (it was 370 to 650), so it lights the night from one side and casts shadows; it stands 40 degrees up across the sky from the sun rather than as far under the horizon as the sun is, and fades in from 8 to 14 degrees after sunset, not 1 to 6, so the dusk's afterglow is not lit from the east. Twilight's sky 600. Both hazes, which Bevy mixes in at the brightness they are given after the exposure, dim to 0.15 as night falls (`Nightfall`), toward the moon's blue; the eye opens 0.4 stops by night, not 1.25; the night grade cooler (0.12) and a touch toward magenta (0.04); a low sun's grade a touch warm, not maroon. All free on the GPU: the moon is the sun's light and shadows. Amended 2026-10-25 (forest night round): by night under a closed canopy the eye opens 3.5 stops, and the haze drawn after the exposure with it, as a dark-adapted eye and Unreal's auto-exposure minimum do; open land keeps the key. Under the crowns the forest walk's night measured mean sRGB 0.000 (path) and 0.003 (crowns) against Sons of the Forest's 0.075; after, 0.053 and 0.079 ([research](../../research/2026-10-25-forest-night.md)). The opening is outdoors only: it is scaled by how much of the sky reaches the air round the eye (`IndoorSky`, D485, D486), so a closed room, whose roof hides the sky as a canopy does, keeps the key and is lit by its own fires; a room open to the sky opens by its share, easing through the door. The crypt's corridor (`node tools/sandbox/indoorlight.ts`) at midnight measured mean 0.235 with the canopy's opening and 0.170 without (noon 0.096); the forest walk's night is unchanged, 0.053 and 0.079.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "shadows seems lightly wrong in it, i feel day is kinda fine, night feels like evening at most". An adversarial review against Sons of the Forest's night frames and moonlit photographs scored night 3, twilight 3.5 and dusk 5.5: night was lit by a sky glow with no direction, its haze as bright as the day's, the eye opening the darkness back, so it read as evening; twilight was darker than night. Three views, before and after: night median 0.059 to 0.019 (SotF 0.025), highlight over median 2.0 to 3.6 (moon shadows), blue over green 0.83 to 1.14 (1.36); twilight 0.051 to 0.052, now brighter than night; the shade at dusk blue over green 0.34 to 0.39.
