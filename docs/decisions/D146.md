# D146: What a construction's pieces are

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Each kind of construction is a kit of pieces sized to the grid (cut stone, half blocks, corners, logs, posts), models from the art pipeline (D177) with a few versions each, laid by the kind's rule on the grid (D48, D181). The pieces are parts of a blueprint picked in the build menu, not things made first and then placed. Walls and fences run in the grid's four directions for now. Carving what is built comes later: by the grid it needs nothing new, finer than it a voxel kind of its own.

## Why

Answered by the developers on 2026-10-12: "i dont want to have to 'build walls then place them', but 'kit pieces' can be blueprints from the [build] menu thats kind of fine"; "we MAY want to make it so you can carve them out… we could always add that later"; "i think we'd just do four directions for now". See [building.md](../building.md#planned-then-constructed).
