# D189: Modular packs and belts

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A pack is a bag and a harness. The bag is any generated model, its own straps cut away, skinned wholly to the back socket's bone (`spine_03`) so it rides the upper spine as one piece, fitted so its corners meet the harness's strap ends. The harness, the straps, is made once per kind on the base body as a garment is (D128), its path and look taken from a model of the base person wearing one, so it bends with the shoulders and fits every body. A pack in the catalogue names its bag and its harness. Things hung from a belt hang from its standard points, by where each hangs (a quiver from its mouth), and swing loose about it as spring bones do. See [characters.md](../characters.md#worn-things-that-do-not-bend-caps-packs-quivers-belt-pouches).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-12: "gear has to be modular", asking what happens with a different backpack. Straps cut from a generated pack are stiff loops in one pose, fitted to no body; straps made per pack would cost work per item (D127, D182). Games keep a few harnesses and many bags, attached at points. Straps coded as routes and painted one colour looked wrong beside realistic textures (D186). The swing is the first part of the spring bones planned for coat tails and hair.
