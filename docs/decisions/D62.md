# D62: The starter world

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Breathable air and the like: the easiest world, where a player begins. Challenging, not a freebie: its winters pose real problems, with goals to prepare for, gentler than Vintage Story's (D3).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10 as what it is "for now"; on 2026-10-12, plainly: "starter world should be challenging, not a freebie, so winter would pose challenges already. Vintage Story makes it a little brutal, but like we want you to have goals". See [climate.md](../climate.md#winter-on-the-starter-world).
