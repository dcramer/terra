# D331: The land's rules in one engine-free crate, and a place's latitude its own

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`terra_land`, free of Bevy (D28): the world's ground as generated (`ground`, which `terra_world::Planet` now builds from) and its climate taken together, which neither knows of the other. The world's **frame** (`frame::Frame`) is the axis the sky turns about, turned as `SkyPath` always turned it (the morning sun where the art was lit, the planet's own start at 40°); every point's latitude follows from it, and so does how its ground leans toward the equator (`Place::sunward`, D323's facing). The sun stays fixed in the world's sky; how high it stands, the light keyed to that and the moon are taken where the camera is (`SkyPath::seen_from`), so a place 5 km north on the 30 km world sees its own lower sun. A slope's sunlight is level ground's that much nearer the equator, warming the air by 0.008 °C a W/m² of difference and melting its snow; a place's growing season, and the tree line where it stops holding trees, are Paulsen and Körner's (a mean of 6.4 °C over at least 94 days above 0.9 °C, `terra_climate::Season`). `cargo xtask fields` draws the field maps (D326) over it, with no GPU.

## Why

The demos need each place's latitude, not the start's, and the climate bent by the ground (docs/demos.md); the field maps must see the same land as the game without the engine, so the ground's construction and the frame moved below Bevy. Measured with the start's climate: the tree line at 1,668 m on level ground at 40°, 1,251 m at 45°, 393 m at 55°, none at 60°; on a 20° slope facing the pole about 60 m lower at 40° and 90 m at 55°; on 20 June the snow line at 2,400 m at 40°.
