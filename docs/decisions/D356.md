# D356: The environment's style written down and checked

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Serves D14 and D311. The art guide's Places section ([docs/art-style.md](../art-style.md#places)) states what each place is aimed at, its reference and the numbers it is held to, and eleven rules every place keeps, as the four places' adversarial reviews found them (shade soft and of the sky's colour, never a hard disc or black; plants communities with height and self-shadow; ground layers blending by height and noise, never along triangles; no debug tint; far things in the near things' light; the horizon never empty; every surface textured; water mirroring its shore), each measured by the check or by review. **The check**, `cargo xtask looks [--place <name>]` (`tools/sandbox/looks.ts`), shoots each place's measured views and prints each number its style holds (`tools/sandbox/style.json`, tolerances about one standard deviation of the reference's frames) against the reference: ours, the target, pass or fail and the view furthest out, failing on any. New measures in `look.ts` for the rules a picture can show: debug tint (saturated smooth squares below the skyline) and lilac; hard shade edges (shading edges, colour kept, changing within four pixels); facets (long straight slanting colour edges in the lower half, at a quarter size); flat colour; each tested on made-up pictures. The four places' references measured again for them, their earlier numbers unchanged. The rules restate what D14, D301, D321 and the chosen references already decide; a new rule of how a place looks is the developers'.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: once the biome reviews are done, "dump down what exactly is the art style we are going for and make sure it works properly", and "whenever we discover something or make a decision that needs to be consistently validated, we document it and build a tool for it". On the reviews' own frames the new measures tell ours from the references': hard shade edges 45% in the forest against Sons of the Forest's 27%, facets 0.07 widths on the tundra against its photographs' 0.02, lilac 1% in the town and tundra against 0.1 to 0.2%, flat colour 15% at the lake against Far Cry 5's 3%.
