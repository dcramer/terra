# D129: How item icons are made

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Icons are image files baked during development and shipped with the art; the game loads them, and an item with none shows its name. `cargo xtask icons` renders every item from its own look in one house style, sized to its cells: the fallback icon and the underdrawing for painting. `tools/icons/paint.ts` paints an item's icon from its render (or, with no look, from a description in `tools/icons/icons.txt`) with the image model, cuts it out and fits it. A manifest records the look and style each came from; a test fails when one is stale; the baker never replaces a painted icon. Variants, when items carry them, are shown on top of the icon (palette shift, frame, badge), not redrawn. See [items.md](../items.md#item-icons) and the [item-icons playbook](../../.agents/skills/item-icons/SKILL.md).

## Why

First built rendering icons in play; the developers doubted it, rightly: most games bake icons, and games with many generated items author one per kind and show variation on top. Rendering from the look stays as the underdrawing, so every icon is composed alike and matches its thing (D93), with no artist (D1). Painting reads far better at slot size. Research in [research/item-icons.md](../../research/item-icons.md).
