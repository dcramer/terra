# D624: Leaves on the line from the third-person lens to the figure open over the figure alone

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D274. Every leaf card the foliage shaders draw (`foliage_fragment.wesl` and `foliage_prepass.wesl`, so the depth and the colour agree; the grown trees' crowns, bushes and young trees alike) dithers out where it stands in front of the figure the main camera follows and within a circle round the figure's chest on screen (`assets/shaders/sight.wesl`): gone within 0.55 m of the line seen at the chest's depth, whole from 0.9 m, as a cone from the lens, so the hole is one size on screen however near the leaf; kept from 0.15 m before the chest, so the leaves round the figure stand. Only in the main camera's own view: the sun's shadows do not move with the camera. Bark keeps D289's fade at the lens only, and a plant in the way still fades whole by D274 where it carries `ObstructionFade`; D274's leaves at the lens (gone within 1.05 m, whole from 1.4 m) stay.

## Why

A grown tree's low crown between the boom's lens and the player hid the player behind a screen of leaf cards (the collision film of 2026-11-05, about 10 s in, core-game-woods j7): grown trees carry no whole-plant fade, as a trunk stops the boom and fading a big tree whole is wrong, and the leaves at the lens fade only within 1.4 m, while the crown's cards in the way stood 1.5 to 4 m off. Unreal third-person games cut these so, per pixel: a SphereMask round the character on screen for pixels nearer than it, through DitherTemporalAA into a masked material's opacity (research/foliage-near-camera.md). D271's tube round the line was dropped by D274 for the dark tunnel it carved through a hazel round the player; a bush round the player now fades whole before the cut matters, and the cut is a cone narrowing to the lens, not a tube, so near the lens it opens little. On that film shot again on the worker (route 30,-30 to 0,-120 at noon, 07-20), main against this at the same frames: from 10.9 to 11.5 s main hid the player wholly behind the bough's cards, and this shows the player whole in every frame in an oval cut, the leaves round it and the tree standing (`~/.cache/terra-review/core-game-woods/j8/`). The shader test (every variant composes and validates) passes; its cost is one dot product, one length and two smoothsteps per leaf fragment in the main camera's passes.
