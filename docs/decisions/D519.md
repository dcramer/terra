# D519: Above the clouds

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Clouds are flown through from the start: from the first volumetric stage they hold up seen from inside and from above as well as from below and from peaks, not only once the game has something that flies. The eye may stand inside a cloud, above a deck or a cumulus field, or beside a cloud's edge, which then shows its close detail rather than a blur. The other options were from below and from peaks until something flies (the recommendation), and from below only. See [sky.md](../sky.md#inside-and-above).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "Flown through from the start". This is not the recommendation, which waited for something that flies because flying through cloud costs most in every engine (close detail, cloud round the world's objects, fog inside cloud); the developers chose to pay for it from the start, so D515's method and budget take the eye inside and above cloud, as Horizon Forbidden West (Nubis Evolved and Cubed) and Microsoft Flight Simulator do. The 30 km world's mountains 2.5 km high (D334) already reach above cloud bases.
