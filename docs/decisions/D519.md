# D519: Above the clouds

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: clouds seen from below and from high ground (a peak above a cumulus field, a valley filled with cloud, a ridge walked in fog) but not flown through until the game has something that flies; then their close detail is built, as Horizon Forbidden West did for its flying mounts. Options: from below and from peaks, as recommended; flown through from the start; or from below only. See [sky.md](../sky.md#open-questions-for-the-developers), question 4.

## Why

Flying through cloud is what costs most in every engine (close detail, cloud round the world's objects); the 30 km world's mountains 2.5 km high (D334) already reach above cloud bases. Whether players fly (mounts, magic, gliders) is a design question the sky waits on.
