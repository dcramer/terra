# D372: A mountain meadow's straw

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

On a world with mountains a meadow's grass is less lush the shorter its growing season (`Planet::cover`): from the tree line's season, up to 0.8 of its lushness given to last year's straw standing in the tussocks, none from 10 °C warmer, so a subalpine meadow reads olive and straw, not a lowland's fresh green.

## Why

The review found the lake's shore grass a saturated cartoon lime in the morning sun; real subalpine meadows keep last year's dead leaves among the new into high summer.
