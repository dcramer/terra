# D276: The lush forest's leaves at their own size, its pines each their own, spruce cards drawn needle by needle

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D247, D268 and D269. **Leaves:** a composed card says how long its leaves are as a share of it (`leaf` in `foliage.ron`, measured by `textures.ts cards`), and a plant is grown so that a leaf is its real size near the camera: hazel, oak and the oak branch tip are composed with more, smaller leaves (14%, 14% and 9% of a card, from 20, 19 and 18%), so a hazel's leaf is 8 to 9 cm, a sapling's 10, a young oak's 10 and an old oak's, high in the canopy, 14; the floor's hazel, once a dome of clumps grown to 1.1 to 2.1 times its size, is young stools at their own. **Shrubs:** hazel grows as stools of four forms, so no two read as one dome: a vase leaning out all round, some stems bowed over; a fan leaning to the light; rods a few years from the cut on bare poles; and overstood stems like small trees from one root; each holds its leaves in flat sprays along its shoots, not balls of cards, a quarter of them missing, so the stems show between. Oak scrub is two to four small spreading oaks from one root. **Spruce:** its card is drawn needle by needle (`needles` recipes in `leaves.ts`): a twig and side shoots clothed all round in needles 2 cm long on a young spruce, those behind its twig darker and the young tips lighter, so its edge is a fringe of single needles rather than a picture's cut outline of tooth-like shoots; its side shoots droop from the twig as a comb, dark (0.06, 0.12, 0.075) with sky between their rows, so it reads as a spruce at 40 m too (D318). **Pines:** eight kinds, pointed, flat-topped, forked and broken (`Form`), each with its own girth (1.6 to 2.6% of its height in radius), lean (a sixtieth to a seventh of its height at the top, one in four hard), bow, crown depth, reach, outline and lopsidedness, the height its grey-brown bark turns to orange plates and how orange, and three to seven grey dead branches under its live crown; each placed 0.85 to 1.18 times as stout as the next of its kind; grown pines stand in knots of a few, 2.7 m apart at the least, with open ground between, on a finer field, while spruces keep their room, since their skirts would close a knot into a wall. Kinds added later are added after the rest, so each keeps the seed it was grown from. **Walks:** a scripted route walk stopped by a trunk or a rock heads off to one side and on again, half as long again each try (to eight seconds), three tries to a side before the other, and gives a spot up after twenty seconds for the next, so a layout that changes does not leave the bench walking into a tree, nor a film stood at a boulder for thirty seconds (review of 2026-10-14).

## Why

Measured before: hazel leaves 13 cm on a stool and 12 to 38 cm on the floor's bushes, a young oak's 21 cm and an old oak's 28; pines' trunks 0.53 to 0.6 m in radius over four kinds, every one within 3% of its height of upright, nearest neighbours 4.7 m at the least, cv 0.34. After: girth's spread over its height cv 0.16 from 0.06, leans 2 to 13% of the height, nearest neighbours cv 0.48, 2.7 m at the least. Walk bench 14.6 ms median, 17.5 ms 99th percentile on the RTX 3080, main 16.3 ms in the same contended hour; lodcheck passes (worst 0.13%).
