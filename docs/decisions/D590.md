# D590: Beating the demo's boss imprints a mark on you

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

In v0's demo (D589) the boss leaves no item: beating it imprints a magic mark on your arm. The mark just applies to you, a fact on your character, so it takes no pack space and can't be dropped, stored, traded or lost on a death (D84). Everyone who fought the boss is marked. A marked player opens the gate, which then stays open for everyone in that world, so a friend who missed the fight isn't shut out.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-01, asked what the boss leaves you with: "the artifact might be more like it forces its palcement in your inventory? and you cant drop/etc its a unique or maybe its just a \"mark\" that you get on you? or a \"title\" ?". Then, choosing: "lets do the \"mark\"", and "its like a thing that just applies to you, the lore is its like a magic mark that imprints on your arm or something". It can't be lost, which a beaten boss staying beaten requires (D499), and it fits the lore's "what you wore is bound with you" ([lore.md](../lore.md)). Who is marked and how the gate opens for others were part of the option they chose ([v0-goals.md](../v0-goals.md#the-demo-arc)).
