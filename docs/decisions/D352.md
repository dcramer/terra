# D352: A market's stalls and their goods: CC0 scans by recipe, a trade a stall by the seed

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

For D350's stalls with varied goods: what a stall shows (baskets of apples, onions and pears, crates of roots and rolls, bread on boards, jars and pots, wooden bowls and plates, tubs, axes and tools, bolts and folds of cloth, brass pans and goblets) is made from Poly Haven's CC0 photoscanned models with their own textures, not generated: each scan cut to 50 to 800 triangles and its maps to 1K or 512 (D304), a woven basket that decimation tears apart remeshed as a shell with its scan baked into it, glazes and CC0 fabrics dyed, cloth wound into bolts and folded into pads in Blender, and composed into one prop a good by a recipe in `art/goods.json` (`tools/props/goods.ts`): pieces stood or laid, and fruit and bread heaped into a container by Blender's rigid bodies onto a false floor, only the top layer modelled, as games fill a crate. 29 goods, 530 to 2,400 triangles each, made to the model contract (D303) and in the catalogue's hidden `Goods`. No CC0 scan of meat or cheese was found, so neither is sold yet. Each stall sells one trade (greengrocer, baker, potter, turner, toolmaker, draper, brazier; `assets/data/market.ron`), the trades dealt to a market's stalls in an order its seed draws, so no market sells one twice while it has no more stalls than trades (`terra_town::town::market`): on its counter (its model's `counter` socket) two to four of its trade's goods, as many as fit with room between, spread along it with a little play and turn; and its stock, one or two of its bigger goods, on the ground under its awning either side of its seller, where the layout keeps room for them (`ThingKind::Goods`, laid with the furnishing, D346). Room beside or before the counter jammed the market's crowd (four times the stuck walkers in `cargo xtask life`), so stock stands behind it. The stall itself is remade empty in the current style (Tripo, D304), its counter top a socket.

## Why

Scans of real fruit, bread, pottery and woodware read as real things beside the textured town (D14) for nothing, where a Tripo model a good would cost 50 credits each; composing them by recipe makes many goods from a few dozen scans, and a heap settled by physics looks piled, not placed.
