# D630: Iso characters keep main Terra's triangle budgets, or the iso camera's own

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Main Terra's budgets ([art-style.md](../art-style.md#budgets)) give a character body 2,500 to 5,000 triangles and a creature up to 4,000; the iso experiment's (D628) give one a pixel of the asset's box at the closest iso camera, up to 24,000 for a hero or creature, so Tripo's meshes are kept as made (the knight 9,638). Recommended by the agent: the iso camera's own while it is an experiment of its own; main Terra's, with levels of detail, if iso characters are to be Terra's (a realm or a view of the same world). Open.

## Why

One art style for every realm (D14) holds that a realm changes parts, materials and light, never the rules, and a budget is one of the rules. Main Terra's are for a first-person view a metre or two off with hand-made levels of detail; the iso camera never brings a figure over 0.20 of the frame, so the budget there is set by its pixels and its share of the frame, and a Tripo mesh kept whole skips a retopology (10 credits) or a decimation for each asset. Whether the experiment's art must be usable in main Terra decides which, and that is what the experiment is for, the developers' call.
