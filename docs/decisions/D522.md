# D522: How dramatic a storm's sky is

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A storm's sky is heightened beyond real: darker, bigger and with more lightning than life, its cumulonimbus taller and quicker to build, its base lower and blacker, its strokes more frequent. It is heightened in scale, darkness and the frequency of lightning, never in how it is lit: its light stays physical (D516), so it is darker because its cloud is deeper and denser than a real one, not because a grade or a painted colour says so (D370), and it still reads as a storm a player can reason about (D122). How far beyond real is data on the climate's storm cells and the cumulonimbus kind, set in review; how often storms come and how dark they may be stay weather.md's questions 1 and 4, now asked against this. The other options were as real storms build (the recommendation), and storms as a deck alone. See [sky.md](../sky.md#wind-rain-snow-and-lightning-in-the-sky).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "Heightened beyond real". This is not the recommendation, which drew the drama from timing and seeing a storm come rather than from exaggeration. It sits with D122 (realistic but fun: where realism would only be a chore, fun wins) as fun winning over the record where a storm as real would be too slight and too seldom to feel, and with D516 (photographic) because what is heightened is the storm's size, darkness and lightning, never the physics that lights it.

The developers asked for storms that read as The Witcher 3's and Red Dead Redemption 2's (weather.md, 2026-10-18) and for a sky that looks "real" (2026-10-26).
