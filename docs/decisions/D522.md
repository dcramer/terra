# D522: How dramatic a storm's sky is

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: storms as real storms build: a summer afternoon's cumulus towering into an anvil over an hour, a shelf cloud on the gust front, rain shafts under the cell and lightning inside it, seen coming and passing; the drama from timing and seeing it come, never from exaggerated shapes. How often (weather.md, question 1) and how dark (question 4) stay their own questions. Options: as real storms, as recommended; heightened (lower, darker, faster skies than real) for drama; or storms as a deck alone. See [sky.md](../sky.md#wind-rain-snow-and-lightning-in-the-sky).

## Why

The developers asked for storms that read as The Witcher 3's and Red Dead Redemption 2's (weather.md, 2026-10-18) and for a sky that looks "real" (2026-10-26); real and dramatic pull apart in a storm's sky, so the balance is theirs.
