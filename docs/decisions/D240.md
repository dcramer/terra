# D240: Crossfades TAA resolves on every material, and eyes that open indoors

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Bevy's standard material dithers its crossfades between levels of detail (`VisibilityRange`) in Jimenez's interleaved gradient noise shifted every frame, as D214's foliage does, so buildings, rocks and scans fade under TAA rather than crawl: the two lines of `bevy_pbr/render/pbr_functions.wesl` (MIT or Apache-2.0) that pick its fixed 4×4 threshold are swapped where the library is loaded (`terra_world::dither`), which the main pass, the prepass and the shadows all use; if Bevy's source changes and they are not found, it says so and keeps Bevy's. With the camera in a room closed and covered (D155's probes) the eye may open five stops rather than one and a half, at its own speed, as an eye does stepping through a door. So it may under the ground in dug space (`terra_world::scene::Underground`, a dungeon's stair as well as its rooms), which the place that digs it says: a stair open to the sky is in no room's box, and the mine's eye was held to the outdoor band all the way down it (mine round 12).

## Why

D213's sections faded in Bevy's fixed pattern, which TAA (D214) cannot resolve; indoors stayed dark under the day's exposure. A per-material shader for every kind of thing that fades would be many copies of Bevy's; patching its one function is the least change (D184).
