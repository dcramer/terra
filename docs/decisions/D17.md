# D17: Worlds

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Many worlds, each different from the others, with no fixed limit: realms, as in *He Who Fights With Monsters*, reached probably by portals (D72). Not a promise to ship unlimited, but nothing in the design constrains the number.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11: "the dozen limit is gone since we're not doing the solar system". Was: several planets, probably about a dozen, not thousands (2026-10-10). Every world must therefore come from rules and its seed alone, and cost nothing until someone is in it; see [worlds.md](../worlds.md#what-it-asks-of-the-engine).
