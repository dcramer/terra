# D580: How long a load may take

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

What load time the game holds to, as D25's 13.3 ms holds a frame: from start to the first playable frame, warm (textures cooked, D188), on the developers' RTX 3080 at the high preset. Recommended: 10 s, the play benchmark (D578) failing past it. Until it is set, the benchmark compares each load with the last only. [launcher.md](../launcher.md#the-play-benchmark-d578).

## Why

The developer saw 25 to 30 s loads in play (perf round 1, 2026-10-29), and the traced release run's first frame alone took 10.3 s. How long a player waits is the developers' call.
