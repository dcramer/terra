# D95: Factions and settlements

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The world needs systems like Kenshi's for how it exists: villages with things going on, and factions. Factions have no fixed set or number: more are added over time, and players discover them, learning about each as they meet it. Settlements can be lost for good; systems can repopulate if needed. Players do not capture people or keep prisoners: it sets the wrong tone. Hostile settlements may hold prisoners of their own, whom players can rescue, which is one way to recruit (D90). Whether players can take over a settlement is open, to decide later.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11. Proposed in [gameplay.md](../gameplay.md#factions-and-settlements-after-kenshi): factions as data, settlements with needs, people travelling, a world that remembers, standing with each faction, a gentler tone than Kenshi's.
