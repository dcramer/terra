# D599: Characters are built on Quaternius's bodies and outfits, in our paint

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Terra's people are built on Quaternius's base bodies (Universal Base Characters, the "Regular" man and woman) and modular outfits (Modular Character Outfits: Fantasy), CC0, on the same 65-joint skeleton as the Universal Animation Library our clips are cut from: their shapes, UVs, normal maps and weights are kept, and their colour and roughness are repainted in our own materials to the art style (D14), with grain, wear and the style's checklist. The man and the woman are two bodies, each with its own copy of every worn look, as Valheim and Kenshi do it, since Quaternius's are two meshes; the build morphs (heavy, thin, strong) stay morphs on each (D112). Their files sit in `art/` with their licence notice, and our tools carry over: sockets, skin patches, `build_bodies.sh` and every check. Tripo's body and its conversion (D115) and the stretch built for it (D593) go once the switch lands, by the jobs in [the research note's plan](../../research/2026-11-02-character-base.md#the-switch-in-jobs), in v0's order; until then today's base stays in the game.

- **Their parts stay as made** (the developers, 2026-11-02). Their vertices, UVs, normals, weights, seams, the pieces' split and the bones are never changed: no clipping push, stretch, scaling, retopology, decimation, or hiding of the body beyond what they ship (how an empty slot shows is D600). Their files sit untouched in `art/characters/quaternius/`, held by a hash list. Everything we make is a separate output built from them, and a check proves each piece comes through vertex for vertex and weight for weight. Only two changes are allowed: paint, as new maps on their UVs, and new pieces they lack, such as our pack, fitted to their body. When a new piece clips, the new piece is fixed, never theirs; when one of theirs does not fit, that is reported with the evidence, not patched.
- **No twist bones** (the developers, 2026-11-02): their 65 joints stay as made, not turned to point at their children, and no twist bones are added nor weights split onto them. If a film of a sword swing shows a forearm wringing, it goes back to the developers with that film.
- **The stretch** (D593): it was built for the Tripo body, whose arms were 11 cm short, and goes with it. Quaternius's man is nearer: reach 1.80 m and crotch 0.90 m in band, his fingertips 2.2 cm lower than the 99th percentile's (0.754 m against 0.732). The rule above leaves his arms as made; the earlier recommendation of a 3 cm stretch at import is withdrawn. The woman is judged against a woman's ANSUR II bands, which `proportions.py` does not have yet.
- **Licences.** Universal Base Characters and Modular Character Outfits: Fantasy, Source editions bought 2026-11-02, are CC0 1.0 (each zip's `License_Source.txt`, kept verbatim beside the files). The Bestiary: Dungeon Monsters Kit is under the Quaternius Asset License v1.0, which allows it in the game but forbids passing its files on, so none of it is committed to this public repository.

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-02, asked on the desk "Should Terra's characters take on Quaternius's cleaner painted look, keep our realistic materials on their shapes, or neither?": "Their shapes, our paint". Then, on the switch's first job: "just dont fuck up their parts/etc", and of the licences, "make sure the license is appropriately defined too since we paid for these". Asked on the desk whether to keep their bones and weights exactly, without our twist bones: "Keep theirs exactly".

From [the research and the trial](../../research/2026-11-02-character-base.md): it is the only ready-made set whose bodies, clothes and clips were made together and whose licence lets them sit in our public repository. The mannequin's 33 clips play on both their bodies with every motion check passing and no retarget, because the skeleton is the one the clips were cut from, joint for joint. A body right before anything is built on it ends the ripple in which every fix to ours broke the gear and clips built on it (D593's faults). Their clothes read better at play distance than ours, with modelled buckles, straps and stitching. Their materials are smooth painted colour with no grain or wear, a step more stylised than D14, hence our paint.

For v0 ([v0-goals.md](../v0-goals.md)): the creator of a few sliders that lets two players tell each other apart (D111) gets a man and a woman, two skin tones and Quaternius's hairstyles to start from; the starting kit, the sword and the shield are refitted on the new bodies; the mark shows on the arm (D590); and the player's locomotion and fight sets, which v0 finishes one job a body at a time, are finished once, on the bodies they will ship on, rather than on a body about to be replaced.
