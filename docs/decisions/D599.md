# D599: Characters are built on Quaternius's bodies and outfits, in our paint

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Terra's people are built on Quaternius's base bodies (Universal Base Characters, the "Regular" man and woman) and modular outfits (Modular Character Outfits: Fantasy), CC0, on the same 65-joint skeleton as the Universal Animation Library our clips are cut from: their shapes, UVs, normal maps and weights are kept, and their colour and roughness are repainted in our own materials to the art style (D14), with grain, wear and the style's checklist. The man and the woman are two bodies, each with its own copy of every worn look, as Valheim and Kenshi do it, since Quaternius's are two meshes; the build morphs (heavy, thin, strong) stay morphs on each (D112). Their files sit in `art/` with their licence notice, and our tools carry over: twist bones, sockets, skin patches, `build_bodies.sh` and every check. Tripo's body and its conversion (D115) and the stretch built for it (D593) go once the switch lands, by the jobs in [the research note's plan](../../research/2026-11-02-character-base.md#the-switch-in-jobs), in v0's order; until then today's base stays in the game.

- **The stretch** (D593, Leaning, not settled by the developer): it was built for the Tripo body, whose arms were 11 cm short, and goes with it. Quaternius's man is nearer: reach 1.80 m and crotch 0.90 m in band, his fingertips 2.2 cm lower than the 99th percentile's (0.754 m against 0.732). **Open, for the developer.** Recommended: stretch his arms once, about 3 cm, to bring the fingertips into band, on the body and every outfit by the same plan (`stretch.py` already carries a plan to dressed models), at import and before anything is built on them, never afterwards; the legs left as they are. The woman is judged against a woman's ANSUR II bands, which `proportions.py` does not have yet, before anyone decides whether she needs it.

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-02, asked on the desk "Should Terra's characters take on Quaternius's cleaner painted look, keep our realistic materials on their shapes, or neither?": "Their shapes, our paint".

From [the research and the trial](../../research/2026-11-02-character-base.md): it is the only ready-made set whose bodies, clothes and clips were made together and whose licence lets them sit in our public repository. The mannequin's 33 clips play on both their bodies with every motion check passing and no retarget, because the skeleton is the one the clips were cut from, joint for joint. A body right before anything is built on it ends the ripple in which every fix to ours broke the gear and clips built on it (D593's faults). Their clothes read better at play distance than ours, with modelled buckles, straps and stitching. Their materials are smooth painted colour with no grain or wear, a step more stylised than D14, hence our paint.

For v0 ([v0-goals.md](../v0-goals.md)): the creator of a few sliders that lets two players tell each other apart (D111) gets a man and a woman, two skin tones and Quaternius's hairstyles to start from; the starting kit, the sword and the shield are refitted on the new bodies; the mark shows on the arm (D590); and the player's locomotion and fight sets, which v0 finishes one job a body at a time, are finished once, on the bodies they will ship on, rather than on a body about to be replaced.
