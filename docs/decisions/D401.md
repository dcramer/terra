# D401: A range breaks into ranks of separate summits, its foothills in front

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D395 and D322. A range is no longer one wall round its edge. Its outer kilometres, the pedestal its floor stood on included, are lowered toward its edge (`FOOTHILLS`, 0.6 of their height at the range's edge, whole by its heart, over a band of the range noise about two and a half times as wide as `inside`'s). They rise in ranks from forested foothills to the high peaks behind. Over the whole range, the ground between separate tops is lowered (`Mountains::tops` 0.75, `tops_m` 1800 m). A ridged sum of three octaves (Musgrave's, as the ridges' own) shapes the tops as crests with branching spurs, falling to saddles along them: up to the whole `tops` share over the outer kilometres, a third of it in the heart. Separate summits of different heights stand with saddles between, and the snow lies only on the highest. The rule only lowers, so nothing rises past `highest_m`. Valley floors within a range keep their height. The pinned places (D393) keep their ground within 700 m to 1.8 km. A pinned place that stands in a range also keeps the mountains round it, within 3.5 to 6 km (`Range::keeping_ranges_round`): a lake's peaks are part of the place, and a valley town's far range is not. All four pins resolve to the same places, the lake at the same level, area, depth and shore walk.

## Why

The developers, after seeing the films: "I think we should do something to make them visually stunning, it doesn't have to be a lot mind you, it can just be less regular, maybe smaller mountains, but more of them, idk, we can try something"; "the mountains need to be much better"; of the settlement, "it has the same problem as the castle meaning environment. we should make mountains smaller and more irregular". The wall behind the castle's valley was the range's pedestal, its floor rising 950 m over the band where the range noise crosses its edge, about 2 km wide, with the ridges' relief on top: one even rampart round every range. Cutting cols into its crest (D395) could not break that. Seen from the town, the wall stood up to 35 degrees and hid everything behind it; now the skyline stands up to 25 degrees, the highest ground near it 2,280 m where it was 3,002 m. With the front lowered and broken into tops, three or four ranks show over the castle, of separate peaks with saddles between them. On seeing them: "i like the mountains better. the face of the mountain needs a bit of variety too".

The cost: the cuts leave more closed hollows on the mountains, which fill as tarns. The lake query looks at 2,222 lakes over the world, where it looked at 1,385. The best lake the demo's query finds over the whole world without its pin is now another one. The tests that stand by the demo's lake take it where `places.ts` pins it (`MountainLake::demo_pinned`). GPU memory: in a `--bench` run at the settlement, meshes on the GPU are 1,600 MiB before and after.
