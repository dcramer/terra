# D330: A market town's layout

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`terra_town::town` lays a market town from its seed and the ground under it, as Emilien et al. lay villages (2012): a square on the flattest ground (long, a triangle where three roads meet, or a widened street), its long side along the contour; streets traced out of it a step at a time, each turned the way the ground is most level, a side street climbing or falling far enough for a row of plots first; plots cut along every street's sides and the square's edges, each building one of the village rules' three hundred in the seed's order, its door to the way and its front set back by its own wings; a yard behind each house only where a lane along its side reaches it, holding a goat pen of low walls, hens, or a woodshed and a chopping block; the things a day is done at (well, trough, stalls, forge, blocks, benches, places to gather, gates and guard posts) placed by the same rules; and the path graph people walk built from all of it, every edge clear of anything built by 0.45 m. `cargo xtask town` draws the plan. Packed as a market town is (amended 2026-10-15): the square's edges built up first, a smaller house taking a slot by a street's mouth or a corner where the next will not fit; houses along every way joined wall to wall, a 1.8 m passage left every third or so for the lane to the yards; streets 3.8 m wide (side streets 3.3) with the fronts 0.3 m off them, so 4 to 5 m front to front; the square smaller (a long one 28 to 34 m by 18 to 21, a triangle 42 to 46 across, a widened street 44 to 50 by 12 to 14), its houses of two storeys where the queue has them and the streets' first 45 m out of it too; the market's stalls in rows 3.2 m apart; every door's way in kept clear of its neighbours, and a door that reaches no street node joined straight across to its street. `cargo xtask town` also prints how enclosed the streets are (`town::enclosure`): front to front, the share of their built-up sides fronted, the gaps, and the share of the square's edge fronted.

## Why

The town demo (D321) needs a town made by rules from the building system as it stands (D108, D236), and life needs a path graph the layout already knows (D324). Three square shapes by two or three streets by the ground make a few dozen towns a player can tell apart (D182); a yard no one can walk to is no yard. See [demos.md](../demos.md#3-a-living-town). The developers, 2026-10-15: "town looks too large still, like it needs to feel clumped crowded, or maybe narrower streets and all that"; measured on seed 2, the streets went from 7.2 m front to front to 4.9, the square's edge fronted from 30% to 40% (64% on a triangle).
