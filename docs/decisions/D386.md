# D386: The material bench

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`cargo xtask material <material>…|--all` (tools/kits/material.ts) is the way a material change is judged: the kit sheet lays the material's preview alone and two scripted runs on the real GPU shoot fixed vantages of it, at noon its wall (a roof's covering) square on from 1, 2 and 5 m, a corner, an opening, its roof and the whole from 12 m, the same wall in the shade, and in the evening its sunlit face again, each measured face beside an 18% grey card in the same light and in its 2 m shot's frame (`sandbox.card`), so one exposure lights both; and the shaded wall again in the **light** view (D385), its red over blue held to the card's (0.75 to 1.2, every material) and its brightness against the card's to its pattern's `sky` target. It measures what agents measured by hand: texel density (its surface's colour texels a metre at the high preset, from its data, `examples/look.rs`), sharpness (the finest octave's share of contrast in the middle of the 1, 2 and 5 m shots), grey-card balance (the card's red over blue, held neutral at noon, D370, and the surface's brightness against the card lit and in shade), value range (its 5th to 95th percentile at 2 m) and stretch (`cargo xtask texels`, each texture set). Each against its pattern's `look` targets (`terra_build::look::Targets`, in the pattern's file, set from the baseline's numbers on the day it was made, so a regression fails); every unmeasured number fails. Output: one JPG under 2 MB a material, its 1 and 2 m shots as 1:1 crops, beside its pack's reference photograph (`reference` in the pack: path, direct source, licence; only CC0 committed, `art/references/`, others kept local as `*.local.jpg`), and a table, in `shots/material/`; `--all` is the baseline (D333).

## Why

The developers' looks took three to six rounds each this week, judged by eye on ad hoc stills, the causes (1K arrays, BC1, TAA blur, a pink grade, inverted heights) found only when an agent measured ([building workflow retro](../../research/building-workflow-retro-2026-10-16.md), item 2). Shipped studios hold materials to a texel density, a value range and a grey card under fixed lighting (a "material lookdev" scene); measuring them in data turns a review into a check.
