# D526: Paid solutions

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A paid tool, library, asset, service or engine source is always fair to look for and propose when it is significantly better than the free ones, solves our problem better, or solves one that would be very hard for us to solve. Before we depend on one, the agent explains it to the developers on the review desk (D422): what it is, what it costs, why we need it, which free options fall short and how, and whether its licence fits a public MIT repository (D38) and a shipped game. The developers decide and buy; nothing is built on it until they have.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26: "its always ok to look for a paid solution if its significantly better than the unpaid offerings, would solve our problems better, or otherwise solves a problem that will be very hard for us to solve. when we do though you will need to explain to the user why we need it and what it is". D1 already allows bought assets, and we pay for Tripo's Studio and Quaternius's animation libraries (D115); this makes the search for paid options routine rather than an exception, and the explanation the price of proposing one.
