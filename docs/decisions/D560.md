# D560: A lit torch is carried on a raised arm, flame up

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A lit torch is carried with its arm raised forward, the flame up and ahead of the body, as in Skyrim and Valheim, not tipped down by the carry rule (D546). It is a carry pose of the torch's own: a one-arm hold laid on the arm that holds it whenever it is carried, the other arm left to the clip.

## Why

Asked on the review desk (2026-10-28) how a lit torch should be carried, after the carry rule turned it head-down and an upright torch on a relaxed arm went 9.5 cm into the neck in a sprint; the developer answered: "Raised arm, flame up". A flame held up lights the way ahead and reads at once as a torch; a flame pointed at the ground does neither.
