# D354: The town's place found on the land, its fields cleared of the forest

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Serves D350's horizon. The market town is found by a query on the land's rules (`terra_land::town`, `--site town` for the sandbox and `cargo xtask fields`), not placed by hand: over a grid at 50 m within 9 km of the planet's start, level ground (under 5° at its middle and 12 m of rise and fall within 120 m, on soil, no lake) whose growing season is at least 3 °C warmer than the tree line's 6.4, for its fields; closed forest within 700 m past its fields on at least 55% of 32 ways out; the highest ground within 7 km at least 900 m over it and 200 m over the tree line, the skyline from the square at least 5° high. Best: level, wooded round, the range high over it and round much of it, near the start. The world's start stands 330 m beside it, on the most level ground away from the range, for the base, the village and the herd. Below the tree line the rules stand closed forest on all level ground, so a town has cleared its own (`Clearing`): fields 300 m round it with an edge that wanders 22% in and out over 160 m and thins over 70 m, and 170 m round the start; the land round the place (`Round::stand`) keeps no trees there, so the grown trees, the far forest's billboards and the ground's canopy agree. The woods are grown round the town, not the start, and its ground cover is laid over the world's within its reach.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "in the distance it should either still be a town, or have some sort of fauna on the horizon / mountains in the horizon. We want to make this visually appealing." 300 m from the small planet's start the town looked out on empty grassland to a bare horizon. On the 30 km world the query finds a valley floor 2.2 km east and 2.1 km north of the start at 25 m (growing season 11.4 °C), the range rising to 2,779 m 3.9 km north, the skyline to 36°.
