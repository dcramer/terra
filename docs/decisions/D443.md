# D443: A creature's paces come from its size

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A kind with no measured speeds takes its walk, trot and run speeds from its hip height h by dynamic similarity: it changes gait at the same Froude number v²/(g·h) as real animals, walk to trot near 0.5 and trot to gallop near 2 to 3, the bands calibrated on the kinds D424 paces from field speeds before any kind relies on them, its plan capping the number a size may reach (a big body never gallops) and a kind free to set its own; D424's gait maker then makes the clips at those speeds. Many-legged plans step a foot past its reach in alternating groups; serpents and swimmers move a wave whose speed sets their pace ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#4-gaits-from-body-size-d443)).

## Why

Alexander and Jayes (1983) found running mammals move alike at the same Froude number, and horses of every size change gait at the same one (Bullimore and Burn 2006); which D424's stride rule already rests on; a generated monster has no field data, and its size is all we know.
