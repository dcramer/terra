# D394: Far mountains drawn from their own cells, not the planet's centre

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D322. The ground shader's far ribs are laid cell by cell, as the ground's gullies are (`fall_stripes`, a port of `Range::stripes`), in three sets 150, 62 and 26 m apart, faded by the pixel's footprint, with snow lying in the widest. Their phase used to be measured across the fall from the planet's centre, 30 km away, so a degree's turn of the normal moved it 520 m and every far triangle drew its own chevrons. The ridges' warp shifts its noises by whole cells apart from the point (`Noise::sample_shifted`), because adding 101, 203 and 307 cells to the point held a single only to 0.6 m and stepped the ground by up to 0.15 m. The couloirs' depth varies by cell (`COULOIR_LEAST`) and over a 2.2 km noise across each face, so they no longer form an even comb. A theme can also run its ranges' fronts out in spurs and raise their crests to peaks and cols (`Mountains::spurs`, `peaks`). The starting realm leaves both at 0, because turned on they move the town, settlement and lake off their pins (D393). No demo site moves; no cost to frame time.

## Why

The developers, watching the castle-and-dragon film: "the mountain is tooo regular in the back, it needs to be fixed up". The wall north of the castle's valley was drawn in a quilt of chevrons that followed the far mesh's triangles, its crest a sawtooth, and its snow a straight stripe. Stills before and after ([research](../../research/2026-10-16-far-mountains.md)): the quilt, sawtooth and stripe are gone. An adversarial review against Tatra and Alps photographs scored the wall 3/10 before and 3.5/10 after. It is still regular: the snow cap is an egg-crate of even blobs, and the wall is one smooth curtain whose shape this does not change.
