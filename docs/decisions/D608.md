# D608: Work after the load runs in priority classes under a per-frame budget

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

What streams after the loading screen lifts (D603's far ground and far cards, and what comes after them) goes through one scheduler in `terra_world::streaming`: tasks in priority classes (near before far, ground before cards before what is only for looks), fed to the pool a few at a time nearest the camera first, their results entering the world under a budget a frame of main-thread milliseconds and upload MiB, larger while the loading screen shows; it logs when each class has drained (`Far view in N s`) and each class's thread-seconds by level, held to D607's budget. Unreal caps loading's game-thread time a frame (`s.AsyncLoadingTimeLimit`, a unified streaming budget larger behind a loading screen), Unity its main-thread integration (`backgroundLoadingPriority`, 2 to 50 ms). [research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md](../../research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md#4-proposals-ranked).

## Why

On 2026-11-02 D603's streaming ran 110 to 260 s beside play with nothing holding it to a share: the far cards' one task waited behind 1473 chunk tasks on the FIFO pool (259 s), town-dusk's game is likely ~2 ms higher while it runs (perf-regress j2, unconfirmed), and a far chunk costs about 0.29 thread-seconds, five times a near one, which no line showed. Both halves exist as unlanded patches (perf-regress j2's nearest-first feed, load-time j5's 8 MiB a frame). Leaning until built and benched on lake-play and town-dusk; it would amend D603.
