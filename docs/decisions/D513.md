# D513: A campfire's worn ground is its model's ash bed, out to 0.8 m, not a terrain decal

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The ground a campfire has worn round its ring (spilt ash, soot and trampled bare earth, broken into the grass in patches) is part of its model: the bed's mesh reaches about 0.8 m from the middle, past the stones, its colour map's alpha cutting it raggedly into the ground (glTF MASK, as a mesh decal's edge is). The catalogue's dims follow the model (`assets/data/builds.ron`, the contract's 2%), so the campfire's footprint is about 1.6 m across. A wider patch, the two or three ring widths a harsh judge asked for against RDR2, waits for a ground decal or a ground mesh-decal outside the catalogue's bounds.

## Why

The harsh reviews of art rounds g39 and 2 found the pit "a flat brown disc with a round edge" from 4 m: the bed stopped just past the stones (0.58 m). Art round 2 looked for a terrain decal and found no road in: Bevy 0.20's `ForwardDecal` needs a `DepthPrepass` no camera has, and clustered decals reach `StandardMaterial` only, not our terrain shader. A wider model is what we can build now, but the dims are the box a walker cannot enter (`Prop.half`, terra_survival props.rs), so a 3 m bed would wall off a 3 m square round every campfire; 1.6 m keeps it about what the stones and the logs' butts already take. Revisit when the terrain takes decals, or if the footprint gets in the way of play.

D558 (the developers, 2026-10-28) makes the worn ground the camp's: a new fire sits on grass, an old camp's ring is worn a metre or two round it. A bed fixed in the model wears every campfire alike, so building D558 revisits this: the ring belongs to the ground round the camp (a decal, or the terrain's own cover worn), the model keeping at most its ash.
