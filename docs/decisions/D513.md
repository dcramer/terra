# D513: A swimmer's figure treads, strokes or dives by its swim and lies along its way

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

How swimming (D74, D478) is shown, presentation only (docs/controller.md "What is presentation"): the figure shows one of three looped clips by the mover's swim, treading water (`Swim_Tread_Loop`) going slower than 0.4 m/s, a surface stroke (`Swim_Fwd_Loop`) faster than 0.6 m/s, a dive's stroke (`Swim_Dive_Loop`) with its mouth under water, blended 400 ms into and out of any of them. The figure is turned from upright toward where it faces by the way it goes: none treading, along its way stroking (level at the surface but for 10 deg of head and shoulders out), pitched along a dive up to 170 deg, eased 95% in half a second about the float height (`terra_walker::swim`). Its feet are let go while it swims. The clips are made standing (Kimodo holds no swimming capture: a crawl as a windmill of the arms, a dive's pull as an overhead sweep, `art/animation/made.json`) and only the tilt lays them down, so the three blend in one frame. The filmed lake (`tools/controller/yard.ts`'s lake run, `swim.ts`) measures it: `swim.pose`, the share of swimming frames showing no swim clip or with the torso more than 15 deg off its way.

## Why

Shipped swimmers are tilted by their pitch rather than authored lying (Unreal's swimming movement mode, BotW's, Subnautica's divers), and a surface swim eases in as a wader leaves its feet (Valheim, Enshrouded). Tilting one upright set keeps the tread and the strokes blendable and the dive's pitch continuous. Taking the way from the body's velocity needs nothing the server does not already send. Better takes (a swimming capture or library) replace the clips without changing this; their look is on the review desk.
