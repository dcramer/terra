# D341: The heath, patterned ground and the climate's wind

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Open ground past the trees is a heath of five communities by exposure and wetness (`terra_land::heath`, docs/demos.md §2): fellfield on scoured rises (Sx under about −6°) and the coldest ground, lichen heath on the dry, dwarf-shrub heath where winter's snow lies over it (the lee, level ground some), tussock sedge from a wetness of about 0.45, moss carpet past 0.75 and at pools' edges; wetness from a hollow under the ground within 30 m, level ground, water within a few metres, permafrost's held water and drainage patches of noise some 60 m across; the heath holds the ground as the season cools past the tree line and the trees thin, `smoothstep(−1.5, 0.6, colder) × (1 − trees)`. It is worked out on the land's 10 m grid round a place, blended over neighbouring cells twice, and given to the ground as a two-layer picture (`planet::LandMap`) it draws as its scanned layers retinted, the dwarf shrubs coloured by their own year (`dwarf_birch`) and the sedge by grass's; the cover's blades are its sedge, and the planet's grass and flora give way to it. Where the land says the year's mean is under −2 °C the ground is patterned (`terra_voxel::Patterned`, laid by `Land::new` from the climate's temperatures): ice-wedge polygons a slice of a jittered 3D Voronoi 22 m apart, troughs 0.4 m deep and 2 m wide, rims 0.15 m, middles 0.2 m low and a fifth 0.8 m, as pools found on a 2 m grid over 1.6 km round the place (`Round::pools`) and drawn as one surface. The one wind (D255) is the climate's at the camera (`terra_world::wind::WorldWind`), at the ground half the day's from the sun 2° under (the evening calm, amended 2026-10-18: a dusk lake as rippled as at noon broke its mirror of the far shore, lake film r7), every 0.002 days of the clock, its strength the speed over the climate's mean, 0.25 to 2.2, laid over each material's own sway.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "i would like to see more of a tundra effect". Real tundra is a mosaic of these communities by wind and wetness, with ice-wedge polygons about 30 m across under half a metre of relief and pools along them ([research](../../research/2026-10-15-biome-demos.md#tundra)); Far Cry 5 and Horizon place by such fields. A tundra's variants (D182): five communities by four seasons by how exposed and how wet, about 40 patches a player tells apart, broken by pool fields, boulders, snowbeds and spruce islands; a new cold realm is new species in the same communities.
