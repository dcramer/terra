# D99: Wear and repair

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

For now: tools, weapons and armour wear with use; a broken one stops working but is never lost; repair is cheap at a station in a claim, quicker with a recruit who knows the craft.

## Why

Agreed by the developers on 2026-10-11 as recommended, "for now". After Valheim. See [crafting.md](../crafting.md#wear-and-repair).
