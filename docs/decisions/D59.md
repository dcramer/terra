# D59: World variety

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every world is thematic and varied: its own terrain, caverns, water features and foliage. A world is a seed and a theme; a new world is a new theme, not new code.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10. The "theme as data" part is my proposal in [worlds.md](../worlds.md).
