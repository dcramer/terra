# D363: The time of day's look by the place's own sun

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

What goes with the hour, the morning's mist and the grade's warmth, follows the hour as the sun keeps it where the camera is (`SkyPath::local_hour`: the clock's hour plus an hour for every 15° the sky turns about the pole between the start and the camera), not the clock's hour at the start.

## Why

The sun stands at each place's own height (D212), but the mist went by the start's clock: the mountain lake, 15 km west on the 30 km world, keeps its own time 2.3 hours behind the start's, so its dawn had no mist and its morning mist burnt off by its seven o'clock.
