# D170: Handing a plan to the tribe

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

Agreed for now, perhaps to be revisited, after the developers suggested switching from blueprint to build: the hammer has two modes. In Build mode, the default, what you lay is a plan as today, and the tribe carries for it at once, so a single wall needs no extra step. In Plan mode, what you lay is a draft the tribe leaves alone. Holding a key at a draft turns its whole building (D155) into plans; holding it at a plan nothing has been put into turns it back. Plan mode gets copying and other tools for drafts. Drafts show only to those building, with the hammer in hand, and to anyone building within the claim.

## Why

Asked by the developers on 2026-10-12: recruits must not start a blueprint "while you're still designing it", and "i need your help coming up with a plan here that is a good player experience". Staking out each building was proposed first; the developers: "it makes sense for one big construction, but what if you're just adding one wall inside? maybe its like you switch from blueprint to build? need to think it through". After Dragon Quest Builders 2, Manor Lords, RimWorld and The Sims, which separate designing from the go-ahead, and against Palworld, which builds the moment a plan is laid. Then, of the two modes: "i think but we may have to revisit this"; and, as an idea, Plan mode might become a different kind of building mode, with a camera that is not your character's, perhaps from above. Then: "copying and other features are extremely nice especially since all we're doing is placing blueprints"; and "drafts/blueprints/whatever, would only show when you're in build mode, and should be visible to anyone in build mode within the claim i guess", build mode read as having the hammer in hand.
