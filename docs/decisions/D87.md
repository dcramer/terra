# D87: Realms

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Realms, not a set number of planets, in the spirit of *He Who Fights With Monsters*: no space between them (D4), reached probably by portals (D72), and no terraforming (D89).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10, first as "maybe", then as "still different realms or planets or something similar". Unaffected: procedural worlds with their own themes and cultures (D59, D86), voxel ground, survival, building, items, skills. Each realm is still a round world (D4). Confirmed on 2026-10-12: "we are not doing 12 planets, we're doing realms per the game design doc". See [worlds.md](../worlds.md#realms-and-portals).
