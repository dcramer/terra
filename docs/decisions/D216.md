# D216: Light under the canopy, and leaves that give way to the camera

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The sky lights shade 1.4 times as brightly as D212's keys, since open sky gives level ground about a sixth of the sun's light and they gave it a sixteenth, so every shadow was near black; the grading adds no contrast in the shadows. Under a canopy the sky light is dimmed and warmed toward what leaves and ground bounce, as Crysis bakes into its trees' vertices: a trunk's vertices carry how much sky they see (little low on the trunk, most in the crown), and everything under the canopy takes its sky light from the sky occlusion volume (D270). Foliage within 1.6 m of the camera fades out in a moving dither for TAA to blend, so the camera does not push through solid leaves; shadows keep it. The eye adapts up to one and a quarter stops brighter (D286), round a metered brightness 1.1 stops below D214's, with no extra saturation: a slightly darker, more muted scene, as the Witcher 3's.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "lets try those" (warmer light under the canopy, leaf shading, fog and sun shafts, leaves fading at the camera, more kinds of plant), after [research/aaa-forest-look.md](../../research/aaa-forest-look.md). In shade the trunks read as blue-black and the litter as black.
