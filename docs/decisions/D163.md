# D163: The magic system

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Proposed in [magic.md](../magic.md), except the parts marked decided. There are four ways to have magic: essence powers, studied crafts, combat styles, and things that hold magic. Three essences and a confluence give five powers each, woken by awakening stones. A power is drawn by weighted chance from a generated pool, hundreds per essence, built the way shipped games generate loot (a base, affixes in families and tiers, tag weights, budgets, uniques) from a shared library of mechanics (blink, flight, shield, summon and so on). Each essence flavours a mechanic with its own look and side effect, and mechanics combine, so they overlap between characters while powers rarely do. Essences beyond the core list are generated per realm. The weights are shaped by the stone, your other essences, the powers and skills you already have, and where you are; a power's whole ladder is fixed when drawn. Decided on 2026-10-12: there is no separate tally of habits. There is one draw, with no choosing among offers, as the developers said the same day; proposed, a stone on an essence has a short list of outcomes, about five to ten, one drawn and then made individual by the loot machine's rolls. Decided on 2026-10-12: awakening is a ritual that shows what is known of the list (everything for a common stone on a common essence, little or nothing for rare and legendary ones) and what can't happen, never numbers or the result. Combat styles come in five forms, one skill book each. Crafts (ritual magic, wardcraft, astral magic, bindcraft, alchemy, inscription, enchanting) are learned from books by rank, with components used up, diagrams in the symbols of magic, chanted spells and backlash, as in the books. Old-school touches run throughout.

## Why

Asked for by the developers on 2026-10-12: "some nostalgia from old school magic, but we also need to remember our world design/lore", with examples for each system and procedural, random learning from essences shaped by what was learned before. Mechanics after the books (D98, [research/hwfwm-magic.md](../../research/hwfwm-magic.md)); every essence, power, style and working ours (D125).
