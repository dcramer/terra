# D171: Coming back from death

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A soul that dies falls into the astral and is lost, unless bound to a hearth by the rite adventurers learn when they rest at one they keep. The tie draws the soul back and the hearth's fire gives it a body made of magic. What you wore comes back with you; what you carried lies where you fell (D84); a little progress toward your next step goes into the new body (D101). You return to the hearth you last rested at in a claim, or a settlement's common hearth. Ordinary people die for good.

## Why

Left to me by the developers on 2026-10-12: "I don't care, you can come up with something meaningful here". It ties the death costs already decided to the lore, and makes a home where you come back to. See [lore.md](../lore.md#coming-back-from-death).
