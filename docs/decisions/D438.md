# D438: Monsters move by real biomechanics, or by rules of their own

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: monsters move as the wildlife does (D424, D443), by a real animal's gaits scaled to their size, heavy things slow and big things never galloping, with exaggeration only in their attacks' wind-ups and tells; a kind may set its own limits where its lore says it moves as no animal could ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#4-gaits-from-body-size-d443)). Open.

## Why

Realistic but fun (D122) says things work as in the world so players can read them; a monster's gait is how a player reads its weight and threat. Whether some monsters break it on purpose is the developers' call.
