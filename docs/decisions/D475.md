# D475: The air's haze over what is drawn starts fifty metres out

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D299. Bevy's aerial perspective (the sky pass's haze and dimming over what is drawn) starts `START_M` = 50 world metres from the eye, as Unreal's sky atmosphere does with its Aerial Perspective Start Depth: nearer, a thing keeps its own colour; past it, the haze is that of the distance beyond the start, at the air's lens scale (D212, D299). `terra_world::air_start` patches Bevy's `render_sky.wesl` where it is loaded, as `haze_start` patches the volume's (D184); if Bevy's lines change, the patch is not made and says so. The volumetric haze between trunks is untouched.

## Why

Forest models round 2 measured the lush forest's crowns reading grey-teal against Sons of the Forest's olive: the leaf pixels' median hue 93 lit and 119 in shade against SotF's 64 and 62. No foliage lighting path moved it (round 1: tint, sky light, fog, transmission and reflection each within 6 degrees). Turning the aerial perspective off took it to 70 and 72; leaves drawn pure red took back 40 to 60% of their green and 70 to 79% of their blue from it. Under the trees our air's distances are nine times the world's (D299), so twenty to fifty metres of air laid a few hundred metres of sunlit haze over the dark crowns the exposure opens for. Start 25, 50 and 120 m gave lit hue 81, 75 and 73: 50 keeps the hills' haze, which begins well past it, and takes most of the gain.

Measured on the worker (RTX 4090, `cargo xtask looks --place lush`, main 17732dd2 against the start): crown hue 72.8 (SotF 63.4, held within 10); shade red over green 0.559 to 0.844 (1.261); hard shade edges 40.2% to 34.4%; trunks across the middle 16.5% to 22.8%; the shade's blue over green 0.462 to 0.228 (0.377), so that row of the looks check now fails: it passed only because the same wrong haze turned a green shade blue. The row is kept as it is, not loosened; its fix is the shade's own make-up, a design question waiting on the developers. Blind panel (D472, 8 judges a build): main 6.11, start 6.10, no difference shown (can show 0.18). Forest bench (RTX 4090) 10.35 ms median against main 10.58; lodcheck frames pass (0 over 0.5%).
