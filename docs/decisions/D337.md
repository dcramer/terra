# D337: The tundra found on the land, and the snow the ground catches

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D323. The tundra is found by a query on the land's rules (`terra_land::tundra`, `--site tundra` for the sandbox and `cargo xtask fields`), not placed: over a 60 km grid at 100 m round the planet's start, level ground (under 7° and 40 m of rise and fall within 300 m) whose growing season is 0.8 to 3 °C colder than the tree line's 6.4, whose year's mean is under −2 °C (permafrost), a closed forest 0.5 to 3 km off, best with the forest's edge 1.5 km away, small lakes round it and near the start. A place's catch of drifting snow (`Place::catch`) is a share of open level ground's, from Winstral's Sx over 100 m against the climate's prevailing wind (`terra_land::drift`): 1 + 1.2 tanh(Sx/8°) in a lee, to 2.2, and 1 + 0.75 tanh(Sx/8°) exposed, to 0.25; the fold adds what falls times it. The land round a place (`Round`) works it out on its lakes' 10 m grid; its snow table is folded at catches of 0.25, 1 and 2.2 and blended between, and the climate maps and samples take the same catch.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "i would like to see more of a tundra effect", in the starting world's own north where its climate puts it (demos.md, question 3, its recommendation). On the 30 km world the query finds a fell plateau 10.5 km north and 15.5 km west of the start at 966 m and 50.6° (growing season 5.6 °C, year −6.1 °C), the forest's edge 1.9 km south, as Hardangervidda is a tundra plateau at 1,100 to 1,200 m; the lowlands north of it are mountain or polar desert by these rules. Lee slopes hold about twice the snow of windward ones ([research](../../research/2026-10-15-biome-demos.md#tundra)); the field maps show it.
