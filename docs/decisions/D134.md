# D134: Day and night

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: a whole day lasts half an hour of play, a little more day than night, as at a middle latitude in early summer; night dark enough that light matters but never so dark the ground is lost. Open: what night does besides being dark, such as more or stranger monsters, and cold ([survival.md](../survival.md), [creatures.md](../creatures.md)).

## Why

Built in the tech demo on 2026-10-12 with these numbers, which are constants to change. The developers asked for a torch in the MVP.
