# D178: How a log wall looks

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Round logs, made to look hewn and grown rather than turned: uneven sections, taper, bend, knots, axe-cut ends, colour per face from the wood roles, chinking. 0.3 m thick, 12 courses to a storey, saddle-notched at corners, their ends at the corner cube's outer face (they ran 0.25 m past until 2026-10-12, which got in the way of what was built beside them); the two axes half a course apart; split logs at the foot and top of one axis; posts where two walls meet in line; openings framed, with the logs beside them cut. What a wall costs is set apart from its pieces: counted by length, and one carried log may set two short pieces. See [building-kits.md](../building-kits.md#logs-d178). Amended 2026-10-15 (by the agent, for the developers: "why do the logs have big gaps between them? thats not how you'd build w/ logs irl"): a wall's logs sit tight, as a chinked or a scribed wall's do: each log rests on the one below, never thinner there than the kit's log for all its taper, scribed flat where they meet a course apart (`timber::round`, a few millimetres into each other), not sagging; the daub between set back in the groove so a line only `joint` tall shows (1.5 cm for pine, 1.6 cm for hewn beams bedded flat on each other; 0 a full scribe), every seed and variant held to at most 2 cm along every wall by a test.

## Why

Left to me by the developers on 2026-10-12: "for visuals lets just keep on theme, you can decide, but having 'visual interest' is good and organic when possible", and "we dont need 1:1 the raw materials to visually look the same in the blueprint… placing one log could place 2". Was Open, proposed the same day.
