# D515: Clouds are volumes drawn from the climate's weather map, ported from Bevy's atmosphere clouds

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Clouds are drawn as every shipped real-time cloud system draws them (Horizon's Nubis, Frostbite, Unreal, RDR2): a shell of air ray-marched through a weather map times a height profile per cloud kind, eroded by 3D noise, lit by Beer's law, a forward-scattering phase and multiple scattering, at reduced resolution rebuilt by temporal reprojection, with a cloud shadow map that shadows the ground and the haze, and the sky light following the cover (from the CIE's clear sky toward its overcast). The weather map is the climate's cloud, kind and precipitation over the sky's reach, the base from temperature and humidity, moved by the climate's wind at cloud height and by the world's clock, never a machine's run time, so every client draws the same sky (D52, D315). Clouds stand at their true heights over the ground but spread in the air's frame (D212, D334). The code is ported from the open Nubis-based clouds on Bevy's atmosphere (Máté Homolya's `volumetric-clouds-nubis` branch, under Bevy's MIT or Apache-2.0) after a spike measures it on our scenes and GPUs; from Nubis's talks if it does not fit; upstream's when it lands. Budget: 1.5 ms at the high preset on D25's target, 2 ms on the integrated GPU at the low preset, which otherwise keeps a 2D layer on the same weather map. See [sky.md](../sky.md).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-26: "we'd like to have things like volumetric clouds and realism for weather, but in general to have it look 'real'"; "clouds wouldn't be in the skybox though they'd be in.. like in the sky itself". The method is the industry's, unchanged in its bones since Horizon Zero Dawn (about 2 ms of a PS4); the Bevy branch is that method on the atmosphere we already draw, with a path-traced reference and RDR2's time-sliced shadow map, so porting it beats writing our own (D196). It is unmerged and on a newer Bevy, so it is measured before anything builds on it ([research](../../research/2026-10-26-volumetric-skies.md)). Leaning until the developers have read sky.md (D49).
