# D649: Players cross between realms one by one, and may be in both realms at once

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A gate takes whoever steps through it, alone: nobody is taken along. In a shared world one player may be in the first realm while another is in the realm beyond, each playing on, and each comes back when they choose. So a world that is shared holds both its realms running at once for as long as anyone is in each.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08: "for realms, players cross individually, so players can be in both realms at the same time". It settles what v0's "they cross, together or apart" asks of the engine: not a party taken across, but two realms served at once, which is the instance host's design (D640: each realm a headless server, a connection routed to one, a crossing handing the player's connection to the other realm).

## Log

- **2026-11-08**: D649 decided (by the developers, as quoted). Built toward it: a joined game is handed between a headless host's realms (crossing.ts). Not built: the game's own hosting on the instance host, which waits on each realm instance holding what a walker meets (D640); until then crossing in a shared world is refused.
