# D426: A mountain's walls stand in stepped faces by range kind, in the height

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Plan step 2 of [the mountain look note](../../research/2026-10-19-mountain-look.md), amends D403 (whose tiers stay in the shader for what the mesh cannot hold). A range's walls are stepped in the ground's height itself (`terra_voxel::Faces`, `Range::stepped`), as Gaea's Stratify and World Machine's Terrace nodes step a heightfield, on the walls alone:
- **Kinds (D182).** `Mountains::faces` lists the theme's kinds with weights; each patch of about 0.6 of `range_m` takes one, blended only at its edges. Start: `HORNS` (Tatras granite, D425: beds 55 m, half of each a sheer riser, stepped 0.65, dip 0.05) and `SAW` (Skellige-like saw ridges: beds 22 m, riser 0.4, stepped 0.7, dip 0.09). What tells kinds apart: bed thickness, riser share, how hard the wall is stepped, and dip.
- **Drains.** A face only cuts down; its riser climbs as a power (1.6) of its bed, so it never stands above the ground's climb. Each ledge keeps a share of the wall's fall (1 − stepped), the beds dip only along the wall, and the warp is gentle, so no ledge holds a pond. The face fades up a wall's height (`upper`) and in by slope from 12 degrees to the kind's cliff angle, never fast across a ledge: fading by slope over a narrow band lifted ledges' outer edges into lips (the test `faces_stand_sheer_over_ledges_that_still_drain`).
- **Uneven beds, cut by the gullies.** Each bed's top strays by its own hash (up to 0.35 of a bed either way), so beds run from a third of the kind's thickness to five thirds, each cut by its own thickness so every ledge falls alike; each bed's riser share strays 35% either way; a third warp folds the beds 30 m over 700 m. The faces fade out down the coarsest gullies' lines (`RIB_FADE`, across the fall, so no ledge is lifted), standing as buttresses between smooth couloirs. Round 3: the even contour stripes break into staggered, uneven ledges; far contrast held (0.077 -> 0.073, noise), far under sky 0.050 unchanged. Per-bed hardness (round 2) lost the gain and stays out.
- **Folded, not a layer cake (round 5).** The beds' warp folds them 120 m over 2 km and 45 m over 800 m (was 60 and 30), horns' beds 75 m and stepped 0.6; the dip is a plane (the fall's share taken out of it did nothing but add a step at creases). Drainage caps the geometry's dip and fold, so the shader carries the rest: its tiers fold by a scalar of 760 m over 2 km and 210 m over 600 m (a dip vector dotted with the position grew with the planet's radius), cut by couloirs and gullies; far off, thin snow on a wall keeps to its couloirs, so level ledges no longer hold white dashes; and faces turned to the sun are warmer, those turned away cooler.
- **Horns, ledges as rock (round 6).** Pyramidal horns stand on the crests (`Mountains::horns_m` 950 m, `horns_across_m` 2.6 km, `Range::horns`): sites on a jittered lattice, four in five kept, only where the ridges are near their crests; each three or four cirque faces at random turns, the distance out along each face less 0.45 of the distance across it squared (a bowl, so the arêtes between stand proud), a near-straight cone `(1 - d)^1.3` out to 830 m. Ported from nothing: no permissive implementation exists (Gaea's, World Machine's and Houdini's are closed). Under the horns, more than half the ground stands past 50 degrees, over twice the share without (the test). A stepped wall's ledges are its rock (`Rise::wall`, past 33 to 37 degrees before stepping), so they no longer draw as level bands of turf; the shader's tiers stand only on cliffs past 50 degrees, their benches shaded as the wall, and far thin snow is cut to the couloirs on bare rock only, so the summits keep their caps. Far rock is broken into 70 m Voronoi facets, its ribs patchy, its shaded faces darker.
- **Jointed walls, fed snow on them (round 8).** The far walls (past ~40 degrees) are cut into blocks in the ground shader (`jointing`, two sets: 40 m strata within a few kilometres' view, 16 m nearer), as Hoek and Bray's joint sets break granite: bedding dipping 20-45 degrees a way that wanders over kilometres, two upright joint sets staggered course to course, each block's fracture face turned its own way with a hard edge, the joints dark (some beds closed), faces turned from the sun shaded by the block's own normal. One block in two holds snow on a lip along its top, broken along it, where the vertex carries any; below the snowline a quarter of that as turf. In `lying`, the range's rock carries the snow 1200 m higher (was 900) up to about 70 degrees (whole from 57), the climate's own snow still cut off steep ribs; the snowline itself stays the climate's, by latitude and height and lean (`terra_land::Snow`). Snow on the range's rock 4% -> 14% in both views, far contrast 0.089 -> 0.185.
- **Fault blocks, ribs and towers, couloir snow (round 9).** The shader's beds and tiers are measured in fault blocks about 1.4 km across (`bedding`, wavering borders): each block's bedding dips 35-70 degrees its own way, in a frame fixed for the block, measured from its centre. The old frame turned with the position and was dotted with the position itself, a planet's radius long, so it gave back the height and every bed ran as a contour line, the layer cake; its joints were noise alone. One upright joint set runs through the beds (upright slabs), the other is staggered bed to bed; one block in four holds a lip. A wall's snow lies in its far couloirs (`couloir_fill`), and ledges hold it only in patches. Past 2.5-7 km the rock and snow dim to 0.78 (`FAR_DIM`), so the far land stays under the sky. In the mesh (`mountains/ribs.rs`), the horns' faces carry buttress ribs (0.12 of the reach apart, 0.05 deep in face distance) and their arêtes carry towers and notches (0.09 apart, 0.03 deep). The ribs are laid over the ground after the gullies and faces, which follow the slope without them (`Range::unribbed`). Stepped by a slope that turned across every rib, the faces' ledges climbed half a metre into lips. Far contrast 0.185 -> 0.147, far under sky 0.044 -> 0.109 (passes); snow 14% -> 11%.
- **Kept.** No face within 700 m of a pinned place, whole past 1.5 km. The field's steepest is 5.5 with faces, for the risers.

## Why

The developers want "mountains that look rocky and tall" (D425); round one's terraces cut into the height everywhere held ponds; the erosion spike gave no spurs. Risers to 65-80 degrees over ledges give real shadow and silhouette at a few kilometres, which the shader's tiers alone could not.
