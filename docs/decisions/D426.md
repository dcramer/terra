# D426: A mountain's walls stand in stepped faces by range kind, in the height

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Plan step 2 of [the mountain look note](../../research/2026-10-19-mountain-look.md), amends D403 (whose tiers stay in the shader for what the mesh cannot hold). A range's walls are stepped in the ground's height itself (`terra_voxel::Faces`, `Range::stepped`), as Gaea's Stratify and World Machine's Terrace nodes step a heightfield, on the walls alone:
- **Kinds (D182).** `Mountains::faces` lists the theme's kinds with weights; each patch of about 0.6 of `range_m` takes one, blended only at its edges. Start: `HORNS` (Tatras granite, D425: beds 55 m, half of each a sheer riser, stepped 0.65, dip 0.05) and `SAW` (Skellige-like saw ridges: beds 22 m, riser 0.4, stepped 0.7, dip 0.09). What tells kinds apart: bed thickness, riser share, how hard the wall is stepped, and dip.
- **Drains.** A face only cuts down; its riser climbs as a power (1.6) of its bed, so it never stands above the ground's climb. Each ledge keeps a share of the wall's fall (1 − stepped), the beds dip only along the wall, and the warp is gentle, so no ledge holds a pond. The face fades up a wall's height (`upper`) and in by slope from 12 degrees to the kind's cliff angle, never fast across a ledge: fading by slope over a narrow band lifted ledges' outer edges into lips (the test `faces_stand_sheer_over_ledges_that_still_drain`).
- **Uneven beds, cut by the gullies.** Each bed's top strays by its own hash (up to 0.35 of a bed either way), so beds run from a third of the kind's thickness to five thirds, each cut by its own thickness so every ledge falls alike; each bed's riser share strays 35% either way; a third warp folds the beds 30 m over 700 m. The faces fade out down the coarsest gullies' lines (`RIB_FADE`, across the fall, so no ledge is lifted), standing as buttresses between smooth couloirs. Round 3: the even contour stripes break into staggered, uneven ledges; far contrast held (0.077 -> 0.073, noise), far under sky 0.050 unchanged. Per-bed hardness (round 2) lost the gain and stays out.
- **Folded, not a layer cake (round 5).** The beds' warp folds them 120 m over 2 km and 45 m over 800 m (was 60 and 30), horns' beds 75 m and stepped 0.6; the dip is a plane (the fall's share taken out of it did nothing but add a step at creases). Drainage caps the geometry's dip and fold, so the shader carries the rest: its tiers fold by a scalar of 760 m over 2 km and 210 m over 600 m (a dip vector dotted with the position grew with the planet's radius), cut by couloirs and gullies; far off, thin snow on a wall keeps to its couloirs, so level ledges no longer hold white dashes; and faces turned to the sun are warmer, those turned away cooler.
- **Kept.** No face within 700 m of a pinned place, whole past 1.5 km. The field's steepest is 5.5 with faces, for the risers.

## Why

The developers want "mountains that look rocky and tall" (D425); round one's terraces cut into the height everywhere held ponds; the erosion spike gave no spurs. Risers to 65-80 degrees over ledges give real shadow and silhouette at a few kilometres, which the shader's tiers alone could not.
