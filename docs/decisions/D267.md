# D267: Solid things fitted to their meshes, and a step onto low ones

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D243 and D248. Every rock, boulder, stump, log and tree stops a walker by convex pieces fitted to its own mesh where it stands, as shipped games fit simple colliders to a visual mesh: each kind of model is read once into its relief, its highest point over each cell of a grid over its plan (a few hundred cells), and each thing placed fits convex outlines with a rounded edge round the cells that show above the ground, cut in two along their length where one outline would leave empty corners, or where it is long, so a log's thin end is a piece of its own (`terra_core::Solid`, Bevy-free, D28). Each piece knows how tall it stands over the ground under it: a walker is stopped by any taller than its 0.4 m step, and steps onto, and is held up by, anything lower, as a character controller's step offset lets it; it stands on a taller one only by jumping up onto it. A tree's trunk is fitted the same way to its finest wood, root flare and all, up to past a body's height, and a skirt is 0.7 of the way out to its boughs within a body's height of the ground at every turn round it, not a circle. `cargo xtask collide` measures against the meshes themselves (`lush.footprint`), frame by frame: it fails when the body stands more than 0.1 m into a mesh taller than a step, stops more than 0.35 m short of a tall thing as drawn, does not step over a low thing or a log's low end, or is lifted more than a step onto a tall one.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "we will need slightly better collision meshes". D243's circles along a bounding box left invisible corners round rocks and stopped the walker short of their faces, and nothing could be stepped onto. collide passes on 23 walks at things of seven kinds, the walker stopping within 0.08 m of every tall thing as drawn and stepping over low rocks and logs' low ends; it fails on 21 walks with the pieces 0.3 m too thin, 19 with them 0.5 m too fat, and 4 (the low ones) with no step. lodcheck passes; the walk bench 12.3 ms median, 13.6 at the 99th percentile, in its one run with the RTX 3080 to itself.
