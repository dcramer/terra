# D218: Bark that reads as a real trunk

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A grown trunk is no longer a smooth tube: its radius varies with shallow flutes up the bark and broad lumps, and five roots spread at its foot, each tree its own by where it grew, on extra rings near the ground and 20 sides in full detail, the normals following the shape. The bark's furrows are relief-mapped from its scanned height (Bevy's parallax mapping, Relief, 16 layers). Each tree's bark is shifted by where it stands, so trees of one kind never show the same bark in the same place. Bark is weathered in the fragment shader: each tree a little lighter or darker by where it stands, moss in patches toward the foot and on what faces up, the foot darker where it stays wet.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "can we make trunk texture more rng generally speaking? it feels like 90s zelda games and i would rather bring it closer home to AAA", and "maybe make bark more visual like bump map or smthing". The walk bench holds at 8.5 ms on the RTX 3080.
