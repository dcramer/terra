# D47: Numerous world objects

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Objects such as trees and rocks are computed from the seed and their location, not stored. Only changes are remembered, as operations. Only nearby ones are drawn, baked together by area, and interaction is by lookup.

## Why

Tried in the sandbox: about a million choppable trees on a 2 km planet for 216 KB of worst-case state and 3.4 ms a frame on the RTX 5090. Not yet tried on voxels, with heavier objects, or in motion. See [research/forest-scale-test.md](../../research/forest-scale-test.md).
