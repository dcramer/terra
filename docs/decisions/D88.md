# D88: Player skills

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

There is a player progress system with a lot of skills, so there is always a sense of progression, and new skills can be picked up. Doing a thing gives you its skill automatically, and doing it more improves it. Other ways of gaining skills are likely but not chosen.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10. Much of it "could be" inspired by *He Who Fights With Monsters*, which is Leaning. Proposal and questions in [progression.md](../progression.md#player-skills).
