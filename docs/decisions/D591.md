# D591: Natural caves are part of core world design

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Natural caves are part of how a world is generated, not a later feature or one realm's special: the ground has hollows of its own, with a world's theme setting how many and how big ([worlds.md](../worlds.md#what-a-world-is)). They are in v0 (D589). The demo's boss, the troll (D590, D402), lives in one, so a seed's land has to hold a cave that fits a 4 m body and a fight, reachable from the surface.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-01, asked whether v0 has caves: "we want natural caves as part of core world design, so the troll can live in one". The generator, streaming below the player and light underground all change to allow them, so they belong in the foundations.
