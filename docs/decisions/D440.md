# D440: A creature's skeleton is fitted to its mesh by its plan

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The skeleton is fitted to the mesh, not typed in: the mesh cut into cross-sections along its length, each chain of the plan matched to a limb found in them, joints at each limb's narrowing or the plan's share of its length, the spine through the sections' centres; Tripo's quadruped and avian rig a second opinion; the fit scored (bones inside and centred, feet on the ground, mirrored, proportions in the plan's range) and stopped with a sheet when it fails; `creature.json` joints only as overrides ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#2-skeleton-fitting-d440)).

## Why

Pinocchio (Baran and Popović 2007) fits a template skeleton into a mesh's interior the same way; hand-placed joints are today's slowest step, and their errors are found only by eye. Research: [research/2026-10-22-creature-rig-pipeline.md](../../research/2026-10-22-creature-rig-pipeline.md).
