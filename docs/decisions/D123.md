# D123: Recruits progress

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

People who join you have their own progression: they get better, as you do. Whether other people in the world progress is open.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11: "npcs would also have their own internal progression system, at least ones that are recruited". How is proposed in [people.md](../people.md#recruits).
