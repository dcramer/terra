# D655: Small life a player could care about is a thing in the world; only what nobody touches is a shader's speck

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The line between the two ways small life is made (D647) is "could a player ever care about this one?". **In the world**: butterflies, moths, dragonflies, fireflies, and bees if they may be caught, are each a thing the game knows: a small model whose place and behaviour the CPU holds (where it is, what it flies to, what it flees), fixed in the world so two players see the same one, with only its repeated motion (a wing's beat) done in the vertex shader, as the fish are (`terra_creatures::fish`, `assets/shaders/fish.wesl`). **A shader's speck**: dust, pollen, midges, down, rain and snow stay the drift (`terra_world::drift`, D379), where a place is a formula of time on the GPU, the field follows the eye and nothing can be aimed at, picked up or frightened. Whether a player may catch them is not chosen: it is why the line is here, not a part of it. Until they are moved, butterflies are the drift's still, as two hinged wings and a few in a view (2026-11-09). The design is [ambient-life.md](../ambient-life.md).

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-09: "butterflies i guess are a shader? they look stupid right now and far too numerous..."; "is a shader the ight solution for things like this? especially if i might want to let you catch bugs"; and of the line above, as the agent put it: "i agree with your line". A drift's butterfly has no entity: its place is worked out in the vertex stage from its luck and the time, in a box that wraps round the eye, so it cannot be caught, land on a flower, flee, or be the same one for two players. The fish show the other way at no cost that matters for dozens.
