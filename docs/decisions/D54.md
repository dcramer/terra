# D54: Models, for now

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Free CC0 low-polygon packs (Quaternius), read for their shapes only and given our own colours in code, so every model shares one palette and one shader. A prop with no free model comes from [tools/props](../../tools/props/README.md) (D147).

## Why

Made the outpost scene possible in a day with no artist, and keeps the door open to replacing any model without touching the look. Untested: whether stock shapes can carry the game's own identity, and what a dead world is built from. Sources and licences are in [THIRD_PARTY.md](../../THIRD_PARTY.md).
