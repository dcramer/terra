# D657: An animal's voice has a budget: of one voice at most two begin in any six seconds from afar and two more near at hand, the nearest first

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Not every animal in earshot is voiced. Of one voice (a set of recorded clips, or a sound made in code: a kind's `cry` or `call`), at most two begin in any six seconds from beyond its clip's reach, the nearest of a frame first, and the rest are not played; one within its reach, where it is as loud as it gets, has two more of its own, so the bird put up at a walker's feet is always heard (`terra_game` `play/sounds/budget.rs`, held in `play::sounds::hear`). At the very most 40 a minute of one voice, 20 from afar. Steps, wings and the moments of a kind's clips are not budgeted: each is heard as it happens. A place's birdsong beyond that is its chorus's (`Chorus`, `play::soundscape`), not more animals voiced. A kind's `calls_s` stays what one animal does; how many of a kind are heard is the budget's, so a kind is never retuned to thin a crowd.

A cry belongs to fright: it is given as an animal is frightened (`Creature::frightened`: running or walking off from someone, put up on the wing, up a trunk), never at a takeoff from perch to perch, and an animal's first call at rest falls anywhere within its longest wait.

`node tools/sandbox/heard.ts` holds both on a walk: no voice heard more than 40 times a minute, and no cry asked for more than that, heard or not.

The two and the six are guesses, to be set by a developer's ear on a walk: a call lasts one to two seconds, so two in six leaves a voice sounding under half the time.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-09: "all our creatuers need proper audio if any other use stock..". With recorded voices in, a three minute walk in the wood (seed 3, 2026-11-10) heard the crow's caw 115 times a minute, the chaffinch's alarm 65 and its song 91. Counted by cause: 305 of the crow's 350 and 198 of the alarm's 203 were takeoffs, nearly all from perch to perch; 32 more of the crow's were first calls, each within 20 s of the bird forming whatever its `calls_s`. The song was the kind's own data: 24 a hectare each singing every 6 to 20 s is a song a second within its 72 m.

Shipped engines cap how many of one sound play and choose which by distance: Unreal's Sound Concurrency ([reference](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/sound-concurrency-reference-guide), read 2026-11-10 [P]) has a group's Max Count, "the maximum number of concurrently active sounds allowed in this group before the Resolution Rule takes effect", the rule "Stop Farthest Then Prevent New", and a Retrigger Time, "the wait time (in seconds) between playing sounds". Wwise's playback limit and FMOD's Max Instances with stealing are said to be the same [M: their pages would not load]. We depart in one thing: a call is never stopped once begun, so the count is of those begun in a span, and the farthest is the one not begun.
