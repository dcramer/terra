# D211: The look of building: on par with Enshrouded

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Building takes an approach to voxels like Enshrouded's, and is to be on par with it in how buildings blend together, not a copy of its look: our art style (D14), our kits and our materials. Cubes stay the truth and stay optimised cubes, merged per section; cubes that touch blend into one structure, with no seams or stacked boxes; and each cube's look changes with what it touches and where it sits (corner, edge, top, base, rough or dressed, against another material, against the ground), as Enshrouded's blocks do. This is the bar every kind, the stone surface (D197), generated timber (D200), roofs (D209) and trims (D210), is judged against, side by side with Enshrouded, and changed where it falls short. Finding the technology that meets it is the agent's: research first ([research/voxel-building-look.md](../../research/voxel-building-look.md)), then spikes compared against Enshrouded, then the chosen way built and measured.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "so enshrouded has cubes right, and then when two cubes touch, it somehow blends it. thats really what we're after"; "they change visually depending on what they're touching or where they're located"; "make sure this is clear that that is our agenda in the plan and you need to find the technology solution to achieve it"; "we need the system to be ON PAR WITH ENSHROUDED"; "this doesnt mean we're copying enshrouded, but we want to take an approach to voxels similar to how they've done it for the way buildings blend together with textures/whatever".
