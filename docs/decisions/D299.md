# D299: Under the trees the air's distances shorter

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The air is drawn as an Earth-sized one with its distances eighteen times the world's (D212), so a few hundred metres of hills are as hazy as mountains; under a closed canopy they are nine times, rising evenly by ratio to eighteen as the sky over the eye opens, as the forest's haze gives way to the open air's (D279). The air between trunks lies in the canopy's shade, not the sunlit sky's, and at eighteen a trunk fifty metres off took the blue of a mountain nine hundred off. Its haze over what is drawn starts fifty metres out (D475): at nine times, the near metres laid a few hundred metres of sunlit haze over the dark crowns, and read them grey-teal.
