# D462: Dual wielding

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Whether a body may hold a one-hander in each hand ([holding.md](../holding.md)). Options:

- (a) Never.
- (b) Paired items only: twin daggers or twin blades as one item, in one slot, with one set, as Elden Ring's claws and twinblades come.
- (c) Any two one-handers that may go in either hand. They get a dual set when they share a family, and otherwise each hand's own light blow (Elden Ring's power stance).
- (d) Any two one-handers.

Recommended: (b) now, for the Wolf's Run style ("two short blades", magic.md); (c) later if it is wanted. A dual set's off-hand blows are the main hand's mirrored (D459), so it needs only a few moves of its own.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, on the review desk: "Any two". This settles the question below in that option's favour.

The developer, 2026-10-25, listed "sometimes two one handed items" among the cases to design for. Sources disagree on whether Enshrouded allows it (research/2026-10-25-loadouts-and-animation-layers.md).
