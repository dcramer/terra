# D572: The axe proof's animations: a ready pose and the felling chop first

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Open

Which animations the one-handed axe gets for D565 step 4's proof (proof B: the nine axes held, swung and carried on the envelope's grid, on every body). Recommended: the axe as it is in the game today, a felling tool (D174): its own ready pose and a felling chop at a trunk, keyed for the reference axe, with the carry it already has; an axe fighting set (guard, light chain, heavy, its weaker one-handed block, D567) comes later as its own work. Other options: the fighting set now (about five clips, each keyed and taken as the sword's were), or both.

## Why

The proof must swing the axe to show its parts stay on the hand with the wrist in range and the edge leading (assembled-items.md, order of work step 4), and the axe has no clips of its own (assets/data/kinds/axe.json: no ready pose, no set). The felling chop is one clip a feature already decided needs (D174), so the proof costs least and builds nothing on an undecided design; an axe fighting set is a design of its own.
