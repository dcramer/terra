# D572: The axe proof's animations: the felling chop and the fighting set both

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Which animations the one-handed axe gets for D565 step 4's proof (proof B: the nine axes held, swung and carried on the envelope's grid, on every body). Both: its own ready pose and a felling chop at a trunk (D174), with the carry it already has, and an axe fighting set (its guard, a light chain, a heavy and its weaker one-handed block, D567), each keyed for the reference axe. Recommended was the chop first and the fighting set later. Proof B waits for the 2026-10-28 step back on why the fight clips still look bad, so it is not keyed through a pipeline that may change.

## Why

The proof must swing the axe to show its parts stay on the hand with the wrist in range and the edge leading (assembled-items.md, order of work step 4), and the axe has no clips of its own (assets/data/kinds/axe.json: no ready pose, no set). The felling chop is one clip a feature already decided needs (D174), so the proof costs least and builds nothing on an undecided design; an axe fighting set is a design of its own. Asked on the review desk (d565-axe-clips).

The developers, 2026-10-28: "Both".
