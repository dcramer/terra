# D641: A realm's gate is placed from its seed on ground a walk from the start reaches

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A world's gate stands where `terra_land::gate::find` puts it: of the land round the start, on the survey's 50 m grid, the cells a walk from the start reaches that are dry, well over the sea and level (8 degrees or less) and 150 to 400 m from the start, failing that out to 800 m; of those the seed picks one. Dry means no water within 15 m as the world draws it, not only on the survey's grid: `terra_land::dry` fills the lakes round the start on the survey's cells over the world's ten kilometres and on its ten-metre cells over three, and the survey's cells they cover are water no walk crosses; the den's mouth (D591) is held to the same, within 25 m. `gate::faults` reads a place against the same rules afresh, and the seed sweep (`cargo xtask worlds`) holds every world to having one. Where in its cell the ring stands, clear of trees and rocks, is the game's to refine when it stands the gate there. **How far the gate stands from the start is a design number, the developers' to tune**; 150 to 400 m is a first value, near enough to find in the demo's arc (v0-goals).

## Why

v0-goals asks for "the gate placed from the seed, each reachable on every seed". Found from the land by rules, as the start (D596) and the town (D321) are, never painted; as shipped worlds place their way onward from the seed (Minecraft's strongholds in rings round the origin, Valheim's altars at seeded places of a biome). The farther reach is there because the sweep found a world without one in the nearer: seed 104's start stands among slopes.
