# D465: How many variants of a move

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

How many takes of each move are kept and drawn without repeats ([holding.md](../holding.md), section 4). Options:

- (a) One take, with variety only from additive layers and personal style.
- (b) Three takes of each light blow, block, idle and reaction; one of each heavy and special.
- (c) Four or more of everything.

Recommended: (b). That is about 450 clips in all, made unattended (D450), with the extra takes where repetition shows most.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-25: "what happens with variance?". Shipped games draw a few takes from a shuffle bag and add small additive layers (research/2026-10-25-loadouts-and-animation-layers.md). How much repetition a player notices is a call for the developers' eye.
