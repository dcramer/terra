# D561: A fire's flames move at 0.65 of the clock

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A hearth's flames, a campfire's and a torch's alike, move at 0.65 of the game clock's pace: their baked loop and its wisps, their tongues, the logs' heat and their embers' crawl, and the flicker and sway of their light and the heat haze over them, all in step. Sparks and smoke keep the clock. How it is built is technical (`fire::PACE`, docs/fire.md "The flames' pace").

The motion gate's bars come from real campfire clips, and a slower flame's tongues live longer by design, so the gate holds a tongue's lifetime to the clips' bars divided by the pace (D472); every other bar stays the clips'.

## Why

The developers, answering the desk's question q-fire-pace (2026-10-28) from a real-time film of the campfire at dusk and night at full pace, 0.8, 0.65 and 0.5: "0.65x".

Before it they had judged the campfire "going too fast". Measured (fire round g88, the gate's still view, 10 s): at full pace it flickered at a median 2.8-3.0 Hz against four real campfires' 1.83-2.33, about 1.3 times as fast; at 0.65 the probe read 2.67 at dusk and 2.33 at night. The real clips' tongue lifetimes (0.04-0.12 s) are their pace's; the developers' pick overrides that pace, so the gate follows it.
