# D349: A material's pack checked as it is added

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

D290's known failure modes, the inputs a material made from a scan brings that the drawing would take without a word, fail loudly when the material is added (`cargo xtask pack`, which then keeps nothing of it) and on every check (`cargo xtask kits`), each saying what is wrong and how to fix it (`terra_build::packs`): its height map weak, inverted (`--invert-height`), noise or not its colour map's, judged by its correlation with its occlusion or colour; a texture that does not tile, its edges' step against the largest inside it; its real size off its pattern's range, stones 10 to 60 cm and flags 15 to 90 cm counted from its height's cells, logs 20 to 40 cm with their bark laid at their girth, a covering's courses 12 to 70 cm; relief and parallax past what its pattern draws well; a role its pattern asks every pack for (block and frame: walls, floors; coursed: face, daub; roof: covering, rafters, its own pieces) left to the pattern's default rather than given by the pack or the pack it is laid over; glossy or metal maps, or a reflectance out of 0.05 to 0.6; a field its pattern never reads (a roof's `timber`, a block or frame kind's daub colour), named with the file that sets it (`packs::fields`). Each limit sits past the worst shipped scan and short of planted bad ones, both tested. Each new material gets a preview sheet to look over once (`tools/kits/preview.ts`): the kit sheet lays its preview alone west of the start, every role and shape of its pattern, pictured at 10 m and 2 m at noon and in the evening; `cargo xtask pack` makes it where a GPU draws the game and prints its command elsewhere. [construction-002](../proposals/construction-002.md#what-each-pattern-can-and-cannot-express) lists what each pattern can and cannot express, so a material needing a new pattern (code) is known before it is made.

## Why

The developers on D290: "what i dont want is 'why does this material look fucked up? oh the system doesnt handle it well because it has this thing we decided on'… i just wanna make sure this is the right design". The design is the industry's (material arrays, trim sheets, shared pieces); its failures are bad inputs, so the inputs are what is checked.
