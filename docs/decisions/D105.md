# D105: The MVP

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A first playable, as a prototype of the core loop in [gameplay.md](../gameplay.md): one world and biome, gathering with tools, crafting, a claim and voxel building, one wildlife and one monster, combat, one settlement and a rescue, skills, hunger and death, a local server, saving. No essences in it; they come first after it.

## Why

Proposed 2026-10-11 after the developers left essences in the MVP to me. Scope and pass criteria in [prototypes.md](../prototypes.md#mvp-the-first-playable), for the developers to edit.
