# D177: How kit pieces are made and drawn

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

One layout per wall, by its kit's rule, serves the blueprint, the part going up and the finished building: the same pieces drawn as a ghost, as some in and some ghost, and merged. Props come from the art pipeline (`tools/props`); a kind's profiles, modules and stones are made in Blender by `tools/kits` (D251, D258), to the style guide (D14), modelled by hand where they have been and by a script from numbers where not yet, which are which kept in one place, [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md#one-material-system-d251-d258); code only places, repeats, cuts by a plane and sweeps them. Drawn from real materials by code: a kind's cubes' surface, roofs' coverings among them (D197, D258), logs (D200), and timber decks, stairs and boarded shapes (D206, D220, D231) until they take modules. A kit is a file in `assets/data/kits/`. A kit sheet on the yard shows every situation in every state. See [building-kits.md](../building-kits.md#how-pieces-are-made-d177-d197-d200-d251). Amended by D290: rule-shaped geometry wearing a hand-made model's bake is allowed, and a kit is a material file over its pattern and pack.

## Why

Mine, for D139 and D146; revised 2026-10-12 when the developers saw pieces drawn by code and said they broke the art direction ("you're just drawing random geometry which is IN VIOLATION OF OUR ART DIRECTION"). The first version made logs in code, from numbers and a seed, because a log's length is set by its wall; stretching a model along its length does that without inventing its look, since a flat-coloured log has no texture to smear.
