# D550: Three timber joints: the post between a window and a door, the end board on a door's head, the eave board at a roof's step

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

How three joints in timber read, judged from stills on the review desk:

- **A window beside a doorway**: the post between them runs the full height from the floor to the head, unbroken, and the window's sill is framed into its side, so the window and the door stay two openings. Replaces 085aed8f's ending, where the post stopped under the sill and the sill's end rested on it, leaving one L-shaped hole above the sill.
- **The end board over a doorway where a timber wall ends against stone** stands on the door's head, which runs through to the stone; under a window it stops at the sill's foot (cb43aa13, kept).
- **An eave at a roof's step**: where a roof steps down a cube, the higher roof's level edge at the step's top is an eave like any other, its fascia hanging plumb and boards closing the step under it; the mesh check measures them from that eave (b8e5e95b, kept, D343's gap 11).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-27, on the review desk: the post between a window and a doorway, "Post runs full height"; the end board on a door's head, "Reads right"; the eave board at a roof's step, "Reads right".
