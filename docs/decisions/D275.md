# D275: The forest floor in layers that settle into each other, with litter lying on it

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D246 and D268. **The ground's layers** (`assets/shaders/ground.wesl`) are scans of what a wood's floor is: Poly Haven's Brown Mud 02 for trodden soil and paths, Dry Decay Leaves for leaf litter, Forest Leaves 04 for needle duff, Roots, and ambientCG's Moss 004 for moss, eight layers with each one's height, its broad swells taken out, in its normal map's blue (`textures.ts stack --height`). The floor's layers are sampled hex-tiled, as Mikkelsen's "Practical Real-Time Hex-Tiling" (JCGT 2022, MIT, ported), so no repeat shows, and laid on each other by height, as terrain splatting in shipped games does, so leaves cover soil leaf by leaf and moss fills the hollows; the cover map is read a little way off by noise, so its edges fray rather than ending in half-metre blobs; hollows are darker and see less of the sky, the light under a canopy (D270), and the relief is half as strong again. **What covers it**: fallen leaves wherever trees stand near, off the paths, not only in deep shade; needles carpet a conifer stand between its trees, giving way under a broadleaf's crown; grass in the shade thins into the litter rather than ending. **Litter as geometry** (`terra_world::cover::litter`), as shipped games scatter debris over their terrain: cards of an atlas composed from ambientCG's scanned leaves (`textures.ts cards`, `litter` recipes in `art/textures/litter`: oak and beech leaves, needles, cones, twigs, ivy) lying humped on the ground in the grass's nearest ring, some 3.5 a square metre on a full floor and twice that against trunks, logs and rocks, ivy in drifts under broadleaves, none in thick grass, sunk into the ground a piece at a time from 8 to 16 m (`litter.wesl`), drawn with no sheen as the canopy hides the sky it would reflect. Dead branches lie as limbs sunk into the litter, forked, some forks propping a thin end up, in brown bark; a trunk's foot has moss in cushions on what faces up and soil over its bark between (`foliage_fragment.wesl` `weather`). How much grass grows under the canopy and the litter's colour are D293; heaps against trunks, rocks' moss and the floor in the sun D294.

## Why

A review against Sons of the Forest (the ground 4.5/10): flat orange-tan ground in the pine stand with no needles, a near-black featureless floor under the oaks, grey sticks, a root flare a smooth green cone, the path one repeating pebble texture, the dirt a hard-edged blob. Measured on the vantage sheet's floor views, the lowest 30% of the picture: small-scale contrast (each 9-pixel square's SD over its mean) 0.21 to 0.29 on the path at 13:00 and 0.20 to 0.24 on the floor at 17:00, featureless pixels 3.1% to 0.6%, the path's strongest repeat 0.20 to 0.10; a harsh review scored the ground 4 before, 5.5 after (still a printed rug beside Sons of the Forest's heaps), its litter texture swapped for larger leaves after it; bare ground within 30 m 7.0% to 6.1%, all of it path. The walk bench on the RTX 3080, shared with other agents (load 7 to 18): GPU 13.1 ms median against main's 12.9 measured beside it, the opaque pass 0.2 ms more; both missed the budget only as contended. lodcheck passes (worst 0.22%).
