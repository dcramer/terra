# D339: Acts on a base worked out off the main thread and swapped in

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

An act on the player's base (D21's operations: a fill, a carve, damage and what it brings down) may be worked out off the main thread (`build::act::Building::act_later`): against a copy of the base, which shares its cubes until they change (D213), the operations are applied and what falls, is chipped off and is only repainted worked out (`build::work_out`) on a thread of the acts' own, so an act of a few milliseconds never waits behind the section workers' longer jobs; the main thread swaps the result in whole (`Build::swap`), the old base let go on a worker, pays for it, keeps it to be undone and logs it, as a streaming engine double-buffers what its workers build. Acts are taken in in the order made, and anything that reads or changes the base for an act (another act, a check of one, an undo, a remote call) first takes in every act still out, so the outcome is the same as acting at once whatever the timing. Harm's damage, the remote's calls in a benchmark (which nothing waits on) and the hammer's steps are worked out so, a step checked on the main thread first so a refusal is told at once, and the next step chosen once it is in, its moment shortened by the wait. A scripted run that is not a benchmark acts at once, so the QA films are the same frame for frame. A change's time to the screen is counted from its act.

## Why

The castle QA's tower fall spent 15 to 100 ms of the main thread on damage and collapse in the frame it was made, and a storey's fills up to 67 ms. The lead: "apply operations, including damage and collapse, asynchronously behind a final swap". With D340, the stone build film's hitches went from 49 to none or few and the tower's worst frame from 45 to under 26.7 ms (RTX 5090).
