# D210: Trims where openings and kinds meet, and the kit checker

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Round every opening in a block kind's wall, where its kit has a `Dressed` stone, a dressed lintel where the wall goes on above, a sill under a window and jamb stones up its sides, long and short by turns, each a little proud of the rubble (`layout::blocks`); without one, a timber lintel. Where a thinner wall of another kind stands on blocks, as logs on a stone plinth or footing, a dressed cap over the blocks' top; where a timber wall ends against another kind's, an end board up the joint over the logs' sawn ends (`layout::trims`). Log walls keep their frames of boards. `cargo xtask kits`, run by `cargo xtask check`, checks every kit file (`terra_build::checker`): the materials its pattern needs and every map of each, life-size numbers, costs in items there are, and the style guide's budgets measured on a cabin laid in it: 700 triangles a metre of log and 500 a square metre of roof. Since D215, the stone round openings and the caps are a kit's dress by role, and the checker checks the dress too.

## Why

Mine, for step 2 of [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md) on 2026-10-13: the trims and the checker the plan lists; dressed stone from Poly Haven's CC0 `rock_boulder_dry`, read from the kit like every material, and the budgets set over what the three kits draw today.
