# D106: Packs

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A pack is a container: an item worn in the pack slot that holds the items in it, and a bigger pack holds more. Take it off and what is in it stays in it. With no pack you carry nothing but what is in your hands, perhaps two hand slots (Leaning).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "a pack IS a container that holds things, a bigger pack holds more things, thats what i mean. if you have no pack, you cant carry anything other than maybe we give you like two hands or something". Floated on 2026-10-11 after Stationeers; replaces D65. Fits death dropping what you carried (D84, D171). See [items.md](../items.md#packs). Not built: the game still grows one backpack.
