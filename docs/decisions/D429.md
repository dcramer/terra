# D429: The looks check knows water by what draws it

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The looks check's debug-tint rule (D356, art-style rule 6) leaves out the water by a material mask, not by its colour. Each view's run (`vantage.ts`, `--water-mask`) takes a second picture six frames after the view's own with every lake drawn flat magenta (`lakes::WaterMask`, `MASK` in lake.wesl); the pixels that turned magenta are water (`look.ts` `waterMask`), and an 8-pixel square with water in it is never a fill. The square still counts, so a picture's share stays of its ground. The 195-245 degree sky-blue band stays for the sea, which draws no mask yet.

## Why

The rule let through only hues 195 to 245 as water, so a mountain lake's smooth green-teal (hue 185 to 195) counted as a debug fill: the lake place's views measured 8.3% against 0.9%, most of it the water itself (wedge handoff, 2026-10-20). Widening the band would also pass a real teal or cyan fill on land, which the rule is there to catch; telling the water by what draws it, as engines' debug views do with a material or stencil ID, passes the water alone. Magenta in both pictures, a flower's, is not water. The second picture costs six frames a view.
