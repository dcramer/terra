# D368: Leaves checked to read as leaves, card by card and in the picture

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Serves D291 and D292, and D356's rules (the art guide's [rule 12](../art-style.md#places), which restates D291). **The cards:** `cargo xtask foliage` (`tools/textures/foliage.ts`, part of `cargo xtask check`), on the CPU, the same every run, measures every card the plants wear, the foliage atlas's fifteen and the litter atlas's two ivies, from its atlas and normal map: its leaves, as regions of one tilt in the normal map, or of one shade on a picture; how long a leaf is in texels (a composed card's `leaf`), at least 90; texels for each pixel it covers at 2 m at 1080p and the game's 55 degrees, from how wide `tools/textures/foliage.json` says it is grown near, at least 1; relief, at least 30% of it tilted past 8 degrees; its leaves tilted 12 degrees from one another; a thickness map; brightness in a leaf spread 0.09 of itself, fine detail 0.015; leaf to leaf 0.08 in brightness and 2.5 degrees in hue; three quarters of its green a living leaf's. Each limit sits between the cards that read as leaves (the lush oaks' `oakbranch`, the Scots pine's `scots`) and made-up flat, mint and alike-tilted cards, tested. Failures already known are listed with why in `foliage.json`; the check fails a new one, and a known one fixed but still listed. **The picture:** `look.ts` measures over leaf pixels under the skyline, in 64-pixel squares at least two fifths leaf: the spread of their saturation (a leaf lit by sun and sky alone keeps its saturation however bright; real ones glint pale, glow yellower lit through, go bluer in shade), flat foliage the share of squares under 0.06, and the share of leaves half as bright again as their square's middle one; `cargo xtask looks` holds the three for each place (`style.json`, about a standard deviation of the reference's frames), the four references measured again for them from their own frames, their other numbers unchanged. Tried and dropped: brightness in small squares, and its range across a crown, which told nothing apart: flat leaves' dark gaps give them as much local contrast as real ones, and the references' films soften theirs.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15, after the oaks (D291), still seeing flat leaves on the forest's bushes and small trees and on the lake's pines: "Idk if theres some cheap deterministic validation that we could build that helps us fix that problem once and for all". Found on the files: the world's pine is a picture with a flat normal map, its card grown 2.6 m wide and magnified 1.3 times at 2 m; holly, birch, fern and herb are pictures with flat normal maps; the world's oaks grow theirs 2.9 m wide, leaves 40 cm long; no atlas has a thickness map; the lush hazel, bilberry, bramble and oak cards measure as the oak branch does (leaves 120 to 216 texels, relief 94 to 96%, tilt 30 to 33 degrees), so their bushes' flatness is in how they are drawn. On frames shot today, before D359 shaded the middle layer as the crowns: the bushes by the forest's path at 07:30 flat foliage 82% against Sons of the Forest's 18%, leaf colour spread 0.045 against 0.113; the forest's film 25% and 0.083, holding; the lake, shot before D366 gave it the lush forest's conifers, 34% against Far Cry 5's 12%, 0.076 against 0.136, leaves catching the light 3.6% against 12%; the town 52 to 100%, 0.035 to 0.062 against 0.107, 0 to 1.8% against 9.7%; the tundra's few leaves 0.100 against 0.145.
