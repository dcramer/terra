# D158: Names and generated lore

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Proposed in [naming.md](../naming.md): the books' names for the core systems (D125), and for everything ours plain English with no metaphor (the water metaphor was dropped on 2026-10-12 as odd), and a generated tongue for each realm's peoples, its sound picked by the realm's magic; places named twice, in their people's tongue and plainly in English; lore generated by one chain from the realm's magic to what is left in its land. The docs keep their working terms until it is agreed.

## Why

Asked for by the developers on 2026-10-12: "come up with a thematic pattern that you can continue to generate names, lore, etc. from (like a documented set of rules or something)", so nothing is named in the books' words (D125).
