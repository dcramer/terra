# D367: One ground under a column, its own once built on

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`Ground::height_cm` is the one answer to how high the ground is under a column, for what holds (`support`, `loss`, `ops`) and what is dressed (`layout::roles`): its sample, or where it has none the mean of those beside it, square on before diagonally, or the base's level. A column keeps a sample of its own from when it is first built on or planned (`Base::put`), as the ground stands then, so what holds a cube never moves with a sample laid beside it; only the ground beside a building, which only dressing asks of, takes its neighbours'. Amends D199.

## Why

Support read level ground under a column with no sample and dressing its neighbours' mean, two answers (research/building-code-health-2026-10-15.md, R5); taking the neighbours' alone failed the property test (seed 3), a plan laid beside unsampled cubes setting them floating. The lead: "Every column holding a cube keeps its own ground sample".
