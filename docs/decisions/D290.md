# D290: One way to draw every construction material, for hundreds of materials

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

As [construction-002.md](../proposals/construction-002.md) says. The goal is the developers': one way for every material, simple to add, hundreds of them, performant. The method is the agent's: every material, wall, floor or roof, is one kind of data drawn by one engine in four patterns (block, coursed, roof, frame), each owning corners, joins, openings, gables, ghosts, part-built, detail by distance and swaps. A material is a small file (its id, pattern, reference pack, cost, weight and overrides), a material instance merged over its pattern's file and its pack (`assets/data/kits/patterns/`, `assets/data/packs/`); pieces are shared per pattern and wear the material's bake, a material's own pieces an optional override; one command (`cargo xtask pack`) makes a pack from a CC0 scan, a Tripo reference or a generator, checks and credits it and registers the material. Each role either wears the bake on geometry the rules shape or places a reference piece whole where its outline is the look, chosen per pattern by visual merit (block: profiles worn, corner stones placed; coursed: members worn, the modelled log an override; roof: covering worn, ridges, verges and eaves placed; frame: members worn). Drawn from texture arrays with a layer per vertex, one draw per pattern per section whatever the number of materials, a texture budget a material, only the layers laid sections use resident on the GPU, and a swap where solidity does not change a lookup of layers. Amends D177 and D251 (rule-shaped geometry wearing a hand-made bake is allowed), D197, D200, D215 and D258 (their kit files become material files over patterns and packs). How long a change of material takes the player stays D232. No code is customised by material (the developers: "thats why its important that these kits are cleanly designed so each one doesnt have to be customized by the game"): a test fails on a material's or pack's name or a kind by number in `terra_build` or `terra_game`, and a settlement names its materials in data. Every placed piece keeps D303's contract: real size (`dims`) from its origin at its anchor, seated by the pattern's rule on a roof's deck or covering, `start` and `end` sockets, one model one size wherever used (the kit checker); a pattern's pieces are shared (`Trim`), a material's own override them (`Piece`).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "my goal is to have uniformity. one way we do these construction materials (or maybe one per type, floors roof, etc) … they need to be efficient when placing materials to quickly swap to the right visual"; "can each material be abstracted into like effectively data files or are we having to micro manage a bunch of complex things?"; "what im aiming for, again, is it being pretty straight forward.. and simple, to add new materials, that way we could have 100s of them. models are fine, generated is fine, whatever is going to make them visually and technically work the best. it also has to be performant". The evidence ([research/uniform-materials.md](../../research/uniform-materials.md)): the Tripo log's texture baked onto the cubes' surface read as logs from the street and as a striped box at a corner; the same bark baked onto the generated logs came most of the way to the modelled log and kept their ends and corners, lacking only its outline, stubs and girth; roof coverings already are surfaces and hold; a swap took 110 to 145 ms to the screen in every candidate, most of it laying the base again.
