# D376: Recording decisions without conflicts

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The log of decisions changed is its own file, `docs/decisions-log.md`, merged by union (`.gitattributes`), so lines agents append at once never conflict. A new decision's number is the next free one, printed by `cargo xtask decision` (the higher of here and origin/main, after a fetch); when two agents take one at once, the rebase stops on the decision's file both added (D389), and whoever pushes second renumbers theirs.

## Why

Every push conflicted on the log at the bottom of `docs/decisions.md`, forcing a rebase and a full check again, and D352 was taken twice (2026-10-15). Union merging is how changelogs are commonly kept. No allocator can stop a race between a fetch and a push without a server, while the check catches every collision before it lands, so the allocator only makes one rare.
