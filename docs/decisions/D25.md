# D25: Performance target

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Developers · Status: Decided

75 fps, comfortably and always: a frame within 13.3 ms at its 99th percentile at 1080p on the developers' GPU (an RTX 3080) at the high preset, with the frame rate a player's setting (vsync, or a cap). 60 fps at 1080p on a mid-range discrete GPU and a 4-core CPU, as a client of a server holding a thousand players (D52), with dozens of them in view. Every prototype reports frame times on the integrated GPU as a low-end check, measured in the real play loop and not only in scripted runs; there it holds 30 fps at the low preset (D239). Nothing ships on an untested performance assumption. The world must be able to show very many objects and let the player interact with any of them, and we look for ways to make that cheap everywhere.

## Why

Performance is a requirement. The developers, 2026-10-15: "performance matters. we will want to have controls over FPS output but generally speaking we would like to comfortably run this game at 75 FPS always."
