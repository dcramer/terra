# D516: How real the sky looks

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The sky is photographic and physically lit: clouds and the sky's light are physically based, as in Horizon Forbidden West, Red Dead Redemption 2 and Microsoft Flight Simulator, and the art style's light stylisation (D14) stays in the materials and the neutral grade, never in a painted or simplified sky. The other options were physically lit but simplified in shape (softer, fewer details, as Ghost of Tsushima's skies), and painted and stylised. See [sky.md](../sky.md#constraints).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "Photographic, physically lit". This settles the question in the recommendation's favour.

The developer, 2026-10-26: "in general to have it look 'real'" and "clouds would have diff shades". D14 asks for textured, lightly stylised realism; a sky has no texture to stylise, and its light lights everything else (D490).
