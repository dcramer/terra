# D516: How real the sky looks

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: clouds and the sky's light physically based and photographic, as in Horizon Forbidden West, Red Dead Redemption 2 and Microsoft Flight Simulator; the art style's light stylisation (D14) stays in the materials and the neutral grade, never in a painted or simplified sky. Options: photographic, as recommended; physically lit but simplified in shape (softer, fewer details, as Ghost of Tsushima's skies); or painted and stylised. See [sky.md](../sky.md#open-questions-for-the-developers), question 1.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-26: "in general to have it look 'real'" and "clouds would have diff shades" (input, not a decision). D14 asks for textured, lightly stylised realism; a sky has no texture to stylise, and its light lights everything else (D490), so how real it looks is the developers' call before stage 2 of sky.md is built.
