# D313: What grows where and when: species' niches and years, the world's climate

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A species carries, as data, its niche (the climate it tolerates: coldest winter, warmth to grow, wetness, shade, soil) and its year (when it leafs out, flowers, fruits, turns and drops its leaves, by warmth and chill counted and the day's length). The world decides what grows where by setting its climate fields against those niches; a biome is what emerges, named afterwards, and a world's theme says which families of species exist there at all. A place whose climate a kind does not tolerate does not regrow it (D150). Vintage Story places and colours its plants by climate so; Far Cry 5 lets species compete for ground (biomes.md). See [climate.md](../climate.md#what-grows-where-and-when-d313).

Left to the agent by the developers on 2026-10-14: "if we have to swap plants within the world, idk how that works. world has to somehow control what grows when were/maybe thats a function fo biome, idk actually, do whats best practice."
