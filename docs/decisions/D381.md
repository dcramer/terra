# D381: A settlement's time-lapse, built by the building system's own steps

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`--timelapse <file>[@frame]` with the town experiment lays the town's streets and plots and puts nothing up. From its frame each building goes up from its plan by the steps the hammer takes (`terra_build::construct`, whose candidates, `Base::next_steps`, the player's hammer shares): the lowest first, a log course across its wall, a stone a block at a time beside the last, a roof a layer and a stage at a time; paced by how much of it is built, eased. Its builders work at its sites while it rises (`ThingKind::Site`, `Life::set_open`, a `Build` activity tried before the rest while one is open). A house's hearth, household, yard and animals, fences, torch, doors and shutters come once it is up, the square's things as the settlement reaches them. The sun runs through the day while the people keep the day's own pace. What happens when is data (`assets/data/timelapse/*.ron`); the camera is a `--cine` shot (`tools/sandbox/timelapse/`), and the ground cover's floor map follows a camera high over the ground, a texel twice as wide and its middle ahead along the way it looks, so a bird's-eye view sees its ground painted. `--site settlement` is the town's place with only its plots cleared of the forest; its meadow a mix of taller drifts, greener and drier patches and shrubs toward the forest's edge, its plots trodden as they go up, its streets trodden out as it grows and its square laid when its market comes. The settlement's site is by water: of the town search's best places, the first whose drawn water (its lakes on the world's own 10 m grid, as `round::Round` finds them, and the sea) has a shore 95 to 450 m from its middle and none within its reach (`MarketTown::water_m`, `Land::lake_beside`); its start away from the lake, and its forest cleared down to the shore. A clearing's edge grows back young (`Clearing::grown`): its trees stand at under half the forest's height where it begins, full grown 30 m into the forest. A cover may sow its grass (`CoverSource::sows`): the ground then takes the cover's grass outright, over the land's bare patches, where otherwise it takes the lower of the land's and the cover's; only a settlement rising's meadow sows. Its meadow is where its clearings keep less than 0.6 of the forest (`MEADOW_KEPT`), the forest round it the world's own cover, its needles, litter and moss; the clearing is mown (`flora::MowAround`), its trees, bushes, logs and stones taken away and its flowers, herbs and ferns left, and the flora grows round the settlement as round the start. A plot is pegged out as its builders are called, its stacked logs and tools beside it; a plot and the square are trodden over three of the film's seconds, the treading first and the grass going after it (`treading`), so no frame jumps from meadow to earth. A building a storey tall and more is scaffolded once its walls pass a builder's reach (`play::scaffold`): standards, ledgers, boards and a guard rail round it, a lift for each its courses reach, struck a lift at a time when it is done, a lift every 1.2 of the film's seconds; two in three of its builders at work stand on its top lift's boards facing its wall (`scaffold::Decks`), the rest at the logs. A lowland lake, as the settlement's on the valley floor, is drawn as one: olive-brown water stained by its woods, dark a metre or two down, over a silt bed, its shore wet silt and sand with its meadow down to it rather than a mountain lake's clear green over a band of scoured granite (`lakes::LOWLAND_*`, ground.wesl `lowland_at`, from 250 to 500 m over the sea, as its shore stones), the shingle the land lays round every lake giving out a few metres over a lowland one's water, and rank grass and sedge thick and tall along its waterline in clumps where the meadow meets it. The film's last take cranes down from over the town's south-west corner onto the middle of what is built. Seen from high, the ground's map is painted some 200 m ahead of the camera. The camera is three takes cut together (`tools/sandbox/timelapse/settlement.json`), each 18 to 34 m up over the settlement as it grows. The film is 20 s, the town built from its claim stone to its full square within it, in an early morning as the forest place's (7.2 to 8.0 h: low warm sun, mist and shafts). The woods beside it are of many ages, in cohorts some 45 m across from poles to veterans, saplings among them, with glades where they stand thin, and below 650 m oak mixes with the pines and spruces on the sunnier ground (`terra_land::woods`, drawn by the lush forest's oaks), as the developers asked ("too regular, we need more diversity in sizes/types of foliage"). A dog at heel stays sat or stood until its owner moves off, not flicking between them frame to frame.

## Why

The developers asked for a film "where it has like a settlement built by us (there is a whole building system) where you have a bird eyes view thing where it shows you constructing your settlement". City-builders and survival builders (Manor Lords, Foundation, Valheim, Kingdom Come's Pribyslavitz) show a settlement growing from plots and frames, never finished houses faded in; and the bench and QA can use a scripted time-lapse of construction, as D71 asks of a tool.
