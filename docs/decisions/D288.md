# D288: Foliage shaded only where it is in front

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Leaves, grass, litter and the plants that sway, all cut by their pictures' alpha, write no depth in the main pass after the depth prepass has written it (`grown::test_depth_early`): a fragment shader that discards keeps the GPU from testing depth before it runs unless the pass leaves depth alone, so every card behind the front ones was shaded and thrown away. The depth test is unchanged, so what is drawn is the same. Unreal's masked materials after its early depth pass do the same.

## Why

Foliage was most of the integrated GPU's frame at the low preset (D239): hiding the grown trees' leaves took the world from 95 to 59 ms. With this, on the Radeon at low, the world's main pass went from 60 to 39 to 42 ms and its frame from 95 to 71 to 77, the lush forest's main pass from 108 to 55 to 60 and its frame from 162 to 105 to 117; pictures from the same place match.
