# D335: Woods and snow by the land's rules, carried on the ground

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

On a world with mountains the land round the start is worked out ahead (`terra_land::round::Round`, 16 km across): its lakes on a 10 m grid, the growing season over its latitudes, heights and leanings (every 0.1° and 25 m), and the snow lying on the clock's date from the folded past (every 0.25° and 50 m), each blended between its points. Trees stand by `terra_land::woods::stand`: closed forest where the growing season is a degree warmer than the tree line's 6.4 °C, thinning and shrinking to krummholz half a degree colder than it, none past 45° or on rock or water, a few on scree and gravel; Norway spruce on shaded slopes, Scots pine on sunny ones. Within 600 m of the start each is a grown conifer (the lush forest's, D366), out to 4.5 km a far billboard in the far forest's shader (D273), merged in 250 m squares, past that the ground's own dark canopy tint. The ground carries, per vertex, its loose stones, snow depth, bare rock or woods and its height over the nearest lake (terra_world `terrain::lying`), and its shader draws scree, gravel, snow on what faces up, the canopy far off and a wet band at a lake's edge.

- **Scree and benches by their slope (mountains round 30):** scree holds trees by its slope, an open forest (0.7) on an apron's settled toe under 16 degrees, thinning to a few (0.12) at the 33 degrees of its angle of repose; bare rock's benches under 22 to 36 degrees hold a few (0.12). At one share however steep, scree cut the forest along a massif's foot: the lake's transect fell from 1.0 to 0.15 at the apron's edge, 450 m under the tree line's warmth (`woods::stand`).
- **Ragged at a steep foot (mountains round 29):** a place's microsite also shifts the steepness its trees hold on, 6 degrees for each degree warmer (`woods::stand_in`), so where a wall's foot steepens the forest climbs the lee and thins over the ribs, not at one slope. Its effect did not show in the round 29 stills.

## Why

The developers want forested slopes and high peaks (docs/demos.md §1); D313 has species' niches decide what grows. A tree line drawn from the growing season is a band that rises and falls with facing, never a contour. Working seasons and snow ahead keeps tens of thousands of trees and every vertex to a lookup: a season is a year of days, a fold most of a year.
