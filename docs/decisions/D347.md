# D347: What people do, made, not taken

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D316. The clips a town's people play at their work and talk are made with Kimodo as the locomotion is (`art/animation/made.json`): a still clip looped between its most alike frames, as long as its entry's `loop` allows, a working one (`busy`) in its busiest stretch, each played at the take's own pace; each measured (`gait_check.py`, `clip_check.py`) and looked at on the base body, and kept only if it reads as the work. Made so far: `Talk_Loop`, standing in conversation, for talk and selling; `Hammer_Loop`, bent over work at waist height striking with the right hand, for the smith; `Lean_Loop`, leaning back with the arms folded, for standing at a doorway. Kimodo follows short plain prompts in the style of its own examples, not long ones naming each limb.

## Why

The adversarial review of the market town: "People don't do anything that shows: pairs face each other in a fighting stance (the talk or idle clip on everyone); nobody carries, sells, hammers, sits or leans; stalls have no seller." Quaternius's Idle_Talking_Loop holds the head 15 degrees down and reads as a fighter's crouch on our bodies, as its idle did (D316).
