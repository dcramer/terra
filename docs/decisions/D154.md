# D154: How placing walls, floors and roofs works

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The Sims' way with our materials: the player draws the shape (walls at any length and height, floors, storeys, openings, a roof's style and pitch over what is enclosed); the game makes the details (how each wall's logs or stones are laid, corners, trims, the roof's rafters and ridge). More shapes, and placing details by hand, come with progress (amended by D226: building by hand, roofs too, from the start). Chosen over Enshrouded's way (any shape, every detail by hand: free, but overwhelming) and Subnautica's (fixed modules: easy, but not unique).

## Why

Asked by the developers on 2026-10-12: "like enshrouded style, or subnautica style. i do like enshroudeds flexibility to design something truly interesting/unique/beautiful but i will admit its also a little overwhelming". The developers find The Sims "not bad" but asked what was proposed; written out as building a log cabin, which they agreed: "oh yeah i like what you're proposing… it seems good", wondering how hard it is technically with many variants of walls and the rest.
