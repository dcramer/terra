# D488: A room shut off from the sky is seen as by night, with a dim neutral fill

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Where the air round the eye takes under a tenth or so of the sky's light (`IndoorSky`, D485, D486; `terra_world::indoor::shut`, wholly at 0.05 and not at all from 0.35, so a house's room at 0.6 is left alone), three things follow, eased with it: the eye's key is the room's own, EV 10.8, whatever the hour (`indoor::key`, in `daylight::open_exposure`), with the eye adaptation's indoor range on top (D240); the fires burn as by night (`indoor::fires_night`, in the fire lights and their glows), nothing outshining them; and a dim fill lights everything in view, 16 cd/m² of a warm near-grey (`indoor::FILL`, a camera `AmbientLight`, taken away outside), the fires' light bounced round the stone, which the room probes do not carry. The smoke over a fire takes the sky's light only as far as it reaches the air round the camera (`fire::smoke::sky`).

## Why

Round 8's three dungeon films were judged to have a red-orange haze over every interior, long near-black stretches and grey searchlight cones over the goal's fire bowls. Measured, the haze was not in the air: with the fire lights out of the volumetric fog, the fog off, bloom off, the distance fog's colour at 0, or the smoke off, the keep's goal still measured R/G 4.5 and R/B 11.5. It was the torch light itself under the day's exposure: a dungeon at noon was exposed for sunlit ground (EV 11.8) with its fires at their day light, so all that read was the torches' saturated orange (`HEARTH_LIGHT`, linear R/G 4.7) on the walls nearest them and black between. The cones were the smoke lit by the whole noon sky underground.

Shipped games light interiors so: Bethesda's interior cells have no sky and an ambient of their own; Unreal's take a low fill and set the eye by the eye adaptation's range, not the sky's exposure. A fill and a fixed indoor key were both open; both are needed, the key so noon and midnight read alike underground, the fill so no stretch is black (an exposure floor alone lifts nothing where no light falls).

## Measured

At EV 9 and a fill of 8 the rooms read as lit orange boxes (keep goal 0.240); 10.8 and 16 keep them dim. The crypt's corridor and `indoorlight.ts`'s views, at the foot of the entrance stair where the sky reaches down it (D485), are unchanged (corridor 0.095/0.153 noon/night before, 0.098/0.149 after; room 0.029/0.035), and its forest view too (0.314/0.140, 0.315/0.143). Still red near the torches: their light is the campfire's (`HEARTH_LIGHT`), and the grade stays neutral (D370).
