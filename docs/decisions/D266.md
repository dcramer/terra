# D266: The haze under trees

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The volumetric haze (D217, D244) takes its sky light at 0.015, a third of before, and the green-grey of light come through leaves rather than the open sky's blue; its own colour is a warm off-white. Under a canopy the sun lights it only in shafts, so that blue fill was most of what the haze showed, a grey-blue veil over everything a few tens of metres off, worst on the dark spruces.

## Why

Found by the trees' work (D264): the veil over the spruces went away with the haze off. The shafts at 17:00 hold.
