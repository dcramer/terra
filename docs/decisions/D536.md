# D536: A creature that wakes: a Wake shape key for its mouth, an emissive map for its eyes, lit by two staged calls

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A creature whose face changes when it wakes (the Cetri, D534) carries the change in its model file, as games do a face: the mouth is a blend shape and the glow an emissive map, and the game eases them by name.

- **The mouth** (`tools/characters/wake.py`, after the rig's conversion and before the export): a ragged horizontal cut through the bark, closed at rest so it reads as a frost crack; a dark mouth bag behind it so an open mouth shows no inside of the trunk; splinter teeth on both lips, tucked inside the trunk until the lips part; a `Wake` shape key that parts them. The face is found on the body, not given: the eyes are the darkest painted pair on the front of the upper trunk, and the mouth sits below them by their spacing.
- **The eyes**: an emissive map, amber where the eye holes are painted, black elsewhere. It ships dark (`export_character.py` exports an emissive-mapped material's factor as 0).
- **The game**: `staged.morph` eases named morph targets to weights and `staged.glow` eases the emissive parts' strength (`terra_creatures::staged::wake`); a mind that wakes the creature later makes the same two changes.

## Why

Cetri round 2-3 (2026-10-26). The reviewers of its concept: asleep it must pass for a tree, so no visible face; awake, its eyes light and its trunk splits into a mouth. A jaw bone would need a skeleton of its own (the Cetri shares the troll's clips on our biped skeleton); a blend shape and an emissive map need no bone, survive our export (glTF morph targets, an emissive texture) and are what Unreal and Unity do for a face's expressions and a glowing eye.
