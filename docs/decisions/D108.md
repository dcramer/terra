# D108: One system for everything built

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Everything built in a world, the player's and the people's alike, is made of the same parts and components: a village's houses are put up from the build catalogue by the same operations, and props and stations such as a well are buildable things too. There are no prefab buildings: a smithy is a building put up from parts with a forge and anvil placed in it. Only stations and props are single models. Not everything seen need be buildable at first; some may be learned or unlocked (D68), how being open.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11: "everything in the world should be fundamentally built on the same components", for the tech demo as well. See [building.md](../building.md#one-system-for-everything-built).
