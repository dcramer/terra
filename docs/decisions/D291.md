# D291: Leaves true to life, not flat

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Amends D14 for foliage. A forest's leaves are drawn as real leaves seen in a real wood, as Sons of the Forest's are: each its own shape, veins, relief and tilt, catching the light and glinting one by one, in the varied greens of real leaves, and lit through from behind; not flat fills of one tone per leaf, which the rest of the guide's "a little shaded, a little simplified" had let them become. The rest of the world keeps D14.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "i think the tree leaves still have this flat color style that we need to fix. these come from the old design system we wanted, but let's make it much better, not this flat style."
