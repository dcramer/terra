# D373: Shade laid on a building's colours as light, and a log's underside shaded once

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D262 and D320. A building's colours are as on screen (sRGB) and made linear to be drawn, so a share of the light laid on them, an occlusion's or a shade's, is laid on as that share to the 1/2.2 (`terra_build::light_share`): the underside's and the sheltered top's shade (D320) and a log's groove (D262) take the share of the light they say, not its 2.2th power (a third taken nine tenths). A wall log's underside is shaded by every kit's underside rule (D320) alone, its groove shading only under the log above, as the two stacked with the screen's occlusion. Each log's shade by its seed lies either side of the kit's tint, not only under it (D262's `variation.shade`).

## Why

The lead on 2026-10-15: log walls going near-black in shade; "shaded logs read as dark wood with visible grain, as in real photos, not black". A log wall's shaded face measured 0.21 of a matte 18% grey in the same shade on the yard; after, 0.35. In the village a gable in the deep shade under its verge, where the 18% grey reads sRGB 30,40,56, went from about 0.2 of it to 0.33 (17,17,19). Ruled out: the room's light probe reaching through the wall (no change with every probe removed), the screen's occlusion (3%), the tint (D262, lighter). The bark texture's occlusion also stacks a height-made occlusion and the bark's lip on the scans' own (`textures.ts blend`, 0.71 against the scans' 0.82), left for the texture's next making.
