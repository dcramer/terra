# D263: Thick timber walls laid as skins along their faces

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A cube of a timber kind whose run across a wall is two or three cubes, each cube of it with its kind either side along the wall, lays its courses along the wall only (`Walls::runs`): a thick wall is skins of logs side by side, one a cube of its thickness, crossing only at its ends and where it turns or another meets it, as a corner does; a pier crosses as a crib. The kit sheet has a row of them, *thick walls and piers*, and a test that no log crosses a thick wall but at its ends.

## Why

The goals review (research/goals-review-2026-10-13.md, finding 6) found thick log walls and piers drawing their faces as grids of sawn ends, the stack of boxes D211 rules out; a log wall shows logs along every face, as Enshrouded's rough wood runs its poles along every face whatever the mass behind it.
