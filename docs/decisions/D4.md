# D4: World paradigm

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Many realms (D17, D87), each a round world, seamless to walk with no loading screens within a world. No space: no solar system and no flight to orbit.

## Why

Changed by the developers on 2026-10-10: "no space". Was: real round planets in a real solar system, seamless from surface to space, after Space Engineers and No Man's Sky. Confirmed the same day: each world is still a round planet.
