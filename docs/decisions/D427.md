# D427: A hero prop's triangle budget

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A prop the player sits beside or works at for minutes, close and large on screen, such as a campfire, is a hero prop: it may have 6,000 to 10,000 triangles, its parts' (a fire's coal bed) included, as shipped games give a hero prop several times a plain prop's. Its fine detail still goes in its maps (art-style.md's rule 1); the triangles buy round silhouettes and smooth curves at a metre: round split firewood with bark and end grain, stones rounded rather than faceted. It is placed by the few, not the thousand, so its cost is a few draws, not a forest's. Far tiers, when a hero prop has them, fall to a plain prop's range (D148).

Why: Tripo's low-poly campfire at 1,500 triangles read as "faceted black prisms with scaly end caps" in the night review (fire round 17, 4/10), and the coordinator's brief for round 18 asked for "a hero campfire model ... about 6-10k, as AAA hero props are". The campfire is made procedurally at about 7,000 (tools/props/hearth.ts).

Reviewed (fire round 19): blind, alone against main's Tripo campfire, 5/10 against 4/10. Its stones' vertex normals had been turned in, so they drew black under any light; hearth.test.ts now holds them out.
