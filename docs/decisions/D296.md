# D296: How big materials are carried and set

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

D140 says logs and other big materials are carried to the plan; how is not chosen, so nothing of it is built. Recommended, after The Forest and Sons of the Forest ([building.md](../building.md#large-materials-and-carrying)): logs first, cut stone the same way later; a felled tree's logs lie where it falls, its branches Wood in the pack; one log on the shoulder at a walk or two slower, nothing else in hand; use at a plan sets the log as its wall's next log, a log wall taking logs, not Wood; logs dropped within a few metres of a plan count for it, a log pile later (D141, Leaning); a sawbench cuts logs into beams and planks.

## Why

For the developers: which materials are big, how many are carried, and how one goes in (D139 left it open) are design. Decided, it can be built on what D295 built: a dropped log counts as storage does.
