# D296: How big materials are carried and set

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

Big materials are carried on the shoulder or in the hands, with no weapon out, in ways that vary by material and its variants, not one way for all; they are piled up at the build, perhaps on a resource pile, and built in with the hammer or whatever other tool the material takes (D491). The agent's earlier proposal, for the details, after The Forest and Sons of the Forest ([building.md](../building.md#large-materials-and-carrying)): logs first, cut stone the same way later; a felled tree's logs lie where it falls, its branches Wood in the pack; one log on the shoulder at a walk or two slower, nothing else in hand; use at a plan sets the log as its wall's next log, a log wall taking logs, not Wood; logs dropped within a few metres of a plan count for it, a log pile later (D141, Leaning); a sawbench cuts logs into beams and planks.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25: "carrying big materials will likely be on shoulder or in hands with no weapon out and this will just vary... it wont be a one size fits all, and i think the idea is you'd pile them up (maybe theres a resource pile or something im not sure) and then use hammer or whatever other tool is available to construct? again, procedural etc this may have variants". Leaning: their "likely" and "not sure".

For the developers: which materials are big, how many are carried, and how one goes in (D139 left it open) are design. Decided, it can be built on what D295 built: a dropped log counts as storage does.
