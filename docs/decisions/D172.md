# D172: Threats, and an organisation across realms

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

No single long threat: each realm's threats vary, from its conditions, monsters and events. Across realms, a dangerous organisation causes problems throughout your journey, perhaps behind some bosses. Proposed: essence users who force power by waking pools and feeding surges, a cell per realm generated with its leader, a few lieutenants who recur; a faction like any other (D95), not a fixed story. All are generated from a set of parts (what drives them, what they do, how far they reach, how big, who leads), and there may be many. **The Opened** are the worked example the parts are built from, after the books' cults that serve something beyond the world: they serve a being of the astral and feed surges to thin the walls between realms.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "normal world conditions, could be also monsters, could be other world events, but I think this could vary realm by realm? There should also be sort of anti-heroes or whatever that are a dangerous organization that causes problems throughout your journey (including maybe influencing bosses etc? idk)". Replaces premise B's spreading threat. The Opened chosen, and more than one allowed, on 2026-10-12: "ok A is fine. We may have more than one btw (procedural!!!!)"; then "we'd probably generate them all from a set of parts, but this is an example we'd build from". See [lore.md](../lore.md#threats).
