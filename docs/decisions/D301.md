# D301: The lush forest a tall conifer stand, looming

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The lush forest is a tall, open conifer stand as Sons of the Forest's: Scots pines and spruces rising high on long clear trunks, their crowns far overhead, so a player walks among trunks and looks up into them; oaks stand in it as the exception, not as a closed low canopy. The floor under it gets more light, and grass with it (D246, D293).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14, asked whether the forest should be a SotF-like tall conifer stand with oaks as the exception or stay an oak wood: "i think it should be like it, as in its pretty large looming thing, i do enjoy that pretty much." Research: [2026-10-14-sotf-forest-look.md](../../research/2026-10-14-sotf-forest-look.md), recommendation 5.
