# D467: A demo is an experiment that stands for others, its keys moving the world's own clock

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A playable demo for the developers is a sandbox experiment that stands for other experiments and options (`STANDS_FOR` in `terra_sandbox`'s experiments, given before the run's own so the run's win), plus a small plugin of its own keys, never a new command-line option (D249). The first is `--experiment forest-walk`: play and the lush forest on the yard at the forest film's path, high summer at noon, with keys for the season (F6), the time of day (F7, J, L), the weather (F8), a help panel (O) and feedback (P).

- **The keys move the clock, nothing else.** Season is a date (07-20 or 01-15, research/2026-10-15-seasons.md), time of day an hour from the sun's height on that date, and a weather the next day within a month that has it at this hour, as `--weather` finds one. Everything drawn follows from the climate at that moment, so a moment is reproduced by `--date <day> --time <hour>` alone.
- **Feedback** is the screen and a JSON note, numbered, in `~/.cache/terra-review/feedback/` (`TERRA_FEEDBACK_DIR` for a check): the moment, the player's and camera's place in `--at` and `--vantage` terms, the weather at the eye, the snow lying and the animals within 50 m, with the command that stands there again.
- **Checked** by `node tools/sandbox/forest-walk.ts`, one scripted run held to what it says; `--film` makes the demo's video.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-25: "take the forest one, and give me a demo where i can walk around, which has some animals i can stumble on, and can give you feedback and easily turn from winter/summer and from time of day and from weather." Moving the clock rather than forcing a weather keeps the demo the climate's own (snow lying where January's past left it, never snowfall in July), and makes every note reproducible with options that exist.
