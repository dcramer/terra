# D273: The forest carried to the horizon

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D224 and D244. As shipped open worlds draw what lies past the trees they really draw (research/far-forest.md): **far trees as billboards**, 48,000 past the lush forest's meadow out to 1 km, placed by its own grove and glade rules, a card each turned to the eye about its upright, its oak, pine or spruce and a shrub belt at its foot drawn and rounded by the shader (`far_forest.wesl`), coloured from the near crowns' leaf pictures and lit by Bevy's lighting, merged 160 m a square into 144 draws, casting no shadow, gone within 25 to 45 m of the eye; **far land in the sky** (`Sky::far_land`, a world's or a site's choice: the lush forest's rolling hills, and past a mountain lake (`--site lake`) a range's peaks, `FarLand::peaks`, 5° at the farthest), ridges below the horizon one behind another between the planet's horizon and the air's, each paler in the air's own colour just over the horizon, read from a 128-pixel cube of Bevy's atmosphere worked out each frame (`air::AirPicture`); **the forest's haze by how open the sky is over the eye**, from the sky occlusion volume (`OpenOverhead`): D224's 240 m of green air under the canopy, 2.5 km in the horizon's colour out of it; **the mist on the ground**: D244's box stands on the ground under the eye. The lush world's ground keeps D242's swells: on the 2 km planet ground past 420 m is under the overlook's horizon, so hills there would need to stand a hundred metres to show. For an eye risen over the mist, 6 to 28 m over the ground and higher, the volumetric haze is drawn up to 60% thinner (`daylight::above_mist`): it stands for the air between the trees seen from among them, and from a bird's-eye camera every slant ray crossed some 20 m of mist glowing with the open sky's light, a flat yellow veil over the ground. After sunset the haze before the far land is the earth's shadow's, 0.6 as bright as the horizon's band behind it and turned 0.45 toward the zenith's blue, so a range at dusk stands darker and cooler than the sky (lake film r7: its peaks as pale and warm as the sky, 139 against 139; r8 107 against 126).

## Why

Review #3 of the lush forest: "The horizon is a dark slab ... the forest still reads as an island with nothing beyond it." Measured: on the 2 km stand-in the ground falls 2.4° under the horizontal at eye height and 12° at the overlook, the Earth-sized air's own horizon 0.6° and 1.1°, and between them Bevy's atmosphere drew its ground; the mist, rising with the eye, hung at the overlook's height and darkened it further. From the overlook at 13:00, rows' grey over the middle of the view: above the horizon 0.42 and under it 0.24 before, 0.67 and 0.62 after, the ridges stepping to 0.52 at the forest's edge. lodcheck passes on floor, path, clearing and edge (worst 0.19%, the middle layer's shrubs). The walk bench was contended by other sessions' sandboxes on the RTX 3080 (load up to 35); on the quietest runs, beside main's made the same hour, GPU 12.1 ms against 12.7, triangles 13.60 million against 13.54.
