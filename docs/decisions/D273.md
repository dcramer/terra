# D273: The forest carried to the horizon

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D224 and D244. As shipped open worlds draw what lies past the trees they really draw (research/far-forest.md): **far trees as billboards**, 48,000 past the lush forest's meadow out to 1 km, placed by its own grove and glade rules, a card each turned to the eye about its upright, its oak, pine or spruce and a shrub belt at its foot drawn and rounded by the shader (`far_forest.wesl`), coloured from the near crowns' leaf pictures and lit by Bevy's lighting, merged 160 m a square into 144 draws, casting no shadow, gone within 25 to 45 m of the eye; **far land in the sky** (`Sky::far_land`, a world's or a site's choice: the lush forest's rolling hills, and past a mountain lake (`--site lake`) a range's peaks, `FarLand::peaks`, 5° at the farthest), ridges below the horizon one behind another between the planet's horizon and the air's, each paler in the air's own colour just over the horizon, read from a 128-pixel cube of Bevy's atmosphere worked out each frame (`air::AirPicture`); **the forest's haze by how open the sky is over the eye**, from the sky occlusion volume (`OpenOverhead`): D224's 240 m of green air under the canopy, 2.5 km in the horizon's colour out of it; **the mist on the ground**: D244's box stands on the ground under the eye. The lush world's ground keeps D242's swells: on the 2 km planet ground past 420 m is under the overlook's horizon, so hills there would need to stand a hundred metres to show. For an eye risen over the mist, 6 to 28 m over the ground and higher, the volumetric haze is drawn up to 60% thinner (`daylight::above_mist`): it stands for the air between the trees seen from among them, and from a bird's-eye camera every slant ray crossed some 20 m of mist glowing with the open sky's light, a flat yellow veil over the ground. After sunset the haze before the far land is the earth's shadow's, 0.6 as bright as the horizon's band behind it and turned 0.45 toward the zenith's blue, so a range at dusk stands darker and cooler than the sky (lake film r7: its peaks as pale and warm as the sky, 139 against 139; r8 107 against 126). The far land's shape is one module (`far_land.wesl`) that the sky draws and the volumetric clouds stop at: the sky writes no depth, so the clouds' march stops at the planet's horizon where a ridge covers the ray, and no cloud behind a range is laid over it (mountains round 3: ghost ranges with clouds seen through them). By day a range's ridges keep no more of their own colour than the ground's aerial perspective leaves at their distance, the nearest at the planet's horizon's and each farther one 0.6 of it again (about 0.37, 0.21, 0.12 and 0.04 from the lake's shore); the nearest stood near black behind the lake's far shore, a void band (mountains round 3). By day that haze is the air's own blue scaled to the horizon band's brightness (ground.wesl's aerial air), the nearest ridge bluest and each farther one nearer the band's colour, and the far caps are dimmed to 0.56 as the ground's far snow is: faded to the band's pale colour with its snow lit whole, the range stood as a mint band brighter than the sky over it (mountains round 5; the nearest ridge 159,171,164 to 124,147,184 under a sky of 153,182,209). By day each ridge's foot, its lowest 0.45 over the nearer ridges' tops (the nearest's over the planet's horizon), fades 0.7 of the way into its own haze, so each stands crisp at its crest and dims down into the air before the next, as RDR2's ranges do layer by layer (mountains round 8; faded into the distance fog from the planet's horizon alone, as before, the foot lay under the world's ground and the nearer ridges in every view and never showed). A ridge's shoulders, where no massif rises, are broken by knolls and notches (`shoulder` takes 0.12 of the fine crests and 0.05 of the jag): the nearest ridge's top ran level for tens of degrees, a flat band under the range.

## Why

Review #3 of the lush forest: "The horizon is a dark slab ... the forest still reads as an island with nothing beyond it." Measured: on the 2 km stand-in the ground falls 2.4° under the horizontal at eye height and 12° at the overlook, the Earth-sized air's own horizon 0.6° and 1.1°, and between them Bevy's atmosphere drew its ground; the mist, rising with the eye, hung at the overlook's height and darkened it further. From the overlook at 13:00, rows' grey over the middle of the view: above the horizon 0.42 and under it 0.24 before, 0.67 and 0.62 after, the ridges stepping to 0.52 at the forest's edge. lodcheck passes on floor, path, clearing and edge (worst 0.19%, the middle layer's shrubs). The walk bench was contended by other sessions' sandboxes on the RTX 3080 (load up to 35); on the quietest runs, beside main's made the same hour, GPU 12.1 ms against 12.7, triangles 13.60 million against 13.54.

Mountains round 8 (the ridge foot and shoulders, against main 26c7135e3 by sha): four fresh judges a build on the five mountains-still views put it at 3.53 against main's 3.49 (no difference shown, p 0.80; lake range 3.30 against 3.27); the lake and lake-range looks tables hold the same rows (7 of 18, 2 of 2; lake-range far contrast 0.244 against 0.248, far under sky 0.079 against 0.080). The judges still name the far range a pale cut-out over a flat blue band in both: the band is not yet solved.
