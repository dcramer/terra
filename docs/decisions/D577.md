# D577: Tracy is the profiler for the detail

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A `tracy` feature on `terra_sandbox` (Bevy's `trace_tracy`, without `trace_tracy_memory`) makes a profiling build; the launcher's `Profiling: tracy` builds it and opens the Tracy profiler connected to the game, and the planned `profile` task's `--tracy` will keep a capture of a capped length (`tracy-capture -s`). The pinned Bevy 0.20 (`tracy-client` 0.19) speaks Tracy 0.14.1, its latest release (2026-08-22); the task fetches that release's tools into `~/.cache/terra/tools/` and the doctor checks the version, which moves with Bevy. Under WSL the game and profiler both run in Windows and meet on its localhost. Tracy is BSD 3-clause and its Rust client MIT or Apache 2.0 (D196); its client goes only into profiling builds. Times from a Tracy build are never a change's measure: the benchmark is (D185). [launcher.md](../launcher.md#live-tracy-d577).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-29, asked for live profiling with Tracy. Bevy supports it and its profiling guide recommends it; it shows every span on a timeline live, which a log line or report cannot, and its captures are compressed and capped where a Chrome trace of four thousand frames ran to 17 GB (perf round 1).
