# D285: A third-person camera that keeps calm among trunks

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D274 and D267. As shipped cameras keep collision apart from occlusion (Cinemachine's Deoccluder, Unreal's spring arm on a camera channel trees ignore; research/foliage-near-camera.md): **the boom** (`terra_game` `play/camera.rs`) is swept only against build walls, the ground and what is broad, 2.5 m across or more and not a tree (`Solid::breadth`): a boulder; trees and small things crossing the line to the figure do not pull it in, they hide the figure for a moment. **The lens** is a 0.3 m sphere that never goes into any solid: it moves from where it was to the boom's end by collide-and-slide, round a trunk in its way, comes back to the end at a rate of 2 a second once past, and slid aside, turns to keep the figure where it was in the picture. A trunk that hides the figure's chest and head from the boom's end for 0.6 s has the boom ease in front of it (a rate of 2.5 a second, no shorter than 2 m) until the figure has been in sight from the end for 0.5 s. The shoulder swap is gone; the boom stays over the right shoulder. **A tree's own solid**, what the lens keeps out of, is its bole, its wood taller than a body; its root flare is added as extra pieces, as a skirt is (D248), so it still stops a walker as before.

## Why

A review of the forest's films (6.0/10), its first gap: the boom pumped, the player's skull filling the frame for 0.2 s and springing back, about eight times in twenty seconds among veteran oaks, the player once out of the frame. Every trunk crossing the line from shoulder to lens pulled the boom in at once, and a tree's solid stood round the lens as wide as its roots spread. On both films (2400 frames each, the camera's place every frame) against main run the same way: punch-ins (the boom 30% shorter within 0.3 s, the look-up apart) 10 and 12, now none; the chest out of the picture in 255 and 194 frames, now none; framing jumps 17 and 12, now none; the nearest the lens comes to the boom's head 0.55 m, now 1.89 m (in the look-up) and 3.69 m; the lens never inside a fitted solid nor over a tree's wood as drawn at its height; the player hidden behind a trunk at most 1.05 s. `collide` reads the same on trees as main (its one failure, a log's low end, is main's too). The walk bench beside main run alternately on the RTX 3080: median 15.0 ms on both, 99th percentile 17.9 ms on both, the game's CPU frame 4.3 to 4.5 ms against 4.35; both over the 16.7 ms budget at the 99th percentile, as main already was.
