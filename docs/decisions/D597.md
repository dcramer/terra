# D597: Trees sway as a hierarchy: trunk, each branch about its own root with its own phase, leaf clusters on their stalks

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Open

Open: whether to replace the one bend every vertex of a plant shares (D255, D282) with wind in levels, as SpeedTree, Crysis and Unreal's Pivot Painter move theirs. Recommended:

- **The trunk** bends from its foot by the height squared, as now, but rotated about the foot and put back at its length (Crysis's main bending), not slid downwind, and swaying across the wind a little as well as along it.
- **Each limb and branch** turns about its own root on its parent, by the gusts at its root, with its own phase from a hash of the branch (SpeedTree's Independence, about 2 to 5) and a stiffness falling with its thickness, so a limb lags, overshoots and settles behind the trunk, two levels deep in games as SpeedTree's are.
- **Each leaf card** flutters about its stalk with its own phase (Crysis's per-leaf phase), its edges flapping along its normal, a few centimetres at a gust, not the whole crown shaking (D282's jelly).
- The generator, which knows every branch, writes each vertex's pivot, axis, level and phase as vertex attributes (Pivot Painter 2 bakes the same into textures because its meshes come from a modelling package); the main, depth and shadow passes run the same function, so what is drawn, shaded and casts shadow stay together, as `foliage.wesl` does now.
- Held to a still-camera film of crowns measured against Enshrouded's (the note's motion map): trunks still, crowns moving at a spray's scale, neighbouring patches of a crown less alike than one sliding block.

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-02: "branches move as one unit". In `foliage.wesl` every vertex moves by the same translation downwind scaled by its height squared, its phase from its place in the world at wavelengths of 27 to 90 m, so a whole crown slides as one block, and its flutter is 1.5 cm. Measured on a still camera under the pines on a clear day: 85% of the crowns' motion is one shared shift, neighbouring patches correlate at 0.91; Enshrouded's Springlands, 11 to 24% and 0.36 to 0.43, trunks still. Every shipped system read moves each branch about its own pivot with its own phase. Costs only vertex work, in the main and every shadow pass; the frame time to be measured on the walk bench before it lands.
