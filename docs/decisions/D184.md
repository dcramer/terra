# D184: How far we change Bevy

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Extend first: our own systems, components, material extensions and render steps beside Bevy's. Replace one of Bevy's plugins or shaders when a measurement shows its version won't do, as our skinning shader replaces Bevy's. Fork a Bevy crate only as a last resort, through Cargo's patch, with the change kept small and written here so it can be carried to each release or offered upstream. Carried now, in `patches/` with how to carry them: `bevy_mesh`, its vertex buffers packed before the upload (D435); `bevy_pbr`, a reflection probe smaller than every irradiance volume holding a spot giving its diffuse light (D485), and its per-material change checks on one thread (D537). Techniques Bevy lacks are ported from an engine that does them well (D196).

## Why

The developers asked whether we are locked to Bevy's limits (2026-10-12): we are not, but the deeper the change, the more each Bevy release costs us (0.20 moved every shader to WESL). Bevy's character limits are in [research/bevy-character-capabilities.md](../../research/bevy-character-capabilities.md); the likeliest to need replacing is joint matrices computed per skinned mesh rather than per skeleton, if the crowd measurement asks for it ([characters.md](../characters.md)).
