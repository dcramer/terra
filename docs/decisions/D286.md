# D286: A forest's shade as dark as Sons of the Forest's, measured

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D216 and D270. Light is tuned against Sons of the Forest's own daytime forest frames, measured as ours are ([research](../../research/2026-10-14-sotf-forest-look.md)). The eye opens at most one and a quarter stops in deep shade (it was three and a quarter); the sky lights the shade 1.2 times the keys (1.6); under the canopy the leaves' glow is halved, the ground's bounce in deep shade a tenth, a crown's underside half as lit by its bounce and by the floor; the day's grade a touch warmer (amended 2026-10-15 by D370, for the developers' "is real life a 'warm grade'? we want close to real life": the grade neutral, the forest's colour from its light); the forest's haze olive. D216 kept the grade from adding shadow contrast because it once crushed the shade: darkness over two fifths of the picture is now the measured target, reached with light, not grading.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "it's still pretty like bright ... in sons of the forest ... even during the day you will feel there's some more shading and climate inside the forest". Four vantage views against twenty SotF frames: mean 0.287 to 0.157 (SotF 0.148), darkest twentieth 0.099 to 0.021 (0.016), below 0.08 2.4% to 40% (46%), red over green 0.85 to 1.01 (1.10), local contrast 0.25 to 0.47 (0.43).
