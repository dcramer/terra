# D480: A building's weathering motifs are solved by wave function collapse within masks of its shape

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A building's weathering is made from its own cubes (`terra_weathering`). Masks of each face's edges, crevices, sills, shelter, height over the ground and age say where each motif may go and how strongly. The motifs (cracks, lost stones, moss, lichen, streaks, soot) are solved there by the overlapping wave function collapse (ported from Gumin's, MIT), one solve per wall plane, from small example patches. Masks cutting noise thresholds are not used. A plane whose solve fails is left without motifs. Maps are 32 texels a face. They are to be stored as BC4: the motif channels at full size, the smooth mask channels at half size (3.7 KB a face). Weathering runs once per building, off the main thread, for buildings near the camera. It stays Off (`assets/data/weathering.ron`) until its look is good enough to turn on, and turning it on in the game is the developers' call.

## Why

The spike ([research/2026-10-25-wfc-weathering.md](../../research/2026-10-25-wfc-weathering.md)) judged one village house in blind D472 panels of 8 judges per build. WFC beat noise masks by +0.36 [+0.12, +0.60] (p 0.008) and +0.42 [+0.26, +0.59] (p 0.000). Its motifs keep their examples' shapes across cube seams, where noise reads as speckle. All 156 of 156 solves succeeded at the first try. It costs 270-340 ms per building against 93-110 ms for masks, once per building and cacheable. Raw maps take 15.6 MB for the house. Mixed BC4 takes 4.6 MB with an RMS error of 1.65 steps. Half size for every channel loses the sharp motifs (max error 219). Both ways score far below the photograph (4.4 of 10, where a clean asset is 3), so this is Leaning until the look's gaps (flat decal moss, a glossy roof with dark panels, a 1 px streak) are closed. The runtime still uploads raw RGBA8; the BC4 upload is the next step.
