# D176: A plan view from above, and heights not storeys

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

An idea for later, not to be built yet: Plan mode (D170) lifting the camera into a view over the claim for drafting, while building stays in the character's view; and, since voxels allow rooms of any height, a cutting plane raised half a metre at a time and stopping at floors, in place of storeys, with each wall its own height. See [building.md](../building.md#ideas-for-later).

## Why

The developers asked on 2026-10-12 whether planning from the character's camera could be improved ("its not always fun to do it from the character camera"), surveyed in [research/planning-mode-cameras.md](../../research/planning-mode-cameras.md); then pointed out that with voxels and variable heights "theres not really stories"; then: "for now lets keep it as an idea/improvement that we may make, but lets not build it yet".
