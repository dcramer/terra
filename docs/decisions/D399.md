# D399: A crash leaves a capsule; state is snapped and diffed

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A panic in the sandbox, and so in the play experiment, the game's only binary for now, writes `shots/crash-<time>/` (`terra_world::crash`, chained after the default hook): `summary.txt`, at most 40 lines, with the panic and where, only the backtrace's frames in our crates, the run's last lines said (from the record `said` reads, `record.rs`), the frame, seed, save and command line, and a command that runs it again; the whole backtrace and log, and a copy of the save, beside it. Its path is the last line printed. Over the remote, `snap NAME` keeps `play.state` and `build.state` in `shots/snaps/`, and `diff A [B|now]` prints a line a change, things in lists matched by what they are and, among several of a kind, by where they are.

## Why

Nothing installed a panic hook, so an agent read hundreds of lines of backtrace and log to find a panic's place and what led to it; and finding what an action did meant reading two state digests side by side. Both are the cheap, repeatable debugging D71 and D69 ask for. The capsule reads the record the remote already keeps, so it costs nothing until a panic.
