# D573: A blow's aim turns its body into a cut that lands off its ahead

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A move may say `aim`, degrees its body faces off the way it strikes (+ to its left, about the up): through the move the body turns to the strike's way turned by `aim`, while its drive and warp still step toward the target. It fits a take whose cut comes down off its ahead to the target without remaking the take, as Unreal's Motion Warping turns a root to a warp target's rotation. The heavy and the partner's heavy turn 15 degrees right (`aim: -15`), and their warps stop at 1.4 m, the take's reach. The duel record measures a blow's reach off its aimed way. A take remade with its cut centred goes back to `aim: 0`.

## Why

The heavy's take of 2026-10-28 (Keyed_Heavy take 7, D565 step 3) comes down about 15 degrees left of ahead and reaches about 1.45 m, where the old take reached 1.5 m ahead. Every blow's drive stops at 1.3 m (`terra_motion::contact::NEAREST_M`), so in the duel the heavy's blade passed 0.11 m clear of the partner's hurt capsule in all its active frames and missed (`film.misses` 1, its baseline 0). Measured on the record's composed pose, the body turned 15 degrees right meets the capsule at 1.3 m by 0.13 m and at 1.4 m by 0.06 m. The move set's other numbers cannot reach it: no window of the cut meets a body at 1.3 m. Remaking the take waits for the key tool (D565 step 4).
