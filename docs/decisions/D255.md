# D255: Gusts

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

One wind for the grass, the bushes and the trees, as Ghost of Tsushima has it: its strength rises and falls as fronts roll downwind at about 6 m/s, two waves 40 and 90 m long at odd angles, sharpened so calm spells are still and gusts come through as fronts, from a fifth to two and a half times the old steady wind; leaves flutter harder in a gust. The same formula in foliage.wesl, grass.wesl and wind.wesl, so a gust crosses grass and canopy together, and the shadow stand-ins move with it, so the sun flecks shift.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13, having played Sons of the Forest: "some random effects of light wind ... that's sort of the effect we wanna go for". Measured from a still view over 10 s, the picture's motion between frames ranges 2.1 to 3.7 (the steady wind: 2.4 to 3.5, with no calm). The walk bench holds at 12.0 ms.
