# D312: Climate shared where it changes play, each machine's where it is only seen

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

What a season or the weather does to play is worked out the same on every machine by the shared crate (D28): the temperature where a body stands, snow depth, ice that holds a walker, wet ground, what a plant offers to gather. What it does only to the look (how far a leaf has turned, frost, breath in the air) is each machine's own. See [climate.md](../climate.md#shared-and-seen-d312).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14, asked whether to share everything that changes play and keep the rest visual: "yeah your recommendation re 1 is correct."
