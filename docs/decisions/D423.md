# D423: Fire is lightly stylised, through the neutral camera

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Fire is drawn in the art style's lightly stylised realism (D14), not photoreal: it is judged against AAA game frames of fire (Witcher 3, Red Dead Redemption 2, Sons of the Forest, Enshrouded), kept outside the repository. Photographs and the professional flipbook stay as physical sanity checks, not the target; where a check's class bounds from photographs and from the game frames differ, the game frames' win. The camera's tonemapper stays KhronosPbrNeutral for every scene: fire is fixed at its source (its ramp, cover, light and scene), never by changing the camera's curve; the fire-only mask (`fire::film`) is allowed.

## Why

The developers' answers on the review desk, 2026-10-19, to the step back's open questions (research/2026-10-19-fire-step-back.md): on style, "Lightly stylised (D14)"; on the camera's tonemapper, "Keep Neutral". One art style for every realm (D14) holds for fire too, and a camera-wide curve would move every scene's look to fix one effect.
