# D230: How far detail reaches, and checking that nothing pops

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Every distance at which something changes its level of detail or comes into view is its own, as authored, times one `DetailScale` (`terra_world::detail`), as the Witcher 3 scales each asset's distances by `FoliageDistanceScale`; grass rings, reach and fade and the crowns' card dropping follow it too. A scripted run's `--detail-sweep` stops the clock and sweeps the scale over frames, and `cargo xtask lodcheck` holds still views through the sweep and fails when a patch larger than a crossfade's scattered pixels appears or goes between two frames. Trees' detail distances are in steps of a twentieth of their height's share, so trees share them: Bevy draws at most 65,535 distinct ranges and past that culls wrongly, which blanked whole parts of the world.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "the shades are clipping in, and some crowns are clipping out. I would like for you to build a validation that would help you identify this pretty consistently so we can make sure we can have veri loop". Comparing moving runs was confused by the camera's own motion. The check, with crossfades turned off, fails three times over; as of this change it fails on one shadow pop on the path, the card stand-in handing over to the solid lumps.
