# D346: A town's furnishing by rules

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`terra_build::town` lays, after the yards and by its numbers in `FURNISHING`: a stone trough along the square's edge nearest the well, out of the streets' mouths; two barrels beside the forge, barrels beside some stalls, against the fronts round the square beside half its doors (where 2.5 m is left to pass) and against the backs of some yards; hand carts in woodpile yards along a side (one beside the market jammed its crowd, so none there yet); and fences round every yard that is not a pen, wattle round hens and split rails round woodpiles, as modules of 2 and 2.5 m with a gate's gap where the lane comes in, its last step then on inside. Each is placed only where it keeps clear of everything built, every thing, place to stand, street, lane and fence; one the path graph cannot reach is taken away. The game draws each thing as its catalogue item and each fence module fitted between its ends along +X, the model contract's module (D303), filed among the obstacles as a capsule, so the player and every body are stopped by it. The fence modules are built in Blender from the kits' own `log_wood` and `wattle` materials (`tools/props/fence.py`); the trough, barrel, cart and the forge, remade in the current style, are Tripo props (D304).

## Why

The town review (round 2) found no fences, carts, barrels or trough, and the forge a flat-colour block-out of the old direction (D14). Shipped towns furnish their streets so: Kingdom Come's and The Witcher 3's yards are fenced and their markets stood about with barrels and carts. Fences of the building kits' own materials match the houses they stand behind.
