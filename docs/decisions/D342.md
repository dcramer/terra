# D342: Trees at their limit stand in islands

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D335. A tree's growing season is warmed or chilled for it by its place's microsite (`terra_land::woods::Microsites`, `stand_in`): 0.5 °C × tanh(Sx / 8°), warmer in the lee and colder on a scoured rise, and up to 0.9 °C either way in islands of noise some 220 m across, so past the closed forest the trees stand in islands thinning and stunting onto the open heath, in the field maps, the woods drawn and the heath alike. Drawn near, a tree under 0.7 of its stature spreads wider as well as shorter, up to 2.6 times as wide for its height at krummholz.

## Why

The forest-tundra (*Waldtundra*) is islands of stunted trees in sheltered ground, not a line (Holtmeier, *Mountain Timberlines*, 2009; [research](../../research/2026-10-15-biome-demos.md#tundra)); on the tundra's level plateau the tree line had followed the latitude, a straight band a kilometre wide.
