# D493: The flame's motion would come from a baked, looping fluid simulation, sampled by the marched field

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The design is [fire-module.md](../fire-module.md)'s last section; the game waits on the developers' answer on the review desk (item `r-fire-sim`); the baker's step is built (`node tools/fire/bake/bake.ts campfire-sim`) and nothing in the game loads it. A baked kind's marched field (D466) reads its shape and motion from a looping 3D volume, the sim's flame and temperature at its own 2.3 cm cells (the box the flame reaches, 40 x 36 x 52 for the campfire, 8 s at 30 fps, 17.1 MiB as BC5), baked offline in Blender's Mantaflow with fire round g23's F settings by the fire baker (`tools/fire/bake`), in place of its lobes and noise; no velocity is stored. The marcher, edge, ramp, light flicker, per-kind settings and gates are unchanged. The loop is made seamless by the baker's offline crossfade, shared by every fire of its bake, each playing it at its own offset, mirrored, turned to the wind and scaled; one bake is one variant a player tells apart (D182), at most three for the campfire. The torch gets its own bake, braziers and house blazes play the campfire's scaled, the candle and breath keep theirs. Stronger wind than the bake's adds the field's shear; rain and going out act as now. It lands on motion.ts's nine targets on the game's film, the fire sheet twice, D472's campfire and torch panels not worse, and `cargo xtask bench` within the near campfire's 0.3 ms.

The BC5 volume is a plain 3D texture (`TextureDimension::D3`, `Bc5RgUnorm`), which needs wgpu's `TEXTURE_COMPRESSION_BC` and `TEXTURE_COMPRESSION_BC_SLICED_3D`. The pinned Bevy (0.20, wgpu 30) asks for every feature the adapter has under its default `WgpuSettingsPriority::Functionality` (bevy_render `renderer/mod.rs`), so no setting is needed, and its DDS loader makes a depth-over-one file a D3 image; wgpu-hal's Vulkan backend reports sliced 3D wherever it reports BC. A probe (create, upload, `textureLoad` each slice, linear filtering across slices) passed on the RTX 3080 (Vulkan, NVIDIA 615.78), the workstation's RTX 4090 (Vulkan, NVIDIA 580.178) and llvmpipe (Mesa 25.2); no integrated GPU was reachable. An adapter without it reads the same bake decoded from the BC5 at load (`Rg8Unorm`, 34 MiB in memory), so the repository carries each bake once, checked through `RenderDevice::features()`; 2D array slices, filtering across slices by hand, only if one is found that lacks it.

## Why

Five rounds tuned the noise field (g17-g21) and its splits stayed at 1.65 or more against the filmed campfires' 1.2 at most, its dusk life at 0.16 s against 0.04-0.12: noise cannot tear one body into tongues. Round g23's F meets eight of the nine motion targets (research/2026-10-25-fire-fluid-sim.md) and misses sheds (0.27 against 1.0), because Blender's wind pushes only the flame's gas, not the air round it; Mantaflow's guides, or EmberGen or Houdini (a purchase, the developers' call), would be the next try. Shipped games take fire's motion from offline sims played back; a live grid would cost about 0.3-0.5 ms a fire (estimate), ten fires 3-5 ms against the village's 1.5 ms. Leaning, not Decided, because shipping at eight of nine or buying tooling for sheds first is the developers' call.
