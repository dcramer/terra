# D139: Structures are planned, then constructed

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A structure is laid out as a plan that costs nothing, then actually constructed part by part with materials and work; doors and windows are carved out of walls, slightly differently. A blueprint shows its parts, as The Forest shows its logs; parts can be small, and what they are depends on the kind of construction. Amended by D491 (2026-10-25): Space Engineers 2's construction loop, [construction-003](../proposals/construction-003.md): a catalogue of prefab parts made of cubes, snapped to the part set against, a bigger part always a ghost first and the one aimed at built in any order once what holds it stands, a single cube placed and built at once from what is carried, costs by mode (D494). The hammer, or whatever tool a material takes, builds the ghost aimed at.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "for structures, you place a blueprint/plan of it, and then you have to actually construct it somehow… it makes it more realistic… we'll wanna think this through". Then: "for part size we can go down to smaller bits.. ideally a blueprint shows its 'parts' if you will, kind of like forest shows the logs, and that can vary based on the type of construction". Then: "you'll actually go up and hit the thing with a hammer or similar? or maybe its like, you bring the resource and place it? like you carry it like the forest/grounded 2? we want it to feel a little immersive". Proposed: big parts carried and set, then hammered fast; small parts hammered in. Worked through, to react to, in [building.md](../building.md#planned-then-constructed). After The Forest and Grounded 2. Then, settled with the parts (D152): "blueprints need to be fundamentally walls/floors/roofs, and you build up piece by piece instead of prebuilt. it just feels better". How each part goes in, carried, set or hammered, is still being worked through. Then, on how: "for all large construction it'd originally be placed as a blueprint, and you'd have to then bring the materials to it to build it (kind of like the forest or grounded 2)".
