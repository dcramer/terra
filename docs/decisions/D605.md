# D605: The mountains' slopes are blended between every so many lattice points

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A mountain's ground point takes the two slopes its shape follows, the gullies' (`Range::gradient` over `SLOPE_STEP_M`, 20 m either side) and the faces' broad one (over `FACES_SLOPE_M`, 150 m), blended bilinearly from where they are measured, not measured at the point: the gullies' at every 8th lattice point, the broad one at every 32nd (`terra_voxel::field::slopes`, `SLOPE_EVERY`, `BROAD_EVERY`), each row's points counted from both of a face's ends so neighbouring faces' points meet along their edge, kept in `Known` beside the nodes. Only the slopes are blended; the ridges' height at the point is still its own. A coarse view's points, which already sit on every so many lattice points, are measured where they are.

- **The ground's bits changed for it**, on purpose: the land is not the same as measured at each point, and a world's fingerprints with it (D596). Seed 1's lake round (`terra_world`'s `lake_load_by_stage`, `PINNED_ROUND`): waters `dc77c45242385f5b` (was `63d413affc9c85a4`), drift `b1c66c95f10b9ccb` (was `e9ef03d96f089214`), heath `fdc2cb9128d6d0da` (was `a5e720e344f96735`); pools, seasons, snow and snowbeds as they were. The sweep of seeds 1 to 129: 129 valid, fingerprints' digest `e988ad8c40dbb27e` (was `83571e483cba5545`), starts' `a48ae4bfab4e5acf` (was `ea38052460bc0003`), the same bit for bit over two runs.
- **Held by** `terra_land`'s `the_mountains_slopes_blended_between_their_points_stray_little_over_the_lakes_view`: over the lake's first view the gullies' way turns median under 0.1° and 99% under 2°, the broad steepness differs median under 0.002 and 99% under 0.03.

## Why

The developers hold the mountain lake's load under a minute (D580). Each mountain point asked the ridges' height 13 times, 12 of them for two slopes that are differences over tens and hundreds of metres, smooth over a metre: some 80% of shaping the first view's metre-apart points. Measured at every 8th and 32nd point instead, each is measured once for every ground point round it, as a terrain's derived maps (its normals, its flow) are kept on a coarser grid than its heights. On the worker, shaping the lake's first view took 63.6 s against 96.3 s (`lake_load_by_stage`, load-time j7).

Blended, the slopes stray little (load-time j7): the gullies' way turns median 0.020°, 99% 0.70°, most 29° at a crest where the slope turns over; the broad steepness differs median 0.0005, 99% 0.012, most 0.042. The coordinator allowed the ground's bits to change for it (load-time j8) on these conditions, all met: the lake, lake-range and tundra looks within noise of main (lake 8 of 18 hold on both, lake-range 2 of 2, tundra 10 of 18 on both), the seed sweep 129 of 129 valid twice with matching digests, pops and lodcheck on the lush forest as on main (median pop 0.08% against 0.09%, lodcheck passes), and terra_land's and terra_voxel's tests passing.
