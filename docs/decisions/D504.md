# D504: The sword-and-shield set carries a strapped heater now, a round shield later

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The prototype's shield becomes a strapped heater or kite shield, worn along the forearm by its straps (enarmes), as the moves already carry it (the forearm strap, D459) and as Enshrouded's shields sit. The round shield, a centre-grip shield held in the fist, comes back later as its own style with its own poses (the forearm pointing at the board, the elbow forward), not on today's strapped hang.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, on the review desk, of three options (a strapped heater or kite; the round shield held in the fist, re-posing every left arm; both, heater now and a round-shield style later): "Both, later". The research (research/2026-10-25-shield-prior-art.md) found the 'floating' shield was a centre-grip round shield worn strapped, which no real round shield is.
