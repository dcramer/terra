# D283: Mesh slabs start at 64 MiB and double

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: — · Status: Superseded by D420

Was: Bevy keeps meshes in shared slabs of GPU memory (its mesh allocator); a full slab is copied into a bigger one, after which every mesh in it is prepared and batched again. Ours start at 64 MiB and double up to Bevy's 512 MiB (`shell`), three growths a slab rather than a dozen from Bevy's 1 MiB by half again.

## Why

The `town300` street walk on the 5090, back to back at load 8 to 15: with Bevy's slabs the 99th percentile was 24 to 32 ms with up to 9 hitches, the trace showing `allocate_and_free_meshes` up to 52 ms and the bind groups and indirect buffers rebuilt after it at up to 27 ms; with these, 12 to 17 ms in three runs of four and no hitch, for 25% more memory held in slabs (3.1 to 3.8 GiB in the town).
