# D323: The ground bends the climate

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D314. `terra_climate::Place` carries how the ground faces the sun and how much drifting snow it catches, worked out by the world from its terrain (the slope's aspect; Winstral's Sx, the steepest upwind slope within 100 m against the prevailing wind), so the climate crate never reads terrain: a sunny slope is warmer, a lee hollow takes more of what falls as snow and keeps it longer, a windward rise less. A place's latitude is read from where it is, not a constant.

## Why

Tree lines rise on sunny slopes; lee slopes hold about twice the snow of windward ones, and late snow patches recur in the same places every year ([research](../../research/2026-10-15-biome-demos.md#tundra)). Without the ground the fields lay the same snow on a ridge as in a gully. Keeping terrain out of the climate crate keeps D28's split and its tests. See [demos.md](../demos.md#2-a-tundra).
