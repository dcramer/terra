# D643: Natural caves are systems placed by rule and carved from the ground's distance field

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A natural cave (D591) is a system of chambers and passages, made of parts by rules and placed from the world's seed where the land has a hillside for its mouth, subtracted from the ground's signed distance with a smooth maximum ([caves.md](../caves.md)). The troll's den is one, made to fit and to be reached on every seed. No 3D noise is thresholded through the whole crust in v0; a theme that wants a honeycombed world can add it later.

## Why

D591 asks that "a seed's land has to hold a cave that fits a 4 m body and a fight, reachable from the surface": a placed system is made to, where a noise cave is found by luck. Shipped games place the caves that matter (Valheim's troll caves are locations chosen when the world is made; Deep Rock Galactic carves authored rooms joined by rule; Minecraft kept its placed carvers beside its noise caves), and Voxel Tools carves by subtracting distances ([research](../../research/natural-caves.md)). A subtraction keeps the ground a distance, so the streaming's chunk rule, the mover and digging need nothing new; a noise threshold is no distance and would have every chunk of the crust asked. Parts by rules give variants a player tells apart (D182).
