# D325: Smoke, sparks and blown snow

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Drawn as instanced soft cards facing the camera, on our own material, moved on the GPU from each emitter's seed and the climate's wind, as fragments are drawn instanced (D305), with no particle dependency: a chimney's column, a cooking fire, a forge's sparks, snow lifting off patches above a wind speed. Capped by the preset.

## Why

There is no particle system, and the few kinds a place needs are cheaper kept on our own path, in step with each Bevy release, than a dependency. If we come to need much more, `bevy_hanabi` is the candidate, checked against the pinned Bevy first. See [demos.md](../demos.md#3-a-living-town).
