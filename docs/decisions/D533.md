# D533: Uthana's takes come on our body uploaded bare, lifted onto our ground and checked against its reference

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Engineering · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`tools/uthana` uploads a body without its animations (`carry.ts` `bare`, cached by the hash of what is uploaded), so Uthana's retarget lands on our skeleton's own nodes and the take is our skeleton's, carried onto every template by `made_clips.py` as any take is. The take's root is raised by our rest forefoot height less the reference character's (`lift`, 3.1 cm on base.glb), and every take is downloaded a second time on Uthana's stock Manny (free) and refused unless it stands as Manny does (`against`: the pelvis's height share within 3 cm while Manny stands on a foot and 10 cm at any time, the trunk's direction within 5°, the lower forefoot within 2 cm of its rest while Manny's is on the ground). One command per clip: `motion.ts <clip>`; a refused take is kept as `.refused.glb` and downloaded again, never paid again.

## Why

Measured on the trial motions (idle mQpwFrKxhYot, sprint mUMkJxi4dwyJ, swim mfGtkGGamgpd), 2026-10-26, downloads only. Uploaded with its 28 clips, base.glb got the idle's pelvis 14.5 times too low and its trunk 30° over, in GLB, BVH and FBX alike; the same body with no clips took the motion as Manny does (trunk within 0.1° of Manny's, pelvis within 2 cm of its share). Applying the Rig's 1.0776 scale or baking the root's −90° rest turn changed nothing, so neither is done. Uthana keeps the forefeet at Manny's height (ours at 1.1 cm in the idle, Manny's at 1.0; at rest ours stand at 3.9, Manny's at 0.8), so our soles sank 2.8 cm until lifted. A fixed bar on pose would refuse a swim or a crouch; against the same motion on Manny it measures only the carry. This replaces `groundScale`, which scaled the translations by what put the feet down and hid the wrong upload.
