# D587: The render world's visibility ranges are kept as they change, not read again whole

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Bevy 0.20's `extract_visibility_ranges` cleared its table of ranged entities and read every `VisibilityRange` in the main world again whenever any one was added, changed or removed (a despawn too). The table is now kept entity by entity: the frame's removed ranges are taken out first, then the added and changed ones written, so an entity whose range is taken away and given again in one frame keeps it; `range_to_index` and the GPU buffer were already never cleared (a range keeps its slot for the app's life). A patch of `bevy_render` 0.20.0 (`patches/bevy_render`, D184), marked `Terra's patch (D587)`; its test `view::visibility::range::tests::ranges_kept_as_they_change_match_a_fresh_read` runs 40 frames of spawns, changes, removals, despawns and a remove-and-give-again in one frame, and checks the table against a fresh read of every ranged entity each frame.

Upstream: not yet offered (2026-10-30).

## Why

The town at dusk holds about 147,000 ranged entities (the woods' trees, the town's props and pieces). A probe of town-dusk's walk (settlement perf round 6) found 164 of the bench's 1,300 frames changing a range: about 100 of them a creature's fade (`creatures::appear::fade` writing the range of each of a fox's, hare's, deer's or pigeon's 1 to 9 meshes) and the rest a house or wall streamed in (1 to 4 entities). Each such frame paid 2.1 to 2.7 ms re-reading all 147,000, among the slowest frames of the session report; 3% of the run's frame time. Bench town-dusk on the RTX 4090, two runs each side (settlement perf round 7, against main 2eb7a79cd): median 10.54-10.61 to 10.33-10.43 ms, frames over the budget 6.2% and 4.8% to 2.7% and 4.2%, 99th percentile 16.48 and 14.63 to 14.31 and 15.41 ms (one run on each side holds the same few-frame stall as the town streams in, 32 ms on main's, 21 ms on the patch's). In the session report `extract_visibility_ranges` drops out of the systems by 99th percentile, where main's has it at 6.95 ms (a loaded box), and the dusk stills match main's to 0.10-0.19% RMSE.
