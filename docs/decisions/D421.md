# D421: Demo and film places leave out the combat lair

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Play keeps its lair of combat creatures (the spider nest) beside every place but the yard. A demo's or film's place leaves it out: every place in `tools/sandbox/places.ts` is run with `--no-lair` unless its data says `lair: true`, so `vantage.ts`, `film.ts`, `trailer.ts` and the looks get it from one rule, and a film run outside a place passes `--no-lair` itself.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-19: "Leave it out of films". A nest of spiders a few dozen metres off every demo place wanders into shots that are about the place, not combat.
