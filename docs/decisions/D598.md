# D598: Conifer crowns fuller, of many smaller sprays in layered masses, and warmer in colour

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Open

Open: whether the lush forest's Scots pines and spruces (also the play world's woods) get fuller crowns built of many smaller sprays in tiers, with dark gaps between, and a warmer green, in place of today's few large flat pads. Recommended:

- **More, smaller sprays.** Sprays about a third to half today's card size, several times as many to a branch, grouped in tiers or pads that each read as a mass. Rule 3 of the art guide: "many car-sized leaf clumps, not five house-sized ones".
- **The live crown deeper** where the stand opens: down to a half of a pine's height at the edges and in clearings, keeping D301's tall clear boles inside the stand. Dead branch stubs on the boles below.
- **Crown colour warm olive.** Median hue about 62° and red against green about 1.1, as Enshrouded's and Sons of the Forest's are, in sun and in rain. Held by the looks check's crown hue.
- Triangles and overdraw measured against the near tree's budget (2,000 to 8,000) and the walk bench before landing. The far levels already thin cards with distance (D223).

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-02: our trees look fake beside Enshrouded's, "canopies feel unhealthy". Measured in the lush forest:

- **Form.** From outside, each pine is a pole with a few flat pads of one dark green; Enshrouded's conifers are full cones of many sprays.
- **Spray size.** Looking up, a few large repeated fan cards: flat foliage 45% and flat crowns 59% in that view.
- **Colour.** Crown hue 77° on a clear day and 90 to 116° on a rainy one, against 62 to 63°. Red against green 0.93 against 1.10.

How full a crown is, is the developers' to say (D301 chose a tall open stand). Research: [2026-11-02-why-our-trees-read-fake.md](../../research/2026-11-02-why-our-trees-read-fake.md).
