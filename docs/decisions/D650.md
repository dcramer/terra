# D650: Where the Drillhog is met in v0

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

The Drillhog (D250) is rigged and in the game's data: a hostile kind that notices a walker at 12 m, waddles after them and gores with the drill on its head. Its decided home is dungeons and places underground, and v0 has neither as a place a player reaches, so it is put about the far realm's forest for now, one or two together, rarely (`assets/data/realms.ron`'s animals, `creatures.ron`'s row): the far realm had no monster of its own (D642), and a row's `lives` moves it wherever it is wanted. Recommended: keep it there for v0 and give it the world's den's tunnels and the dungeons when they are places. Open: whether it belongs in the far realm at all, how hard it hits, and whether it should burrow, which it does not. It is slow (2 m/s at its fastest: its legs are stubs and its run is the stride they carry), so a walker outruns it; whether that is right for it is part of the question.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-13: "These are the characters that are gonna be mobs or enemies that are designed by my daughter. Obviously, they have to fit the style of the game." Where a designed kind lives and how it fights are the developers' (D412); the placement here is an assumption so the kind is seen in play, not a decision.
