# D486: A room's sky light is what its openings let in

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A room's light probe (D320) dims the sky's light by the room's share of it, from 0 to 1 (`terra_build::sky`, `Base::room_sky`), not by one figure for every room: an opening (`Fill::Open`, a doorway or window) onto the open air brings in what the air just beyond it sees of the sky, all of it above the ground as generated (every house's door) and under it, as in a dug well or a dungeon's stair, the share of rays over its hemisphere, weighed by how steeply they fall, that leave the ground within 40 m (`terra_bases::light::sky_seen`); a doorway into another room passes on half that room's share, as a room-and-portal engine carries an area's ambient; a room with no way out has none and is lit only by its own fires. The probe's light is the sky's times 0.6 times the share, so a house's room is lit as before and a dungeon's deep rooms go dark by day as by night.

## Why

A closed room took six tenths of the sky's light whatever led to it, so a dungeon's rooms four levels down were lit as a cottage's. Shipped games bake this per room or per cell, Unreal's sky light occlusion in its volumetric lightmaps and id Tech's and Source's area portals carrying an area's ambient; worked out from the cubes it holds for a house, a cellar and a dungeon alike, and changes when a door is cut. It needs D485 to be seen under a sky volume.
