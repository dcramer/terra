# D280: Villages and scale scenes put up by the operations

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

As D108 asks, a village's buildings, the town and castle the scale benches draw, and the bench village are put up by the checked operations a player's are (`Base::put_up`, `Build::put_up`): the shortcuts still say what each building is, then it is laid as plans, a part a kind, its openings opened, and built a layer at a time from the bottom, every step checked (nothing floats, built from the bottom, a roof on what holds it), in its kits' terms; a refusal is logged and the shortcuts' base kept. A test puts up every building of the village, a town and the castle and finds the same cubes. Two of the village's buildings are built by hand a cube at a time: a log cabin with a dormer, and a stone house with a storey of timber over its roof (D226). The kit sheet keeps the shortcuts, as D201 allows.

## Why

Mine, from the second goals review's finding 3 on 2026-10-14, as the lead asked: everything the developers were shown, and every scale bench, was put up by shortcuts no operation checked, against D108; and nothing in the demo was built by hand. Laying the shortcuts' result through the operations, rather than rewriting each plan as operations, keeps the variety (D229) and makes every rule a check on it.
