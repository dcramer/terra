# D206: Footings, floors and steps in code

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

As `terra_build` lays them (D194): footings are cubes of the kind of the floor they carry where that is a block kind, as a stone floor stands on stone, else of the plan's footing kind, the first block kit's (stone); their own part, laid first by a plan: under every ground-storey wall from the floor's layer, and under a floor's edges from the layer below, each column down to the cube the ground passes through, measured at the lowest of its corners less a few centimetres, at most eight cubes; a room's floor is laid inside its footing; on a layer the part aimed at goes in first, then the part laid first, then the nearest, so a footing is whole before its floor. A storey below the ground storey closes a room in only where it has a floor, so the space a footing rings is none. The shortcuts lay footings as built cubes from the planet's ground. A room's cut in the ground stops at its walls' outer faces. A block kind draws all its cubes, walls, floors and footings, as one surface, its tops in the kit's `Floor` material (flagstones for stone); a timber kind's floors and flat roofs are decks from the timber code: two sawn boards to a cube's width, joints staggered over joists and run on under walls to the logs' faces, a rim on every open side, joists where the underside is in the air and bearers over its posts (D306), each board drawn from one plank of its texture (`strips`). Door steps are cubes since D261: stairs and halves of the footing kind from each doorway down to the ground, the ground not changed.

## Why

Mine, building footings, floors and steps on 2026-10-13: the grey slab and steps under every build stood in for them, and footings were not in plans. Footings as cubes make support, cost and order the rules' own; steps as cubes wait for stairs (D193's shapes).
