# D348: The voxel contract: who draws what between cubes, and every junction proved

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

[building-voxels.md](../building-voxels.md) is the contract for construction cubes: the cube and its 32 shapes, who owns every face, edge, corner and piece between two cubes (each exactly one cube, line or prop), ten invariants that keep a building closed (the cubes the truth, one owner, every shown face and junction closed, an eighth of a cube, sections agree, every detail and every state closes, a material adds no junction, what cannot close is refused, every junction proved), and the junction matrix: each pair of neighbours by pattern, shape and state, with the rule that closes it and the test or inspected case that proves it; a row without one is a gap, listed there with its recommended rule. Where roofs and walls meet is building-plan.md's design (its R1 to R7), cited by reference. A change to how cubes are laid or drawn keeps the invariants or changes the page in the same commit; a new pattern adds its rows. The matrix is data (`docs/building-voxels.json`): each row names its rule, the code that closes it and its tests and registered cases; the docs check writes and holds the page to it (`node tools/docs/docs.ts --render`) and fails on any test the page names that no crate defines, terra_build's `every_row_of_the_voxel_contract_is_proved` fails on a closed row with no test or case, or with code, a test or a case not there, a test ignored, or a case only in scenes with faults recorded, and the push's artifacts ratchet holds the scenes its rows cite (D287).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "i think its worthwhile to have a very clear design doc on how the voxels need to work so we can make sure there's no gaps?", after "we should implement it correct, but the issue is we had gaps, because we didnt think this through". Mine: the rules were spread over construction-001, building-kits.md, building-drawing.md and the code, and no one place said which junctions had a rule and a check. Probing each junction with the mesh checker for the page found nine gaps, among them walls through roofs, roofs falling to walls, cubes meeting only along an edge, roof kinds cut to stairs, holes in roofs and the states the checker never sees. Two invariants untested until now gained tests: every edge with a role dressed by one cube (`roles`), and every open side of a roof's covering closed by one edge piece (`roofs`).
