# D614: The air's transmittance and multiscattering tables are drawn only when the air changes

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Bevy 0.20 draws all four of the atmosphere's lookup tables every frame. Two of them, the transmittance and the multiple-scattering tables, read only the planet's radii and ground albedo, the medium's density and scattering tables and their own sizes and sample counts, never the view, the sun or `sun_gain` (D212), so a frame whose air is the last frame's draws them again to the same texels. They are now kept while their inputs hold, as Hillaire's technique has them ("A Scalable and Production Ready Sky and Atmosphere Rendering Technique", EGSR 2020: the two are "only updated when the atmosphere properties change") and Unreal's SkyAtmosphere does; the sky-view and aerial-view tables, which follow the camera and the sun, are drawn every frame as before. A patch of `bevy_pbr` 0.20.0 (D184), marked `Terra's patch (D614)`:

- `atmosphere/kept_luts.rs`: `prepare_kept_atmosphere_luts` (in `PrepareBindGroups`) keys each atmosphere view on those inputs, the medium's two texture views and the two tables' own texture views, and marks the view `KeptAtmosphereLuts(true)` when the key is the one its tables were last drawn with; a frame whose LUT pipelines are not all compiled (the pass returns before it dispatches) records nothing, so the next draws them.
- `atmosphere/node.rs`'s `atmosphere_luts` skips the two dispatches on a kept view.
- The tunable `render.kept_atmosphere_luts` (`terra_world::air`, default 1) sets the static `KEEP_UNCHANGED_LUTS` each frame; at 0 they are drawn every frame, Bevy's way, so a probe measures the one against the other in one load (D609).

Upstream: Bevy's main still draws the four every frame. Not yet offered.

## Why

At the lake's route view (1901x1550, the worker's 4090) `atmosphere_luts` costs 0.184 ms drawn every frame and 0.154 kept, and the frame's GPU median falls by 0.033-0.051 ms, every frame against kept in one load, three loads (job 20261103-191440-aa7d-patch): 7.17 -> 7.12, 7.07 -> 7.03, 6.99 -> 6.95 ms. It draws the same: the lake's three views, the tundra's three and the route's, kept against every frame shot twice, differ from the every-frame shots no more than those differ from each other (mean 0.03-0.15 a channel against an A/A of 0.03-0.17; job 20261103-192230-9556-patch), with no shader error. A medium changed (the day's dust, `air::clear_cold_air`) is a new texture, so the tables follow it. Tests: `kept_luts`'s four (drawn the first frame and kept while the inputs hold, drawn again on a changed input or texture, nothing kept before the pass can draw or when off, the sun's gain no input).
