# D150: Regrowth

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

What is cut or harvested grows back, each kind at its own pace, set as data. By default nothing grows back inside a claim, and removing a stump stops what was cut from regrowing; both to revisit if they cause trouble. How is open: at minimum by time passed, and perhaps seen to grow rather than coming back whole. Proposed in [biomes.md](../biomes.md#regrowth): in stages worked out from the time since, changing only out of sight, the stored change forgotten once grown.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "yes things should grow back but we have to figure out that system yet… at minimum time passed, but you might need to 'see' them grow back vs be instantly full regrown"; "the speed of growth will need to vary based on what it is"; "maybe by default nothing regrows in claim and we can redecide that later"; "removing a stump can prevent it from regrowing and that's fine". Answers part of D43.
