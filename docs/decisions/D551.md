# D551: Where a put-away weapon or tool shows on the body

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: (b), what the hands' slots hold shows on the body when put away, one thing a socket: a one-handed blade at the hip away from the main hand, a one-handed tool or axe at the hip on the main hand's side, a shield on the back, a bow and any two-hander or spear across the back (a shield there first, the long one beside it), the quiver where it hangs today; whatever else is carried stays in the pack, unseen. Options: (a) nothing shows when put away, the shield on the arm (Enshrouded); (b) as recommended (Kenshi's two weapon slots, RDR2's capped slots); (c) everything on the bar shows. Open ([attachment.md](../attachment.md#stowed-d551)).

## Why

Holding.md already puts an off-hand item on a stowed socket while a two-hander is out (a shield on the back, a blade at the hip, as Elden Ring does), but says nothing of tools, bows, spears or how many show. Realistic but fun (D122) favours seeing what you carry: arrows ride in a quiver on your belt. What the inspirations do (researched 2026-10-27, sources in attachment.md): Kenshi shows both weapon slots, small weapons at the hip and large on the back, at most two on the back and one at the hip; RDR2 shows at most four long and short arms on the body and keeps the rest on the horse; Enshrouded shows nothing put away but the shield, which stays on the arm and players call an eyesore; no game found shows everything carried. Unconfirmed for Soulmask, Medieval Dynasty and Dune: Awakening.
