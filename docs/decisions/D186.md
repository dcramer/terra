# D186: What the base body wears

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The base bodies wear plain, close-fitting underwear (briefs; on the female, briefs and a band top), painted on with almost no relief, so no one is ever naked and nothing stands off the skin for gear to clear or for the skin tint to tell from skin. What a character starts in, such as today's shorts and undershirt, is ordinary worn items (D64, D128) that can be taken off or replaced.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-12, after the shorts and tops sculpted into the base bodies were found in the way of tight gear and the skin tint: "yeah thats probably easier, lets do that". Survival games (Rust, ARK, Valheim, Conan Exiles), Skyrim and WoW do the same. The base bodies are redrawn in it, then their rigs, morphs and gear rebuilt; see [characters.md](../characters.md).
