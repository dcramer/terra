# D182: Hundreds of variants, not millions

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A core tenet: almost everything is modular and procedural, but thematic. Things are made from parts by rules, as Borderlands makes guns and No Man's Sky makes planets, and every variant belongs to a theme (a realm, a maker, a people) that shapes which parts and rules it draws from. Aim for hundreds or thousands of variants of a kind, each different in a way a player notices and cares about, not millions of near-copies made only to be numerous. A generator says how many distinct variants it makes and what tells them apart; when it makes more than players can tell apart, it is narrowed or given more structure rather than widened.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "almost everything in this game we want to be modular and procedural, but to be thematic ... we dont want infinite low value things just to have infinite things. so think 100s or 1000s of variants, not millions", as a core tenet to keep work on track. D127 says systems must cope with many of everything; this says how many is worth making. Answers what vision.md already left of No Man's Sky (its infinite universe and shallow planets) and gives D59 (every world thematic) a rule for everything else.
