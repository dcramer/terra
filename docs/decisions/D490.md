# D490: The sky's light varies by direction, as the drawn sky does

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The light the sky gives the shade (`skylight`, D190, D392) varies round the sky as the drawn sky does (`sky.wesl`'s glow round the sun, its clouds and deck), not by height alone: brighter and warmer toward the sun, so a shaded side facing the sun's half of the sky takes more and warmer light than one facing away. How it is done is technical (the skylight handoff's plan: brightness by angle to the sun after the CIE standard general sky, the diffuse light as spherical harmonics, the specular left to Bevy's filter); D489's per-height painting gives way where this needs it.

## Why

The painted sky lit every side the same at one height, though the drawn sky has a glow round the sun: "i dont want the sky looking uniformly the same" ... "thats based on realism... which is a primary rule" (the developers, 2026-10-25; D122). Settled: "the sky light should vary by direction like the drawn sky (brighter and warmer toward the sun)".
