# D459: What each hand holds, and how its animation is chosen, by rule from data

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The technical parts of [holding.md](../holding.md).

- **Data and rule.** Each hand holds an item or nothing. An item's `hold` entry in `items.ron` gives its class, its grip (`Main`, `Either`, `Off`, `Two`, with the `lead` hand for `Two`), whether it is only `carried`, and its `length`, `mass` and `balance`. The stance comes from the pair in the hands by a table in `terra_motion`, the side that asks (D198); it is simulation, as the move and its take are (D51).
- **Move sets.** A set is a file per class and stance (`moves/<class>.<stance>.ron`). It may start `from` another set, and falls back from class to family to the stance's generic set. Combat styles (D124) add moves to a set. Locomotion is shared by every person.
- **Layers.** The stance's hold pose is laid over locomotion at run time by Bevy 0.20's mask groups. That replaces D458's baked `Fight_Walk` once the pose layer exists (movement.md stage 2).
- **Mirroring.** Left-handed bodies, and a dual set's off-hand blows, play clips mirrored by one table of the skeleton's `_l` and `_r` bone pairs. The table is Unreal's Mirror Data Table, built from its documentation. The mirroring is baked at export by `tools/characters`, not done at run time, and only the hold and move layers are mirrored, never locomotion.
- **Timing and reach by rule.** Tempo is `sqrt(I_ref / I)` of the item's moment of inertia about its grip, clamped to 0.85 to 1.15; reach and the warp's stop come from the item's length; active frames are measured again on the item's own tip path.
- **Fit.** A two-handed item's other hand meets its `second` grip by the two-bone IK already ported for the feet (D455). A shield rides a strap on the forearm, as its file's `strap` extras say (`terra_walker::strap`). A strapped shield (the prototype's heater, D504) has its origin at its hand strap, its forearm running across its board from the arm strap (+Y) and its hand strap square to that (+X); the move check's grip rows measure the fist's middle off the hand strap and the hand strap off the line across the palm (`held.shield.grip_hand`, `grip_across_palm`, redefined from the round shield's centre grip in combat round 24). Today's clips carry the board out of the forearm's little-finger side with the fist on the far side of the forearm, so both rows stay held (23 cm, 90°) until the left hand is rolled onto the strap, about 90° about the forearm, by a clip hold or the hand IK.
- **Variants.** Up to a set's `takes` distinct passing Kimodo takes a move (D450), drawn from a shuffle bag seeded by the body and the move's count, so server and client agree. A move's takes share their phases within a frame and their reach within 10 cm. An additive layer moves a hand no more than 2 cm. Body size plays at Froude rate (D443).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-25: "we have various weapons, two arms, sometimes two handed items, sometimes two one handed items, sometimes one one handed item... sometimes one in a left arm sometimes one in the right arm (it varies right). and then how do animations work? what happens with variance? i dont know if we figured that out vs just yeeting shit into here".

Every shipped game looked at does it this way (research/2026-10-25-loadouts-and-animation-layers.md):

- **Lyra**: one graph, with a per-weapon layer that only fills in clips and numbers, chosen by rule.
- **FromSoftware**: a moveset per weapon class shared by its weapons; stance from the two hands; a two-hander puts the off item away; an off-hand light blow is the main hand's mirrored.

That keeps a new weapon to data (D136) and the sets to about 20 (D182).

- **Why mirroring is baked**: the move check (D447) measures what is played; it costs nothing per frame for a thousand players; and Bevy has no mirror node.
- **Why a square root of inertia**: the same arm turning a heavier thing takes time as the square root of its inertia. The clamp keeps the class's clips believable; past it, an item is another class.
- **Why the take is seeded**: blows are judged on the server from baked tracks (movement.md), so both sides must play the same take.

The strap had no decision of its own; it is recorded here. Leaning, not Decided: nothing is built on it until the developers have read holding.md (D49) and answered D460 to D465.
