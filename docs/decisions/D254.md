# D254: Tone mapping for a forest's range

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The camera tone maps with Khronos PBR Neutral, saturation at 0.82, in place of AgX (D212): Neutral keeps the saturation AgX drained, and plants' colours were made under AgX, so at full saturation undergrowth read neon. Measured on three forest views at 17:00, AgX held the middle 90% of the picture to 106 of 256 levels whatever else changed (mist, haze or eye adaptation off moved it by two levels at most), so shade under the canopy and the sun flecks in it read alike; Neutral spans 118 and puts the darkest tenth near black (sRGB 15 against 39), the range the forests we look to have.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13, having played Sons of the Forest: "how the light/shades work, that's sort of the effect we wanna go for"; the second review found the light "flat in value, everything mid-tone".
