# D293: The lush forest's floor green under its trees, its litter umber

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D246 and D275, as research/2026-10-14-sotf-forest-look.md's recommendation 4 asks. Under a closed canopy grass grows at 0.62 of a lit lawn's, before its tufts (`SHADE_GRASS` in `lush/floor.rs`; it was 0.06), so the floor off the paths is shade grass in clumps, with taller sedge in clumps among it, thinned only a little on needles (0.15, from 0.55); it comes up to within a few tenths of a metre of a path's edge (it was kept a metre and a half off), and a path keeps tussocks along it, more toward its edges, fewer leaves drifted onto it. Bare litter is left at the trees' feet, by logs and rocks and on the paths. The litter is a dark umber rather than lit orange: the ground's leaf and needle tints about 30% darker and redder hues taken out, the leaf scan's own colour kept at 0.3 rather than 0.5, the litter cards drawn at 0.72 of their scans and less red, the forest's soil darker and greyer. The undergrowth is placed as before, so the walk follows the same way. Amended by D503: the litter is mixed, green-brown decay and moss in the umber.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "the ground is still brown most of the time, i want see more grass." Measured with the research note's script, the lower third of the picture, against main beside it: the vantage sheet's five ground views, orange 37.8% to 15.4%, green and olive 52.3% to 69.5%, lit orange litter 14.0% to 6.7%; the walk's film, ten stills, orange 39.3% to 8.4%, green and olive 41.3% to 68.3%, lit litter 4.5% to 1.1% (Sons of the Forest 3.4%); the pine-stand film, orange 47.9% to 29.4%, green and olive 39.2% to 49.6%, lit litter 9.2% to 6.0%. The walk bench on the RTX 3080, alone on the GPU: 11.59 ms median and 12.48 ms at the 99th percentile against main's 11.42 and 12.14, within budget; meshes on the GPU 1,408 MiB against 960. lodcheck's pops pass every view (worst 0.10%); its black-frame check fails on the path and clearing views on main already since D286 (2.03%, 1.68%; with this 2.11%, 2.00%).
