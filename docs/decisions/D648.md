# D648: A creature has no health bar; how hurt it is shows on the creature

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

No bar over or beside a creature says how much health it has, the boss's included. How near it is to falling is read from the creature itself: bloodied, limping or the like. What that looks like is not designed and is not v0's: until it is, a fight is read from the creature's moves and from what the player's own blows do.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08, on seeing a bar naming the troll with its health: "i dont want health bars btw - we'll probably have it visually show if a mob is low like its bloody or limping or something but thats a future concern".

## Log

- **2026-11-08**: D648 decided (by the developers, as quoted). The bar the agent had put up that day for the foe at hand is taken down again: with a sword drawn against a creature no bar shows; the sparring prototype's two bars (D405) show only while a sparring partner stands.
