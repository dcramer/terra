# D326: Checks for places

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Every place a demo makes is checked by: `cargo xtask fields`, a CPU-only run over the Bevy-free crates writing maps and numbers of a region's rules on a date (height, slope, the growing season's temperature, snow, exposure, lakes, each species' fit, the tree line's height) and answering site queries; one table of places (`tools/sandbox/places.ts`) that `vantage`, `lodcheck`, `film`, `pops`, `collide` and the bench scenes take as `--place`; `look.ts` measuring by band (sky, far land, middle, near); and, for settlements, `cargo xtask life`, a game day at a fast clock read over the remote protocol, failing on anyone stuck, through a wall, sliding their feet or late for their schedule, and counting the people, animals and smoke in view.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "whenever we discover something or make a decision that needs to be consistently validated, we document it and build a tool for it". The rules decide every place, so they are checked first, without the GPU; today every view and route is written for the lush forest alone. See [demos.md](../demos.md#tools-to-build-shared).
