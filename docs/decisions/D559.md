# D559: The hewn mine is the second dungeon theme built, after the barrow halls

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The dungeon themes of D527 are built in this order so far: the barrow halls (today's crypt) first, then the **hewn mine**. The mine is built from the same machinery the crypt uses: the mission graph, placement on the 4 m grid, dressing, the stamp, the run-in and the furnishing by room type (D539), with its own kit, rooms and props as data in its theme file. The crypt stands as it is for now. Its hero props wait for Tripo credits.

What is not settled: the order after the mine (natural caverns or ancient engineered halls), and whether the keep stays a theme of its own.

## Why

The developers, answering the desk's question q-barrow-next (2026-10-28), "Start the mine": the crypt is good for now, and the hewn mine comes next from the same kit, dressing and stamp.

D527 left the order to the developers. The mine shares the crypt's 4 m grid and its built corridors, so it reuses the most of what the crypt has proved. Caverns need a coarser grid and volumes in place of cells.
