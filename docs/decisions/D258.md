# D258: One material system, built: surfaces, modules and stones

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

D251 built as [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md#one-material-system-d251-d258) says: a kind is its material's textures, its pieces by role and its pattern. Pieces are profiles swept along edges, modules (a length of mesh repeated along a line, in whole copies stretched to fit, on a plane or draped over a crease, capped at its ends) and stones set at points, written into the kit's pieces file by `tools/kits`. As first built, every piece was made by a script in Blender from numbers in `art/kits/<kit>/pieces.json`: not modelled, so not yet the hand-made models D251 asks for (goals review 2026-10-14, finding 1). Modelled pieces, from Tripo and cleaned up in Blender, textured in their own UVs, replace the scripted ones role by role, each kept in `art/kits/<kit>/` and read by `tools/kits` in place of numbers; code only places, cuts to length and fits them. Their concepts and models were written and judged by the agent; the nine on the approval sheet (thatch's ridge, verge and eave, shakes' ridge cap, the weathered board, the board valley, slate's ridge tiles, wattle's oak member and the whole log) are accepted as D290's shared reference pieces, the call left to the agent on 2026-10-14 ("Like get it done"). Which pieces are modelled so far is building-plan.md's to say: since 2026-10-14 every roof's ridge, hip, verge and eave, shakes' and slate's valleys and flashing, and wattle's frame, each baked from its model into its kit's atlas (`Piece`). A roof's covering is its cubes' sloped surface in the covering's material, with thickness and relief from its height map, its rows on level lines; its ridges, hips, valleys, eaves, verges, apexes and flashing are its kit's modules and stones, and its rafters, ridge beam and battens modules too (amends D241: no generated courses, caps or boards). A framed kind's members are modules laid by `layout::frames`'s rule (amends D236). Each role a kind dresses takes a piece of its own materials (D215, extended to roofs and frames). Far detail from each module's far mesh. Logs stay generated (D200) and can take a module when the developers choose. Amended by D290: modules and stones are the pattern's, a material's own an override in its pack.

## Why

Mine, building D251 on 2026-10-13: the developers asked for "hand made models ... we are doing what enshrouded does", and Enshrouded's roof galleries show exactly this: a displaced material surface with modelled ridge, verge and eave pieces. Their screenshot of a thatch gable showed what generated coverings gave: a half-disc ridge with sky under it, thin flat verge sheets with a sawtooth, a sawn log end at the apex.
