# D383: How ore shows in a rock

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A rock with ore in it is the plain rock, its own colour and scan, with the ore showing up close as thin rust-stained veins through the stone and the rust their water has carried down its faces below them, strongest on steep faces and fading with distance (`rust` in flora.ron, `rusted` in flora.wesl); from 20 m it is a grey boulder among grey boulders, as weathered ironstone is. No rock is coloured whole for what is in it.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-15, choosing between a whole-boulder colour and a subtle one: "Subtle tint". A boulder tinted iron-red read as salmon granite at the tundra's start, and the orange lichen scan before it as painted coral.
