# D192: Rules for construction pieces

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

[construction-001.md](../proposals/construction-001.md): Enshrouded's way on our cubes, keeping plans and ghosts. The cube is the truth; nothing leaves its cubes but eaves, footings and trims, which may stand out up to an eighth of a cube (cornices, copings, sills, plinths, quoins), drawn only, never colliding and never stopping a neighbour being placed (2026-10-13); any arrangement is drawn; a piece is chosen by its neighbourhood, long members on runs and blocks on the dual grid; plans are the same cubes, drawn as ghosts and walked through, and only what is built collides; doors and windows are props fitted into holes that fit them; the ground outside a building is not changed; nothing floats. Block kinds go to the dual grid only if its spike shows conformed Tripo modules look as good as the stone sections.

## Why

The developers asked for guard rails "similar to how enshrouded does", said "our core needs to be voxels" and that rendering each voxel by its neighbours "seems like a really good system if we can adopt it and keep our build ghosts", asked for every case to be thought through, then answered its questions or left them to me: "make the best default call that we can solve from a technical angle and is a good player experience" (2026-10-12). On trims standing out past their cubes, as Enshrouded's cornices do: "yeah i want it to not be flat, so thats ok to draw stuff" (2026-10-13).
