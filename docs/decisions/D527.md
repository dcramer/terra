# D527: Four dungeon themes on a bigger grid: barrow halls, hewn mine, natural caverns, ancient engineered halls

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Dungeons come in four themes, each a kit reused across many dungeons: **barrow halls** (today's crypt; vaulted burial halls), **hewn mine** (today's flooded mine; drifts, stopes and shafts in carved rock with timber sets), **natural caverns** (carved voxel rock, passages winding to a great cavern) and **ancient engineered halls** (a lost people's monumental halls set in a cavern). The built themes stand on a bigger grid: cells 4 m square, so corridors are 3 m clear between walls a cube thick, and levels 6 m apart, a slab and 5.5 m clear, so a vaulted room is 4.5-5 m high; caverns take a coarser grid of their own (8 m cells, volumes in place of cells). What each theme is built from and its sizes are in [dungeons.md](../dungeons.md#themes-d527); the order they are built in and whether the keep stays a theme of its own are still the developers'.

## Why

Asked on the review desk (q-dungeon-themes) whether to build the four themes proposed from [research/2026-10-26-dungeon-themes.md](../../research/2026-10-26-dungeon-themes.md) and the bigger grid, the developers on 2026-10-26: "Yes, all four, bigger grid". On the door film of the crypt on 3 m cells and 4 m levels, the same day: "this dungeon feels too cramped, it needs to be bigger". Every game the research compared builds its dungeons on a grid about twice ours (Skyrim's 7.3 m footprints and 3.7 m halls, Valheim's 8 m rooms in 4 m steps); ours was the bare minimum for a hall.
