# D219: What lies on the forest floor

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The forest floor has its fourth storey from biomes.md: scanned mossy rocks and boulders, ferns in the shade, stumps, and logs fallen from the forest's own kind of tree, each placed by how it likes the shade and kept off paths and trunks, rocks and logs and stumps stopping a walker, each drawn out to its own distance. Scans come from Poly Haven (CC0) and are made placeable by `tools/blender/scan.py`: each piece welded, cut to a triangle budget with its textures kept, its foot at the origin. Bevy 0.20 loads a glTF's material as a `GltfMaterial`, which is copied into the `StandardMaterial` the scan is drawn with once loaded.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13 agreed to "more kinds of plants: ferns, saplings, fallen logs, rocks". The developers OK'd free and royalty-free assets the same day. The walk bench on the RTX 3080 is 8.6 ms, within budget.
