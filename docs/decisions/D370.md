# D370: A neutral grade, the colour from the light

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The colour grade is neutral everywhere and at every hour, open world and forest alike: its white balance stays at D65, as a camera on its daylight setting, never warmed, cooled or tinted. Warmth and colour come from physically based light and from what things are made of: the direct sun about 5,500 to 6,500 K at noon, warming toward sunrise and sunset through the air (D212); shade lit by the blue sky; light through leaves a little green; the eye's exposure adapting as a camera's would (D216, D286), and by night colour fading as an eye's does (D302's saturation, no longer its cooling). Supersedes D369 and amends D244, D286 and D302, whose grade warmed a low sun, the forest by day and cooled and tinted the night. Measured with matte grey cubes ([research](../../research/colour-balance-2026-10-15.md)): in the open at noon an 18% grey's sunlit face 137,134,133, the direct sun 5,600 K, the sky-lit shade 15,000 K; at 18:00 the sun 3,000 K; under the canopy at noon 129,126,118 (139,125,116 under the warm grade), at 17:30 55,65,66 (67,63,60). The lush forest now measures greener than Sons of the Forest's warm-graded frames (looks check, D356: red/green 1.07 to 0.90 against 1.10, shade red/green 1.01 to 0.59 against 1.26; 14 to 13 of 18 holding), and the lake's red/green stays under Far Cry 5's: those references, both warm-graded games, are to be measured again against real photographs.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "i feel like it should have a neutral grade idk", then "is real life a 'warm grade'? we want close to real life". Before, an 18% grey at noon read sRGB 142,126,124 on the yard and 82,69,67 in the village (red over blue 1.33 and 1.52), five sixths of it the day grade's constant warmth.
