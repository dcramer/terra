# D287: Building's junk checked by meshes and by frames, and ratcheted

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

What a renderer would draw as junk is checked without a person, two ways ([docs/building-drawing.md](../building-drawing.md)). From the meshes, with no GPU, `cargo xtask artifacts` (`terra_build::inspect`) counts faults of each kind in each kit's bases (not finite, black, inward, coplanar, doubled, see-through, a slot at a floor's edge beside a wall or a post among them, under the roof, poking out, buried under a surface's relief) against a checked-in baseline, `crates/terra_build/examples/artifacts-baseline.tsv`: its scenes are the scene registry's (`terra_build::scenes::registry`), where every scene the game ships or a check looks at is registered once (every roof over every plan alone and mixed, the QA cabin, the junctions and roofs-and-walls families, the rectangle in every state, the kit sheet's rows, the previews, the village's buildings, the example base, the castle and the burg), so a scene added is checked with nothing else to do; the full check run before every push (D37) ratchets `--quick`, the scenes the registry gates so (some of every family but the castles, the states, the previews and the example), and fails when any count rises; `cargo xtask artifacts --ratchet` holds every scene registered; `--record` lowers it as fixes land. A fuzz pass (`cargo xtask fuzz`, `scenes::fuzz`) builds small bases at random from seeds, out of the hammer's plans through the checked operations, inspects each, and shrinks a base with a fault to its fewest elements, printed as a test; the full check ratchets seeds 0 to 200 on the same baseline. The drawing audit in `cargo xtask rules` measures z-fighting with the checker's own measure (`inspect::fights`), over the registry's push scenes, and fails on any. From frames on the GPU, at the high and the low preset, `cargo xtask artifacts --frames` holds still views (acne, crawl, mip shimmer), the sun moving (acne, cascades), a glide down a street (a pixel flipping for a frame) and a house going up outside and in (flashes, TAA trails, the picture's brightness jumping) to limits. A room's light probe fades in and out over 0.8 s as the room closes or opens, as engines blend a probe's weight.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "make sure we got no flicker or other junk rendering issues"; the third goals review: 31,710 faults and nothing gating on them. The frames found one: a room's probe dimmed it by two fifths in one frame as its last roof cube went in; the rest were clean at both presets, and stayed so with the sun's shadow biases taken to nothing. The fifth goals review (2026-10-16): 42 faults in wood and stone outside any gate, the commit check holding only the rectangles; and the audit counting as z-fighting a log's daub 2 mm behind its sawn end and a board 7 mm over a floor, which a planted case shows are not. The workflow retro (2026-10-16): faults came back because each check held only the cases someone had listed; registering every scene found 133 faults in the kit sheet's rows no check had seen, and the fuzz pass 90 of 200 seeds with a fault, in nine sorts of junction (the voxel contract's gaps 10 to 19).
