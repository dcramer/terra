# D272: Leaves cut by their picture alone, and lit through by the sky above them

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A leaf's alpha in the main pass is its picture's: Bevy takes a vertex's colour, alpha too, into the base colour that the alpha test cuts by, and the crowns' vertex alpha is how much sky a point sees (D216), while the prepass ignores vertex colours; so where a leaf saw little sky the main pass cut it and the prepass kept its depth, and it stood black against the sky in its own shape. The foliage shader divides that alpha out again. And a leaf passes on, in its own colour, the light that falls on its upper face, as the sky occlusion volume (D270) holds it there: Bevy's own diffuse transmission took the open sky, unshaded by the crowns, and the colour after the underside was darkened.

## Why

Review of 2026-10-14: crowns from below read as "black paper cutouts"; near-black was a fifth of the view looking up, from 3.6% before the steeper crown insides of D269 sent more of their leaves below the cut. With the fix 0.2 to 1.3%.
