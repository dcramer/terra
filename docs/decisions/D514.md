# D514: A swimmer's figure treads, strokes or dives by its swim and lies along its way

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

How swimming (D74, D478) is shown, presentation only (docs/controller.md "What is presentation"): the figure shows one of three looped clips by the mover's swim, treading water (`Swim_Tread_Loop`) going slower than 0.4 m/s, a surface stroke (`Swim_Fwd_Loop`) faster than 0.6 m/s, a dive's stroke (`Swim_Dive_Loop`) with its mouth under water, blended 400 ms into and out of any of them. The clips are made in the water (Uthana's text-to-motion, D532): the tread upright, the strokes lying level with their hips afloat at the man's float line (`art/animation/made.json` `afloat`), so the clip holds the lie (D584). The game only pitches a stroke along its way up or down, to head first straight up or down, eased 95% in half a second about the float height (`terra_walker::swim`); level at the surface, none treading. Its feet are let go while it swims. The filmed lake (`tools/controller/yard.ts`'s lake run, `swim.ts`) measures it: `swim.pose`, the share of swimming frames showing no swim clip or with the torso more than 15 deg off its way; `tools/characters/swim_clips.test.ts` holds the clips lying, kicking from the hip and afloat.

## Why

Shipped swimmers are authored lying in the water and pitched by their velocity or view (Unreal's swimming movement mode, Subnautica's divers), and a surface swim eases in as a wader leaves its feet (Valheim, Enshrouded). A stroke keyed standing and tilted down read as kneeling and pedalling (research/2026-10-29-why-the-skeleton-moves-wrong.md; the developer: "swimming looks stupid"). Taking the way from the body's velocity needs nothing the server does not already send.
