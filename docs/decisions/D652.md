# D652: State is sent as the most recent, by area and priority within a byte budget; world changes as ordered operations kept by region

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A thing on the wire is an entity made of parts, each a small versioned record its rules crate encodes. The server sends each client, every second tick, one unreliable packet of at most 1,200 bytes holding the parts that client is owed (its acknowledged version is older), picked by a priority that grows with waiting, kind and nearness; an entity leaving a client's interest is a version too. Interest is by cells of a hashed grid (32 m, a 128 m radius to start, one cell of hysteresis). World changes (D21, D29) go reliable and ordered, numbered in their 256 m region's record; a client entering a region is sent what follows the number it holds, which is also how a late joiner catches up. The design is [netcode.md](../netcode.md).

## Why

Most-recent-state by priority (Tribes, Halo Reach, Unreal; Fiedler's priority accumulator) bounds the bytes whatever a scene holds and costs nothing for things at rest, which a large world of mostly still things aimed at many players (D52) needs; a whole-snapshot delta (Quake 3, Source) grows with the scene. Encoding a changed part once and sharing the bytes makes the cost by what changed, not by clients times entities. Records by region are multiplayer.md's "per-area records of change". The numbers are first values to measure, not tuned.
