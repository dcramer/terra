# D271: Plants make way for the camera and the player

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D216 and D264 for a camera that follows a figure. As shipped open worlds do (`terra_world::parting`, research/foliage-near-camera.md): **foliage interaction**, shrubs, ferns, conifer sprays and grass within a body's reach (its radius, 0.4 m, and 0.3 m more since D274), between its feet and head, are pushed out round it and a little down in the vertex shader, by as much as they bend freely (from their wind weights, a tree's trunk hardly), in shadows too, and from where each body stood a frame ago for motion vectors; and a **camera-obstruction fade**, leaves (not bark) at a third-person camera's lens and, since D274, whole plants between the eye and the figure's chest (once a tube round that line), dither out in the moving pattern of D214, in the forward pass and the prepass alike, only in the main camera's own view, so shadows keep them; grass at the lens shrinks into its roots. One small buffer every foliage and grass material shares holds the eye, the figure it follows (`CameraSubject`, set by the walker's camera) and the eight bodies nearest it that part plants (`PartsPlants`, every player), written in place each frame so no material changes. The camera's boom meets trunks and rocks since D274; shrubs are not solid (D243).

## Why

A review of the lush forest's film (5.5/10), its first gap: 8 of 20 frames with a shrub or spruce over 30% of the screen at the lens, one all green, the player running through a hazel as if it were not there, fronds sweeping the lens. D216's fade was there, narrowed to 0.3 to 0.9 m by D264; the leaves filling the frames were 1 to 3 m from the lens. Measured on the film against main filmed at the same time (80 frames, every 0.5 s): near-lens foliage over 30% of the screen in 11 frames, now 6, over 80% in 2, now none, its mean share 13.7%, now 9.3%; the player lost in foliage in about 10, now none; frames whose difference from the last is over 19 (of 255), 112, now 83. The walk bench was contended (other sessions' sandboxes on the same GPU); on its quietest runs, beside main's made at the same time on the RTX 3080, the GPU's frame is 10.4 ms, now 11.0 ms (main pass 4.46 to 4.97, prepass 1.48 to 1.59), within the 16.7 ms budget: pushing and the buffer cost 0.1 ms, the fade's sums nothing measurable, and the rest is the forest seen past the leaves it clears. lodcheck passes (worst 0.05%).
