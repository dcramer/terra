# D253: The hammer leads with building by hand

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The hammer's menu starts with building by hand: *Block* first and in hand by default, with its shape, material and brush, then *Carve*, *Change material* and taking apart; *Room*, *Wall* and *Add a roof* follow as conveniences (D226).

## Why

Left to me by the developers on 2026-10-13, when the goals review found *Block* seventh, after *Room*, *Roof* and *Wall*: "do whatever is logical and good for a player experience".
