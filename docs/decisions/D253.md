# D253: The hammer leads with building by hand

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The hammer's menu starts with building by hand: *Block* first and in hand by default, with its shape, material and brush, then *Carve*, *Change material* and taking apart; *Room*, *Wall* and *Add a roof* follow as conveniences (D226). Amended by D491 (2026-10-25): Space Engineers 2's construction loop, [construction-003](../proposals/construction-003.md): a catalogue of prefab parts made of cubes, snapped to the part set against, a bigger part always a ghost first and the one aimed at built in any order once what holds it stands, a single cube placed and built at once from what is carried, costs by mode (D494). The hammer's menu leads with the parts.

## Why

Left to me by the developers on 2026-10-13, when the goals review found *Block* seventh, after *Room*, *Roof* and *Wall*: "do whatever is logical and good for a player experience".
