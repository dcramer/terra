# D535: Pose fix-ups after the clips: the hanging hands kept off the hips, the head kept on the aim

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

After every clip and hold has posed a body (after `AnimationSystems` and `Fitted`, before propagation), terra_walker fixes what any clip gets wrong on our bodies by reading the pose, so it holds over whichever clip plays (the library's idle, a bought one, a blow) with no clip changed. Each turns bones about the body's axes at their joints (`terra_walker::bend`, Unreal's "Transform (Modify) Bone", shared with the staged figures' bends) from the clip's pose each frame, never on top of its own last turn.

- **The hands off the hips** (`terra_walker::arms`): an upper arm whose hand hangs by the hip (up to 0.12-0.22 m over the hip joint, within 0.20-0.32 m ahead or behind it) nearer the body's middle than twice the hip joint's distance from it is turned out at the shoulder just enough to clear it, at most 25°. A hand already clear (the guard's, a blow's in front or above) is left as the clip has it. What the hand holds counts as the hand does (ALS's arm spacing counts a held item): a look on a hand socket gives the few points that bound it (its `Reach`, each of 8 slices along its handle's extremes), placed as it is held, carried or used, so an axe's haft carried past the thigh turns the arm out until it clears, with the item's `carry_tilt` and every clip kept.
- **The head on the aim** (`terra_walker::gaze`, the look-at shipped engines lay after the clips, [research](../../research/2026-10-26-head-look-at.md)): last of these, after the foot lock and the arms, the neck and head are turned about the body's up so the head looks within 8° of the way the body faces (its aim: the foe it is locked on, else the camera's ahead), 40% of the turn on `neck_01` and 60% on `Head`, at most 70°, let go as the head tilts 35-60° off upright (a roll). Yaw alone: the clip keeps its nod, and the chest, shoulders and every arm are left as the clip swings them. A full correction each frame from the clip's pose, not a follow that eases after it, so a blow cannot yank the head. Every walker body with a `neck_01` and `Head` gets it, the partner too.

## Why

Combat round 33 (2026-10-26). The developer, after playing: "the idle animation still looks stupid and the guys arms clip into the body". The composed pose (D530) measured the sheathed walk's and idle's right fingers 6.2 and 5.6 cm into the right thigh (`film.pose.arms_in_body`, bar 2 cm): the library's clips were made on the mannequin. The idle may be replaced by a bought or generated one (D532), so the fix must not be baked into `Idle_Loop` (a `hold.py` over or a hold laid at run time would also freeze the walk's arm swing): a fix-up after the clips that turns an arm out only when its hand is at the hip fits any clip and leaves every clear pose alone, as the staged figures' bends do for a troll's belly with a fixed turn.

The head, the same round's brief after playing: "my head turns like 45 degree to the left when aiming forward and swinging". The composed pose measured it 40.5° left of the aim at worst (the backhand), 35° on average through the blows, 26° on guard (`film.pose.head_yaw_blows`, bar 15°): the clips turn the head with the chest. Re-keying every blow's head would have to be done again for each new take; a look-at after the clips holds over all of them, as Godot's `LookAtModifier3D`, Unreal's Look At node and Unity's `SetLookAtWeight` do.
