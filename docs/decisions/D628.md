# D628: The iso experiment's art comes from Tripo, its people on the game's skeleton and clips, made game-ready in Node by one command a stage

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

For the 2.5D isometric experiment ([tools/iso-art](../../tools/iso-art/README.md)), a hero, creature, NPC, prop or piece of scenery goes concept, model, rig, animate or convert, export, check, each a command of `tools/iso-art/iso-art.ts` over the Tripo CLI. Each character is one mesh with its gear, modelled and rigged by Tripo. **People play the game's clips** (amended 2026-11-08): the hero in play is the game's own body, armour and move set (terra_walker's character and `sword.shielded`, iso-hero, D635); a biped of iso's cast is Tripo's rig file (its 4K textures, never Tripo's `export --texture-size` copy, which breaks the conversion) put on the game's skeleton by `tools/characters/finish_character.sh` (`TEXTURES=1024`, its kind's size, cut after the conversion), then `iso-art.ts convert` keeps the game's clips under iso's names (`kinds.ts` GAME_CLIPS: idle, walk, run, attack, a sword's or `--unarmed` a punch, hit, death), drops the rest, centres the walk and run over the body and places it life size, facing +Z, on y=0. **Any other body keeps Tripo's rig and V3 presets as they are**: a creature gets Tripo's one walk (`march` for serpentine and aquatic); Tripo has no hit preset (`hurt` is 13.8 s lying wounded from its first frame), so a hit is the game's flinch and hit-stop, or a `--prompt` motion at 20 credits. Export reads and writes the GLB's JSON and binary chunk in Node, with no Blender and no dependency: it names the clips ours, takes the walk's and run's travel out of the pelvis (the drift end against start, as Mixamo's and Unreal's in-place cuts), cuts a clip to its `window` (the attack to 0.75 to 2.75 s of Tripo's 6.6 s slash), makes each loop a loop (cut to the stride whose ends meet best and the rest spread over it, as Unity's loop match and Loop Pose), and puts it under a root node life size, facing +Z, on y=0. A triangle budget is one a pixel of the asset's box at the closest camera, between 1,000 and its kind's cap (hero 24k, creature 24k, NPC 16k, prop 8k, scenery 20k: each kind's share of about a million triangles a frame by how many are on screen), over it decimated or retopologised. The game's copies are `assets/models/iso/NAME.glb`; each asset's ledger `art/iso/NAME/asset.json` holds each stage's credits, minutes and result; credits are read from tools/tripo/README.md's table, and a spending stage refuses before spending when the balance is short. Every check reads the GLB in Node without a GPU; the tool depends on no spike (D133).

## Why

The developer asked for "a replicable art pipeline" for the isometric experiment. Shipped iso ARPGs play one mesh a character with its gear from a camera 15 to 25 m off, where a figure is 100 to 200 px tall ([research/2026-11-05-iso-arpg-art-pipeline.md](../../research/2026-11-05-iso-arpg-art-pipeline.md)), so Tripo's own rig and presets were tried first as they came, helped by what the game adds. Tripo's files showed the rest on 2026-11-05: its in-place export keeps only its `root` joint still while the pelvis walks 2.8 m; its slash returns slowly to guard for 3 s an ARPG does not wait for; its run is 1.25 s of nearly two strides whose ends are 15 to 23% of the height apart, while one stride inside it meets within 4 to 6%. No studio publishes a polygon figure, so budgets come from pixels: past one visible triangle a covered pixel the GPU shades 2x2 quads without a pixel more of shape; the caps are an estimate for a bench of the ruin scene to confirm (D185). Whether iso characters should keep main Terra's budgets instead is D630's.

The people moved to the game's clips on 2026-11-08. D635 made iso one game with the main one ("one game, just make sure its modularized enough to not collide"), so its hero is the game's body and moves, called, never copied; and the developer asked for the cast's "better animations ... (no auto magic)" than Tripo's presets. main Terra's conversion (convert_rig.py, the character skill) already puts a Tripo rig on our skeleton; the scavenger through it passed iso's 30 checks, where Tripo's free 1K export of the same rig turned bones up to 152.7 deg and failed its run loop and death. A creature has no game skeleton to go onto, so it keeps Tripo's.
