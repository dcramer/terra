# D646: The troll's own blows: a stomp and a thrown boulder

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

The troll, v0's boss (D590), fights with a roar, a smash and a sweep, one blow at a time on whoever is nearest. The developers ask for blows that are its own: a great stomp, and a boulder picked up and thrown. Recommended, not built: the stomp first (one clip, a blow that lands on everyone within a ring of it, dust and a shake of the view), then the throw (one clip, a boulder from the rock scans, a thrown thing with an arc: the first blow a creature deals from afar). Their clips made by the text-to-motion pipeline the sword's clips came from, retargeted to the troll's skeleton, before anything is bought; a premade pack only if those do not read as heavy, and then one whose licence allows its files in a public repository (Mixamo's does not).

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08: "the troll should have some cool combat stuff but we might need premade assets for that? like a big stomp, maybe picking up a boulder and throwing it?" A thought out loud, not a decision (D412): which blows, how they are told and what they do are the developers' to settle.

## Log

- **2026-11-08**: D646 opened (by the agent, from the developers' question): the troll's stomp and thrown boulder, recommended in that order with generated clips first; nothing is built on it.
