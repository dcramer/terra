# D558: A campfire's ground depends on its camp: a new fire sits on grass, an old camp's ring wears to earth and ash

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A fire just lit sits on the ground as it was, grass and all, round its stones. An old camp, a fire burnt in one place time after time, has worn its ring to trampled earth and ash a metre or two round it, fading out into the grass. So the worn ground is the camp's, not the fire's: how far it reaches and how bare it is follow how long and how often the place has been a camp, and the same campfire model stands in both.

What is not settled: how a camp's age is counted (fires lit, time burnt, people round it), how fast the ring wears and grows back, and whether trampled paths lead off it. How it is drawn is technical: today the model's own ash bed out to 0.8 m (D513), which an old camp's wider ring outgrows.

## Why

The developers, answering the desk's question q-camp-ground (2026-10-28), "Depends on the camp": a new fire sits on grass; an old camp's ring wears to trampled earth and ash a metre or two round it, fading into the grass.

Realistic but fun (D122): a player reads a place's history off its ground, a ring of bare earth telling a camp used for years from a fire lit last night. The harsh reviews asked for a wider worn patch against RDR2's camps (D513); this says when the ground is worn and when it is not.
