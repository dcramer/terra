# D375: The agents' routine

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every agent and lead session follows the routine in `AGENTS.md`. At the start, the hook fast-forwards the main checkout if it is clean, brings the shared seed build up to date and seeds the worktree (D183); if any of it fails, `cargo xtask doctor` first. While working, `git pull --rebase origin main` before each push; commits run the quick check and pushes the full one, never with `--no-verify` (D37); log lines go in `docs/decisions-log.md` (D376). At the end, the commit confirmed on origin/main, then `cargo xtask worktrees --done` (D259). A lead session runs at most three or four agents at once.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-16, after agents spent much of their time building from nothing, rebasing over the decisions log and waiting on the full check: "part of the routine somehow is gonna be keeping main updated, and shared build etc", and they asked for it written into `AGENTS.md`. At most three or four agents, so builds and measurements stay fast and trustworthy.
