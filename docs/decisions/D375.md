# D375: The agents' routine

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every agent and lead session follows the routine in `AGENTS.md`. At the start, the hook fast-forwards the main checkout if it is clean, brings the shared seed build up to date and seeds the worktree (D183); if any of it fails, `cargo xtask doctor` first. Landing is `git pull --rebase origin main`, then `git push origin HEAD:main`; the hooks only format and lint, never skipped with `--no-verify` (D37); log lines go in `docs/decisions-log.md` (D376). At the end, the commit confirmed on origin/main, then `cargo xtask worktrees --done` (D259). At most three or four agents build or check at once, subagents included (2026-10-19, the developers: "subagents count in the agent limit").

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-16, after agents spent much of their time building from nothing, rebasing over the decisions log and waiting on the full check: "part of the routine somehow is gonna be keeping main updated, and shared build etc", and they asked for it written into `AGENTS.md`. At most three or four agents, so builds and measurements stay fast and trustworthy.
