# D639: Host and Join on the title screen: one connection kept through the load, a profile beside the worlds

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The title screen offers **Host** (a page of the player's name and their worlds: choosing one plays it and opens it to others on UDP port 46464) and **Join** (the player's name, the host's address as typed, the port only when it is not that one). A join is one connection: the host's welcome names its land (seed and size), the joining game makes that land under its loading screen and plays it unsaved, and its link is kept all the while, heard and answered each frame with no tick walked. The host's first word is a greeting with no body; the joining game says it is ready on its first walked tick, its land made, and only then is its body made and it welcomed with it, so nobody sees a body stand idle while a game loads. Who the player is (name, identity secret, the address last joined) is `player.ron` beside the worlds, the secret from the operating system's randomness, made once (D592). Whatever ends a session not by the player's choice brings their title screen back saying why in words: nobody answered, another version, a full world, the host left, the connection lost.

## Why

The developers' v0 session: "They host it; a friend joins from their own title screen." Valheim and Enshrouded host from the world list and join by address the same way. One connection kept, not a first to ask the seed and a second to play: the welcome already carries the realm (D651's second seam), a second handshake would show the others a player coming and going, and Unreal's and Source's clients likewise connect, are told the level, load it and only then play. The order is Unreal's and Source's: connect, be told the level, load it, then join (`terra_net`'s `Server::greet`, `Up::Ready`, `Event::Ready`). How the pages look and read is the developers' to change (D579): this is the least that makes the session's second step work.
