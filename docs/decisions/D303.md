# D303: The model contract

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Every model the game places obeys one checked contract ([art-style.md](../art-style.md#the-model-contract)): its catalogue entry states its real size (`dims`, metres) and its file is that size within 2% or a centimetre; glTF's axes, +Y up and its front +Z; its origin a defined anchor by kind (a floor prop's base middle, a leaf's hinge foot, a wall-hung prop's back, a frame's opening bottom middle, a kit piece's grid place, D208); named sockets as `socket.<name>` empties (a leaf's `hinge` and `handle`, a seat's `seat`) that code uses; and a door's or shutters' leaf fills the first hole it fits. `tools/props` makes models so from `prop.json`; `cargo xtask contract` checks them, in `cargo xtask check`; the workbench draws each one's box, origin and sockets. No per-model offset, turn or scale in code or catalogue: the catalogue's `size`, `height`, `lift`, `seat` and a fitting's `hinge` side are gone, refused by the catalogue's reader. Amended 2026-10-15 (by the agent, for the fourth goals review: "'no scale in code' contradicts the stretched doors and frame members"): fitting by sockets is the contract's one stretch. A fitted model is stretched only along the axes its kind fits along, by what the layout fits it to, never by a number of its own: a leaf across and up its hole from its hinge, a kit's module along its line from its `start` socket to its `end` in whole copies; generated members (logs, frame members) are made to their line, not stretched models. Every kit piece states its size and the kit checker holds it: a module its `dims` and sockets, and now a corner stone (quoins, jamb stones) its `dims`, its dressed faces' box from the wall's corner at its origin.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "one requirement of all these is dimensions/center points/etc because otherwise props like doors or windows will never render correctly (or position correctly)", and "thats why its important that these kits are cleanly designed so each one doesnt have to be customized by the game". Shipped pipelines hold meshes to a pivot and socket convention (Unreal's pivots and sockets) for the same reason.
