# D384: Ambient encounters staged to be seen

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A meeting ahead of a player on the move (D378) is staged where it shows: within half a second of setting off and then every 3 to 6 s, at the best of 18 spots tried, out to one side where it passes through the open third of the picture, 15° to 30° off the camera's way (a small animal 16 to 24 m ahead and 4 to 6 m aside, a big one 30 to 40 m and 6 to 9 m), never on the way where the player's body hides it; a spot counts only if the camera sees the animal's middle or either end from where it will be at both of those angles (no trunk, rock, log, crown or ground between), weighed by that, the light (the canopy's shade) and bare or short ground; every third meeting on the move, the first among them, is with a big kind (a body 0.3 m wide or more: a deer, a boar) where one lives, the others with the small, a kind met near already much the less likely again; in a group of the larger sort its kind keeps; or, half the time and three times in four for a big kind, a crossing: a group already put up, from 28-36 m ahead and 8-12 m aside, just inside the trees, running or flying across the way, timed by its speed and the player's to cross 10 m ahead of them, kept only if the camera will see it there and halfway or three quarters of the way to it; a few met standing, the way the player faces; within a budget of its own, so the cells round a player, full, never leave nothing to meet. What follows is the animals' own: a runner that notices someone coming within two and a half times its flight distance stops and watches them (a hare sitting tight to 5 m); a shy animal startled by someone coming on breaks across their way and on ahead (four times in ten, with 6 m of lead, across to the other side), a climber to the trunk that way, a flier into the air that way, onto a crown or, in a town, a roof's ridge (`Perches`, the town's ridges), where town birds are also put out sitting in rows. Each animal is a little bigger or smaller than another of its kind and keeps its own beat at rest; a seasonal coat (`coat` in `creatures.ron`, `play::coats`) follows the date and the climate, brown in summer and white from mid-October to the end of November and back from April into May, earlier and further on colder land, each on its own days. A film measures it: twice a second of a shot, the animals drawn and not hidden (by what the world knows stands between: trunks, rocks, logs, crowns, the ground, the player, grass over most of an animal), each by its skeleton's span on the picture (`play::sighted`), summed per film by the trailer.

## Why

The review of round 1 of the biomes' life (2026-10-16) found the animals met ahead 3 to 12 px for a moment and half hidden in cover: "the systems work; the staging doesn't". Red Dead Redemption 2 and Far Cry stage ambient wildlife near, in the open, crossing the player's line of sight or running alongside, in groups, with time in frame; prey breaks at an angle to what comes at it. Real mountain hares and arctic foxes moult by the day's length, their timing and how white they go by the climate. A measure of what a film sees replaces squinting at frames (D69). The forest's review (2026-10-17, 4/10) found no deer in 20 s, five to seven animals drawn but hidden behind trunks, and boars put on the walk's line behind the walker's back: spots were tried near the axis and checked from where the camera stood when they were staged, not from where it would be.
