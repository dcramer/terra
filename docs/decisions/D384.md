# D384: Ambient encounters staged to be seen

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A meeting ahead of a player on the move (D378) is staged where it shows: within half a second of setting off and then every 3 to 6 s, at the best of 18 spots tried, out to one side where it passes through the open third of the picture, 15° to 30° off the camera's way (a small animal 16 to 24 m ahead and 4 to 6 m aside, a big one 30 to 40 m and 6 to 9 m), never on the way where the player's body hides it, and a shy kind moved out from it past the distance it bolts at, by its group's spread and a metre (a heron 13 m, a hind with her calf 12.7 m), where it is still of its niche and seen, so one keeping to the way passes it by and it watches or walks off; a spot counts only if the camera sees the animal's middle or either end from where it will be at both of those angles (no trunk, rock, log, crown or ground between), weighed by that, the light (the canopy's shade) and bare or short ground; every third meeting on the move, the first among them, is with a big kind (a body 0.3 m wide or more: a deer, a boar) where one lives, the others with the small, a kind met near already much the less likely again; in a group of the larger sort its kind keeps; or, one time in five and three in ten for a big kind, a crossing: a group already put up, from 34-44 m ahead and 8-12 m aside, just inside the trees, going calmly across the way (a brisk walk, its walk 1.6 times quicker, at least 1.2 m/s; a flier at 0.7 of its speed) and breaking into a run only once the player comes within its bolting distance, timed by its speed and the player's to cross 18 m ahead of them and walk on 6 s into the trees, kept only if the camera will see it there and halfway or three quarters of the way to it; a few met standing, the way the player faces; within a budget of its own, so the cells round a player, full, never leave nothing to meet. What follows is the animals' own: a runner that notices someone coming within two and a half times its flight distance stops and watches them (a hare sitting tight to 5 m) once they are within one and a half times it; farther off one grazing feeds on, head down three times in four and looking up between bouts, as feeding deer share their time with vigilance, and one that can graze stands idle a quarter of its rest, not two fifths (lake film r7: every animal beside the runner's way stood head up toward him, as if posed); a shy animal startled by someone coming on runs at three quarters of its full run (a flier 0.85 of its speed), only one hurt or frightened by a dragon or a fire flat out (`creatures::gait`), and breaks across their way and on ahead (three times in twenty, with 6 m of lead, across to the other side), a climber to the trunk that way, a flier into the air that way, onto a crown or, in a town, a roof's ridge (`Perches`, the town's ridges), where town birds are also put out sitting in rows. Each animal is a little bigger or smaller than another of its kind and keeps its own beat at rest; a seasonal coat (`coat` in `creatures.ron`, `play::coats`) follows the date and the climate, brown in summer and white from mid-October to the end of November and back from April into May, earlier and further on colder land, each on its own days. A film measures it: twice a second of a shot, the animals drawn and not hidden (by what the world knows stands between: trunks, rocks, logs, crowns, the ground, the player, grass over most of an animal), each by its skeleton's span on the picture (`play::sighted`), summed per film by the trailer. A shy one at rest that someone comes within its kind's flight distance of first makes off at a walk, straight away and watching them (a deer walking off, a duck paddling, a heron stalking along the shallows); it runs, climbs or takes wing only once they come within 0.4 of that distance, and no nearer than 4 m, or when hurt or frightened (`creatures::gait::BOLTS_WITHIN`), and any kind, not only a runner, stops to watch one coming from farther off. Amended 2026-10-18 (lake film r8, the developer: "animals need to fucking chill there too"): an animal watches and stares only at someone whose way, kept to over the next 8 s, comes within its bolting distance, or who stands about; one passed wide of goes on with its day (as deer beside a trail feed on while walkers pass, Taylor and Knight 2003, Stankowich 2008), and none wanders toward a player within 1.2 times its flight distance. Amended again 2026-10-18 (lake film r9: a stag met crossing 10 m ahead broke into a run within its flight distance, dashed back across the runner's way and stood beside him at the end; logged each second): a crossing is the rarer meeting, passes well ahead and walks on, and keeps to a walk until someone comes within its bolting distance. Amended a third time 2026-10-18 (lake film r10: a fox met ahead sat square to the lens through the last 2.5 s): a look at someone coming lasts 1.2 s at the most; then, them still within one and a half times its flight distance, it walks off from them, and farther off one that does not graze (a fox) goes about its business three times in four, a few metres its own way and never toward them, as a grazer feeds; and each animal is put out facing its own way.

## Why

The review of round 1 of the biomes' life (2026-10-16) found the animals met ahead 3 to 12 px for a moment and half hidden in cover: "the systems work; the staging doesn't". Red Dead Redemption 2 and Far Cry stage ambient wildlife near, in the open, crossing the player's line of sight or running alongside, in groups, with time in frame; prey breaks at an angle to what comes at it. Real mountain hares and arctic foxes moult by the day's length, their timing and how white they go by the climate. A measure of what a film sees replaces squinting at frames (D69). The forest's review (2026-10-17, 4/10) found no deer in 20 s, five to seven animals drawn but hidden behind trunks, and boars put on the walk's line behind the walker's back: spots were tried near the axis and checked from where the camera stood when they were staged, not from where it would be. The developer on the forest film's second round (2026-10-17): "forest is mostly done, just make sure animals are running a bit slower, not full on deer-in-headlight mode"; every crossing had galloped at its kind's full run, and every startled animal too, which reads as panic, where Red Dead Redemption 2's deer walk across the trail and bound off only as the rider comes near. Of the lake's film the developer said: "lake - animals need to fucking chill there too"; its ducks and heron took wing as the runner came within 14 and 25 m, the flight path the calmer crossings never touched. Its second lake film (2026-10-18) opened on a heron taking wing: met 4 to 6 m beside the runner's way, it was passed well inside the 10 m it bolts at.
