# D364: Frozen ground rolls: hummocky moraine and kettles

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D337 and D341. Where the ground is frozen at depth (the patterned ground's strength, D341), the ground also rolls as the ice sheets left it: low ridges up to 14 m some 500 m apart and hummocks and kettle hollows up to 7 m some 85 m apart (`GeneratedPlanet::rolling_m`, terra_voxel), fading in with the frozen ground, so the forest, the mountain lake's basin and the town's fields keep their ground. The land's rules do the rest: kettles fill as ponds, the heath's communities follow the hummocks (lichen on the tops, sedge and moss in the hollows), snow lies in their lees. The tundra's trailer run goes north up the valley between the snowy ranges on September 12, the plateau bare of snow and the fells white.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15, of the tundra's film: "too flat, too bare, needs more diversity of some sorts and more mountains". The plateau the tundra's query found rose 24 m within 300 m: the hills' noise is some 47 km across on the 30 km world and its finer detail under a metre, so nothing lay between. With the moraine the 1.6 km round the place rises and falls 125 m, with some 6% of it in kettle ponds, and the heath map's mosaic follows the hummocks; fells stand 300 m and more over it in six of eight directions within 6 km, so the skyline was a matter of where the run looks (the query's sites with nearer fells were colder, lichen and snow by September).
