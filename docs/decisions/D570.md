# D570: The sparring partner parries only when its parry is timed

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The sparring partner parries a blow only when its own parry timing lines up, by the same rule a player's parry follows (combat.md: a parry raised just as a blow starts); a fresh guard alone never turns a blow. So a heavy timed into its guard can land when the partner mistimes, and the duel shows both a landed heavy and a parry.

## Why

In the duel of 2026-10-28 the partner parried any blow landing in the first moments of its guard, a window that restarted each time it raised the guard, so the player's heavy was always turned. The developers, asked whether that is what they want from a sparring partner, picked the recommendation, 2026-10-28: "Parry only when timed".
