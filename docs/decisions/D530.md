# D530: Combat is also checked on the game's composed pose, from the duel's record

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The duel's fight record (`--fight-log` in a `--duel` run, `terra_creatures::melee::record`) carries, every frame, the pose each body shows: every bone of its model in the model's space and every held look's place, read in `Last`, after every pose writer (the clips and the hold layer, bends, the foot lock, the lift, a strap) and the transforms' propagation (`melee::pose`); a fighter's under `pose`, the player's before the draw under `sheathed`. `tools/sandbox/duel/pose.ts` measures that pose with the move check's geometry from its home (`glb.ts`, `clash.ts`, `shield_path.ts`): the player's head off its aim (the game camera's look) through its blows, the board's middle over the hips with the block not held, and the strapped board against the forearm it rides (the forearm off its plane, off its back, the hand strap off the fist). Each is a `--film` row of the combat scorecard (`film.pose.*`, D452). The duel script sheathes after the fight and walks, so the record covers the sheathed idle and walk beside the guard, the strafes, the partner's walk-in and every move, the fight's own frames unchanged.

## Why

Combat round 30 (2026-10-26). The developer played the game and saw, none of it caught: "my head turns like 45 degree to the left when aiming forward and swinging", "the shield is raised up even when not holding down the block button", "the guys arms clip into the body", and on the heater "you'd have this wrapped around the arm in real life and in some of your stances its not uniform with the arm geometry". The move check (D447) measures each clip alone on the exported body; what the game shows is that clip under the stance's hold (D458), bent, foot-locked and with the shield strapped (D504), so a check must read the pose after all of them. The fight record already ran in `Last` with the camera and the shield's middle; a pose there is what was drawn, with no second run. Bones in the model's space are what `glb.ts`'s `posed` gives, so the existing measures apply unchanged.
