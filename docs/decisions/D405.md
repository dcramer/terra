# D405: A playable sword-and-shield prototype comes before the movement system's layers

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Before the layers of docs/movement.md are built, a prototype in `terra_game` (`play/melee`) lets the developers feel sideways movement, jumps, rolls and a sword used to attack, block and parry, against a sparring partner, in the yard and in `make play`. Its moves are data (`assets/data/moves/sword_and_shield.ron`): a light chain, a heavy blow, a roll with i-frames, a block whose first 0.18 s is a parry, the partner's blows. Their active frames are measured from the clips (`tools/characters/phases.ts`). Shortcuts it takes, to be replaced by the layers: drive curves stand in for root motion; a guard is judged by facing, not the shield's volume; a body's hurt zone is one capsule; a clip is picked by the dominant direction, not a blend space; the sword and shield are given on drawing, not taken from the inventory; its health is apart from Vitals.

## Why

The developers: "FOr movement, mostly focus on getting a prototype of sideways movement, jumps, rolls and using actual sword for defense/parry/attack working. I need to experience it to give feedback."
