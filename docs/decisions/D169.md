# D169: Recruits' bodies, needs and death

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Recruits are generally much like a player: health, stamina and mana, hunger, thirst and body temperature. Cold or heat out of comfort slows their work. They sleep at night and need a bed, which they claim on their own, as they do work; without one, their stamina stays very low, so they work very slowly all the time; potentially they sleep on the ground, badly rested, getting a little stamina back (Leaning). They die when their health runs out; they also die of hunger and thirst, but slowly, so it is a hard mistake to make. A way to revive them may come later. When one dies, what they had drops where they fell; graves may come later. What recruits forage for themselves is poor food; cooked food is better, filling more hunger, and may give other boosts. Monsters likely do not form inside an outpost, but danger needs to be close to home: protecting the outpost, with walls, guards or otherwise, is part of having one.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "they'll need basic survival things like food/water … it'd be nice if they had to sleep? could be they just sleep at night … they'll need to deal w/ temperature as well as itll slow down their work … stamina might actually be useful (along with mana) if we need them in combat. i guess npcs generally should be pretty similar to a player"; "they'd die from not having health. maybe we'll allow a way to revive them in the future … if we 'spawn monsters' we likely wont spawn them 'in our town' and part of having this tribe/village you build is you will need to actually protect it … they SHOULD also die of starvation and thirst but it should be protracted and a hard mistake to make". Then: hunger and thirst, once empty, "slowly eat away at health over days, not crazy fast", weakening them; foraged food might be poor and cooked food better, unsure, then: "yeah thats my thinking, it'd basically give you more hunger and maybe other boosts"; graves "could be a feature addition later, for now they could just drop their items". Then: "lets just say sleep at night for now is the plan. npcs will need a bed. if they dont have one they're going to have basically no stamina which means they'll work very slow continuously", and "beds they can auto claim or something? similar to how the tribe itself auto sims"; then "we could let npcs sleep on the ground potentially … they wouldn't be very well rested but it could let them reclaim a tiny bit of stamina". Replaces the recommendation that recruits are downed before dead. See [tribe.md](../tribe.md#needs).
