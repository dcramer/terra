# D235: The lush forest's kinds of tree

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The lush forest grows three kinds (`experiments/lush/species.rs`), 21 variants: eight grown oaks in three forms (tall forest oaks with a clear bole a third of their height, broad ones, ones forked low into two stems), three young oaks, four Scots pines and three Norway spruces, and three young spruces. An oak's trunk runs on up into its crown, its five to nine great limbs leaving it one by one in a spiral, each crooked (kinked as it grows), branching along its length and again into twigs; its leaves are clumps along the outer branches and at their ends, each great limb's a mass whose cards' normals lean out of it as well as out of the crown, the outline bulging in a few lobes. A pine has a clean bole under a high ragged crown of up-turned whorls, a spruce a cone of drooping whorls to the ground. Leaves are cards of the shared atlas (D222): a new oak branch tip of about forty small leaves and a spruce spray, made as the other cards were, and the pine bough. Oaks' bark is two scans, Bark Willow 02 and Bark Willow, by variant; pines' and spruces' Pine Bark, growing less moss, which now runs in streaks down the bark. Pines grow in groves where broad noise is high, spruces in their hollows, oaks elsewhere. Veteran oaks stand at glades' edges, no two within 40 m: their trunk and limbs are modelled (two Tripo models from concepts drawn to the style guide, textured, cut to 9,000 triangles), their crowns grown as cards at the clumps `tools/blender/tips.py` finds along their outer branches by distance from the foot over the surface, so a model brings the gnarl, burrs and roots a grown tube cannot, and the leaves still thin card by card (D223). Leaf cards fade out, a pixel at a time, as they turn edge-on to the eye, as SpeedTree's do, rather than show as smears.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "the trees look dumb", "let's do more tree types ... I would assume there can be other types like some pines", "see if the bark can be better textured", with The Forest and ARK as inspiration. The oak split into all its limbs at one point, its crown a ball of leaves to the ground, each card two to four metres across carrying a dozen leaves the size of plates, clumps only at twig ends. 23 models in all, told apart by kind, form and height, each drawn turned, scaled and tinted where it stands. Measured on the RTX 3080 against the commit before: the walk bench 11.0 ms median (99th percentile 12.1) against 11.2 (12.9); `lodcheck` worst frame 0.05% on the forest floor against 0.33%, 0.01% on the path as before.
