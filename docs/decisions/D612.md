# D612: A sun's cascades measure levels of detail from their own camera, and shadows' LOD origin is the main camera

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Bevy 0.20's `prepare_view_uniforms` set a directional light's cascade's `lod_view_world_position` by looking its camera's main-world entity (`RetainedViewEntity::auxiliary_entity`) up among the render world's views, whose entities are others: the lookup found nothing, and took the shadow LOD origin, or found whichever view happened to share the number. It now finds the camera by its main-world entity among the frame's camera views (`camera_positions`, `cascade_lod_position`), a patch of `bevy_render` 0.20.0 (`patches/bevy_render`, D184) marked `Terra's patch (D612)`. And the main camera carries `ShadowLodOrigin` (`terra_world::planet::entering::spawn_camera`), so point and spot lights' shadows, which have no camera of their own, measure from it rather than from the camera of the lowest entity, which may be the interface's or an icon's.

Upstream: the same lookup on Bevy's main (2026-11-03); not yet offered.

## Why

The crowns' shadow stand-ins shrink with their distance from `lod_view_world_position` in the shadow pass (D234, `foliage.wesl`), as the CPU keeps them by their distance from the camera. With the position wrong, every stand-in shrank to nothing in some loads of lake-play and not in others: the sun's shadow pass drew 0.16 ms against 0.41 at the route view for the same code (play-lake-fps j14), and the lake's trees once lost their sun shadows in a still (j12), skewing probes and benches between loads. Measured on the worker's 4090 at the route view (job 20261103-173149-8dac-patch), the cascades' position logged in each load: with main's lookup it found nothing in all four loads, and the fallback was the main camera in three (shadow pass 0.41 ms) and the origin of the world, the planet's middle, in the fourth (0.17 ms); with the patch and the origin on the main camera, every load measured from the camera (0.42, 0.43 ms, and four loads at 0.40-0.42 in job 20261103-172308-38f0-patch).
