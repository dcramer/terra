# D453: The locked-on fight camera stands off the line to the target

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Locked on to an enemy, the fight camera (D451) stands 15 to 20 degrees to one side of the line from the player to the target, so the enemy shows beside the player rather than behind them, as most action games' lock-on cameras do. The distance and framing stay D451's.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-24, on the review desk, choosing among off the line, straight behind and closer: "Off the line". Straight behind the player, the partner was mostly hidden at close range (the duel's before/after stills).
