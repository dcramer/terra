# D191: Where materials come from

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Tileable materials (ground, plaster, planks, stone) come first from free CC0 libraries, Poly Haven foremost, fetched by `tools/textures`; what no library has, such as a roof of shakes, is generated with Tripo and made to tile by the same tool; models, such as a kit's stone, come from `tools/props`. Bought packs only where their licence allows use in a game shared as a public repository. Every material's source is in `assets/materials/README.md`.

## Why

The developers asked whether bought packs would be needed and said they were acceptable "as long as it wont prevent us from achieving the very large set we'll ultimately need" (2026-10-12). CC0 needs no licence tracking and no money, and covers the common surfaces.
