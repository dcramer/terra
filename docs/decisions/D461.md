# D461: Which stances come first, after sword and shield

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Which of [holding.md](../holding.md)'s stances are built, and in what order. The stances are unarmed, single, shielded, dual, two-handed, shield alone and tool. Options:

- (a) Sword and shield only, until it feels right.
- (b) Two-handed next (a greatsword), then single one-handed, then unarmed.
- (c) All of them at once.

Recommended: (b). The two-handed stance proves the model, a new class and stance made of data, clips and hand IK. Unarmed is what everyone fights with before they have a weapon.

Also open here: whether a single one-hander may block on its blade (a smaller block, a parry at its start). Enshrouded blocks only with a shield or a two-hander. Recommended: yes, smaller.

## Why

The developers want fluid combat, "albeit limited in scope right now" (2026-10-24, movement.md). Each stance is a move set, its clips and a duel, so each is weeks of rounds.
