# D392: Skylight is daylight, and the ground bounces the sun that reaches it

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Follows D370. The painted sky the shade is lit by (`skylight`, D256) is moved onto CIE's daylight locus by day, as warm or cool as it was and as bright: mixed along a straight line from a low sun's orange horizon to the zenith's blue, and toward the shade key's lilac, it passed through magenta, which real skylight never does; the mist's sky light likewise. The ground's bounce in it is lit by the sun as it reaches the ground, Bevy's atmosphere's transmittance worked out on the CPU from the same air (`air::sun_through`, Kasten and Young's air mass), not by the sun's colour above the air, so a low sun's bounce is as dim and as warm as the light that makes it. No cost to frame time.

In twilight the sky leaves the locus: the weight that moves it there falls with the square of how low the sun is (`low`, 1 at 4 degrees and under, 0 from 22), so twilight's sky keeps the violet that ozone's Chappuis band gives it (Hulburt 1953) and the shade keys of D302 ask for, while a dawn of 10 degrees and up keeps most of the locus. The ground's sky-lit part is lit in the sky's hue overhead, not white, so grass no longer bounces its own green into a low sun's shade.

## Why

The town, tundra and lake were reviewed with lilac or indigo shade (handoff, 2026-10-15), their agents pointing at the shared light. Measured on the four places' looks views (D356), main against this: the tundra at its 11:00 trailer hour lilac 3.0% to 0.3% (at most 0.7%), 10 to 11 of 18 holding; the town at 10:30 shade blue over green 1.06 to 0.95 (Kingdom Come's 0.77), 14 to 15 holding; the lake at 10:06 shade blue over green 0.90 to 0.79 (0.65), blue over green 1.09 to 1.08; the lush forest within its numbers, 13 to 14 of 18 holding at 07:30 and 13 at 13:00.

The settlement's dusk (round 21): the castle's shaded walls at the film's end measured sRGB (67, 75, 60) in round 20, green over both, the shade key's warm violet moved onto the locus as a neutral grey and tinted by the grass's bounce; with this the keep's shaded face at 17 s is (63, 59, 64).
