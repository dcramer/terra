# D147: Props with no free model

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A prop no free pack covers is made by [tools/props](../../tools/props/README.md): a concept drawn to the style guide ([art-style.md](../art-style.md)), a low-poly Tripo model of it (P1, textured at 2K with its normal, roughness and metal maps, about 50 Studio credits), then a free clean-up in Blender that keeps its textures (D304) and makes it to the model contract (D303); a tool and the modelled trees are still given the nearest of the palette roles their `prop.json` lists, and coloured by role in the game (D54). Its concept, model and `prop.json` are source art in `art/props/`; the game's file is remade from them for free. Nothing is shaped from primitives in code any more. Every catalogue item states how wide, high and deep a real one is, and the model contract's checker holds its model to that (D110, D303); the sandbox's workbench lines props up beside a body as the game draws them (`--prop`).

## Why

The bake-off's route (research/ai-models-for-art.md), kept as a tool since we keep needing props (D71). Generated models match their concepts far better than modelling by code, and the clean-up keeps them in one palette and one shader. The clean-up still flat-shades every face; the style guide wants normals split only at creases (D14).
