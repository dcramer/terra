# D274: The third-person camera on a boom, and plants that fade whole

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D271. As shipped third-person games do (research/foliage-near-camera.md): **the camera's boom** (`terra_game` `play/camera.rs`) is swept as a sphere, as Unreal's spring arm, against build walls, the ground and the fitted solids that stop the walker (D267: trunks, rocks, stumps, logs; not a spruce's skirt, which is boughs), a 0.3 m sphere it never passes and a 0.6 m one it eases in to ahead of it, quickly in (14 a second) and slowly out (2.5); hemmed in behind the right shoulder, it swings to the left one and back once the right is clear; where the ground behind is in the way, as looking up, it shortens no shorter than 3 m and then the lens slides up over the ground; it keeps 0.55 m off the line up the figure's middle. **Whole-plant obstruction fade** (`terra_world::parting::ObstructionFade`, on every shrub, herb and young tree): while the line from the eye to the figure's chest passes through 0.8 of a plant's box, every part of it, stems and leaves, dithers out together over 0.3 s, all the way, its shadow kept, through a per-instance value the foliage shaders read (`MeshTag`), written only when it changes; the tube cut round the line is gone. Leaves at a third-person lens are gone within 1.05 m and whole from 1.4 m (D271's 1.65 to 3 m). Plants lean from a body within 0.3 m of its side at 0.6 of D271's push, not 0.6 m.

## Why

A review of the film (6/10), its first gap: a screen-door stipple where foliage over 30 to 50% of the frame sat partly faded, smeared by TAA; a dark round tunnel through a hazel round the player; the camera sinking into the player's shoulder and a trunk at the look-up; plants parting like doors. A sparse dither left of a faded bush (a fifth, a twelfth) stippled half the screen with the camera in a hazel and TAA streaked it, so the fade goes all the way. On the film against main filmed at the same time (80 frames every 0.5 s; 2400 camera places): frames with leaf-green stipple over 5% of the screen 3, now none, over 2% 11, now 7, the worst 13.5%, now 3.8%; near-lens foliage over 30% in 5 frames, now 1, its mean share 11.5%, now 9.4%; the lens inside a trunk or rock in 91 frames, 44 of them in the look-up, now none, never nearer bark than 0.3 m. The walk bench was heavily contended (two other sessions' sandboxes on the RTX 3080, load up to 36 on 24 cores), so only its quietest runs compare: the GPU's frame 12.97 ms on main benched alternately with it, 13.27 ms with this (main pass 6.57 to 6.76, prepass 1.94 to 1.93), the game's CPU frame alike (4.8 to 6.3 ms on both). lodcheck passes (worst 0.19%).
