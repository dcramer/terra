# D495: Mining by hits, and ore in deposits

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

**Mining is hitting the ground** with the tool in hand, a pickaxe now, other gear later, perhaps drills: every one to three hits chip a chunk away, how many by the tool and how hard the ground is, and what comes out is collected into the pack by the volume of each material (`assets/data/ground.ron`, as now). It replaces holding the pickaxe for a scoop every 0.9 s (`terra_survival::dig`). A tool too weak for a material does not cut it (crafting.md). **Ore lies in deposits**: bodies of ore of several kinds, uneven across a world by region and depth, so most ground has none and a deposit is a place worth reaching, found by its signs where it meets the surface (D383's stain, D93). It replaces one ore in veins at the same share through all stone (`GeneratedPlanet`, 9 m noise). Proposed in [construction-003](../proposals/construction-003.md) after Space Engineers 2 (D491); not its continuous drill.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, answering construction-003: "mining should be like, not drill, but you hit it like a pickaxe or whatever gear you have (it might vary.. maybe drills come later). for the most part hitting it 1-3 times should chip away a chunk and collect the resources"; "ore will be deposits, but isnt that the same as veins everywhere?" Not the same: today's veins put ore in every stone at one rate everywhere; deposits make ore somewhere to find and travel to, as SE2 spreads resources unevenly across its worlds.
