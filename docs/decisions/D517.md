# D517: Which clouds the sky has, and in what order

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The clouds come fair-weather first: cumulus, stratocumulus and the overcast deck first, as one volumetric layer; then cirrus and altocumulus, so a front can be read arriving; then cumulonimbus with weather.md's storms; then fog and cloud filling valleys and wrapping peaks. Every kind is the climate's, never placed by hand. The other options were the overcast and storm sky first, and only cumulus and overcast for now. See [sky.md](../sky.md#order-of-work).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "Fair-weather first". This settles the question in the recommendation's favour.

The developer, 2026-10-26: "clouds would have diff shades, there could be rain, wind, sun, moon". Which kinds come first sets sky.md's order of work and what a front looks like as it comes; the kinds and their weather are in sky.md's table and the [research](../../research/2026-10-26-volumetric-skies.md).
