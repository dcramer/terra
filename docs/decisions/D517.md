# D517: Which clouds the sky has, and in what order

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: cumulus, stratocumulus and the overcast deck first, as one volumetric layer; cirrus and altocumulus next, so a front can be read arriving; cumulonimbus with weather.md's storms; fog and cloud filling valleys and wrapping peaks last. Every kind is the climate's, never placed by hand. Options: that order; the overcast and storm sky first, since weather.md's storms and winter are the current work; or only cumulus and overcast for now. See [sky.md](../sky.md#clouds).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-26: "clouds would have diff shades, there could be rain, wind, sun, moon". Which kinds a world gets and which come first sets the order of work and what a front looks like as it comes; the kinds and their weather are in sky.md's table and the [research](../../research/2026-10-26-volumetric-skies.md).
