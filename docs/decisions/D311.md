# D311: A place's look measured against references kept as data

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A biome's look is aimed at a reference: frames of the look wanted (another game's own, photographs), measured by `tools/sandbox/look.ts` into `tools/sandbox/looks/<name>.json` (brightness and its spread, near black, warmth, local contrast, the shade's and the middle distance's colour, lit litter below, lit lime above), the pictures themselves kept outside the repository; the vantage sheet and any film or shots are measured against one. How to build and judge a place's look, and the traps met in the lush forest, is the `environment` skill.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "anything we learned that we need to document about environment building, and/or codify tools that are used for validation here? we will eventually do more biomes and we would like this to be done well." The lush forest's rounds were won by measuring against Sons of the Forest's own frames and lost by tuning by eye; a reference built for its night at once showed our night a stop and a half too dark, colour right.
