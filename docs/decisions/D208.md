# D208: Pieces placed in grid units

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A layout's pieces are placed in the base's grid in ticks of a 256th of a cube, about 2 mm (`layout::Place`, `layout::TICKS`), not metres: points, boxes and lengths along a piece are whole numbers of ticks, and only drawing turns them into metres. A piece's version is still a seed hashed from its place.

## Why

Mine, for [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md)'s rule that a layout gives piece instances in grid units, so what machines share holds no floating point a machine could round otherwise; a power of two of a cube, so the grid's lines and the courses' heights are exact, and fine enough that no piece is seen to move.
