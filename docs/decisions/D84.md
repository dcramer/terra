# D84: Inventory baseline

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Subnautica, for now: a backpack grid where items take up shape and nothing stacks, equipment slots by kind, an action bar that points into the backpack, storage as more grids, and no weight. What you carry drops where you go down, and dropped items decay and disappear after a while. See [items.md](../items.md).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10: "lets say the baseline is just subnautica for now", and that weight and the like may be revisited.
