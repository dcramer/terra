# D101: Food and death

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Food is essential: going without weakens you and in the end kills you. Once hunger or thirst is empty, health wears away slowly, over days, and your attributes are weaker meanwhile; starving takes three in-game days, and thirst two. The same for recruits (D169). Death costs a little progress toward your next step of skills, never a step, rank, essence or ability (D171), besides what you carried (D84). Still no fail state (D18). Whether good food gives buffs is for later.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11; on 2026-10-12, of recruits and players alike: "their hunger gauge is fine, but like it would slowly eat away at health over days, not crazy fast. same w/ the player. but it'd slow their attributes or make them weaker"; then "3 in game days seems fine", and of thirst, "i guess 2 days is fine". The death cost was settled with D171. See [survival.md](../survival.md).
