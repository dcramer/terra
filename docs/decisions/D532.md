# D532: Clips come from generators and free sets, not capture or bought packs

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The clips Kimodo makes badly (idles, sprint, swimming, reactions, climbing) come from generators and CC0 sets: Uthana's text-to-motion on paid credits (output ours; `tools/uthana`) for those clips, Kimodo with its keys (D450) for the fight's moves, and Quaternius's CC0 clips where they fit the style. Only clips made on paid credits enter the repository; free-tier clips are Uthana's (CC BY). Nobody performs: no capture suit, studio or video-to-motion of ourselves, and no store packs. What goes wrong in a clip is still found by the checks (D447, D530) and fixed where the clip is made.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-26, on the sourcing research (research/2026-10-26-animation-sourcing.md): "im not buying a capture suit you are insane", and on the desk: "the first two are way out of budget, and im not confident store packs solve our problems"; then "i was hoping to avoid literally recording ourselves". Earlier: "if there were some solution that we could purchase that would help us solve all of these (not just swimming) let me know / because tihngs like the idle animation also still suck / but we have to be able to future proof". Store packs can't sit in the public repository or steer Kimodo, so they would not future-proof the set.

The trial, 2026-10-26: idle, sprint and swim on text-to-motion 3.0, one take each on the free tier, retargeted onto our skeleton's own bones (73 of 73) and closed into loops by Uthana's looping (1–8 cm seams). The developer: "they look pretty good", and bought credits.
