# D425: Mountains: horns and Witcher-like ranges first, two thirds rock, sites re-found once

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The three questions of [the mountain look note](../../research/2026-10-19-mountain-look.md):

- **Range kinds first:** Tatras-like granite horns, arêtes and cirques, and Witcher 3-like ranges (Skellige's and Velen's mountains: tall, rocky, dramatic). The other kinds of the note's table come after.
- **Rock against snow:** above the tree line in summer, about two thirds bare rock and scree and one third snow (in couloirs, on ledges and north faces); the share changes with the date through the climate.
- **Pinned sites:** the lake's and the settlement's sites may move once, re-found after drainage erosion is baked into the ground, rather than the mountains being faded round them again.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-19, on the review desk: range kinds, "tatras horns/witcher ones are great"; rock against snow, "Two thirds rock, one third snow"; the pinned sites, "Yes, re-find them once". Earlier, on the settlement's mountains: "i like mountains that look rocky and tall, but it needs to have variety and look great. i dont care if they are smaller vs. bigger as long as they just look really proper."
