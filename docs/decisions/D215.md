# D215: Dressing a kit's edges by their role

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Each exposed face, edge and corner of a kit's cubes has a role worked out from the cubes and the ground's samples when a section is laid, never stored (`terra_build::layout::roles`): an arris up an outside corner, an inside corner, a jamb up an opening's side, a crest along a top, a nose along a step, a sill and a head round an opening, a soffit under an overhang, a foot where it stands on the ground or another kind or the ground passes it, a border along a floor it stands on, a meet where a thinner kind stands on it; each edge belongs to one cube, so a piece laid for it is in, planned or carved with that cube. A kit's file dresses each role (`dress`): a profile from its pieces swept along the edge a cube at a time, mitred out and back at corners, jointed and capped (`Piece::Trim`); corner stones a course at a time, long and short by turns (`Piece::Corner`); or a long member of its timber (a plate, a sill, a post, a step's nose and stringers). Profiles and corner stones are made in Blender from a source under `art/kits/` (`tools/kits`) into a RON file the kit names; a foot steps with the ground a course at a time and runs on down to it; masks shade what a kit draws by the ground's dirt, worn edges and weathering. Pieces stand out up to an eighth of a cube (D192). Roles of what is in are worked out from what is built, a plan's ghosts from the plan (amended by D358). Extends D197 and D200 under D211; the stored cube, sections, operations and saves do not change. Whole modules per face (the spike's fourth candidate) stay possible as a refined kit's option within the same roles. Every piece is of the raw materials its kind is built from, so stone is dressed in stone and logs in timber; a piece of another material puts that material in the kind's cost (earth's oak lintels make wood part of earth's), and `cargo xtask kits` checks it. Amends D197: a block kind is its cubes' surface dressed by its roles. Extended by D258 to roofs and frames: modules along lines and stones at points, besides profiles. Amended by D290: the roles' pieces are the pattern's, shared by its materials and drawn in each one's bake. Amended 2026-10-15 (the developers, through the lead: "a quoin modelled for fieldstone, worn by another material, can look wrong"; amends D290): corner and opening stones are made, not modelled. Quoins up outside corners and jamb stones round openings are dressed blocks cut from the material's own block surface, its own texture and height map, each face the inside of one of its own largest stones, long and short by turns, standing a little proud with their own bevel, at sizes the pattern's file gives (`Corners(long, short, proud, bevel)`); the shared quoin and jamb models are gone from the block pattern. Swept profiles (coping, sill, plinth, lintel, fascia) stay, textured by the material; roof pieces stay shared. The escape hatch is data only: a material's pack may dress any role with its own piece or turn it off (`Off`), under the model contract, with no game code, the exception and not the norm (construction-002). Amended 2026-10-15 by the agent, as the lead asked (D362): a corner stone's face is drawn at its wall's own scale, never larger, the inside of one of the wall's larger stones laid again mirrored beside itself as far as the face reaches, where it was one stone blown up two to three times (`cargo xtask texels` with the real height maps: stone's walls 15.2% past 1.5:1 before, 0.2% after). Amended 2026-10-16 by the agent (the fifth goals review's finding 7): the swept profiles too wear the material's own wall texture set, as its corner stones do, unless its pack gives a dressed stone of its own; the pattern's shared dressed stone is only the fallback.

## Why

Mine, building step 2a of [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md) from the role pieces spike ([research/role-pieces.md](../../research/role-pieces.md)): of the four candidates, profiles and corner pieces over the surface reached the bar for stone, carved, joined and built part by part with no special cases, sat on the cube store and layouts as they are, and cost about one draw per kind per section and a few hundred triangles a cube near; a new look is a kit file and its source. The developers ratified it on 2026-10-13, asked whether it should stand: "whatever thematically makes sense. they need to ideally generally come from the raw materials we'll use to build something".
