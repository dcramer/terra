# D336: Under the trees the low sky is hidden by stems, and reflections follow the sky open round the eye

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D270 and D317. The sky-occlusion bake counts a trunk under every crown, from the ground up to it, as hiding d / A of a level look a metre (d the trunk's girth, a sixtieth of the tree's height; A the cell's floor), as probe bakes treat a stand of stems. The sky's reflection is occluded per fragment, for every material: where an irradiance volume holds a fragment, the view's environment map's share of its reflection is scaled by the volume's light toward the reflection's direction against the open sky's diffuse light from it, mixed in by the volume's weight (`sky_reflection_seen`, `patches/bevy_pbr`'s `environment_map.wesl`), as Frostbite derives specular occlusion from the diffuse visibility; the map itself is the open sky's everywhere. Until 2026-11-09 the map's brightness followed the square of the sky open round the eye (`SkySeen::around_at`), which dimmed open land whenever the camera stood under the trees and left a den mirroring the whole sky. It costs every fragment in a view with a volume one more volume lookup and a cube sample; not benched.

## Why

The player's new jacket, brown leather of sRGB (74, 59, 47), read purple walking under the trees (the sleeve (20, 12, 23) beside ground of (15, 19, 4)); with the reflection off it read brown, so it was the sky's reflection: under a tall stand's crowns 20 m up every low look was open to the horizon, and a third of the sky round the eye was still sky. The vantage views measure a few hundredths of the sky seen at eye height, hence the square.
