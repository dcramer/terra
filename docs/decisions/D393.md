# D393: A demo's place pinned where its query found it

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A place the land's rules make is found by its query once and then pinned there: `--site <name>@<east km>,<north km>` (the sandbox, `cargo xtask fields`, the bench scenes, `tools/sandbox/places.ts`) keeps, of the places its query finds fitting within 200 m of the pin (`terra_land::site::PINNED_WITHIN_KM`), the nearest, the very cell or lake it was; a change to the land that spoils it stops the run saying so, and `--site <name>` alone still searches, to see where the query would put it now. Pinned: the lake at -15.34, 1.68 (1,317 m, 0.55 km²), the tundra at -15.54, 10.52, the town and the castle's valley at 2.22, 2.12, the settlement by its larger lake at 2.41, 2.51 (D381).

## Why

Reshaping the ranges (the walls behind the castle) moved every demo: the lake's query, which scores the walls looming over a lake, took a new lake 4 km off or found none, and the town's moved 1.4 km, taking the castle, settlement and town films with them. The coordinator, 2026-10-16: "go with (a). Build the pinning: each demo place in places.ts carries its spot (and the lake its lake), with the query kept only as the way a spot was first found and as a check."
