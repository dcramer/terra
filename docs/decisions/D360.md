# D360: A stride carried by aim, its arms layered

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D316. A made stride (`art/animation/made.json`) is fixed over its take, not by remaking it. On each body it is carried by where the mannequin's limbs point (thigh, shin, foot, upper arm, forearm, hand), not by its bones' rotations, its legs turned in at the hip to land the feet `step` apart (`tools/characters/aim.py`), as Unreal's IK Retargeter and Unity's Humanoid retarget onto a reference pose. On the mannequin, before any body, an arm pass is laid over it, its legs and spine untouched (`made_clips.py`'s `swing_arms`, the entry's `arms`): the upper arms' swing mapped onto a set range, both timed by the left arm; the elbow closing in front and opening behind; the forearm kept from crossing the body; the wrist in line; the hand rolled thumb side up; and the fingers closed into a loose fist (`fist`, from the library's). The jog swings 38 forward to 42 back of the trunk, elbows 80 to 100; the sprint keeps its own swing.

## Why

The developer, of the jog: "the guy looks like he needs to poop", then "the legs are great, the hands are kinda weird", then that the forest film's jog was right, "its just hands being bad". Measured on the base: feet landed 30 cm apart from the body's splayed rest legs; the hands hovered at the belly, met near the middle, palms down and fingers straight, the right arm hardly swung, and the elbow bent most behind (research/2026-10-15-run.md).
