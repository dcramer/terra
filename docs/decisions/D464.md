# D464: Holding a one-handed weapon in two hands

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Whether a one-hander can be held in both hands for other moves ([holding.md](../holding.md)). Options:

- (a) Never.
- (b) A `versatile` grip on a few classes (sword, axe, spear), each with its own two-handed set. It is taken up on a key, with the off item stowed.
- (c) Any one-hander, with one generic two-handed set.

Recommended: (b), once the two-handed stance exists (D461). It costs one set per versatile class.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, on the review desk: "Versatile grips". This settles the question below in that option's favour.

Elden Ring and Dark Souls let any weapon be two-handed, with the class's own two-handed moveset. Mordhau's alt modes change a grip (research/2026-10-25-loadouts-and-animation-layers.md). The developer listed "sometimes two handed items" (2026-10-25).
