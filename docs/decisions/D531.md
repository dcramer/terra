# D531: The drawn shield rests low and rises only while the block is held

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

With a shield drawn and the block not held, the shield rests low at the left hip and side, as Enshrouded's guard idle carries it (research/2026-10-25-shield-prior-art.md: "at the left hip"), standing, walking and between blows; it is raised across the body only while the block is held, and goes back down when the block is let go. The rest is the stance's hold (holding.md section 2, **ready**), so it is what the drawn idle, the drawn walk and a reel show, and where a blow starts and ends; the block's raise keeps its eased hold (D454). The composed pose's `film.pose.shield_rest_high` (D530, bar 10 cm over the hips) measures it. The set's guard is `Low_Guard` (combat round 32, D454): the keyed guard with the blows' low shield arm, which the blows and the strafes hold too and the block raises from and lowers to.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-26, after playing the game: "the shield is raised up even when not holding down the block button". The intent as the coordinator relayed it: the shield rests low, as Enshrouded's guard idle, and rises only while the block is held. Combat round 30 measured it on the game's composed pose: the guard (`Keyed_Guard`) carried the board 27.7 cm over the hips with the block not held, against 61 cm in the block, so the two read alike.
