# D304: Props drawn in their own textures

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A prop keeps the textures Tripo made, by default: `props.ts model` adds Tripo's normal, roughness and metal maps to a P1 model (`pbr`, 5 credits), and `refine` keeps colour and normal maps at 2K for a prop 1.5 m or more and 1K below, roughness and metal at half, stored as JPEG, tangents exported. The game draws each textured part in its own material, textures loaded by label from the file through Bevy's glTF loader (mipmapped, D14); a part named `Fire` glows in its own texture; a flat part of an older model is still coloured by role. A tint by role or material goes in the material's base colour, never by flattening. `palette: true` keeps a tool or tree in roles. GPU-compressed textures (KTX2, BC7) wait for the asset pipeline step D188 names.

## Why

The prop renderer read only points and normals, so every prop had been flattened to role colours, against D14 and the demos' look; the chest, the well and the stall stood flat beside textured houses. The textures were in the source models all along.
