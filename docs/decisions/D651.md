# D651: The server's tick answers intent; a client predicts its own body and shows the rest in the past

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A client sends its intent for each numbered tick (D138), repeated until acknowledged; the server applies tick T's intent at T. The client runs ahead of the server by half the round trip plus a margin, held by speeding or slowing its tick by at most 2%; it predicts its own body with the same mover, keeps each tick's intent and result, and on an answer that differs sets the body to it and replays the intents since. Every other body is drawn between the last two states received, two send intervals back. World changes are not predicted in v0. The design is [netcode.md](../netcode.md).

## Why

It is what shipped server-authoritative games do (Source, Overwatch, Rocket League, Unreal's character movement; [research/2026-11-06-netcode.md](../../research/2026-11-06-netcode.md)), and D28, D30 and D138 already gave it what it needs: a plain mover that is a repeatable function of state and intent on a fixed tick. Lockstep was not chosen: a drop-in world cannot wait for its slowest player.
