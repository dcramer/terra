# D328: Body temperature in code

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

D153's first effects, as climate.md orders them. A body's heat balance (`terra_climate::body`, shared, D312): it makes 58 W a square metre a met (one at rest, three walking or working, six sprinting) and takes 12% of the sun on level ground and 35% of a fire's heat on its side, and loses from 34 °C skin through its clothing (0.155 m²K/W a clo, half of it soaked) and the air (radiation 4.7, convection the greater of 3.1 and 8.3 v^0.6, ISO 7730's). Shivering makes up 100 W/m², sweating 220; what they cannot pushes the body from 37 °C by 0.03 °C a W/m², and it drifts there exponentially over 300 s of play. 36 to 38 °C is comfortable; past 35 or 39.5, health falls 0.5 a second a degree and does not heal (`Vitals::expose`). `BodyHeat` on players and people (gameplay's `warmth`), from their `ClimateSample` and their gear's (a person's outfit's) `warmth` in clo (`items.ron`: leather coat 1, cap 0.15; a body under its gear 0.1); rain soaks at a 120th a second a mm/h, snow half that, and dries over 240 s, faster by a fire; wading soaks at once. It says when it crosses an edge, in words, with no meter (D93). A person out of the band works a station slower (`body::pace`): half as fast at the edge of danger, never under a third (D169). A burning fire gives off 5 kW as `Heat`; a body in a room's probe is `Sheltered`. The ground never slows a walker (D149): the going (a walker's pace times 1/(1 + snow/0.3 m), up to a sixth off for mud, never below 0.35, `body::going`) was removed on 2026-10-25.

## Why

Standard thermal comfort and heat balance models, so cold, wind, wet and clothing do what players expect; numbers checked against known cases (a coat and a fire hold a winter's night, a bare body does not; resting in the shade of a 36 °C noon is bearable, hard work in its sun is not). The drift is five minutes so a night out is felt and survivable to act on, not instant (D134's half-hour day). In the game on 15 January at 03:00, bare, the player was cold in 20 s and freezing in 33.
