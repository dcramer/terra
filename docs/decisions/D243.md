# D243: What stops a walker in the forest, and checking it

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Every rock, boulder, stump and fallen log in the lush forest stops a walker by its own footprint, worked out from its mesh once Bevy has it: convex pieces fitted to it, each with its height (D267, which replaced the circles along its bounds this first had). `cargo xtask collide` finds the forest's things, walks the player into a sample of every kind and checks where it stops against the meshes (D267); `world.obstacles` answers what stops a walker near a spot, and `sandbox.find` gives places east and south of the start.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "make sure that hitboxes and collision work well with these". Logs stopped nothing, small rocks nothing, and a 3 m boulder had a 0.6 m circle. The check passes on 35 things of seven kinds, and fails on every rock with the floor's footprints switched off.
