# D458: A drawn body walks with the guard's arms laid over the library's walk

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A body with the sword and shield drawn walks forward and back in `Fight_Walk`: the library's `Walk_Loop` with both arms, from the clavicles down, held as the guard (`Keyed_Guard`, D450) holds them at its first pose, in their own frames, so they ride on the walking chest and the legs stride beneath them as before. It is the layered blend per bone of D454 (`tools/characters/hold.py`, a list entry of `art/animation/sets/fight.json` with `"of"` and `"hold"`), the way shipped games lay a weapon's upper-body pose over shared locomotion (Unreal's Layered Blend per Bone, Unity's avatar masks, Godot's AnimationTree blend filters), baked at export rather than blended at run time; its pace and floor lifts are measured from it as any other clip's. The player's drawn walk (`melee::walk_clip`) and the partner's walk in (`melee::partner`) play it; the strafes and the stand were already keyed or held to the guard. The move check (`moves.ts --also`, D447) measures the clips a drawn body shows besides its moves: the guards, the strafes, `Fight_Walk` and `Jump_Loop`. `Idle_Loop` left that list: a drawn body stands in the set's guard (the stand was `Fight_Guard` from D447's first day, `Keyed_Guard` since D450), and the draw and the walk choice run in one chained tick, so the library's idle is never shown with a sword in hand.

## Why

Combat round 13 (2026-10-24). The library's walk and idle were made unarmed: their arms swing at the sides, so the drawn sword went 10.2 cm into the right thigh walking and the right hand 6.2 cm into it (`moves.ts`, every body template). Holding both arms at the guard's first pose clears them: the sword 3.8 cm off the body, the hands 2.8 cm, on every template, and no other row moves (moves.ts's numbers over the set are the same to 0.01). New Kimodo takes keyed to the guard (D450) were not needed: a walk's arms carry nothing the legs need, and the clip step runs on the processor while CUDA is broken under WSL. `move.Idle_Loop.held` (21.4 cm, the sword into the pelvis) measured a clip a drawn body does not show, so it was dropped, not fixed; `Jump_Loop`, which a drawn body shows in the air and nothing measured, took its place and passes. Whether arms held still on a walking chest read well, against a walk with a little arm sway as shipped games add, is for the developers' eye on the duel film.
