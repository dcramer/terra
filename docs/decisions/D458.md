# D458: A drawn body walks with the guard's arms laid over the library's walk

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A body with the sword and shield drawn walks forward and back in the library's `Walk_Loop`, every body's walk, with both arms, from the clavicles down, held as its set's guard (`Keyed_Guard`, D450) holds them at its first pose, in their own frames, so they ride on the walking chest and the legs stride beneath them. The arms are laid at run time by a bone mask, the hold layer of holding.md (section 2; Unreal's Layered Blend per Bone, Unity's avatar masks, ALS's overlay layer), not baked into a clip: `terra_walker::layer` puts every bone in one of two mask groups (the arms below `HOLD_MASK`, `clavicle_l` and `clavicle_r`, and the rest), gives each clip of the figure's graph two copies masked off the arms and one on the arms alone, and a clip asked with a `hold` (`Act::hold`, `Staged::tell_play_held`) plays its copy off the arms with the hold's clip, paused at its first pose, on the arms, taking the weight the transitions give the clips under it, so the arms blend from and to whatever played before as the legs do. The hold is the set's `guard`, data (D136). It lives in the walker layer (D198), beside the figure it poses; the pelvis, legs and feet stay the locomotion clip's, so the feet lock (D455) reads the clip under the hold alone. The move check lays the same mask (`glb.ts`'s `layered`, a row named `Walk_Loop+Keyed_Guard`) and measures the hold over the walk, jog and sprint (`moves.ts --also`). The strafes keep their own arms (Kimodo takes keyed to the guard) and the drawn jump the library's `Jump_Loop` with the hand on the strap: the guard laid over them puts the sword 18-20 cm into the lifting thigh (strafes) and 15 cm (the jump's tuck), so they need a hold pose of their own or stay as they are. The move check (`moves.ts --also`, D447) measures the clips a drawn body shows besides its moves: the guards, the strafes, the walk, jog and sprint under the guard's hold, and `Jump_Loop`. `Idle_Loop` left that list: a drawn body stands in the set's guard (the stand was `Fight_Guard` from D447's first day, `Keyed_Guard` since D450), and the draw and the walk choice run in one chained tick, so the library's idle is never shown with a sword in hand.

With a shield the guard laid over the walk rests it low at the left hip, raised only by the block (D531, the developers); `Keyed_Guard` carries it at the chest until combat round 31 changes the set's guard.

## Why

Combat round 13 (2026-10-24). The library's walk and idle were made unarmed: their arms swing at the sides, so the drawn sword went 10.2 cm into the right thigh walking and the right hand 6.2 cm into it (`moves.ts`, every body template). Holding both arms at the guard's first pose clears them: the sword 3.8 cm off the body, the hands 2.8 cm, on every template, and no other row moves (moves.ts's numbers over the set are the same to 0.01). New Kimodo takes keyed to the guard (D450) were not needed: a walk's arms carry nothing the legs need, and the clip step runs on the processor while CUDA is broken under WSL. `move.Idle_Loop.held` (21.4 cm, the sword into the pelvis) measured a clip a drawn body does not show, so it was dropped, not fixed; `Jump_Loop`, which a drawn body shows in the air and nothing measured, took its place and passes. Whether arms held still on a walking chest read well, against a walk with a little arm sway as shipped games add, is for the developers' eye on the duel film.
