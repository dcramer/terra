# D584: A body moves as its clip says; the game adds only foot placement, look-at and hand IK

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

What a body shows is the clip made for what it does, carried onto it (D542), with a few standard adjustments after it. Shipped games stop there; they don't rewrite a pose by rule.

- **The source holds the motion.**
  - A swim is made lying in the water, a carry made carrying, a walk made walking. The motion comes from D532's sources: Quaternius's CC0 clips (hand-keyed by an animator, not captured) where they fit, and Uthana's text-to-motion for what they lack (swims, idles).
  - The walk and jog stay ours (D316) until a new take is nearer a person's gait (gait-lab norms), not nearer Quaternius's: his current walk and jog (UAL1 v3.0, unchanged since) lean and bounce like a cartoon and were turned down for it. The jog is Uthana's locomotion take since 2026-10-30, the nearest a person's of the takes measured, with its own arms.
  - Nothing tips, twists or re-arms a clip that was made for something else: no whole-body tilt laying a standing stroke down, no formula arms, no standing fix-up baked in.
- **After the clip, only three kinds of layer:**
  - **Foot placement** on uneven ground: on flat ground it bends a leg a few degrees at most.
  - **Look-at**: the head and neck turned toward the aim.
  - **Hand IK**: a hand kept on what it holds, and a finger grip pose.
- **Everything else goes.** Each per-pose fix-up (D535's arms off the hips, the carried forearm turned every frame, a whole-body tilt) is deleted once the clip it covered for is replaced, not tuned.
- **The audit holds the line.** A layer change passes the bone audit: degrees each layer moves each bone off its clip, worst and mean, per motion (research/2026-10-29-why-the-skeleton-moves-wrong.md).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-29: "the skeleton just doesnt move right", and "one issue we've had is you twist joints and do weird shit instead of applying the animation? like swimming looks stupid".

The ladder (research/2026-10-29-why-the-skeleton-moves-wrong.md) measured each stage:
- **The carry is faithful.** Quaternius's walk and jog land on our body 0.0° apart, limb for limb.
- **Our clips are poor.**
  - The swim was keyed standing: knees up to 168°, arms windmilling. It is then laid down 80°, so it reads as kneeling and pedalling.
  - The walk's knee softens little as the heel lands, and its hips turn lopsidedly.
  - The jog's arms are written by a formula.
  - Their lean is not too upright: 0-2° walking and 11-18° jogging (already a little over), where a person leans a few degrees walking and 5-10° at a steady jog (research/2026-10-14-animation.md). Quaternius's hand-keyed walk and jog lean 11° and 23-29° with a 23 cm bob in the jog, the hunched charge D316 replaced.
- **Two layers wrench a running body.**
  - The foot lock bends the foot up to 95° and the thigh up to 40° on flat ground, 10-18° on average.
  - The carried-tool rule turns the forearm up to 71°.

Each of these layers was added to cover for a clip that was wrong. Fixing the source removes the need for them.

Amends D535: its fix-ups are retired as their clips are replaced; its look-at stays.
