# D449: Out of stamina in a fight

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

What a body with too little stamina may still do with a sword and shield: tired, not stopped (`terra_motion::stamina`, the move set's `tired`):

- A roll or a heavy blow without its full cost does not start.
- A light blow with too little comes out tired: its clip at 0.7 of its pace, doing half its damage, and it takes what stamina there is.
- A blow on a guard with less stamina left than it costs breaks the guard, as a heavy blow does.

The walker's own roll, sheathed, still rolls on any stamina left, as before; the costs are the move set's (light 10, finisher 12, heavy 22, roll 18, a blow on the guard 1 a point of its damage), placeholders to tune in play.

The sheathed roll is to follow the same rule. Passed over: any move on any stamina above nothing; a tired body moving and guarding slower but striking as hard; blows free.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-24, on the review desk, of four options: "Tired, not stopped".

D61 (decided by the developers) says attacking, dodging, blocking, sprinting and climbing draw on stamina, and the developers' first play of the prototype (movement.md, "Second round") found none of the fight costing any. What happens at the bottom of the bar is a design call. The recommendation keeps a tired player able to do something (a weak light blow) so an empty bar slows a fight rather than stopping it, while the moves that win a fight (the roll's i-frames, a guard-breaking heavy) need the stamina saved for them.
