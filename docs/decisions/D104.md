# D104: Claim size

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

A claim starts at a reasonable size and can be upgraded; the numbers are adjustable. Proposed: 40 metres on a side, upgradable to 80 and 160 (Enshrouded's upgraded size).

## Why

The developers set the rule on 2026-10-11 and left the size to be chosen.
