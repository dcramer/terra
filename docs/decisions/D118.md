# D118: Ammunition in a quiver

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A quiver is a container holding a stack of arrows, worn on the belt in a slot of its own. Arrows stack inside it; the quiver is one item. How many it holds is open.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11, answering the question in [items.md](../items.md): "a quiver + stack of arrows in it.. a quiver is basically a container". An exception to nothing stacking (D84), inside a container.
