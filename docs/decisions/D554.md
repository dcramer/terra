# D554: A shield pose authors the board's place against the chest; the arm is solved to it

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Each shield pose (the guard, the strafes' arm, the block's raise, the blows' carry) authors one thing: where the board sits against the chest, its place in spine_03's frame: its centre (m), its face (the way its painted front looks) and its up (the way its top looks), in the chest's frame taken as spine_03's joint turned as the chest has turned from its rest, so the body's own axes (+Y up, +Z ahead) and metres, whatever way the bone's own axes run. Clearance from the head, the thighs and the sword is a property of that place. The targets are data, `art/animation/shield_targets.json`, each with the commit and body template it was captured from; `tools/characters/shield_place.ts` reads any pose's place on any template from any commit's assets (`--at`) and captures a target (`--capture`). The strap is physical and per body: the hand strap at that body's fist, the board along its true forearm (the strap's `fist`, the hand socket under the hand pose the clips hold, docs/attachment.md): no place on the bone set for every body. The arm (shoulder, elbow and the forearm's roll) is solved at bake time so the strapped board lands on its target, per body (hold.py's `shield` op). Changing the strap or the bones re-solves the arm; the board stays. Run-time IK only corrects what a template misses by centimetres (holding.md section 3).

The move check reports each pose with a target against it (moves.ts, the row "<pose> board", worst of its clip on every template; the scorecard's `move.guard.board_target` and `_turn`). The first target is `Low_Guard`'s, captured at 27fa529c on the `male` template both fighters wear, before D542's rebuild of the bones: the guard the developer approved (D531).

## Why

The shield-arm review (2026-10-27, the coordinator's plan): the board's place was the product of two numbers in bone frames, the strap's `at` in lowerarm_l's frame and hold.py's aim and roll per pose; neither names where the board sits against the body, so any change to the bones or the strap moved it (D542's rebuild did; `Guard_Shield_Out` repeated it). Shipped games author the arm with the prop present (an IK control on the prop in the DCC, baked to FK, as Unreal's Control Rig and Maya's prop rigs are used), and run-time IK only corrects centimetres; our clips come from Kimodo and Uthana, which cannot hold a prop, so we do that step ourselves at bake time. The chest's frame rather than the bone's own, so a rebuild that re-lays the bones along their limbs leaves the targets as they are.
- **2026-10-28**: the strap made physical and per body (shield-arm round 2): `{"bone", "fist", "face", "grip"}`, the look's origin at that body's hand socket and its grip along that body's elbow-to-wrist, kept there each frame by `terra_walker::strap` and hung so by `held.ts`; the hand socket serves as the forearm's socket, so no new socket was added.
- **2026-10-28**: the bake and the body (shield-arm job 2b, the coordinator's call, technical): every body shape plays the one fight.glb and only the female template moves the arm's joints (shoulder 5.4 cm in, forearm 19.4 against 21.2 cm), so hold.py's `shield` op solves the arm on the base body (a hold's `"shield": "<pose>"`, the board as `shield_targets.json`'s `board` gives it, written by `shield_place.ts --board`), and each body is corrected at run time after the clips: the shoulder blade, upper arm and forearm turned the least way onto the nearest target, wholly where the base body's board is within 5 (cm, or degrees / 3) of it and not at all from 10, read on the base arm so every shape of a pose is pulled alike (Unreal's IK Rig and Control Rig hand IK do this for proportions). The run-time half, `terra_walker::shield_arm` and its twin `tools/characters/shield_arm.ts` used by moves.ts and shield_place.ts, lands next; until it does the female's guard board is 9.3 cm off.
