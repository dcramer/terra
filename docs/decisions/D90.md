# D90: Answering threats

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Part of survival is establishing claims and outposts and holding them (D166). Recruits help defend them (D161, D169); protecting an outpost, with walls, guards or otherwise, is part of having one. Built defences, automation, may come later.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11 ("I could imagine", "possible"), then settled by D161, D166 and D169 on 2026-10-12: recruits defend if you are attacked near your claim, and "part of having this tribe/village you build is you will need to actually protect it". Recruits come mostly by rescue or as a reward for helping a settlement. Replaces the automation half of D3. See [tribe.md](../tribe.md#defence).
