# D455: A fighter's planted feet are locked to the ground and step when the body leaves them

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

After the clip has posed a fighter and before the joints' world places are worked out, each foot the clip has on the ground (its ball within 5.5 cm of the body's ground and not swinging, under 3.2 m/s in the body's own frame) is locked where it landed, and the leg is bent to it by a two-bone solve, Godot's TwoBoneIK3D's (MIT): the knee where two circles meet, on the side the clip's knee bends. A move's drive, its warp or a turn to the foe moves the body over the locked foot. The foot steps, lifting 5 to 9 cm and coming down still where the clip's foot was 70% of the way, when it is 22 cm or 35° from the clip's foot while the other is down (38 cm, 70°, or the leg straight, at once), and once the body has stood still a quarter second with it more than 6 cm off (a settle step). It goes back to the clip, height first, when the clip lifts it past 7 cm, the body is down, or the move drives faster than 4 m/s (a roll). The rules are engine-free (`terra_motion::feet`); the game turns the thigh, calf and foot (`terra_creatures::melee::feet`), after the body's proportions and lift (`terra_walker::Fitted`).

## Why

Combat round 10 (2026-10-24). The duel film found 35 of 36 plants sliding, median 28 cm, worst 166 cm. Measured on the fight records: the clips' feet stand still while the move's drive and warp carry the body up to 1.1 m in a wind-up, the body turns to the foe over its feet, and the stride falls short of the walk. Shipped games give a move root motion its clip steps in, and keep the feet of a capsule moved by code (locomotion, a warp) by locking planted feet and releasing them past an unplant radius, as Unreal's Foot Placement node does; ours drive moves by curves until clips carry their own root (docs/movement.md, the prototype's shortcuts), and re-keying every blow with steps would not reach the turns or the walk. A lock and a step fix all three in one place, the fix-up layer docs/movement.md already plans (feet on the ground by two-bone IK, ported from Godot). Foot speed in the world cannot tell a swinging foot from one the body carries; speed in the body's own frame can (a drive or a turn moves the body, not the foot in it). A foot let go slid across while still low until it was lifted first.
