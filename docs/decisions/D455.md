# D455: A body's planted feet are locked to the ground and step when the body leaves them

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

After the clip has posed a body (a fighter, a walker, whatever it holds) and before the joints' world places are worked out, each foot the clip has on the ground (its ball within the height its clips stand a planted foot at, data by the clips it plays: 5.5 cm for a fighter's stance, its move set's, 7.5 cm for a man's jog and sprint, his kind's in `movers.ron`, as a stride rolls heel to toe; and not swinging, under 3.2 m/s over the ground the clip's feet stand on: the body's own frame for a fighter, whatever it plays, its walk too; for a walker's clip that travels, played in place at its pace, that frame with the ground passing under it as fast as the clip strides, so a stride's planted foot reads still) is locked where it landed, and the leg is bent to it by a two-bone solve, Godot's TwoBoneIK3D's (MIT): the knee where two circles meet, on the side the clip's knee bends. A stride, a move's drive, its warp or a turn to the foe moves the body over the locked foot. The foot steps, lifting 5 to 9 cm and coming down still where the clip's foot was 70% of the way, when it is 22 cm or 35° from the clip's foot while the other is down (38 cm, 70°, or the leg straight, at once), and once the body has stood still a quarter second with it more than 6 cm off (a settle step). It goes back to the clip, height first, when the clip lifts it past the let-go height (7 cm for a fighter, 9 cm for a man's stride), the body is down or off the ground, or the move drives faster than 4 m/s (a roll). The rules are engine-free (`terra_motion::feet`); the game turns the thigh, calf and foot of every body (`terra_walker::feet`; the figure sets the ground its clip strides on, the fight a fighter's stance and when its feet are let go, `terra_creatures::melee::feet`), after the body's proportions and lift (`terra_walker::Fitted`).

## Why

Combat round 10 (2026-10-24). The duel film found 35 of 36 plants sliding, median 28 cm, worst 166 cm. Measured on the fight records: the clips' feet stand still while the move's drive and warp carry the body up to 1.1 m in a wind-up, the body turns to the foe over its feet, and the stride falls short of the walk. Shipped games give a move root motion its clip steps in, and keep the feet of a capsule moved by code (locomotion, a warp) by locking planted feet and releasing them past an unplant radius, as Unreal's Foot Placement node does; ours drive moves by curves until clips carry their own root (docs/movement.md, the prototype's shortcuts), and re-keying every blow with steps would not reach the turns or the walk. A lock and a step fix all three in one place, the fix-up layer docs/movement.md already plans (feet on the ground by two-bone IK, ported from Godot). Foot speed in the world cannot tell a swinging foot from one the body carries; speed in the body's own frame can (a drive or a turn moves the body, not the foot in it). A foot let go slid across while still low until it was lifted first.
