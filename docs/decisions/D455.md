# D455: A body's planted feet are locked to the ground and step when the body leaves them

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

After the clip has posed a body (a fighter, a walker, whatever it holds) and before the joints' world places are worked out, each foot is locked by its clips' own lock curve, as ALS's FootLock curves and motion matching's contact labels do: baked per clip into its file (`tools/characters/markers.ts`), 1 while its heel or toe tip is still over the ground the clip strides on (the clip's one pace for a stride played in place, the body for a clip that stands; under 0.5 m/s or a fifth of the pace; height only a loose gate, 7 cm over that point's lowest, as a toe-off rolls the ball up nearly still), from touchdown (its heel or toe within 4 cm of its lowest, up to 0.15 s before it goes still), falling to 0 over 0.1 s as it leaves; a stride that skates over 6 cm in a plant or a walk with neither foot down fails the bake. The game sums the curves of the clips coming in on the legs by their weight (a fade is read by the pose it goes to, as inertialization does); the lock weight falls with that curve, no faster than 0.1 s from 1 to 0, and rises again only once it is back at 1. A locked foot keeps its ball of the foot where it was locked over the ground and its heading, taking the clip's height and roll (it flattens and rolls off its toe as the clip does); as the weight falls it is eased back to the clip over an arc half as high as the way it goes. A foot whose clip swings it faster than 3.2 m/s is not locked; the frame a body comes down, a foot the clip has on the ground (its ankle within 15 cm) is locked however it swings. The leg is bent to the foot by a two-bone solve, Godot's TwoBoneIK3D's (MIT): the knee where two circles meet, on the side the clip's knee bends. A stride, a move's drive, its warp or a turn to the foe moves the body over the locked foot. The foot steps, lifting 5 to 9 cm and coming down still where the clip's foot was 70% of the way, when it is 22 cm or turned 45° about the up (a turn in place, Lyra's and ALS's threshold) from the clip's foot while the other is down (38 cm, 70°, or the leg straight, at once), and once the body has stood still a quarter second with it more than 6 cm off (a settle step). It goes as the clip has it when the body is down or off the ground, or the move drives faster than 4 m/s (a roll). A body whose feet cannot be kept (no legs, no animation player, no clip with markers) says so in the log and the fight record (`gated`); one showing its rest pose with no clip stands on both feet. The rules are engine-free (`terra_motion::feet`); the game turns the thigh, calf and foot of every body (`terra_walker::feet`; the figure sets the ground its clip strides on, the fight a fighter's stance and when its feet are let go, `terra_creatures::melee::feet`), after the body's proportions and lift (`terra_walker::Fitted`).

## Why

Combat round 10 (2026-10-24). The duel film found 35 of 36 plants sliding, median 28 cm, worst 166 cm. Measured on the fight records: the clips' feet stand still while the move's drive and warp carry the body up to 1.1 m in a wind-up, the body turns to the foe over its feet, and the stride falls short of the walk. Shipped games give a move root motion its clip steps in, and keep the feet of a capsule moved by code (locomotion, a warp) by locking planted feet and releasing them past an unplant radius, as Unreal's Foot Placement node does; ours drive moves by curves until clips carry their own root (docs/movement.md, the prototype's shortcuts), and re-keying every blow with steps would not reach the turns or the walk. A lock and a step fix all three in one place, the fix-up layer docs/movement.md already plans (feet on the ground by two-bone IK, ported from Godot). Foot speed in the world cannot tell a swinging foot from one the body carries; speed in the body's own frame can (a drive or a turn moves the body, not the foot in it). A foot let go slid across while still low until it was lifted first.
