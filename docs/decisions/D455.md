# D455: A body's planted feet are locked to the ground and step when the body leaves them

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

After the clip has posed a body (a fighter, a walker, whatever it holds) and before the joints' world places are worked out, each foot is locked by its clips' own lock curve, as ALS's FootLock curves and motion matching's contact labels do: baked per clip into its file (`tools/characters/markers.ts`), 1 while its heel or toe tip is still over the ground the clip strides on (the clip's one pace for a stride played in place, the body for a clip that stands; under 0.5 m/s or a fifth of the pace; height only a loose gate, 7 cm over that point's lowest, as a toe-off rolls the ball up nearly still), from touchdown (its heel or toe within 4 cm of its lowest, up to 0.15 s before it goes still), falling to 0 over 0.1 s as it leaves; a stride that skates over 6 cm in a plant or a walk with neither foot down fails the bake. The game sums the curves of the clips coming in on the legs by their weight (a fade is read by the pose it goes to, as inertialization does); the lock weight falls with that curve, no faster than 0.1 s from 1 to 0, and rises again only once it is back at 1. A locked foot keeps its ball of the foot where it was locked over the ground and its heading, taking the clip's height and roll (it flattens and rolls off its toe as the clip does); as the weight falls it is eased back to the clip from the foot as held (its ball where it was locked, its heading, the clip's height, pitch and roll), not from the lock's own pose, over an arc half as high as the way it goes (easing back to the flat foot laid at the heel's strike while the clip rolled off its toe bent the jog's ankle 100°, D584's bone audit; fading only the offset the lock held, as Unreal's Foot Placement unplants, carried a foot across the ground with its clip in a landing and a turning start, so the foot goes across only as it lifts). A foot whose clip swings it faster than 3.2 m/s is not locked; the frame a body comes down, a foot the clip has on the ground (its ankle within 15 cm) is locked however it swings; and a foot within 3 cm of where it rests under a full curve is locked however fast the pose sweeps it (a crossfade over a stride, Idle fading in at a wall: ALS's foot lock), as is a foot blending back that its clips have down again, and its step on to the clip is a recovery step, 0.12 s against a step's 0.16. A new lock starts only once the foot as placed is down, its sole's lower point (heel or ball) no more than 7 cm over where that point rests locked, read over the ground as probed (not as eased, and a raise lagging under it read as on it) (Unreal's Foot Placement plants within PlantSettings' DistanceToGround; the bake's height gate), so a clip coming in at 1 while the pose shown still brings the foot down (a stop's fade), or a foot over lower ground the pelvis has not yet come down to, is locked when it lands, not in the air; a lock already held is not let go for it. Nor is a sole down whose ball hangs over its ground more than 7 cm past its heel (a heel on a step down's edge, the forefoot over the drop; a ball on a step up with the heel hanging stands), and a new lock is read again on its own probe in its first frame (Unreal plants on the trace it makes that frame), refused there and placed free until it is down. Each foot is then put on the ground under it, as Unreal's Foot Placement node and ALS place feet (`terra_motion::placement`, through the walk's own world, `terra_walker::underfoot`): a locked foot is probed under its heel and ball once, at its lock, and that ground (its height in the world and its normal) is frozen for the lock's life (a lock straddling a riser is probed again while it straddles, and set on the ground under it once it no longer does); a swinging foot is probed under its heel and ball once a frame, down from over the body's ground (not the shown figure's, eased under the body up a stair); the foot is pitched by the heel's and ball's ground and rolled by its normal within 30° (a sole straddling a riser, its heel's and ball's grounds steeper apart than that, is not pitched toward the lower tread but stands flat on the higher as its normal has it, ALS's alignment to the normal its trace meets, the point over the edge read as over the lower tread by a lock's start), turned about its ball (so a locked ball stays put, the turn made only as far as the leg reaches), and raised or lowered until neither heel nor ball is under its ground and one rests on it (a sole rests on the highest ground under it), eased over 0.04 s in the world (the body stepping a tread moves no foot's ground), out of the ground at once and a swinging foot's raise lagging its ground by no more than half its clip's clearance, a locked foot's not at all (a swinging foot over a higher tread touches it only as its clip's foot comes down, when the lock takes it). The pelvis drops by the lower foot's need, as ALS's and Unity's foot IK offset it, on a critically damped spring (45 rad/s, Unreal's Foot Placement pelvis spring) eased in the world (a body stepping down onto ground the pelvis went to does not dip again); as Unreal bounds it by Min/MaxExtensionRatio, it drops no further than folds a planted leg's hip-ankle to 0.4 of its length (ground lower is a drop: that foot is left out and stays short), and a lowered foot is put no farther from the lowered hip than the clip has it or 0.98 of the leg. The leg is bent to the foot by a two-bone solve, Godot's TwoBoneIK3D's (MIT): the knee where two circles meet, on the side the clip's knee bends. A stride clip faster than its play rate covers is warped longer first, each foot along the way from its hip, so a lock takes the warped foot (Unreal's Stride Warping), and the pelvis lowered for a planted leg to reach it no farther than the clip's (a foot being let go does not pull it down: a warped sprint's trailing foot did as it lifted, bending the other knee 50-70°, D584's bone audit). A stride, a move's drive, its warp or a turn to the foe moves the body over the locked foot. The foot steps from where it is shown (held, not the lock's own pose), lifting 5 to 9 cm and coming down still where the clip's foot was 70% of the way, when its ball is 22 cm from the clip's or it is turned 45° about the up (a turn in place, Lyra's and ALS's threshold) from the clip's foot while the other is down (38 cm, 70°, or the leg straight with its ball over 5 cm off, at once: a clip's own foot out of reach is reached no better by stepping to it), and once the body has stood still a quarter second with it more than 6 cm off (a settle step); how far off is the ball's, the point the lock holds, its heading judged apart. It goes as the clip has it when the body is down or off the ground, or the move drives faster than 4 m/s (a roll). A body whose feet cannot be kept (no legs, no animation player, no clip with markers) says so in the log and the fight record (`gated`); one showing its rest pose with no clip stands on both feet. The rules are engine-free (`terra_motion::feet`); the game turns the thigh, calf and foot of every body (`terra_walker::feet`; the figure sets the ground its clip strides on, the fight a fighter's stance and when its feet are let go, `terra_creatures::melee::feet`), after the body's proportions and lift (`terra_walker::Fitted`).

## Why

Combat round 10 (2026-10-24). The duel film found 35 of 36 plants sliding, median 28 cm, worst 166 cm. Measured on the fight records: the clips' feet stand still while the move's drive and warp carry the body up to 1.1 m in a wind-up, the body turns to the foe over its feet, and the stride falls short of the walk. Shipped games give a move root motion its clip steps in, and keep the feet of a capsule moved by code (locomotion, a warp) by locking planted feet and releasing them past an unplant radius, as Unreal's Foot Placement node does; ours drive moves by curves until clips carry their own root (docs/movement.md, the prototype's shortcuts), and re-keying every blow with steps would not reach the turns or the walk. A lock and a step fix all three in one place, the fix-up layer docs/movement.md already plans (feet on the ground by two-bone IK, ported from Godot). Foot speed in the world cannot tell a swinging foot from one the body carries; speed in the body's own frame can (a drive or a turn moves the body, not the foot in it). A foot let go slid across while still low until it was lifted first.
