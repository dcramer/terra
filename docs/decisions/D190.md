# D190: How the ground and the light are drawn

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Covered ground is drawn from real textures, soil, grass, rock and sand in one texture array, projected along all three axes (triplanar) and blended by what grows there, each tinted by the planet's colour for it so a planet still sets its ground's colours. The shade is lit by the sky above and the sunlit ground below, from a small cube map painted from the sky's colours and filtered by Bevy each frame (`GeneratedEnvironmentMapLight`), not one flat ambient colour. The air, the exposure and tone mapping are D212.

## Why

The developers said the scene had gaps toward realism, "might be lighting and ground textures" (2026-10-12). Triplanar texturing and sky light are what shipped games with voxel ground do; a painted cube map follows our own sky and its day, where a captured one would not.
