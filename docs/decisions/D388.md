# D388: Quiet gates

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`cargo xtask check` and `--quick` print a line a stage that passes (`✓ clippy (41.2s)`) and one at the end. A stage that fails prints `✗ <stage>` and only what fixes it, at most 50 lines: each failing test with its panic (its backtrace left to the log), the compiler's errors in cargo's short format, a checker's faults as it prints them; then its full log, `target/check/<stage>.log`, and the command that runs it alone. The stages use cargo's own quiet modes (`-q`, `--message-format=short`) and keep everything they say in their logs (`xtask/src/stage.rs`). The seed's builds, and the builds `bench` and `play` start with, run the same way. `TERRA_VERBOSE=1`, for any task and the hooks, or `check --verbose`, streams everything as before. The stages and what passes are as they were (D37).

## Why

The gates' output landed whole in the committing agent's context on every commit and push, about 650 commits in four days, and was paid again on every later turn: a passing `check --quick` said 2,457 lines, 140 kB, and now says 10 lines, 391 bytes; a planted failing test now comes out in 13 lines, a planted clippy fault in 7, each with its message. Long output is cut at about 30,000 characters, sometimes before the failure.
