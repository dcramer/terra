# D320: What every kit's surfaces are shaded and mapped by, beyond its own data

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Three rules in shared code, so no kit needs its own (the developers: kits "cleanly designed so each one doesnt have to be customized by the game"): a face looking down is shaded as a modelled piece's baked occlusion would shade it, up to two thirds of its light within about 55 degrees of straight down (`dress::underside`, every surface a kit draws), because the sky light lights an underside by the sunlit ground's whole bounce; each sawn end takes its member's shade and warmth and then its own, a tenth lighter or darker and greyed toward its lightness by up to six tenths (`timber::end_colour`), so a wall's ends read as cut at different times and weathered differently rather than one sticker; and a board drawn from one plank of its texture (`strips`) takes the plank across every face of it, running on from face to face round its section, not one column smeared down its sides; and a long wall's joints shifted from course to course by the golden ratio of a log's length, so no two courses within four of each other are jointed together (D178's stagger had lined up every other course), none in a cube another wall crosses, and the daub jointed a few centimetres off the logs' joint; a modelled verge board seated square to its roof, unsheared, its ends cut plumb (`Line::plumb`); a ridge capping turned over as much of its width as its thickness swings through at the ridge (`roofing::crest_blend`); and relief mapping faded out as the view grazes a surface or where it turns sharply, over tangents weighted by area as MikkTSpace weighs them; a flight of ramps' copings run on from stone to stone, mitred to a level coping at a ramp's head and foot, run down over a half beside its foot, its square ends cut plumb; hips' cappings turned as the ridge's but near their tops; and a room's light probe its own light from every way and its own reflection, a fifth of it, not the open sky's (D155); and what faces up under a roof shaded as its sky is hidden (`dress::sheltered`). `cargo xtask texels` measures how far each kit's textures are stretched, from the meshes.

## Why

The kit review ([research/kit-review-2026-10-14.md](../../research/kit-review-2026-10-14.md)): lintels' soffits and every quoin's underside lit brighter and greener than the wall's shaded face; every log end and hewn end one tint, bright beside weathered faces; joists' and rim beams' sides one column of their plank, 99:1. The developers: "theres still imperfections in some of the kits".
