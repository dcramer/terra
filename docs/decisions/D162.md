# D162: How recruits take work

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Recruits take part on their own, in whatever way the simulation makes sense; the player does not assign them. Proposed for how: the best-skilled takes each piece of work, then whoever likes it, then whoever is nearest; who does what is worked out again only when something changes; asking a recruit is the one override; those with nothing to do help carry and haul.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12, first as likely ("they likely will automatically 'claim' a job based on either some stats superiority or something else"), then: "i like the idea that you are able to recruit folks to your tribe/village, bring them back safely, and then they will automatically just participate in whatever way the simulation makes sense". Skills grown by doing risk locking a recruit into the first work they took, which liking is proposed to counter ([tribe.md](../tribe.md#who-does-what)).
