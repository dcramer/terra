# D193: What holds, what falls, and plans' limits

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Nothing is taken away while anything is attached to it or rests on it: an item on a floor, a roof on its walls, a door in its hole; a player is refused and told what holds it, so nothing has to drop. What loses its support without being asked, to an attack or the ground giving way, comes down; nothing floats. What is destroyed or comes down bursts into fragments of its own material that fall onto the ground, cannot be touched, and fade once they have settled (2026-10-15, amending debris that gave back part of its materials); giving back materials from what comes down is put off while building takes no real resources. A cube is whole, a stair, a slope or a half, more shapes as kinds need them. Ground is cut only inside a building's footprint, down to its floor (D131 narrowed). A claim has a budget of planned cubes, and plans are seen only by its tribe, with the hammer in hand, nearby. Taking apart gives back exactly what went in, to the claim's storage when the taker did not pay for it, and who may take apart is by rank; a cube taken apart goes with its plan, nothing left behind as a blueprint, and undo puts it back (2026-10-15). Claims do not overlap, keep a margin, and everything built, eaves and footings too, stays inside its claim.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-12, answering [construction-001.md](../proposals/construction-001.md#open-for-the-developers)'s questions 8 to 14: "broadly we should refuse to remove things with other things attach. otherwise we have to add another system to 'drop' those things"; "i agree collapse into debris"; "cube shapes are fine for stair slope and half"; "i think the rest makes sense". On taking apart, asked whether a building taken apart should stay as blueprints: "it should deconstruct and return materials" (2026-10-15). On destruction, seeing debris piles (2026-10-15): "for destruction it needs to kind of.. 'explode' into voxels if that makes sense.. like a bunch of cubes that fall onto the ground and you cant interact with that might fade when done? like when it falls down or gets destroyed or w/e", "dont scope creep", building with infinite pretend resources for now; and "all the destroy thing needs to be performant AND look sick".
