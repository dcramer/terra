# D297: Recruits at a build

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

D161 says recruits help by carrying to a build, and perhaps building (Leaning); nothing of it is built, as there are no recruits who take work yet: a freed prisoner follows you and a smith works a forge, nothing more (`play::people`). Recommended: since building draws on storage (D141, D295), small materials are never hauled to a plan, and recruits carry only big ones (D296) from where they lie or are piled to the plans the tribe works on (D170), as RimWorld's colonists haul to blueprints and Sons of the Forest's companion fetches logs; recruits put parts in only once that has been played, as D161 leaves Leaning. First, the tribe's list of work (D162, [tribe.md](../tribe.md#who-does-what)).

## Why

For the developers: what recruits do at a build, and in what order the tribe's work comes, are design. It waits on D296, and on recruits who take work.
