# D236: Earth, and wattle and daub as a framed kind

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

D227's kits, as data (D136): **earth** (`earth.ron`) is a block kind drawn as its surface (D197) in ambientCG's CC0 `Ground104` (`packed_earth`, fetched by `tools/textures`' `ambientcg:`), gritty earth with small stones in ridged layers, displaced 7 cm by its own height and its grit in parallax, its yellow taken to a grey-brown by the kit's `tint`, packed in its shape (the kit's `packed`, `surface::Packed`): lumps of a low-frequency noise and each lift rounded out between its seams, which follow the strata's, so it bulges, its lifts read as ridges and its rounded 12 cm edges wander; its lifts a little lighter and darker by turns with a darker seam between (the masks' `strata` and `lift`), weathered by role from its cubes: darker over its own foot (`splash`), streaked under sills and open tops but not under eaves (`streaks`), paler arrises (`wear`) and paler from a storey up, dry under the eaves (`dry`); dressed by role (D215) with a rubble plinth at its foot, a stone coping with a drip on a top open to the sky, and oak lintels, sills, sole plates, fascias and treads (so it costs a little Wood, D215), an opening's head and sill each one run across its full width over its highest row and under its lowest, however ragged the hole (`dressing`), set into the wall, its face bedded behind it (`dress::Bed`) so the lintel sits all but flush and the earth is built up unevenly over its edges (2026-10-14) (a profile's `material`, its texture then running along it as grain), its corners and reveals left round. **Wattle and daub** (`wattle.ron`) is a block kind with a `framing`: its cubes the infill, pale daub (`patterned_clay_plaster`) with a little relief; a frame of squared oak laid over them by rule from the cubes (`layout::frames`, `Piece::Beam`, drawn by the timber code's adzed squared section, D200): a sill plate, a plate and a doubled girt between storeys, a post every `bay` cubes on the grid but none within a metre of a row's end, end posts at inside corners and against other kinds, rails at the kit's heights, under their line in a storey's lower half and over it in its upper so they run into an opening's frame, braces in the lowest and highest panels at each end, a square post up every outside corner, through-wall jambs, heads and sills round every hole, and a gable, the wall carried up into its roof (D241), framed as a triangle by the same rule, not as its cubes' steps (a girt under it, studs over the posts cut off under the roof, rails out to the roof, a brace at each end parallel to the roof); every member 3 cm proud of the daub. **Lime-washed earth** (`limewash.ron`, Poly Haven's `medieval_wall_01`) is earth's rules in a second kind, data only. A framed kind's `core` (`Piece::Wattle`, woven hazel made from a generated image by `tools/textures`, D191) is drawn where a cube is in but not finished, or is being carved, and goes in a layer ahead of the daub; its frame and core go in at a cube's first step (four steps: frame and wattle, then three coats). Framed kinds' floors stand on stone footings, and the footing kind is the block kind of lowest id that is not framed (`Kits::footing`). Meets (D232): earth weighs 3 and runs through timber and wattle, carrying an oak sole plate under them, and stone runs through it; wattle weighs 1 and every other kind runs through it. A further framed kind (plaster, brick nogging) is a kit file. Since D258, the frame's members are the kit's hand-made modules, laid by the same rule. Brick, once a third block kind here, was removed on 2026-10-15 by the developers: "you scope creeped on brick and it looks dumb"; no material is added unless they ask.

## Why

Mine, building D227's kits on 2026-10-13: a frame is how wattle and daub, and every half-timbered wall, is built, and laying it by rule from the cubes keeps it one structure with no special pieces, as the role dress does; the frame as data lets plaster and brick nogging follow as files. No library has wattle, so it was generated and tiled as D191 says. Earth first in Poly Haven's `clay_plaster` read as flat orange card (2026-10-13); no Poly Haven material has earth packed in lifts, and ambientCG's scan of layered gritty earth gives the relief and colour that read as hand-built. On brick costing stone, asked on 2026-10-15: "not important right now we're focused on visuals mostly atm"; brick's cost is a placeholder until they take it up.
