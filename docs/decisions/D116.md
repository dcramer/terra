# D116: Settings and generated realms

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

Every realm's theme fills the same gameplay roles, as models fill palette roles, so dozens of generated realms fit one lore; rank is the only power axis. Realms differ in how much magic they have. A theme's weapons differ in how they play, as swords from staves, not in strength. Loot, procedural loot included, may become part of the game, not a large part. Settings may mix within a realm later, carefully. How realms are explained is left to the generator.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11, reacting to [settings.md](../settings.md): "roughly on point". Each setting's speciality is undecided.
