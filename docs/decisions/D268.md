# D268: The lush forest's middle layer, and thin grass in tussocks

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D246. Between the floor and the canopy stands a middle layer one to five metres tall (`experiments/lush/thicket.rs`), grown by the trees' own rules as SpeedTree grows shrubs from its trees: hazel stools of four to nine leaning stems, holly as a dense egg of dark leaves, young spruces, saplings of oak or hazel two to five metres, bramble as a tangle of arching canes, and snags, small trees long dead, broken off and bare, which stop a walker (fitted as D267, in `collide`). Holly and bramble are new cards of the shared atlas, made as the others were. They are placed as D246's undergrowth, thickest in half shade at the paths' and the clearing's edges and in gaps, holly taking the deep shade under oaks and young spruces the gaps under conifers, the drifts leaving glades and the paths open. Each is drawn near and, past 28 m, with fewer, larger cards, then fades out by 85 to 110 m; its shadow is a sparse copy that shrinks away by 24 to 45 m (D234), and only plants in 16 m squares near the camera have one, spawned and despawned as it moves (`lush/shade.rs`), since Bevy checks every shadow caster against every cascade every frame. On the floor, thin grass grows in tussocks, only some clumps and those thick at their middle, with bare ground between, rather than an even stubble (`cover/blades.rs`); moss round a tree's foot only in broken patches and on the floor only in the dampest hollows, darker; the forest's soil a dark grey-brown loam and its litter and needles less orange; the floor's cards greyed 12% and darkened 10% and lit through less (0.35, from 0.55), hazel and ferns tinted toward a shaded green. Twig litter, a dozen to thirty thin twigs a patch, lies under the canopy. Rocks are many small and a few big, a third of them half buried or more so only their mossy backs show, as the floor's hummocks; moss grows only on their tops and ledges, and pale lichen crusts the bare stone. The vantage sheet measures how much of an eye-height view within 20 m the undergrowth hides: at sixteen places on a spiral, eye 1.7 m, looking level, the clock and wind stopped, each view pictured with what is within 1.2 m of the eye left out, as a walker steps aside from a bush, again with the undergrowth within 20 m hidden (`lush.undergrowth`), and again to know the noise; the share of the band round the horizon, the middle fifth of the picture, that changed.

## Why

A review against Sons of the Forest (5/10, the undergrowth 4/10): nothing between knee height and the canopy, sightlines of 50 to 80 m between trunks, a tidy park; grass as even sprigs over bare dirt, moss halos round every trunk, salmon dirt, lime shrubs. The developers want "lots of grass, shrubs, trees, good quality". Measured: the undergrowth hid 6.3% of the horizon band (median 5.9%), now 44.0% (median 36.5%; places from 2% to 91%), against an aim of 30 to 60%. The walk bench on the RTX 3080 is 12.95 ms median and 15.71 ms at the 99th percentile, holding the budget (main before: 12.6 and 14.2), its slowest frames the camera passing through a thicket; with every shadow copy spawned at once the game's work was 1.5 ms more a frame. lodcheck (worst 0.05%) and collide pass.
