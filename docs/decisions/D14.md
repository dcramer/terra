# D14: Art direction

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Textured, lightly stylised realism: real materials in detailed textures, a little shaded and simplified, neither photoreal nor cartoon, with strong silhouettes and true proportions at life size (D110). The main guideline for everything the game draws, one style guide for every realm and setting: [art-style.md](../art-style.md). Models carry their textures (colour, normal and roughness maps, as Tripo makes them); a realm brings its own materials rather than a palette.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-12, after seeing a log cabin with flat colour and with Tripo's textures kept ([research/realistic-building-textures.md](../../research/realistic-building-textures.md)): "We can use realistic textures but they should be a little shaded. We're not aiming for photo realistic, but not cartoon-y." Earlier the same day: direction D, graphic layers, flat colour by palette role, chosen from the mood boards to be tried; the developers found it "a little too cartoony" and "low poly".
