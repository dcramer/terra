# D109: Crafting at stations

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Making takes time. You click a recipe, without holding, and it joins the station's queue, one per click; what it takes is paid when queued and given back if cancelled, after Enshrouded. Most stations work only while you stay at them. Some, such as a smelter, work through their queue on their own, and what they make waits in them. A station's screen is like the build menu: tiles of the recipes you have learned.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11, answering the questions in [crafting.md](../crafting.md#the-station-screen). Paying on queueing and the screen's layout are from the proposal, which they accepted. Fuel for stations that work on their own is open.
