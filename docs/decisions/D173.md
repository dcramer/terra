# D173: Knowledge of essences, and essence users among people

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

What a world knows of what stones give grows as anyone in it awakens an outcome not yet known, and knowing ritual magic shows one or two outcomes beyond it. Leaning: some areas have people who hold essences, or special kinds of people who do; what they have is known locally.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "yeah I think so, especially because maybe NPCs will get essences in some areas", and of ritual magic showing more, "kind of nice idea because then it rewards you for progressing into other stuff". See [magic.md](../magic.md#before-you-awaken-what-is-known), [people.md](../people.md#essence-users).
