# D362: Construction materials' texture detail near

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

How D290's texture arrays carry detail a metre off, as shipped games do ([research/texture-detail-2026-10-15.md](../../research/texture-detail-2026-10-15.md)). **Texel density**: a texture set's colour and normal map are 2,048 texels a side at the high and medium presets and 1,024 at the low (`Quality::texture_side`, a game's texture quality setting; a change of preset makes the arrays again), its occlusion-roughness-metal and height, which carry no fine detail, 1,024 at all; packs are made at 2,048 (`tools/kits/pack.ts`). **Compression**: colour BC7 (mode 6, its line fitted by least squares as bc7enc does), normal BC5, ARM BC1, height BC4, encoded on a worker as a set is made resident and kept on disk once made (`arrays::cache`, the user's cache folder, keyed by the pictures and how they are stored, as an engine keeps cooked textures), the pieces' atlases alike; mipmaps averaged in linear light for colour and kept unit for normals; 16x anisotropic filtering and TAA's mip bias of -1 as before. 12 MiB a set at high, 4 at low, the arrays under 400 MiB for a village of every baseline material; they grow 8 slots at a time past their first 16. **Detail maps**: each pattern lays a small texture of its material's fine grain over its textures near (`details` in its file, per material slot: wood's every 40 cm on timber at 0.8, stone's every half metre on walls, floors and dressed stone at 0.6; a roof pack names its covering's, slate's stone at 0.4), faded out from 3 to 8 m and not drawn at low; its colour grey about the middle multiplied in as Unity's detail albedo is, its normal added (UDN); made by `tools/textures detail` from a CC0 scan with its broad swells taken out and brought to a set spread; checked by `cargo xtask kits` (grey, 0.1 to 1 m, strength to 1.5, over a texture it has). **Sharpening**: the picture is sharpened after TAA at every preset (`Quality::sharpening`, AMD's contrast-adaptive sharpening, 0.6 at high, 0.5 at low as before). **The log's bark** is baked at 2,048 from the Tripo log's 4K maps, its rows read between the bake's (not the nearest, which doubled every fifth), and the light it was painted in taken out round it (D262). Then, as the lead asked: the log's bark replaced by a 4K CC0 scan in patches over bare wood, sharper at every distance than the rebaked Tripo bark (D262); the pack tool fetching scans at 4K, where it fetched them at 1K; corner stones drawn at their wall's own scale (D215); `cargo xtask texels` reading the real height maps, so it sees corner stones.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "the log textures still look pretty bad. stone might need more improved details too? its important that the detail on our textures is great." Measured at 1 m on the RTX 5090: the arrays halved every 2K scan (a stone wall 512 texels a metre against 1,037 pixels a metre on screen), BC1 kept four shades a block, TAA took 58% of the finest detail, and the Tripo bark held a sixth of a scan's fine relief, six times darker underneath than on top.
