# D107: Disclosing AI

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The game says plainly that its assets are made with AI, on Steam's disclosure and wherever else it applies. How players or the press react is not a reason to avoid a tool.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11: "We'd disclose that everything is AI so that's not a big deal, I don't care about that." Follows from D1. Steam requires disclosing content made with AI that ships in the game; see [research/ai-models-for-art.md](../../research/ai-models-for-art.md#rights-and-reception).
