# D148: How a world fills with life

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The goals in [biomes.md](../biomes.md), with the developers' answers of 2026-10-12: forests as dense as Sons of the Forest's; biomes made by rules, never chosen from a list (proposed: species with niches competing for ground); worn paths a stretch goal; leaves as cards or clumps undecided. "Realistic" is the forest's density, not the look: the developers like Sons of the Forest's art style but think it likely beyond us, so D14 stands. The technical approach (objects, cover and far tiers; rules as a stack of fields; placement after Horizon Zero Dawn) is mine and Leaning until the first step measures it. Leaves as cards or clumps are chosen by eye later.

## Why

Asked for by the developers on 2026-10-12 after naming Sons of the Forest for its world design (D85): "Sons of the Forest is more realistic, and that's our goal"; "(worn paths) would be fucking amazing as a goal, but probably hard?"; biomes "TBD, remember procedural is the goal". Agreed the same day: "if we've locked in those decisions that's already great". Today plants are set once around the start and the ground beyond is bare. See [research/procedural-vegetation.md](../../research/procedural-vegetation.md).
