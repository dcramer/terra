# D194: Construction defaults

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Footings fill down to the ground and stop there, costed by the cube, the floor at the highest ground under the building. What goes in is a cube's fill: a log fills its course in every cube it spans, a block kind goes in a cube at a time, holding the key keeps going. Each kit's corners as its material really is: crisp cut stone and brick, round plaster and earth. Walls of any thickness. Stone, brick, plaster and earth are blocks; logs, timber framing, planks and wattle long members. The game lays footings into the plan as their own part, built first, stone by default, posts, stilts or none by choice. Nothing floats in the MVP (refines D142).

## Why

Left to me by the developers on 2026-10-12: "make the best default call that we can solve from a technical angle and is a good player experience", answering [construction-001.md](../proposals/construction-001.md#open-for-the-developers)'s questions 1 to 7 with its recommendations.
