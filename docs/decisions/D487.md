# D487: A landed blow shows a burst where the blade met the body, not a flash of the whole body

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Recommended by the agent (combat round 17): amend D456's flash. A blow that lands shows a white-hot glow at the contact point for 3 to 4 frames at 60, lighting what is near it, and a spray of sparks flung on the way the blade was going, gone in about 0.15 s (the move set's `landed.burst`); the struck body keeps its own colours. D456's hit-stop of at most 0.05 s and its camera kick stay as they are. Open until the developers judge the before and after films: keep the whole-body flash (D456 as it is), the burst alone (recommended), or both.

Shipped (combat round 18): the whole-body flash's code (`melee::flash`, the set's `landed.flash` and `flash_s`) is deleted, not kept as a knob at 0: nothing would use it, and a struck body lit whole is the 2D convention this decided against; git keeps it (round 17, 3f9f3513) should a later effect (a status tint, say) want a body lit. The scorecard's `hit.flash.frames` now holds the struck body unlit (bar 0 frames), beside `hit.burst.frames` for the contact.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, on the review desk: "Contact burst only". This settles the question below in that option's favour.

The developer, 2026-10-25, on the duel film with the whole-body flash (combat round 12): "idk that the flash is good". Re-read frame by frame, our reference's 3 to 4 frame flash is at the contact, not on the body: a glow a hand's width across with spark streaks flung out of it, then an orange half-ring, the dummy keeping its colour on every frame (Enshrouded Update 8, 247.717-247.783 s). Elden Ring, Sekiro, Monster Hunter World and God of War (2018) all mark a landed hit at the contact (sparks, blood, a spark burst) with a flinch, a kick and a short hit-stop; none lights the whole struck body, a 2D convention ([research](../../research/2026-10-25-what-a-landed-hit-shows.md)). Built so it can be seen (combat round 17): the burst is data (`landed.burst`, its glow and sparks 0 turn it off) and the whole-body flash a strength (`landed.flash`, 1 D456's, 0 none); the shipped set keeps D456's flash at 1 with the burst added, and the film the developers judge sets the flash to 0. A flinch layered over the struck body's move (an additive hit reaction) is the next step if wanted; today a hit plays its flinch as a whole clip.
