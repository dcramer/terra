# D450: Combat clips made by Kimodo, keyed to the guard pose

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D316 for combat. A move's clip is made with Kimodo's latest model (Kimodo-SOMA-RP-v1.2), from a prompt plus constraints, not from a prompt alone. The constraints are the set's guard pose, as a full-body keyframe at the clip's first and last frames, and the blade's path, as right-hand (and, for a shield move, left-hand) end-effector keys at the wind-up and the follow-through. The hand keys' poses come from two-bone IK on Kimodo's skeleton, to grip points written in the spec. A capture library's clips and blows keyed by hand are not the source; they stay the reference and the hand keys.

The player's light chain (slash, backhand, finisher) and heavy are Quaternius's CC0 UAL2 sword blows instead, as made and at their own speed (`Sword_Light_A` and `_B` each joined to its recovery, `Sword_Light_D`, `Sword_Heavy_D`; `Swap_*` in fight.json, from the committed cuts `art/animation/ual2_fight.glb` and `_rm.glb`). Each is led in from the guard, its first pose held 0.08 s while the whole body eases from the guard over the clip's calm start (so the lead is the blow's wind-up, not a swing of its own), and eased back to the guard at its end, as a montage blends in and out; the shield arm is held at the guard's throughout (D454, D531), and the body is driven by the clip's own root motion, scaled to the body (D573). Kimodo keyed to the guard stays the way to make a move no free set has; the partner's blows stay Kimodo's (`Open_*`).

The clip step is one command a move (round 4 builds it): spec, then guard and hand keys, then Kimodo takes, then made onto the mannequin (`made_clips.py`), then carried onto every body (D542) and exported, then `moves.ts` (the take kept is the first that passes), then its phases (`phases.ts`) written into the move set. It fails on `moves.ts` and on a sweep against the spec's.

## Why

The bake-off made a guard, a slash, a backhand and a block from each source and checked them with `moves.ts` on the base body, all carried by aim (research/2026-10-24-combat-clip-sources.md). Kimodo with only the guard keyed failed 4 checks, all of them the shield on the hand socket, which every source shares. Its blows started and ended within 0.03 m of the guard and chained at 0.03 m, its hands stayed clear and its contacts landed ahead. Today's prompt-only takes failed 11, Quaternius's CC0 UAL2 sword clips 18 (one-handed, so the shield arm hangs), and blows keyed by IK 6. Only Kimodo makes a new move from a prompt and keys unattended, which 250 moves need (movement.md, "Variants"). Its one fault of its own, a backhand sweeping the wrong way, is what a prompt cannot set and a hand key does.

The developers: "toolchain/pipeline is most important here, because you're an agent and we're newbs".

The bought blows (2026-10-30, the fight-swap trial, on the developer's desk as fight-trial-j3 and chosen by the agent, overrulable there): our keyed blows stood like a post (the hips turning 2-3 degrees, the hand at 3-4 m/s; research/2026-10-30-why-the-fight-clips-look-bad.md), where Quaternius's step, drop and turn (the hips 78-135 degrees, the hand 17-24 m/s), step in on their own root motion and land 1.25-1.76 m ahead. What had failed them in the bake-off was the shield arm hanging and their start away from our guard; the arm held at the guard's and the lead-in from the guard answer both, and the Enshrouded timing bars hold (`cargo xtask combat`). Eased in over 0.14 s, the finisher's and heavy's whole bodies turned 85-105 degrees to the clip's stance and whipped the tip round at 39-64 m/s before the blow; over 0.35-0.55 s, into the clip's own calm start, the tip stays under half the strike's pace (phases.test.ts).
