# D450: Combat clips made by Kimodo, keyed to the guard pose

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D316 for combat. A move's clip is made with Kimodo's latest model (Kimodo-SOMA-RP-v1.2), from a prompt plus constraints, not from a prompt alone. The constraints are the set's guard pose, as a full-body keyframe at the clip's first and last frames, and the blade's path, as right-hand (and, for a shield move, left-hand) end-effector keys at the wind-up and the follow-through. The hand keys' poses come from two-bone IK on Kimodo's skeleton, to grip points written in the spec. A capture library's clips and blows keyed by hand are not the source; they stay the reference and the hand keys.

The clip step is one command a move (round 4 builds it): spec, then guard and hand keys, then Kimodo takes, then made onto the mannequin (`made_clips.py`), then carried by aim onto every body and exported, then `moves.ts` (the take kept is the first that passes), then its phases (`phases.ts`) written into the move set. It fails on `moves.ts` and on a sweep against the spec's.

## Why

The bake-off made a guard, a slash, a backhand and a block from each source and checked them with `moves.ts` on the base body, all carried by aim (research/2026-10-24-combat-clip-sources.md). Kimodo with only the guard keyed failed 4 checks, all of them the shield on the hand socket, which every source shares. Its blows started and ended within 0.03 m of the guard and chained at 0.03 m, its hands stayed clear and its contacts landed ahead. Today's prompt-only takes failed 11, Quaternius's CC0 UAL2 sword clips 18 (one-handed, so the shield arm hangs), and blows keyed by IK 6. Only Kimodo makes a new move from a prompt and keys unattended, which 250 moves need (movement.md, "Variants"). Its one fault of its own, a backhand sweeping the wrong way, is what a prompt cannot set and a hand key does.

The developers: "toolchain/pipeline is most important here, because you're an agent and we're newbs".
