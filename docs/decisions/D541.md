# D541: Building meshes go up to a budget a frame, not a mesh a section past it

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The sections about to be shown are put on the GPU oldest first within one budget a frame, 120,000 corners for building and 40,000 for groups' details (D213), and only one mesh past it, if the frame has put none yet, so a mesh larger than the budget goes up alone in a frame of its own (`draw::budget`). Before, every section waiting let a mesh past the budget each frame, so a change of many large sections went up nearly whole in one frame. Shipped engines bound streaming uploads per frame, however many things ask: Unity's async upload pipeline gives texture and mesh uploads a time slice a frame (`QualitySettings.asyncUploadTimeSlice`), and Unreal's Nanite installs at most `r.Nanite.Streaming.MaxPageInstallsPerFrame` pages a frame. Bevy's own `RenderAssetBytesPerFrame` (D420) cannot do it: its mesh allocator writes every extracted mesh into its slabs whatever the cap, so the budget has to hold before the mesh is an asset.

## Why

In `castle` on the Radeon 8060S, the worst frame was 21 to 24 ms in every run, in the render world: `allocate_and_free_meshes` 13.9 ms and `prepare_erased_assets` 10.4 in one frame. A probe of `draw::upload` showed 4 to 8 sections laid again as the camera neared putting 42 to 58 MiB up in one frame (1.0 to 1.35 million corners, a mesh each of 250,000 to 600,000), and the frame three later 12 to 13 ms with the render world busy 10 to 12 ms.

`cargo xtask gpu bench -- cargo xtask bench castle town300 going-up world`, three runs each, at 569101bc and with this: `castle` median 4.95-5.00 to 4.86 ms (the two quiet runs before; the rest of that bench was busy), 99th percentile 7.6-8.1 to 8.2, worst 22.3-23.0 to 17.0-18.5; the sections laid again as the camera moved on screen in 995-1001 ms to 947-969, the castle drawn first to last in 1811-1891 to 1652-2162 ms. `town300` 5.48 to 5.42 ms (99th 8.51 to 8.38, first to last on screen 16,975 to 16,909 ms), `going-up` 4.30 to 4.31, `world` 7.51 to 7.44, all within the runs' spread.

What is left of castle's worst frame is not upload: it comes in the frame a change is swapped in, when nothing large is extracted, and a trace with pipelined rendering on puts it in the allocator (12 ms) beside the sea's material prepared again (10 ms, `planet::sea::sea_waves` changes it as the camera moves); round 3 of the performance work (~/.cache/terra-review/perf/handoff-r2.md). It was the allocator taking a new 256 MiB block of GPU memory from the driver, the sea's material only waiting on it (D543).

## Log

- **2026-10-27**: D541 decided (by the agent, engine performance round 2): building's meshes go up within one budget a frame, one mesh past it at most, not a mesh a section past it; castle's worst frame 22-23 to 17-18.5 ms, the sections as quick to screen, the other scenes as they were.
