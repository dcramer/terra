# D278: Bases far off: a hull, merged by cell to the horizon, and streamed by distance

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D213's far detail and D238. What is drawn of a base by distance (as authored, times the detail scale, D230): every piece as made to 25 m, the textured middle detail to 60 m, the baked far detail to 120 m (its ridge, hip and valley caps drawn twice their modelled length, `modules::FAR_REPEAT`, a roof's edge as modelled); from 120 m each section is the **hull** of its cubes (`terra_build::hull`, Unreal's approximated mesh): faces open to the air, coplanar ones of a colour merged into rectangles, cut cubes' tops at their shape's corner heights so roofs keep their pitch, each kit's materials' mean colour baked in, a slope's course one plane, 630 triangles a house where D238's proxy was 5,400; a section past 155 m (laid again within 140) holds its hull alone (`Reach::Hull`); from 600 m (handing over across ±10%, since a section and its cell are measured from different middles) each **cell** of a base, four sections a side, is one merged hull (`build::hlod`, as Unreal's HLOD merges a cell), to the horizon at 3 km, made from the cubes alone on a worker and again after a change once the camera is 300 m off. **Streaming**: a section further than 740 m is let go (its entity, meshes and pieces) and one is laid again inside 690 m; a base with nothing laid lets go of its copy and its laid pieces, so a base far off holds its cubes and its cells. Every base but the one being built is laid and drawn a **group** of sections at a time, four a side, set about its middle so a house is one (`store::GROUP`): its sections laid and drawn whole and put together, one mesh a detail and material, its meshes hung off its base, placed at the middle of its sections with anything in them and its ranges measured from there less as far as they reach and plus half a section, as an HLOD cluster switches (measured to its box, `use_aabb`, a whole house popped as it switched); the base being built keeps a section and its eight parts at a time, so an edit draws again only what it reached. Only the details in use are held: a section is laid near within 32 m (36 kept) and with its middle detail within 70 m (76 kept), each a few metres past where it is seen; a group of a base nobody is building holds each detail from a few metres before it is seen to a few after, or a second ahead of the camera, and those not wanted while 192 MiB of them fit at the high preset, none below (D213); every mesh's corners the same in every way welded into one (`Indexed::welded`), and every mesh but the base being built is packed (`draw::packed`, Bevy's compression: normals and tangents octahedral, texture places 16-bit, colours 8-bit, 16-bit indices where they fit; positions stay 32-bit, as Bevy 0.20's 16-bit positions drew nothing here). Each base's memory, CPU and GPU, is counted (`build.memory` over the remote; `Builds hold:` in a benchmark). Impostors (pictures from several sides) are not used: a merged hull is fewer pixels' worth of work and keeps light and shadow right; they are for shapes a hull cannot carry, as D203 found for trees. Instancing identical bases' cells is left to Bevy's batching, as bases are unique by their seeds. Its colours are each kit's tint over its materials' mean (a roof's covering's, a block kind's, a timber kind's logs'), as its near detail multiplies them, so a house far off is the colour it is near.

## Why

Mine, on 2026-10-14, at the developers' "we're also going to need to make sure our building system is performant, both when they're in the distance (idk if they'd even be rendered?)": bases vanished past 600 m, and every section of every base stayed laid with its far and proxy meshes wherever the camera was, 860 MiB of meshes for three hundred houses seen as specks ([research/building-scale.md](../../research/building-scale.md)).
