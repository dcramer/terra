# D334: The air's scale is the world's

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D212 and D299; D544 sets the vertical: the air at an eye is that of the real height its height stands for, never its height times this scale. Its dust is as much of that haze as the day's warmth where the start is leaves (`air::haze_for`): water's saturated vapour pressure there over a mild 10 °C day's, a third to all of it, so cold air is clear (D345). Every distance through the air is `AirScale::for_world` times the world's: as far through the air as the small planet's farthest ground was (its horizon from its highest hill times 18, about 14 km), for the farthest ground a world shows (the horizon from its highest ground), never less than the true air and never more than 18. The small planet, the lush forest's rolling ground and the flat 30 km yard keep 18; the 30 km world with mountains 2.5 km high sees 12 km and has nearly the true air. Under trees the forest's scale stays half the open air's (D299), never under 1; out in the open the haze lets one see as far through the air as before, so farther in the world, and a world whose views reach kilometres with no canopy volume stands in the open air rather than the forest's haze. The dust (Mie) is held per world metre instead, 1.5 times a clear day's at 18, but never more than Earth's clean continental air on a clear day (`air::CLEAR_DAY_DUST`, 12 times: an aerosol optical depth of 0.064 at 550 nm, OPAC's, Hess, Koepke and Schult 1998), which is what the mountains' 1.08 gets: some 70 km of visibility on the valley floor, the far ridges still whitened over 10 to 30 km, the gases' blue the true air's. Held per metre it was 25 times there (an optical depth of 0.13, a hazy day of 40 km), and the valley's range stood only 0.05 darker than its sky against the references' 0.2 (mountains r4: 0.051 to 0.103, far contrast 0.073 to 0.105; the lake, tundra and town within noise). So the eye stands in the air at the real height its height over the sea stands for (`air::real_height_m`, D544's), never the scale times its height (`air::centre`), and no reachable height draws the black sky of 27 km up.

## Why

At 18 times, the mountain lake's camera 1.3 km over the sea stood 24 km up the air, its sky near black, and a peak 5 km off hazed as 90 km of real air (docs/demos.md, the air's scale); the forest's 240 m haze, which a world with no canopy volume kept, hid everything past the shore. With the true air's dust the lake's sky measured half as bright as the references' and twice as saturated, and its far land darker than theirs (look.ts by band against `mountain-lake-day` and `morskie-oko-day`). The scale stretched the eye's height too, so the 2 km stand-in's eye 1.5 km up stood 27 km up the air, its sky near black and its horizon an orbit's (sky stage 2, round 3); the air one sees through is the scale's question, the air one stands in the height's.
