# D334: The air's scale is the world's

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D212 and D299. Its dust is as much of that haze as the day's warmth where the start is leaves (`air::haze_for`): water's saturated vapour pressure there over a mild 10 °C day's, a third to all of it, so cold air is clear (D345). Every distance through the air is `AirScale::for_world` times the world's: as far through the air as the small planet's farthest ground was (its horizon from its highest hill times 18, about 14 km), for the farthest ground a world shows (the horizon from its highest ground), never less than the true air and never more than 18. The small planet, the lush forest's rolling ground and the flat 30 km yard keep 18; the 30 km world with mountains 2.5 km high sees 12 km and has nearly the true air. Under trees the forest's scale stays half the open air's (D299), never under 1; out in the open the haze lets one see as far through the air as before, so farther in the world, and a world whose views reach kilometres with no canopy volume stands in the open air rather than the forest's haze. The dust (Mie) is held per world metre instead, 1.5 times a clear day's at 18 and so about 25 times at the mountains' 1.08: a hazy summer's day with some 40 km of visibility, its far ridges whitened and its sky pale, while the gases' blue is the true air's.

## Why

At 18 times, the mountain lake's camera 1.3 km over the sea stood 24 km up the air, its sky near black, and a peak 5 km off hazed as 90 km of real air (docs/demos.md, the air's scale); the forest's 240 m haze, which a world with no canopy volume kept, hid everything past the shore. With the true air's dust the lake's sky measured half as bright as the references' and twice as saturated, and its far land darker than theirs (look.ts by band against `mountain-lake-day` and `morskie-oko-day`).
