# D317: Under the trees shiny things mirror leaves, not the open sky

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D270 and D286. The sky's reflection is scaled by how much sky is open over the eye (`sky_reflected`: specular occlusion, once for the eye's place, which is right for what stands near it), and a crown's glow where it hides the sky is a quarter of that sky's light, not a twelfth, so what is not a leaf is lit by the leaves above as well as by the gaps. No cost to frame time.

## Why

The irradiance volume hid the sky's diffuse light under the crowns but not its reflection, so the player's leather, skin and pack mirrored the whole open sky in deep shade and read lilac by a low sun; the same body is brown on the workbench. Found by turning each off in code at one spot at 07:15; shipped engines occlude reflections where little sky is seen. At 13:00 on the vantage views: mean 0.120 (Sons of the Forest 0.148), below 0.08 55% (46%), shade blue over green 0.28 (0.38): most of the shade's blue was the false reflection.
