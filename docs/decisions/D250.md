# D250: Worman, Drillhog and Seawish

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Monsters drawn by a developer's daughter, made in the game's style ([creatures.md](../creatures.md#designed-kinds)): the Worman, a giant earthworm disguised as a traveller in a cap and dark glasses, with red lips and a knife, that attacks when you come close; and the Drillhog, a boar-sized groundhog with drills growing from its back and head, which it digs and attacks with, met in dungeons and underground; and the Seawish, a fish standing on its tail with seaweed growing on its head, fighting with a sword and its seaweed, met on beaches and hidden in the water as a clump of seaweed. Models exist; rigs, animations and behaviour are to come.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-13: "These are the characters that are gonna be mobs or enemies that are designed by my daughter. Obviously, they have to fit the style of the game." 2026-10-14: "this is seawish and its a fish with seaweed in its head. you can imagine how it would disguise itself in water"
