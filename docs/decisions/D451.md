# D451: The fight camera

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D274. While a weapon is drawn the player's camera frames the fight as Enshrouded does (D180, measured in tools/combat/refs/enshrouded.json): the boom hangs 0.15 m over the head with no shoulder offset, the player centred and low, and is as long as it takes for the player's 1.8 m to fill 0.235 of the frame's height at the field of view of the moment (7.4 m, 4.1 bodies, at the default 55°), the middle of Enshrouded's 0.20 to 0.27; the wheel's zoom waits until it is sheathed. Locked on, the camera stands behind the player, 17.5° round from the line to the target on the player's right (D453's 15 to 20°, so the target shows beside the player), 14° down, the boom hung from 0.3 of the way from the player's head to the target's, so both stay in the frame wherever the target stands, as action games' lock-on cameras do. It eases in over 0.5 s on drawing, out over 0.6 s on sheathing and to and from the lock over 0.35 s, so building and walking keep the camera they had; the boom's collision (D274) is unchanged and takes the boom from there. The numbers are data (`assets/data/camera.ron`); the walker owns the framing (`terra_walker::camera::framing`) and the fight asks for it with `FightView` on the player. `cargo xtask duel`'s `player framed` check holds the game's camera to the target on every drawn frame of the duel.

## Why

Enshrouded is the baseline (D180), and measured, ours framed the player 1.4 to 1.9 times larger than it does and 0.6 m to the right (research/2026-10-24-enshrouded-combat-measured.md). On the yard's duel, today's camera filled 0.362 of the frame's height with the player (median of 1305 drawn frames, none inside 0.20 to 0.27) and put the player 7% of the frame's width off its middle; with this, 0.234 (99% of the frames inside; the rest the ease after drawing), the player 0% off the middle and the partner whole in the frame on every drawn frame. Locked on with the fighters 1.4 m apart, the partner stands mostly behind the player from straight behind, so the developers had the locked camera stand 15 to 20° round from the line (D453); the right, the walk's shoulder side (D274), and the middle of their range, 17.5°, are the agent's (`camera.locked_off_line` in tools/combat/criteria.json holds it, 15 to 20° from 1.4 to 15 m and the target's chest clear of the player's in the picture).
