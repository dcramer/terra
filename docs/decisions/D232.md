# D232: The material in hand, changing it in place, and how kinds meet

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The hammer holds a material (`Builder::material`), every plan it lays laid in it: Ctrl and the wheel step through every kit in `assets/data/kits` while a part is in hand, as Enshrouded's do, Ctrl being the roll's key, which waits then, as Shift and the wheel share the sprint's (keys are never shared, D80); the middle button on a cube picks its material; the legend under the aiming mark shows it. *Change material* marks a brush's box of a cube's other kinds to change (`Op::Replace`); building takes the old out a step at a time from under what stays, giving it back, swaps it (`Pending::Swapped`, so the old is kept for undo), builds the new in, and clears the mark (`Base::next_change`), each step refused like any other; a cube being changed, like one carved, may be part in under what stays over it. `Op::Changed` is clearing that mark's inverse. Where kinds meet is each kit's `meets` table: its weight (stone 3, timber 2), the heavier running through a joint and dressed there as though the lighter were not, the lighter ending against it with its `end` trim (`Board`), the heavier carrying its `carries` trim (`Cap`) where a lighter stands on it, and per kind by id what differs; a kit without one meets as its pattern did (D179, D210). A foot over another kind does not step down over it. Amended by D298: between two kinds of one pattern the change is a repaint at once.

## Why

Mine, building gaps 1 and 5 of the parity note on 2026-10-13: Ctrl and the wheel is building.md's recommendation and Enshrouded's; the roll and the material both on Ctrl, rather than another key, keeps one key one action and costs only the roll while a part is out. A swapped state rather than inferring it keeps every step of a change exact to undo, as the property tests ask.
