# D166: Tribes and outposts

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A tribe is its players and recruits: you can recruit other players too. A tribe holds its claims, each an outpost, and all its outposts are shared within it. Recruits are not shared between outposts: each lives and works at one; whether they travel between them is to be decided later. A player is in one tribe at a time. Tribes are like clans or guilds, with two ranks, member and leader: members build, use the stores and recruit; leaders also invite, remove and rank players, place and give up claims, and take down anything. A player on their own is a tribe of one. Moving a recruit to another outpost is likely done at a station, for now the claim stone, and you take them with you or let them walk on their own at the risk of their death (Leaning).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "i think a tribe belongs to the claim, and everyone in the tribe (including other players, as you can recruit them too) are part of it". Then: "one tribe across all claims i think, but each 'claim' is like an outpost … you might need different npcs at different ones? or they'd have to travel between them … for now, tribes, and outposts, and all outposts are shared amongst one tribe, but npcs will be not be shared amongst outposts"; "players can only be in one tribe, and you'll have tribes kind of like clans/guilds so at least two ranks". Then: the proposed split of ranks and a tribe of one were agreed ("yep thats fine", "that seems fine"); "moving recruits between outposts will be some kind of station you use to do something, and you probably have to take them with you, or let them walk on their own and risk death?"; then, of the station: "it can be the claim stone for now?" Gives D94's "claims belong to someone, a player or a group" its group. See [tribe.md](../tribe.md#tribes-and-outposts).
