# D528: A runner stops in about two steps

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A body decelerates at about 15 m/s² (was 40): from a jog it stops in about two steps (0.6 m), still responsive, and its stop clips fit the distance so the feet stay planted. A placeholder to tune in a playtest.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk, of one-step stops (40 m/s², short clips), two-step stops (~15) and softer (~10): "Two-step stops". At 40 m/s² no stop clip could show a one-step stop from a sprint without the feet sliding.
