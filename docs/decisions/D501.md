# D501: Crossing is a short fade, whatever the entrance

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A crossing is covered by a short fade, under 3 s, the same for a found portal, a built one or a dungeon's door: no separate look for each kind of entrance. A dungeon walked into without leaving its realm (D497) needs no cover; a door that moves the player to another place in the realm, as Valheim's do, uses the same fade.

A crossing whose cover lasts past 0.75 s shows on the dark what it waits for: a turning ring, the bar and the status line, never the title (`terra_world::loading`, `SHOW_AFTER_S`); a shorter one stays the dark alone. The cover is never a still, dark window.

## Why

Asked what the crossing should look like and how long it may take, for a portal against a dungeon door (the agent's default: a short fade, under 3 s), the developers on 2026-10-25: "short fade is fine regardless of what happens to be the \"entrance\"". D72 rules out a traditional loading screen; the fade is the crossing's cover. Amended 2026-11-09, the crossing then measured at 4 to 6 s of dark with nothing on it: "the game freezes when you walk through the portal. if it has to load it at least needs like some kind of animated loading screen".
- **2026-11-09**: D501 amended (by the developers: "if it has to load it at least needs like some kind of animated loading screen"): a cover past 0.75 s shows a turning ring, the bar and what it waits for; under that it stays the dark alone.
