# D207: Openings cut into what is built, and doors that swing

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

An opening asked for where a wall is built marks its cubes to be carved (`Op::Carve`); building then takes them out a course at a time from the top, a cube at a time for blocks, giving back what went in, and makes them open; a cube being carved may come out from under what is over it, which stays, held by the wall either side as a lintel spans it, so long as everything stays joined, and nothing else rests on it (a roof's eaves, a prop, an item); a carving left half done under something cannot be cancelled. Layouts draw a cube as an opening from its first cut, framed. A door's model was cut into a `Frame` that stays and a `Leaf` that swings on the low edge of its own box; no door model is used since D223.
