# D350: The market town paved, with shops, women and a horizon

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Amends D321's town. The town's streets and square are paved (setts of the local stone laid in packed earth, earth and mud where the paving gives out); stalls and shops round the square sell varied goods, food among them, drawn per stall by its seed; its people vary, women among them; and from its streets and square the distance is more town or forest and mountains, never empty. Its people walk.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-15, after watching round 2: "re town: brick road, town square, shops, a few different NPCs, women etc. shops, with random merchandise, food etc. and its not walk, it's still run. in the distance it should either still be a town, or have some sort of fauna on the horizon / mountains in the horizon. We want to make this visually appealing."
