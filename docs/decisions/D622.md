# D622: The woods' grown trees stream round the player in blocks of their grid, the far cards laid at the start standing for every one

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Serves D619 and D608. The woods' grid of tree cells (`terra_land::woods`, a cell 6.5 m wide about the woods' middle) is cut into blocks of 16 cells, 104 m a side. A block's grown trees are worked out from the seed on the streaming's pool (`Woods::trees_in_cells`, the same trees the one pass over the grid lays, so a block asked again is the block it was), once its nearest edge comes within 360 m of the camera on the woods' map, and stood a few a frame under the streaming's budget (class `NearWoods`, after the near ground), the nearest block's first. A block goes, its trees, trunks and solids with it, once its nearest edge is past 460 m: the 100 m between is the hysteresis, so walking to and fro at an edge neither lays nor drops one each step. 360 m outreaches the farthest a grown tree's last level reaches (280 m for the tallest), so a walker meets none coming in. Only the blocks within 360 m of the start and of the woods' middle are laid before the loading screen lifts, where 600 m of trees were.

- **Every far card stands for its grown tree, drawn or not yet**: a card comes in where its tree's last level will end (`ConiferModels::drawn`, what `draw` gives without drawing), so a block streamed in meets its cards with no tree drawn twice and none missing.
- **A felled tree stays felled**: its foot is kept (`NearWoods::felled`), and its block streamed in again leaves it out; its far card was already merged out (D619's felling).
- **The floor follows the trees**: a block's trees go on the woods' floor (`cover::forest::GrownFloor`, the cover's, shared) as soon as they are worked out, before the first stands, and come off it with their block; the cover within reach of a change is grown again, so the grass, litter, needles and moss under every streamed tree are the forest's (D619). What stands on that floor is laid within 220 m of the woods' middle on loading, and past it streams (below).
- **What stands on the floor streams too**: past 220 m of the woods' middle, its undergrowth, rocks, logs and stumps come in round the camera in pieces of 16 m (`woods_floor::stream`), laid by the load's rules with the kit it made once, the nearest piece within 200 m of the camera whose trees are in (`NearWoods::trees_in`) at most one a frame, under the streaming's budget (class `Flora`); a piece goes past 260 m, what it filed on the floor with it, and the cover is grown again under both. A piece rolls its own dice by where it is and is laid as if the streamed pieces beside it were not there, so it lays the same things wherever the camera came from. The flora's own understory is sown nowhere among the woods' trees.
- **Within the near cards' reach only** (1.6 km of the woods' middle): past it the coarse cards are another grid, so no grown tree stands there until the cards stream too.

As shipped open worlds place their foliage: Unreal's PCG runtime generation over World Partition (a grid of cells generated round each generation source, cleaned up past a larger radius, deterministic per cell from its seed), Horizon Zero Dawn's placement (Guerrilla, GDC 2017: tiles round the player, the same every time from the seed).

Rejected: laying the grown trees once out to a larger radius. 600 m already took most of the woods' load, and a player walking a kilometre still walked out of it.

## Why

The brief for core-game-woods j5: "Today full trees grow only within 600 m of where a world is entered (`woods::plant` runs once) ... A player who walks away finds cards and bare cover. Stream both round the player in cells, the way shipped open worlds do."
