# D260: Parts that touch and agree are one

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D199's parts: a part groups cubes for the interface, the order they go in and their refunds, so cubes laid by hand (*Block*, a brush's box) join the part of the cubes of their kind beside them laid on the same terms, built or planned, and every such part they touch is merged into it; taking cubes out of the plan splits a part they leave in pieces, the piece with the first cube keeping its number (`terra_build::parts`). The moves are `Op::Regroup`, checked and undone like any other, a new part taking the old one's terms, so refunds stay exact. Taking apart a planned cube of a part partly built takes it out of the plan. The room, wall and roof tools still lay a part each.

## Why

Mine, from the goals review's finding 3 on 2026-10-13 ([goals-review-2026-10-13.md](../../research/goals-review-2026-10-13.md)): a part a click filled a section's palette of 255 long before its 4,096 cubes, and the hammer refused ("Too many parts meet here"), which blocked D226. Merging only parts that agree keeps refunds exact without a cost per cube; a property test lays thousands of cubes by hand across sections with no refusal (`ops/tests/by_hand.rs`).
