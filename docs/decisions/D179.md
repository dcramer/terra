# D179: Posts, and mixing kinds of construction

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

As [building-kits.md](../building-kits.md#posts-anywhere-d179) says: a post is a part on any grid point, which walls meet as at an in-line join, at a corner giving a corner-post look, in the open holding up a porch; every part has its own kind, and where kinds meet the heavier runs through (stone, logs, planks, wattle); a wall over a lower one of another kind makes a footing.

## Why

Asked by the developers on 2026-10-12: "add a pillar to break up walls… arbitrarily?" and "how do you have one wall be log and one be stone". Agreed the same day: "seems fine", posts "at the snap points… on the grid".
