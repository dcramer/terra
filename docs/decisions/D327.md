# D327: Climate samples, maps and plants' years in code

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

`terra_world::climate` (D314's shape). A `ClimateSample` on what cares (the walker's player now) is taken again every half second within 48 m of a player, every 3 s within 200 m and every 20 s beyond, at most 0.6 ms of a frame; local things bend it as components the layers above give: `Heat` (a fire's radiant watts, spread over a sphere round it), `Sheltered` (0 to 1: the wind to a tenth, nothing falling and no sun, a fire's heat held in, doubled) and the canopy, from the sky occlusion volume (D270: the sun cut by the sky hidden, the rain and wind to two fifths where it is closed). The past at a point is its 32 m cell's, taken at the cell's middle so every machine asks the same (`ClimateFields::place`, where D323's aspect, drifting and latitude from where it is will come in; the start's latitude everywhere until then), going from its day's start to the next day's through the day. Two maps a kilometre round the camera, a cell a texel, painted on another thread when the camera has gone 128 m or the clock a quarter-hour (`ClimateMaps`): the ground's (snow depth to 0.5 m, its wetness, rain now, frost) and the leaves', a layer a kind, how many leaves and how far turned, each for a plant early and late in its kind's year, its seed choosing between. A kind's year is data (`assets/data/plant_years.ron`, `terra_climate::LeafYear`): its leaves out by warmth, turned and fallen by chill counted once the day is shorter than its length (fully an hour on), how much of them turn and drop, and for grass how it browns in a drought; a plant counts up to a quarter faster or slower by its seed, so a stand turns over days and none is ever ahead before the count begins. `--date <month-day or day>` sets the clock to a day of an Earth calendar, as that share of the world's year after midwinter, or a day of the clock; the log says the climate at the camera when the maps are first painted.

## Why

Standard models: budburst by growing degree-days, leaf colouring by cold degree-days under a shortening day (Delpierre et al. 2009). Interpolating the day's past keeps snow from arriving at midnight; early and late pictures per kind let each plant be its own without a tick. Measured: a sample a few microseconds, a kept cell a day 1.8 µs; the maps painted afresh, 1024 cells folded over a year, in under a second on another thread, after that a few milliseconds there; nothing on the GPU yet.
