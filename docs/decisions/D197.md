# D197: How block kinds are drawn

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Stone, brick, plaster and earth are drawn as their cubes' own surface, rounded along every outside edge and displaced by a real material from the pipeline (`terra_build::surface`), as Enshrouded seems to, not as modelled pieces, so any shape, carved anywhere, is one wall with no seams. Long members (logs, beams, planks) stay modelled pieces; doors, windows, trims and details stay props. An exception to D177 for block kinds. Dressed by its roles in pieces of its own materials (D215); walls, floors and roofs all drawn this way (D251). Amended by D290: a block kind is a material of the block pattern, its file a few lines over the pattern and its pack.

## Why

The spike (2026-10-12, [research/block-modules.md](../../research/block-modules.md)): Tripo's stone cut into pieces chosen by their neighbours read as a grid of metre blocks and broke where a carving interrupted it; the displaced surface read as one natural wall. The developers: "ok that looks pretty good tbqh, ok do that".
