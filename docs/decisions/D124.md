# D124: Where growth comes from

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Magic and special abilities come from essences (D98). Everything else comes from other sources: skills from doing them (D88), and techniques from books and practice, such as combat styles and disciplines of working with magic like astral magic. Which styles and disciplines there are is ours to invent (D125); how they are learned is D163.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11, of the books, and assumed by D125's styles and disciplines. See [progression.md](../progression.md#three-layers).
