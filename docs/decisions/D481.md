# D481: The move check measures the sword through its own shield and the shield through the sword arm

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The move check (`tools/characters/moves.ts`, D447) measures each held look against the other, every frame of every move and drawn clip on every body template: how deep the sword (blade, guard or hilt) goes through its fighter's own shield's board, and how deep the board goes through the sword arm (the right forearm and hand), in cm, with where (the move, the time, the part, the template), as its `held` rows do; negative is the gap when clear. The shapes are measured from the looks' meshes (`tools/characters/clash.ts`): the board a disc across the shield's thinnest axis, as wide as its rim, its faces the median of rays across it between a third and four fifths of the way out, so the boss and the grip are left out (the round shield: 0.81 m across, 2.8 cm thick); the sword balls a centimetre apart along and across it, as thick as it is at each station, named blade, guard and hilt by its width; the arm balls along the forearm padded as the hands check pads it. The depth is the least push that clears the board: out its front, out its back or out past its rim, the minimum translation distance of collision detection, so a blade crossing the board reads how far it must come back, not the board's thickness. The scorecard (D452) scores it as two rows a move, `move.<move>.sword_in_shield` and `move.<move>.shield_in_arm`, bar 0 cm. moves.ts counts them among the faults of every move and every drawn clip it checks (the blows, the block, the roll, the guards, strafes, walk under the guard and jump; bar 0 cm), so the clip step (D450) refuses a take whose sword crosses its shield, and a hold, guard or block that clashes fails the move check.

## Why

Combat round 14 (2026-10-25). The developer, on the duel film: "they're poking through the shield to stab (instead of the shield arm moving the shield out of the way as part of it...)" and "sometimes the sword is swung through the shield (you cant really hold a shield to your face AND swing)". moves.ts measured each look against the body only, so the scorecard passed what the developer saw. A point's depth inside a board a few centimetres thick says nothing of a blade through it, so the depth is the push that would clear it, as collision detection's penetration depth is defined. Every light blow, the heavy and the block fail today (the sword 16.5 to 27.5 cm through, the board 7.5 to 15.2 cm through the sword hand); the guards, strafes, walk, jump and roll stand clear. Counting the rows as a take's faults before any clip passes them would leave the clip step no take to keep; that waited on a spec that clears them. Round 15 keyed the slash's shield out to the left and back through the blow (research/2026-10-25-shield-arm-in-a-swing.md): all 32 takes over three batches cleared both (by 0.5 to 7 cm, one 0.8 cm through on the way back to the guard), so the bar no longer leaves the clip step without takes, and a blow's clip kept from now on must clear it. Combat round 29 (2026-10-26): with the heater (D504) every move and drawn clip stood clear (sword -5.2 cm at worst, the slash; shield in arm -9.3 cm, the keyed guard), while the bare `Keyed_Block` still put the blade 14 cm through the board and passed as a clip, so the bar became every move's and clip's fault.
