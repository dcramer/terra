# D168: Fragments of essences

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Small fragments of one essence's kind, as the books' quintessence, left by monsters and pools of that kind: components for workings, enchanting and items of that kind. Not absorbable. Named as in the books: quintessence.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "yes". See [magic.md](../magic.md#fragments-of-essences).
