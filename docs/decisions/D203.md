# D203: How trees change detail

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A tree's levels of detail switch at distances that grow with its height (30, 75, 180 and 420 m for a 20 m tree, clamped to half and 1.4 times that), so detail follows its size on screen, as Unreal's screen-size LODs do; each change dithers over a fifth of its distance (Bevy's `VisibilityRange`, measured from the camera in the shadow pass too), so no hysteresis is needed. A crown's shadow stand-ins (cards, then solid lumps) switch at the tree's own levels, never between them. Past the third level a tree is its crown's solid lumps in the leaves' colour over its simplest trunk, out to 420 m, so the forest carries to the horizon. A bush is drawn once, its shadow from a copy only the sun sees. Real impostors (pictures of each tree from several sides) only if the far lumps prove wrong up close to their distance. Checked with `cargo xtask pops`, which finds what pops or flickers between consecutive frames.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "things flicker in and out, the shades are working weird". Detail switched at fixed distances whatever a tree's size, crossfades were a flat 6 m, crown shadows switched at 25 and 80 m while the trees switched at 30 and 75, a bush dithered between two copies of itself over 2 m, and every tree vanished at 180 m. The walk bench on the RTX 3080 went from 6.2 to 5.8 ms.
