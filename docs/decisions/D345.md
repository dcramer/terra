# D345: The heath's dwarf shrubs, and blown snow

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Willow scrub, knee- to thigh-high on the birch's year, stands in the heath's wet hollows ("Willow scrub" in flora.ron, chosen by the hollow's wetness). Near, the heath lies in patches of its communities a few metres across, each mostly one, in the shares its 10 m map gives (`cover::heath::patches`): its dwarf shrubs as short leafy shoots among the cover's blades in bearberry's crimson, bilberry's wine, dwarf birch's rust and gold and crowberry's dark brown, which the grass shader keeps rather than browning to straw; lichen as low cream cushions; its sedge straw and bronze, not a meadow's green; nothing grows through water; far off, a shrub heath lighter and greyer than its leaves. Water, a lake's or a pool's, fades out where it is a few centimetres deep over the ground it rises onto (`lake.wesl`, by the depth behind it), and the pools' water reaches a cell past their own under that ground, so a pool's edge is where the ground rises out of it, not its cells' straight edges; its ripples carry two finer sets, down to a few centimetres. Amends D325 and D341. Past the trees the flora grows dwarf shrubs (`Grows::Heath`, "Dwarf shrub" in flora.ron: the berry bush's cards, 0.18 to 0.45 m) within 110 m of the start on a 1.7 m grid, as often as the heath's dwarf-shrub share (×1.3) and a little among its lichen, in clumps by noise; their leaves turn and fall as the bilberry's year says, which names the berry cards; the dwarf birch's year colours the heath's ground. Blown snow is smoke's cards (`smoke::Burning::Snow`): up to 64 emitters on the deepest patches lying within 160 m of the start, on a 9 m grid, as thick as the snow is deep, carried at the wind's own speed, life 2.6 s, rising 0.18 m/s, 0.5 to 2.4 m wide; it lifts from 4.5 m/s and is thickest by 9. Smoke's north is the world's frame's. On patterned ground a lake on the 10 m grid is one deeper than 1.5 m or wider than 5,000 m², the rest the pools' grid's; the heath's layers keep only their scans' light and dark in their community's hue; its boulders lie in fields on the fellfield and scoured rises, lichen-grown, and no stump and log lie by the start where no trees grow. In the heath's tall wet sedge a tenth of the blades are cotton grass, straight and a third taller, a white tuft over their top fifth; the bare-ground measure counts the heath as cover. Amends D341: a polygon's pool stands as water once 0.35 m deep and 8 m² (the low middles a hand deep are wet hollows), drawn on its own cells only, so no sheet of water lies over the troughs round it; and the heath holds the ground under the forest-tundra's islands of trees too, only a closed forest a degree and a half warmer than the tree line keeping its own floor.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "i would like to see more of a tundra effect"; demos.md §2 asks for dwarf shrubs on the heath and snow lifting off patches above a speed. Loose snow starts to drift at about 5 m/s and blows in sheets by 10 (snow transport thresholds, Li and Pomeroy 1997).
