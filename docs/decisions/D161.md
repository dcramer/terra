# D161: The tribe: recruits who look after themselves

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

You recruit people, bring them back safely, and they live in your tribe and take part in its work: working crafting stations and helping haul resources. It is not a colony sim: you do not micromanage them, and they are functional on their own. They have stats they level up as you do, so they get better at the work they do. Building help is carrying things to a building, and no one starts on a plan you are still designing; they should potentially help build too (Leaning). They take their gear on their own, from containers that are the tribe's; a container is either personal or the tribe's. Needs, and how they die, are D169. They can die. They will travel and fight beside you, later. No ageing for now; families are wishful thinking. Kenshi is the basis to some degree, and Medieval Dynasty a source of inspiration.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "we want to be able to recruit npcs … and have them 'live' in our tribe … the main delta is we dont want this to be a colony sim … they should automatically be functional … basically you'll have a little automatically managed tribe simulator". Answering the questions the same day: "building help is useful when you need to carry things to a building, but … you dont want an npc magically to start building your blueprint while you're still designing it"; "they'd take their gear on their own but its possible we'll need systems that help you decide what they can and cant use"; "families is neat, but thats more of a wishful thinking. we dont need age in the game … for now"; "recruits can die"; travelling with you is "a future TODO". Then: "recruits should help build potentially yes"; "maybe a personal chest is actually enough (or maybe you can configure how each storage container is used?)"; "needs we should document some ideas, but figure out later". Then, of containers: "personal vs tribe seems enough". Builds on D90 and D123.
