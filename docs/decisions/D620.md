# D620: Every world with mountains lays its first market town by the player's start

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Serves D95 and D354. A world's land stands its market towns (`terra_world::start::Towns`), laid as the world is entered with no experiment (`terra_folk::town`): on a world with mountains and no `--site`, its first town is found round the player's start by the demo's rules (D354's `MarketTown::DEMO` as `MarketTown::FIRST`, `terra_land::Land::first_town`): within 3 km of the start, failing that within 9 km, and at least 450 m from it, so its fields do not take the player's plot, with no water within 180 m of its middle as the world draws it (the lakes and pools of the land worked out round the start, on their own grids, `Round::wet_within`; a window of lakes round a place past that land), so no house, yard or street stands in a pond the search's 50 m grid misses; best by D354's score, which with the nearer search weighs nearness more. Its fields are cleared of the forest as the demo's are; it is laid from the realm's seed (`town::seed_in`, the realm's seed derived for the town by `terra_gen::hash`; seed 1's is the demo's), its people living their day, the player arriving at the start as before. `--site town` or `settlement` gives the site it found instead; `--site lake` and `tundra` lay none. A world without mountains (under the 30 km world's radius) has none. A world kept on disk keeps the towns found when it was first entered, none found among them, in its `world.ron` by the planet's radius, as its start (D596), and takes them again on entering (`planet::entering::KnownTowns`), so the search runs once a world; a `--site` run's are not kept.

The `town` experiment only takes the player to its main street once it is laid (`town::Visit`), and `town-empty` lays it with nobody in it; the town is no longer laid 300 m from a small world's start.

## Why

The developers on 2026-11-04 of the demos' settlements: "make sure THEY are core part of the game". The demo's town was laid only when the `town` experiment asked for it, so a player starting a world from the title screen never found one. Round the player's start, with the demo's rules, seeds 1, 2, 3, 7, 42, 555 and 9001 find a town within 3 km (0.3 to 2.4 s on the worker) and seed 102 one 7.1 km off in the far search (2.5 s); seed 1's is the demo's own place, 2.22 km east and 2.12 km north of the start.

The developers on 2026-11-05, asked whether a world wants more towns than its first: "One near the start is enough for now", so a world lays the one town by the player's start and no others.
