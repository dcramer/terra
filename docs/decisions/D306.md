# D306: Decks framed on their posts, and stairs that bear

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D206 and D220. A timber deck is framed within its layer of cubes as a carpenter frames one (`layout::floors`): boards on top; joists across them on every line between cubes, the outermost its edges, where it is in the air; a rim joist as deep as they are across their ends on every open side, a ledger where they run into a wall, and on the ground a rim the layer's whole depth; and at the layer's foot a bearer, the kit's heavy timber as it dresses `Role::Crest`, along every line of posts under the deck, the fewer of the two ways (else the joists square to the walls the deck meets, else the boards along its longer side). Every post under a deck ends at the bearer's underside, so a deck on posts in its corner cubes overhangs its bearers by the rest of the cube. A post's top cube with nothing on it beside a deck on its layer, as the hammer's post a storey high leaves under a floor laid at its top, is the deck's: boarded over, the post or stone pillar ending under it (`floors::post_in_deck`). A layout sets what rests on a member against its real size from the kit's data (`timber::section`), never an offset kept for a kit, as the model contract (D303) asks of every piece. A flight's stringers are seated on the floor at their foot and run up until their lower corner meets the back of the top step, where they bear on a deck's rim, which the deck lays where a flight meets it; posts stand under a stringer only to keep its span within 1.8 m, evenly along it, and under its head where nothing stands behind its top step. The hammer lays a footing under a post or a flight wherever its foot rests on the ground, on a hillside a storey up too, not only on the ground storey; a post's footing has its top at the ground, filling the cube the ground passes through, so the post is never buried (`ground::post_on_ground`); and a flight climbs onto what is ahead of it, a deck two storeys over its foot as well as one (`ground::flight_onto`). Checked without a picture by `terra_build/tests/decks.rs`; the kit sheet's `decks` rows show each case in logs, hewn timber and on stone piers.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14, of the cabin's deck: "the poles or w/e shouldnt be disconnected on the sides, they should be under the flat part", and "its important that these kits are cleanly designed so each one doesnt have to be customized by the game". The hammer's posts stood up through the deck's corner cubes; the bolster under a deck was set by a log's diameter where a hewn member is a course deep, so it hung 7 cm under the deck; the rim was a 46 cm stack of boxes; stringers stopped short of the deck and their posts stood a spacing apart regardless of span. Real decks and Valheim's and Enshrouded's stand posts under bearers, joists on those and boards on the joists.
