# D202: How ground cover is drawn

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The cover tier of [biomes.md](../biomes.md#three-tiers-by-who-has-to-agree) is `terra_world::cover`, fed by a `CoverRules` the world fills in: a field of how much grass grows at each place, how lush, where paths are, and how much leaf litter and moss. Grass is blades generated round the camera in 8 m squares on other threads, after Ghost of Tsushima: each blade placed alone on a jittered grid (about 280 a square metre at most, a third of them short, and half-thick grass keeping most of its blades), belonging to the nearest of a scatter of clumps that share height, facing and colour, each a Bézier curve with rounded normals; three rings of detail, the far ones a subset of the near one's blades chosen by rank, a square changing ring by its nearest point with hysteresis, and the shader thinning blades one by one by rank before a ring change so none pops (the far-lasting blades are wider in every ring, so none changes width); the vertex shader shrinks blades into their roots from 30 m, so the grass has no edge. The ground under it is painted by the same field, as Horizon Zero Dawn's placement paints its terrain: a 128 m map round the camera that the ground shader reads, turning grass to leaf litter under trees (Poly Haven's Forest Leaves 02, a fifth layer of the ground's stack), adding moss, and darkening the ground under thick grass as the blades' own shade would. The bare-ground measure of biomes.md (the share of ground within 30 m that nothing covers, and of that, paths) is printed with every `--shots` picture and on the vantage sheet.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "grass is pretty bad right now. We should take care of it and make sure that grass can be nice and not spotty", and the forest's other foliage should look like the realistic world we are moving to. The spike's tufts on a 0.35 m grid left most of the ground showing between them. On the RTX 3080 the walk bench is unchanged (6.2 ms median); the lush forest's floor went from 22% bare to 9%, nearly all of it paths.
