# D498: One copy of each dungeon, shared by the whole server

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Each dungeon exists once and is shared by everyone on the server, not copied per party, tribe or player. Anyone may follow another player in, since it is one place in its realm (D497).

## Why

Asked who shares a dungeon (the whole server, one per party or tribe, or one per player), the developers on 2026-10-25: "whole server". Was the agent's default for the demo, one per player.
