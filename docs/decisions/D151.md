# D151: Expanding a station

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Stations are expanded and upgraded, and their capabilities vary by how: an expansion may open new things to make, raise capacity, or improve speed, quality and other attributes. Not a fixed item per expansion: variations, which look different and organic. Expansions count anywhere in the station's building (D155). Proposed for the rest: an expansion is a role a station names (a forge's bellows, grindstone, tool rack), filled by any of several variations, each saying as data what it adds; a station outside any building counts what is within a generous reach; suggested places that snap, no visible slots, and the station's own look grows with what it has.

## Why

Asked for by the developers on 2026-10-12: "workstations should be expandable… I dont want fixed items for expansions, there should probably be variations or similar?" Then: "crafting stations will vary in capabilities when they expand/upgrade, and we want things to visually look different but also organic… it might unlock new craftables, improve capacit or other attributes, etc". On placing, between Soulmask's slots and Valheim's radius: "valheim gives you greater flexibility so maybe its a better approach (the one negative of valheim is the radius was always too tight)", then settled by D155: "lets make them count anywhere in the building". See [crafting.md](../crafting.md#expanding-a-station).
