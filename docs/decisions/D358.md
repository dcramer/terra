# D358: A building going up drawn from what is built

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every role of a kind's cubes that are in (D215: arrises and quoins, copings and crests, feet, sills and heads, log ends and plates, a roof's eaves and verges) is worked out from what is built, not from the plan: a wall built halfway shows a proper top, end and corner where it stops, a middle cube built alone is dressed all round, and as neighbours are built those trims are laid away again. A cube going in counts as built as far as it is filled: its courses are a wall's top, and it covers its neighbours' foot and sides but not their top. A plan's ghosts keep their own look, drawn from the plan. In code a base with anything not yet built is laid twice, its plan and what stands of it (`Base::what_stands`, `sections::Whole`), what is in taken from the second and the ghosts from the first, and what stands laid again only where an edit changed it (D281). Amends D215, which worked roles out from the plan, and the voxel contract's invariant 7 (D348).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "i wonder if the voxels - the way they render - should only be based on what _is already built_ not based on what the blueprint is? e.g. right now if you build a middle voxel it doesnt have the edge trim because the blueprint has it as a middle, but theres no edge yet so maybe it'd make more sense to render it with the edge and re-render as its built?" As Enshrouded and most voxel builders draw what stands. Tested in `sections` (`a_stone_wall_built_halfway_along_has_an_end_and_a_top_where_it_stops`, `a_middle_cube_built_alone_has_its_edges_and_finishing_its_neighbour_takes_them_away`, `a_log_wall_going_up_ends_its_courses_where_what_is_in_stops`, `a_house_going_up_laid_again_a_fill_at_a_time_is_as_laid_whole`) and the mesh checker's states of the rectangle.
