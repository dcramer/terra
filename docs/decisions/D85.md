# D85: Our inspirations

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Kenshi, Enshrouded, Subnautica 2, *He Who Fights With Monsters*, Soulmask, Red Dead Redemption 2 for a world that feels alive, The Forest for its building, Terraria for discovering and unlocking, Dune: Awakening for its graphics, atmosphere and lore, and Sons of the Forest for its world design. What we take from each is in [vision.md](../vision.md#our-inspirations).

## Why

Named by the developers on 2026-10-11; Red Dead Redemption 2 added by them on 2026-10-12, "as the world feels very alive"; The Forest the same day, for "a nice building system, the way you get logs/etc is nice for larger projects"; and Terraria, "similar to how we'll have realms… you go to the other zones, find new materials, unlock new kinds of things you can craft and build (lots would just be visual aesthetic)"; and Dune: Awakening, "great graphics, 'ok' building system, cool atmosphere and good lore"; and Sons of the Forest, whose world design is "beautiful, full, looks organic". Was "Enshrouded and Subnautica are probably the two biggest" (2026-10-10). A game's look is separate from its design and systems: taking Enshrouded's systems does not mean taking its look, which is the art direction's question (D14). Building in voxels is settled (D9). The tension with D3 was settled by D90.
