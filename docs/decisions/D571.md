# D571: The axe's iron collar is darker than its head

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The iron collar (the axe's trim where the head meets the haft) is blackened iron at about half the head's lightness, so it reads as its own piece while the axe keeps one metal: a darker colour target when its look is baked, no Tripo credits. Not chosen: a bronze collar (a second metal on the tool), lighter heads (they would wash out against the sky).

## Why

The axe sheet of 2026-10-28 (D565 step 4, proof A job 4e, `tools/items/sheet.ts axe`) shows the collar the same light grey as the head at noon, so it merges into the head and fails art-style.md's separate-values check. Both take their colour from the approved style picture (art/style/reference.png), where they are about the same grey (122 against 125-145 of 255). Darkening the collar costs no Tripo credits: a darker colour target when its look is baked. Asked on the review desk (d565-collar).

The developers, 2026-10-28: "Darker, blackened iron".
