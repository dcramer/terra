# D557: A window or door where another wall crosses or meets its wall

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Whether an opening may be cut in the cube where another wall crosses its wall or meets it end on. Recommended by the agent: refuse it (or clip the opening to stop a cube short of the crossing), the same rule covering gap 13's corner case ([building-voxels.md](../building-voxels.md)). The alternative, letting only the wall the opening runs along frame it, was tried and opened 12 see-through gaps.

## Why

Fuzz seed 185 (building round 8, 2026-10-27): a window in the cube where two hewn walls cross leaves a cube walled on three sides that can't be seen through, and both walls' window frames cross inside it. Framing it from one wall only went from 3 faults to 12. Real walls are not opened where another wall bears on them. On the developer's desk as `building-window-at-crossing`.
