# D621: With no world kept, the land behind the title screen is the new world it first offers

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Serves D579 and D13. The title screen keeps the world itself behind it (D579). With a world kept that is Continue's, as before. With none, it is the world New world first offers: the dice New world rolls its seeds from (`terra_game::play::worlds::Dice`, the clock read once at the start) are read before the land behind is chosen, and the land is made from their first roll. New world > Create on that seed plays on the land already loaded, as Continue does, with no crossing. Another roll or a typed seed crosses to its own land, as any other world does. A first launch loads its 30 km land once, behind the menu, not a backdrop and then the world.

Rejected: a cheap scene behind the menu, the 2 km test planet or a still panorama. Minecraft opens on a panorama, Valheim and Enshrouded on a fixed menu scene, and the menu shows in seconds. But a new world's land then loads only after Create, so the whole wait stays, and the menu shows a land the player will never play. D579's design keeps the world itself behind.

## Why

From the core-game-world j1 log, on the worker, 2026-11-04: a first launch with no world kept loaded seed 1's 30 km land behind the title (42-93 s), then crossed again for New world (28-74 s). The brief for core-game-world j2: "A first launch with no saved world loads 30 km once, not twice."
