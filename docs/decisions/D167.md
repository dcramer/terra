# D167: Money is magic

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Money works as in the books: the currency is magic condensed and ranked, iron to diamond, left by monsters and formed where magic pools thickly. It is spent, burned as fuel for workings and items, used as a component, and absorbed above your rank for a short surge of strength that leaves you exhausted. The same in every realm. Named as in the books: spirit coins.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "I like the way money and stuff works in the book that can also be burned for other things". See [magic.md](../magic.md#money-that-is-magic).
