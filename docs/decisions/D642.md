# D642: Realm two is a fantasy and science-fiction world, where the first is real and medieval

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

The second realm, the one beyond the first world's gate (v0-tracks' Realm two), is **a little fantasy and science fiction, where the first realm is real and medieval**, and it must be great and plainly another world: its sky, its light, its plants, its land and what stands on it are its own, not the first realm's in another colour ([art-style.md](../art-style.md): a realm changes parts, materials and light, never the rules). Its first asked theme, industrial and cyberpunk-like, gave the built remains it may keep: old machines among something stranger.

Proposed by the agent within that, each for the developers to overrule ([realm-two.md](../realm-two.md)): a violet sky over a cool rose sun; a forest whose leaves are teal and blue-green over rose and plum grass on dark soil; plants of its own (great fungi, fronded and spired trees, things that glow at night); a land of its own recipe; light of its own at night from what grows; standing remains of an older, stranger making (the pylons, tanks and signs built first, then crystal and stone). Wildlife, monsters and sound come after, by their tracks.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08, asked for the theme: "For the theme, lets do a little bit more futuristic. so industrial cyber punk esque?". Leaning, not Decided: said as a question. Then, 2026-11-08, on seeing it: "You NEED to make that second world great, and different than the first world. It should be a little fantasy/science fiction i think, vs the first world which is more real/medieval". It reads apart from the first realm's forest at a glance (v0-tracks: "a stranger could tell them apart at a glance while seeing one game"), and its parts are things the model pipeline makes well: hard-surfaced, built, man-sized or larger.
