# D642: Realm two is industrial and a little futuristic, cyberpunk-like

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

The second realm, the one beyond the first world's gate (v0-tracks' Realm two), is **industrial, a little more futuristic than the first, cyberpunk-like**: a worked-out, built-over land of concrete and rusting steel under a hazed sky, with a few lights still burning. Its motifs are the style guide's "panels and cables" ([art-style.md](../art-style.md): a realm changes parts, materials and light, never the rules): pylons and cable runs, pipes and vent stacks, tanks and derelict machines, signs that glow. Glow stays an accent (art-style's "glow is still sparing"): neon and sodium light against a dull ground, not a lit city.

Proposed by the agent within that, each for the developers to overrule: the land a lower, flatter recipe of hardpan, cracked slabs and slag heaps; the day an amber-grey overcast with a low orange sun, the night teal and magenta from what still glows; plants few and hardy (scrub, lichen, bare trees); its first parts a handful of large props scattered by the seed. Wildlife, monsters and sound come after, by their tracks. [realm-two.md](../realm-two.md) is its design.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-08, asked for the theme: "For the theme, lets do a little bit more futuristic. so industrial cyber punk esque?". Leaning, not Decided: said as a question. It reads apart from the first realm's forest at a glance (v0-tracks: "a stranger could tell them apart at a glance while seeing one game"), and its parts are things the model pipeline makes well: hard-surfaced, built, man-sized or larger.
