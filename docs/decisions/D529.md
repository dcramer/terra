# D529: Odd pairs in the hands

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

From docs/holding.md's loadout model: (1) a carried torch has its own off-arm pose, held out and low, and a sword with a torch is playable now with a sword.single set; (2) a tool in the main hand keeps the off-hand item, and a tool's two-handed action (a drawn bow, a pickaxe swing) stows it for the action; (3) a blade in the off hand with the main hand empty plays as a single blade, mirrored (D463); (4) the sparring partner keeps its own slower, readable moves rather than the player's set at a style's pace.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "Recommended".
