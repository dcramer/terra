# D460: Where the off hand's item comes from

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

How a shield, a torch or a second blade gets into the off hand ([holding.md](../holding.md), section 1). Options:

- (a) An off-hand slot on the equipment sheet (D64), in hand whenever the main hand's item leaves the off hand free; the bar (D66) still picks the main hand's item.
- (b) A bar slot holds a pair, main and off.
- (c) Two bars, one for each hand.

Recommended: (a). It is one more slot, leaves the bar as D66 has it, and switching from a sword to an axe keeps the shield on.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, on the review desk: "Off-hand slot". This settles the question below in that option's favour.

D66 puts the picked bar slot's item in hand, which covers one hand only. Today the sword and shield are given on drawing (D405's shortcut). Elden Ring has a left and a right set of slots; Enshrouded pairs a one-hander with a shield (research/2026-10-25-loadouts-and-animation-layers.md).
