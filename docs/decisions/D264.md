# D264: Trees under and close: leaves lit through every layer, bark at its real albedo, roots and modelled trunks in the grown bark

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D221, D235, D247. **Leaves:** a crown's cards all have normals leaning out of it, so from inside or below one shows its far side; there it reflects 0.55 as much, takes the sky's light as the leaves round it bounce it (green, not blue) and no specular, since Bevy's Fresnel at a normal turned from the eye reflected the whole sky off it; a crown's lower face gets 0.4 of the painted sky's light from below, which assumes sunlit ground, not a shaded forest floor. Lit through (D247), a leaf takes the sun through the leaves above it as well as in flecks, a tenth of it at a crown's inside, its glow in its own colour and broader toward the sun. Cards fade only nearly edge-on (0.04 to 0.16 of facing), and leaves within 0.3 to 0.9 m of the eye, not 0.5 to 1.6 m: wider, a bough by the camera dithered into a see-through smear. **Bark:** each scan tinted to bark's real albedo, about 0.08 to 0.1 linear (sRGB 80 to 90) on average rather than 0.03 to 0.07, as Neutral tone mapping (D254) shows albedo as it is; a trunk's damp dark foot is its lowest metre or so, not up to eye height; Bark Willow, mud without plates, gave way to Bark Willow 02 larger and greyer. Up a grown limb each repeat of the picture is taken from a place of its own, blending over the middle of each into the next (as Quilez's texture-repetition and Unreal's texture variation), so a tall trunk shows no bands; a Scots pine's bark is grey-brown low on its bole and orange from about half its height. **Shape:** a trunk's foot flares a little (0.6 of its radius for an oak, 0.45 a pine, 0.35 a spruce, from 1.1, 0.8 and 0.6) and swells into six to eight roots of their own size, width and height, curling a little round it, its bark measured along its sloping surface, on rings 0.12 m apart near the ground and 36 sides near the camera, normals following how the radius changes along the trunk as well as round it; limbs rounder (12, 7 and 4 sides near) and narrowing to a point over five of their widths. A spruce's dead lower branches are a third of a metre to under a metre, fewer and farther apart, not bare sticks as long as its live boughs, whose needles run from an eighth of their length rather than a quarter. **Veteran oaks:** their Tripo texture, baked shadows on a dark atlas, read as blue-black clay, so the model is drawn in the grown oaks' bark, laid on from three sides by where each point is (triplanar, as terrain and rock shaders are; a mesh without wind weights is a model), with how open each vertex is to the sky from rays cast against itself; `tools/blender/trunk.py` makes it smooth, 20,000 triangles near and 5,000 beyond.

## Why

An adversarial review against Sons of the Forest (5/10; trees 5 near and far): from below the canopy one even mid-green hedge; branches faceted tubes with blunt ends; trunk feet a four-pointed star; the veteran oak blue-black clay; trunks near black in shade. Measured: trunks in shade at eye height from sRGB (15, 17, 20) to (31, 33, 34); `lodcheck` worst frame 0.07%, `collide` passes; the walk bench 12.3 ms median and 14.1 at the 99th percentile in its one run with the RTX 3080 to itself.
