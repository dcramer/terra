# D607: Performance budgets are one data file the bench gates, with a settle phase after the load

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The game's performance budgets live in one data file (`assets/data/perf/budgets.ron`), read by `cargo xtask bench` and the game's readout, in place of the constants spread over `terra_world::debug::FRAME_BUDGET_MS`'s copies, `xtask/src/bench/ticks.rs` `GPU_FLAT` and `xtask/src/bench/play.rs` `LOAD_MOST_S`: by scene and phase, the frame (D25), the ticks (D138), the load (once the developers settle D580), a share a schedule or named group of systems (the fixed loop, creatures, terrain's receive, woods, render prepare; the recorder, D576, times schedules in every build and groups as spans), background thread-seconds (D608), GPU time a pass group, and the world's content (meshes, triangles, mesh memory, draws). The bench times a **settle** phase, the first 30 s after the loading screen lifts, against its own budget, instead of skipping it, and a scene that misses names the group over its share. A budget is raised by a change with its why and lowered only by the full run (D414). As Unreal keeps its targets as data (`FEnginePerformanceTargets`) and checks CSV-profiler captures against them. [research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md](../../research/2026-11-03-performance-systems.md#4-proposals-ranked).

## Why

On 2026-11-02 every miss read "the frame is slow" and was found by bisect: the creatures' share of the tick (the death spiral), the load moved into play by D603 (its first seconds were skipped, so it showed only as hitches and drift), town-dusk's +2 ms (bisected by hand over 41 commits, unconfirmed). A budget a group names the owner in the miss itself. Leaning until the file and the settle phase are built and the scenes' groups named.
