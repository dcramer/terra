# D476: A long fall hurts by landing speed

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A fall hurts by the speed it lands at, which the mover measures: harmless below about 12 m/s (a drop of about 4.5 m at our gravity), rising to fatal near 25 m/s; landing in water breaks a fall. Numbers are placeholders to tune.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, on the review desk, choosing among by landing speed, by height thresholds and none: "By landing speed".
