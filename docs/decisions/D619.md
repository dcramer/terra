# D619: The lush forest's trees, floor and wind move into the world, and forest-run stays on the yard reading the same rules

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D366. The lush forest's look becomes the world's, in `terra_world`, the lowest layer that holds trees, materials and ground cover (D198), and the sandbox's lush experiment keeps only what is the yard's own (its paths, glades and where it plants):

- **Trees** in `terra_world::trees`: what a tree is (`Tree`, `Species`, `Form`), its meshes (`build`, `wood`, `crown`, `card`, `lump`), then its species' growth rules, its kinds as data (D136), its looks and levels of detail, and the woods' models (today `lush/conifers.rs`'s answer to `Conifers`), so every world's woods are drawn from them with no layer above answering.
- **Material**: one, `terra_world::grown::Foliage`, which every grown plant draws in; a material says its own prepass draw priority (D618).
- **Wind** in levels (D597) is the world's already (`terra_world::grown::pivots`).
- **Floor cover and undergrowth** next: the lush floor's map, its eye-height layer in drifts, logs and rocks, laid as a `CoverSource` round the woods' own trees, so a wood on the round world has the forest walk's floor where `cover::heath` grows heath blades today.
- **forest-run stays on the yard**, `--experiment forest-walk` on its rolling ground, reading the same `terra_world` trees and floor rules the round world's woods do: it is the frame-time scene the lush forest's work is measured on (D467, D610), its path and trailer route are laid in the yard's coordinates, and a site on the round world would change its scene, its numbers and its baselines at once. The round world's woods are shown on the normal play path instead (`--experiment play --radius 30000`).

Moved in steps, each checked alone: a file over the size ratchet's limits is split as it moves (D396), never carried with its baseline.

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-04, of the forest walk's trees and ground: "make sure THEY are core part of the game". Measured at the 30 km world's start: the woods were already the lush forest's oaks, pines and spruces, given by the sandbox (D366), but their floor was the heath's even grass, without the ferns, bracken, bushes, logs and moss of the forest walk; and the trees reached the world only because the sandbox answered for them. D366 had left the grower in the sandbox because another agent was changing its 3,000 lines; that work has landed.
