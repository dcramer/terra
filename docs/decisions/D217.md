# D217: Haze and sun shafts

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A thin haze round the camera that the sun lights in shafts through the canopy, as Red Dead Redemption 2's and Sons of the Forest's forests have: Bevy's volumetric fog (`VolumetricFog` on the camera, 48 steps jittered for TAA; `VolumetricLight` on the sun; a `FogVolume` 240 by 60 m following the camera, density 0.004 (0.008 greyed trees 30 to 100 m off by a fifth to a third), scattering thrown forward, asymmetry 0.7). Leaves let half the light behind them through.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13 agreed to fog and sun shafts after [research/aaa-forest-look.md](../../research/aaa-forest-look.md) named them most of the mood of the forests we look to. It costs about 1.3 ms on the RTX 3080 (walk 7.1 to 8.4 ms); to be measured on the integrated GPU, where it may need fewer steps or a smaller box.
