# D9: Terrain representation

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Smooth voxels (a density field) on level-of-detail terrain, fully diggable. Structures are separate from the terrain: modular pieces, or a voxel volume of their own on the base's grid.

## Why

Blocky voxels don't tile a sphere. The spike rendered it from orbit to ground and dug it without a hitch. Widened on 2026-10-11 so that building in voxels, as Enshrouded does, does not need the terrain itself to hold buildings: a base's blocks keep their own grid and meshing, and the terrain's levels of detail and edits stay untouched. The developers confirmed the same day: building in voxels is fine, as long as the terrain is smooth voxels.
