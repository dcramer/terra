# D371: A lake's morning mist, and the haze lit by the open sky

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Lakes of 5 ha and more steam on a calm morning: banks of soft cards standing on the water every 40 m, 40 to 75 m wide and 3 to 13 m tall, their tops torn, turned about the up to the eye, their stuff a world-laid noise drifting downwind, soft against what stands behind them in the depth prepass and from the waterline up, thinned near the eye and where many stack far off, lit by Bevy's lighting (`lakes.rs`, `mist.wesl`), as games lay mist banks over water. Thickest from before dawn to two hours after sunrise by the place's own sun (D363), gone by mid-morning, torn away by a breeze. Out in the open the volumetric haze's sky light is four times what it is under the trees and takes the sky's shade colour, so mist glows pale with the sun behind.

## Why

The trailer's look is a morning with mist over the water (the developers, 2026-10-15). Bevy's volumetric fog over the whole lake drew dark, stepped bands: lit only by the sun with the trees' shadows through it and by a sky light tuned for under a canopy (0.015), it hung as a brown smear wherever no sunbeam crossed it.
