# D152: Walls, not rooms

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Blueprints are walls, floors and roofs, built up piece by piece; nothing is prebuilt. A room is what they enclose, not a thing built; the room tool is a shortcut that lays a rectangle's walls and floor. Indoors is worked out from them. How placing works is D154.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "maybe we should get rid of 'rooms' and only allow walls? … a room should be N walls anyways its a shortcut idk … but its weird to place blueprints of rooms". Then: "i think blueprints need to be fundamentally walls/floors/roofs, and you build up piece by piece instead of prebuilt. it just feels better". See [building.md](../building.md#planned-then-constructed); `terra_build` now holds walls, floors and roofs on cubes (D181).
