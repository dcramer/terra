# D521: The sky's effects

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The sky has the real set of effects, each only where its physics puts it: rainbows (42° round the point opposite the sun, when it is behind and rain is lit), ice halos and sundogs in cirrus, crepuscular rays through cloud gaps, cloud tops lit after sunset, and lightning glowing inside cloud; auroras come later, as realm data for cold or magical realms. The other options were fewer (rays and lightning only), and more, with the realms' magic in the sky. See [sky.md](../sky.md#sun-moon-and-stars).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "The real set". This settles the question in the recommendation's favour.

Each is cheap and testable against its geometry ([research](../../research/2026-10-26-volumetric-skies.md)), and each is something a player can reason from: rain is falling behind me, ice cloud means a front (D122, D93).
