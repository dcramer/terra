# D521: The sky's effects

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: rainbows (42° round the point opposite the sun, when it is behind and rain is lit), ice halos and sundogs in cirrus, crepuscular rays through cloud gaps, cloud tops lit after sunset, and lightning glowing inside cloud, each only where its physics puts it; auroras later, for cold or magical realms as their data. Options: those, as recommended; fewer (rays and lightning only); or more, with effects of the realms' magic in the sky. See [sky.md](../sky.md#sun-moon-and-stars).

## Why

Each is cheap and testable against its geometry ([research](../../research/2026-10-26-volumetric-skies.md)), and each is something a player can reason from: rain is falling behind me, ice cloud means a front (D122, D93). How many the sky gets is scope.
