# D48: Construction

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Building rests on each base's voxel grid, as Enshrouded's does (D181): parts are walls, floors and roofs (D152), placed the Sims' way (D154). On that basis, the improvements in [building.md](../building.md#enshrouded-or-subnautica-how-placing-works): bases that matter, building on round worlds, building together, and perhaps structural strength.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11: "Subnautica 2 is just easier, but as long as the basis is almost like this voxel grid we could have flexibility in time", agreeing with the improvements. Was Leaning: Subnautica 2's shaped rooms as the inspiration (2026-10-10), then under review; rooms went on 2026-10-12 (D152).
