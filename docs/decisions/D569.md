# D569: The block raises the shield over the face and the top of the head

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

While the block is held (D531), the sword-and-shield set's block raises the shield until it covers the face and the top of the head, still upright in front of the body, never tipped back over the head. Beside the head with its top level with the head's top, as the duel of 2026-10-28 showed it, leaves the face partly open and is too low.

## Why

The duel of 2026-10-28 showed the block's shield beside the head, its top level with the top of the head; before, it tipped back over the head and went 5.7 cm into it. The developers, asked whether to raise it or keep it, picked the recommendation, 2026-10-28: "Raise it higher". On the same day's still of the raised block they noted "the left side is better to some degree, the right side has the wrong angle i feel like", so the board's angle seen from the right is to be fixed with the height.
