# D340: mimalloc as the global allocator

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The sandbox, which is the game's program, allocates with mimalloc (the `mimalloc` crate, MIT, Microsoft's) rather than the system allocator, as engines replace it (Unreal ships mimalloc and TBB options).

## Why

The game's workers make and free hundreds of megabytes of section meshes, face caches and base copies while the main and render threads run; on Windows the system heap's lock made them wait 20 to 30 ms behind the workers, frames with nothing of their own to do (the castle tower's fall: Bevy preparing no new asset 24 ms, the main thread 30 ms). With mimalloc the tower's range had no frame over 26.7 ms in three runs at loads of 19 to 54 on 32 cores, where it had one to six.
