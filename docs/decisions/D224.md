# D224: Haze between the trees

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A distance haze at the forest's own scale (Bevy's `DistanceFog`, squared exponential, 240 m visibility) over the physical air's haze at the scale of hills: the horizon's colour greyed toward the canopy's green and dimmed, warm toward the sun, set each frame by the hour. Far trees fade into it before their detail changes, as the Witcher 3's do. Under the trees only, since D273: out of them it gives way to the open air's.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "one thing that fixes the big distance shit is making sure we have some haziness".
