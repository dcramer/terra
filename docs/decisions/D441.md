# D441: Creature weights from the plan's template body, or zoned heat, whichever measures better

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

A creature's weights are made two ways and the creature check (D442) keeps the better: transferred from its plan's template body, a body whose weights passed the check, by closest surface where the normals agree and the rest filled smoothly (Robust Skin Weights Transfer, 2023); and bone heat or geodesic voxel binding on a closed voxel copy, then the plan's zones (the belly to the spine and chest, the rump to the pelvis, the neck's base to neck and chest), smoothed within them; four bones a vertex, normalised. Joints the plan says twist get the game's weight-blended dual quaternions ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#3-automatic-weights-d441)).

The zones are built first, for four-legged runners (`zoned` in `animal.py`, `zone_weights` in `measures.py`): each leg bone keeps its heat weight by a smoothstep on the distance to its leg's bones, all of it within a quarter of the upper leg's length and none past six tenths (`zones` in creature.json, set per kind by the check: the boar 0.15 and 0.45, the fox and hares 0.12 and 0.35, the deer 0.05 and 0.2), never below the wrist or hock (a broad paw or a hare's long foot reaches past the bar from its bones), and what it gives up goes to the trunk chain by nearness along it.

Amended 2026-10-24 (round 8): the ease from a leg's zone to the trunk is at least `blend` (0.04) of the trunk's length wide, so a deer's 0.05-0.2 zone is no step on a moose; and on the outer flank only, skin led by a leg's top bone that shares weight with the trunk is smoothed among them over the mesh, as many passes as spread it across that band (none where the band is under an edge wide), since bone heat parts a thigh from the flank along a sharp line that opened a crease (the zoo's wolf hip, lynx thigh). Smoothed on the inner thigh and belly too, the trunk stretched past the bar (a squirrel's run 61% to 109%). A kind whose trunk the ease stretches sets `blend` 0 (the boar).

## Why

Measured 2026-10-22: bone heat gives the stag's belly 52% of its weight to leg bones and the boar's nearly all, and their bellies' edges stretch 118% and 194% at the 99th percentile in the gallop; Quaternius's hand-rigged deer gives its belly 7% to the legs and stretches 10.5%. Heat gives skin to the nearest bone, and under a deep chest that is a leg. Studios carry weights from a painted template; bounded biharmonic weights are better still but need a tetrahedral mesh that generated bodies rarely give.
