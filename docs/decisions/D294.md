# D294: Bark, moss and the floor's surface without repeats or felt

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D264 and D275. **Bark**: a veteran oak's triplanar bark is hex-tiled on the two sides a trunk faces (Mikkelsen's hex tiling as ground.wesl's, each of the three copies one class of a three-colouring of the lattice's corners, so Bevy's own derivative sampling shows no seam), and a scan's broad light and dark is taken out of every bark lookup (its colour over its picture at an eighth of a repeat, times its mean), so Bark Brown 02's pale and green stripes no longer repeat round a trunk as a plaid; a grown trunk's rows blend keeping their contrast (Heitz and Neyret's variance-preserving blend). Moss on bark has cushions bumped from their own height (Mikkelsen's surface-gradient bump mapping) and strands. **Rocks' moss** is the ground's Moss 004 scan, projected triplanar at two sizes, its colour, relief and height, cushions bulging, its edge given out tuft by tuft by its height, darker in its hollows, a dark lip on the stone below its edge, olive rather than lime (`mossy.wesl`, the ground's texture arrays bound to it). **The floor in the sun**: its hollows micro-shadowed in direct light, as Uncharted 4 and HDRP do, from the layers' heights; trodden soil's scan contrast drawn out, damp patches darker, small stones worked up. **Litter**: leaves wherever broadleaves stand, sun or shade, as needles where pines stand; litter heaps against trunks, logs and rocks, a piece or two on another, leaning up onto them (a `heap` field of the cover), so a trunk goes down into it; under each piece of ivy a darker copy on the ground, its shade.

## Why

The review of 2026-10-14 against Sons of the Forest (6.0, the ground 5.5): a mid-ground trunk a repeating plaid of squares; moss on rocks flat felt, a lime triangle in the sun; sunlit floor plain tan; the floor a printed rug, trunks meeting it along a line, ivy floating. Measured in crops before and after: a veteran's bark a grid of knots before, scattered after; a grown oak's rows' banding 0.035 to 0.030; a rock's moss cap's small-scale contrast (9-pixel squares' SD over mean) 0.029 to 0.084, featureless squares 92.6% to 12.3%, mean luma 0.89 to 0.60; sunlit needles, featureless 41% to 9%, contrast 0.128 to 0.151; sunlit path soil's spread 0.17 to 0.23, contrast 0.11 to 0.17. Litter heaping is held by a test. Bench and lodcheck as D293.
