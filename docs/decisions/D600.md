# D600: What shows in an empty clothing slot on Quaternius's bodies

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Open: Quaternius's outfits replace the body rather than cover it (each piece is worn on the head alone, and the arms piece carries its own hands and forearms), so with a piece taken off nothing is underneath. Two ways are being tried before the developers choose, both within D599's rule that their parts stay as made:

- **Swap to a bare piece** (the developers' leaning): each slot always holds one piece; taking the coat off puts a bare-skin piece in its place. The packs ship bare pieces for two slots only (checked in both zips, char-switch j3): the head (`OnlyHead`) and the head with bare shoulders, arms and hands (`UpperBody`, an exact cut of the full body), for the man and the woman alike. There is no bare torso, legs or feet piece, and no underclothes piece: the underwear (his briefs, her briefs and top) is painted on the full body's skin texture, and the "Regular" textures in the outfit pack are that same skin texture, which the arms pieces use for their hands. So a bare arm is a swap of `OnlyHead` for `UpperBody` within the rule, while a bare torso, legs or feet needs the full body cut (which D599 forbids), a bare piece of our own, or the second way.
- **Full body under, hidden while covered**: their whole body stays under every outfit, unchanged, and the shader hides the region of skin each worn piece stands in for (`body_mask.wesl` collapsing patches, as for our gear today); nothing is cut, only not drawn while covered.

A third, every slot always clothed (an undershirt, plain trousers), was offered and not taken up. The trial measures each on the man through the clips: poke-through, seams at the slot borders, and draw cost, with stills side by side.

**Measured** (char-switch j5, `tools/characters/slots.ts male Ranger`, 204 moments of the 34 clips, stills in `slot_stills.py`). The swap's torso, legs and feet are exact cuts of their full body (`cut_slots.py`, by face height: feet under 0.44 m, legs under 1.03 m). The hidden body hides each worn slot's whole region, the faces that slot's bare piece holds (the arms: their `UpperBody` less their `OnlyHead`), its patches made within the regions (char-switch j5b), as Skyrim's body partitions and WoW's geosets give each slot its region of skin. They leave the region out of the draw; we keep one body and collapse its patches. Our gear's rule, a patch hidden when 90% of it meets a worn piece within 12 cm looking out from 2 mm off the skin, was tried first (j5) and hid none of the arm: their sleeves and leggings lie on the skin, flush to 0.0 mm, so the ray finds nothing (0 of 404 arm and hand patches hidden), and the skin showed through the sleeves in patches.

- **The two ways look the same**: the hidden body draws the swap's triangles exactly in every state, so poke, open border, see-through and open rims match point for point (the border counted in points, one a position). They differ only in cost and plumbing.

- **Seams**: where two bare cuts meet, all 212 border points have a twin in the next piece with the same weights and normals, so no crack and no shading line; the hidden body is one mesh and has no such seam. The gaps of both are where a region's edge meets a worn piece:
  - the bare feet's edge dips to 0.42 m at the back of the calf, a centimetre below the Ranger leggings' hem (0.43 m), because the cut runs along triangles;
  - with the coat off, the torso's edge at 1.03 m sits outside the leggings' waistband (19 points showing, the cloth under them, not see-through).
- **The swap's costs**: a bare torso, legs and feet cut from their full body (D599), kept for every body and morph; a mesh swapped in or out at each change.
- **The hidden body's costs**:
  - 9.0k more triangles sent dressed (collapsed, not drawn) and 12.4k more vertices: the patches split the body's vertices (8.3k to 15.3k as drawn bare);
  - a patch layer their body has no room for: it already has two UV sets (the second unused by their materials), and the game reads the patch from the second, so the shader needs its own attribute;
  - its patches must lie within the slot regions (`cut_slots.py` makes them so);
  - one draw fewer with a slot empty (5 to 9 against 6 to 10), and a slot change is a mask, the same one our gear sets.
- **Both ways alike**: poke-through in motion is the same skin under the same cloth (worst 59–82 mm, the thighs through the tunic in a swim or crouch; their own leggings poke 44 mm through it in a crouch). With the coat off, the belly stands 22 mm proud of the leggings' waistband, which was made to sit under the tunic.
- **Mixed legs and feet** (`feet=Peasant`): the Ranger leggings' hem runs at 0.423–0.434 m and the Peasant shoes' top at 0.425–0.447 m, so no single cut height lies inside both.
  - Worn together they are clean at the join.
  - Feet off is as with the Ranger boots.
  - Legs off over the Peasant shoes leaves 29 see-through points at rest (15 over the boots; worst 34) and the shoe's top rim open at 46 points, both ways alike.
  - An ankle band cut between the two heights, shown while either slot is bare, would close it.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-02, asked on the desk what shows when a player takes the coat off: "Swap to their bare piece", with the note "i think we want this but im not sure how well itll work. maybe test first two options".
