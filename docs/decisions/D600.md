# D600: What shows in an empty clothing slot on Quaternius's bodies

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Open: Quaternius's outfits replace the body rather than cover it (each piece is worn on the head alone, and the arms piece carries its own hands and forearms), so with a piece taken off nothing is underneath. Two ways are being tried before the developers choose, both within D599's rule that their parts stay as made:

- **Swap to a bare piece** (the developers' leaning): each slot always holds one piece; taking the coat off puts a bare-skin piece in its place. The packs ship bare pieces for two slots only (checked in both zips, char-switch j3): the head (`OnlyHead`) and the head with bare shoulders, arms and hands (`UpperBody`, an exact cut of the full body), for the man and the woman alike. There is no bare torso, legs or feet piece, and no underclothes piece: the underwear (his briefs, her briefs and top) is painted on the full body's skin texture, and the "Regular" textures in the outfit pack are that same skin texture, which the arms pieces use for their hands. So a bare arm is a swap of `OnlyHead` for `UpperBody` within the rule, while a bare torso, legs or feet needs the full body cut (which D599 forbids), a bare piece of our own, or the second way.
- **Full body under, hidden while covered**: their whole body stays under every outfit, unchanged, and the shader hides the patches a worn piece covers (`body_mask.wesl`, as our gear does today); nothing is cut, only not drawn while covered.

A third, every slot always clothed (an undershirt, plain trousers), was offered and not taken up. The trial measures each on the man through the clips: poke-through, seams at the slot borders, and draw cost, with stills side by side.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-02, asked on the desk what shows when a player takes the coat off: "Swap to their bare piece", with the note "i think we want this but im not sure how well itll work. maybe test first two options".
