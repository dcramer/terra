# D228: Decay and overgrowth

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Moss, ivy, weathering and decay on built cubes are the world's doing for now (age, ruins, villages, by seed), kept per section beside the cubes, not in them; players do not paint them yet.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13 chose "World only for now" over a decorate tool like Enshrouded's withered and overgrown states.
