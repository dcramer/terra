# D645: Who may build in a claim in a shared world

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

In a hosted world the host's base and what stands in it are shown in every game (`play::base_net`, `play::props_net`), but a claim is each player's own: a guest has none where its host's claim stone stands, so its game refuses it there ("That's outside your claim") and friends cannot build one house together. Recommended by the agent: in v0 everyone in a hosted world may build in its claims, a guest taking the claim of each claim stone it is shown, as Valheim's wards let anyone build until one is raised against them; permissions by player (who is let in, who is not) wait for the server to own characters (D592).

## Why

v0's session has two friends in one world (v0-goals); building together is what a base is for, and the netcode now carries the base both ways but for this rule. It is a rule of the game, not of the engine: who may change what a player owns is the developers' to say (D412).
