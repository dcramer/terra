# D645: Anyone may build in any claim

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

**Anyone may build in any claim.** A claim says where building is allowed, not whose it is: whoever is in the world builds and takes apart inside any claim standing in it, their own or another's. Built so far (2026-11-09): the rule, among the claims a game knows: a builder builds in the claim they stand in, their own first (`terra_survival::claim::builds_in`, asked by `terra_building`'s aim and `act::claim_of`). Across the network too (`terra_survival::claim_net`): every game is told where each claim's stone stands and its step, a joined game asking the host to hold its own, so a friend builds in its host's claim and the host in a friend's. Before that a claim was a component on its player (`terra_survival::claim::Claim`), and in a shared world a game is shown another's claim stone as a prop but not how far its claim reaches, so a guest is refused in its host's claim ("That is outside your claim"). What it asks: a claim becomes its stone's, a thing of the world every game is told of with how far it has grown (`play::props_net` carries the stone already; its step is to go with it), and the aim and the build operations take any claim that covers the place. One claim stone each, how a claim grows and what it costs stay as they are (D94). Permissions by player (a claim closed to someone) are not asked for; they would wait for the server to own characters (D592).

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-09: "for claims, anyone can build in any claim". v0's session has two friends in one world (v0-goals); building together is what a base is for, and the netcode carries the base both ways but for this rule.
