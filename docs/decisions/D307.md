# D307: The low preset drawn as integrated GPUs need

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D239. What the low preset draws is cut where an integrated GPU spends its frame, as shipped games' low presets do, measured pass by pass. The ground is another build of its shader (`GROUND_LOW`): each layer's share worked out first, as a splat map holds them, and only the two that show most sampled (a top-two splat, as Far Cry 5's terrain keeps a few layers a texel), the floor's litter, needles, grass and moss settled by height and the rest laid over as the high preset lays them; each projected along the one or two axes the surface faces most (Quilez's biplanar mapping), not three; flat in its layers' own colours past 45 to 75 m, which is what their smallest mips show there; and without the soil's stones or the finer fraying of the cover map. The high preset's ground is unchanged.

## Why

The developers asked for the low preset to meet D239's 30 frames a second in the world, the forest, the village, a house going up and the town on the Ryzen 9 9950X3D's two-unit Radeon. The ground cost the world's main pass about 15 ms of its 46 there: with this the world went from 84 and 86 ms to 74 and 67, its main pass from 45 and 48 to 33 and 29, on a machine shared with other agents' builds (load 30 to 90 on 32 cores); pictures from the same place match but for the floor's finest detail ([research/integrated-gpu.md](../../research/integrated-gpu.md)).
