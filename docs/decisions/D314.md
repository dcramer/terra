# D314: Climate in code: fields, an integrated past, samples and maps

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Temperature, daylight, precipitation, wind and the air's wetness are pure functions of a place and a time in a Bevy-free crate, from the world's seed and clock, so the server sends only the time; snow, wetness, ice and plants' warmth and chill are folded over recent weather per coarse cell, kept by the day, never saved; things that care carry a `ClimateSample` refreshed by distance from players, bent by local components (fire, shelter, shade, clothing) and read by small systems (a body's temperature, creatures, walking, ice); plants do not tick but are worked out from their cell, their kind's year and their seed; the renderer reads climate maps baked round the camera, as it reads the cover map. Built in the order in climate.md, proved in the lush forest.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-14: "i like treating it systemic as in there's a temperature measure and that affects the world, like think of how this would work in ECS"; "/goal around all of that, im fine with it." Costs a few texture reads on the GPU and a fold per cell per day on the CPU.
