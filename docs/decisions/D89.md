# D89: Direction: a living world

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The game's design is [gameplay.md](../gameplay.md) and the system pages it links (once game design 001): still a sandbox with some survival, but focused on a living world you take part in, with NPCs, monsters, wildlife and events, across realms reached by portals; closer to Enshrouded, a sandbox with RPG in it; *He Who Fights With Monsters* as the basis of the lore, as world-building rather than content ([lore.md](../lore.md), D125). No terraforming, and no oxygen tanks or science fiction for now. After this file, those pages are the source of truth for design; each item in them is as settled as the decision it cites.

## Why

Considered by the developers on 2026-10-11; adopted on 2026-10-12: "we're doing realms per the game design doc, which you can consider now the source of truth and can be interleaved elsewhere". Its pages moved from proposals into docs, and the pitch (vision.md), survival, progression and the old gameplay page were brought in line.
