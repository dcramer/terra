# D181: Structures strictly on the voxel grid

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Everything built is half-metre cubes of a kind of construction on its base's grid, as D48 says: walls are rows of cubes a cube thick, posts columns of them, floors layers, openings cubes left empty, of any size and shape, with doors (1 by 2 m) and windows (sill 1 m, head 2 m) as default sizes, Enshrouded's way. Roofs are cubes too, cut to slopes and built by hand, since D226 and D241; they were first the one exception, a surface over the cubes. Kits draw the cubes as their pieces (D146, D177), so logs are still seen and built one by one. Height is counted in cubes; a storey of 3 m is a wall's default height. See [building-tech.md](../building-tech.md#the-model).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-12: "i think it sould be strictly aligned to the voxel grid, no?", then "Yes" to writing it as the plan, and "windows could be flexible in size tho, this is more just a default like enshrouded". What it gives: posts, carving, mixing kinds and thick walls from one rule. What it costs: walls a cube thick, so rooms are a metre smaller than drawn; floors a cube thick, so 2.5 m clear in a 3 m storey; doors 2.0 m high, not 2.1. Structures were rebuilt on cubes the same day.
