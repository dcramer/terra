# D512: A door is pushed open by walking into it; a locked one needs its key

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A door that needs no key opens when a body walks into it, as in The Witcher 3: there is no button, the leaves swing away from whoever pushes and stay open. A locked door stays shut until its key (every key of its lock) is held, then opens the same way. A one-way door is barred on one side: pushed from the other it opens like any door, and once open it stays open for both. How keys are held (an item carried, or an object of the dungeon) stays open in [dungeons.md](../dungeons.md).

## Why

Asked on the review desk (r-dungeons) whether and how doors open, the developers on 2026-10-26: "doors can open like in witcher where if they dont require key they are just pushed open". Was open in docs/dungeons.md; the demo stood every leaf open.
