# D538: A job agent carries the routine

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A coordinator starts every build job (one agent's bounded unit of work, D400) with the `job` agent in `.claude/agents/` and isolation "worktree". The agent file holds the routine: seed and pull, one plain command a call, edits by Edit and Write, scratch in the worktree's `target/job/`, waits on completion notices, the step-back and bound rules, and the handoff (`~/.cache/terra-handoff/<topic>-j<n>.md`) and report with `FOR REVIEW:` and `FOUND:` lines. The brief says only the task, the skill, the bound and the handoff files. The word is the developers' (2026-10-27), replacing "round": a line of work keeps its numbers, so combat's job 35 follows its round 34, and handoffs already written keep their `-r<n>` names. A job here is never a render worker's job (`cargo xtask remote-render`, tools/render-worker), which a doc names as such where both appear.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-27: "Yes: build the round agent type"; then, of the word, "'round' is such an awful word", choosing "Rename to job". The transcripts of 2026-10-24 to 26 (12 sessions, 200 subagents) show coordinators writing about 180 briefs of 2 to 7k characters by hand, each restating AGENTS.md's routine in its own order. The jobs (then called rounds) lost about 770 turns to refusals: 565 by the worktree guard (chained `cd`/git commands, Python heredoc edits, a variable holding the coordinator's scratchpad), 127 by the wait-loop hook and the sleep block, and 75 `update_plan` calls a subagent cannot make. Two once ran in one worktree. A job's own file fixes these once instead of in every brief.
