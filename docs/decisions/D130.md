# D130: What item icons look like

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

In the art style (D14, now direction D, [art-style.md](../art-style.md)); every icon is still painted in direction C (hand-painted surfaces, chunky readable forms, slightly exaggerated proportions), to be repainted in D once a D style reference is approved. A bold silhouette, warm light from the upper left, no background. Composed by the render: held things along the icon's diagonal, square on, working end up and to the right; everything else from the front, 15 degrees above, worn things as worn with no body in them. See [items.md](../items.md#item-icons).

## Why

The developers wanted icons in the art style and left the rest to me. Renders were compared five ways, then painted icons against renders: painted read clearly better at slot size and held one style, so every item was painted on 2026-10-12. Leaning until the developers have looked at the full set.
