# D265: Claims keep ten metres apart, and clear of villages

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

D193's "claims do not overlap, keep a margin" enforced: a claim stone is refused, and a claim does not grow, where its edge would come within 10 m of another claim's edge, or of anywhere a village reaches (along its street, and each building's door, back and corners), measured across the ground (`play::claim::too_near`). The hammer says why.

## Why

Mine, on 2026-10-14, as the lead asked: D193 said it and nothing enforced it, between claims or against the village. Ten metres leaves room to walk round a base and keeps what stands out at a claim's edge, eaves and footings, off a neighbour's; growth is checked too, as a claim that grows into another overlaps as surely as one placed there.
