# D344: A town's ground worn, its smoke grey, its people carrying their tools and going round visitors

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D324 and D325. The ground cover says how trodden a place is (`Growing::trodden`, the cover's second map's alpha); the ground shader lays a settlement's ways as packed earth of their own grey-brown (linear 0.15, 0.132, 0.108), its scan's grain kept but not its hue, stones worked up, mud dark in hollows along the middle and straw at the edges and in yards, over the planet's soil. A hearth's smoke has a grey's albedo (0.3) and a card a wisp's opacity (0.2), and a house cooks on a big fire or a small one by its own luck, so a few columns stand over the roofs. What someone holds is data by activity (`holds` in `townsfolk.ron`: the smith's hammer, the woodcutter's axe, the guard's spear), carried on the way and at it in the hand's socket. Players and the camera are bodies the town's walkers go round by ORCA (`Life::visitors`), and someone out of doors is a fixed body a player stops at. Measured against `town-market-day` (eight KCD1, KCD2 and Witcher 3 frames).

## Why

The adversarial review of 2026-10-15 scored the town 4/10: one orange trodden soil, red/green 1.32 against the reference's 1.00, shade red/green 2.3 against 1.13, lit litter 36% against 8% (the planet's warm soil under the cover's duff, its contrast drawn out); white pillars of smoke brighter than the land at dusk (an albedo of 0.62 lit from every side); nobody carrying anything; the camera into a face and a player through people.
