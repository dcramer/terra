# D164: Attributes

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Four attributes, as in the books: Power, Speed, Spirit and Recovery. Each essence binds to one when absorbed; each has its own rank, and a person's rank is a function of them. Proposed in [magic.md](../magic.md#attributes): they rise as the powers bound to them rise, and what each raises.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "keep the attributes yes".
