# D497: A dungeon is a place in its realm, not a world of its own

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A dungeon is part of the realm it is found in, as in Valheim and Enshrouded: it is dug into that realm's ground at its site and belongs to that realm's world instance (D468), so going into one unloads nothing. Only crossing to another realm, through a portal (D72), loads one world out and another in ([instances.md](../instances.md)).

## Why

Asked on the review desk whether a dungeon is its own instance or a place carved into its realm, the developers on 2026-10-25: "hm i think its more like valheim/enshrouded actually". Was the agent's default, a dungeon as its own instance behind a door.
