# D466: Fire is one self-contained module, and every kind's flame body is one marched flame field

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The technical design of [fire-module.md](../fire-module.md), extending D415.

- **Boundaries.** `terra_world::fire` is one plugin. The game talks to it only by spawning a fire of a kind at a place, setting its fuel and heat, putting it out, and asking where fire is. Kinds are presets in `assets/data/fire.ron` (D136); its shaders and textures are its own. What needs no engine (presets, sources, timing, ramp keys, wind and rain rules) moves to a plain crate, `terra_fire` (D28). Coupled on purpose: the film mask and tonemapper, burnables' materials, building's fire sim, and snow reading fire's heat discs.
- **The general flame.** Every kind's body is one marched flame field in a box round the fire: teardrop lobes over its sources, displaced by domain-warped noise scrolled up through it, a thin torn edge over a threshold, heat by depth and height through the shared ramp, emission and absorption front to back, writing the film mask and its depth as the marched breath does (D430). Cards stay only for sparks, embers, smoke, the flare and the far LOD. A baked 3D loop may replace the procedural noise later (the hybrid) if a measured gap asks.
- **Weather.** Wind shears and stretches the field by Thomas's flame-tilt correlation, smoke more; rain dims an open fire and can put it out, with steam; snow melts within fire's heat discs.
- **The bench and the measure.** A sandbox place with every kind side by side, under the forest-walk demo's time, season and weather keys once they land. A shared change lands on the fire sheet's rows across kinds (more into class than out) and blind motion panels across kinds (pooled +0.25).
- **Order.** A spike on the campfire first, against main's cards in a motion panel; the field becomes a preset's body only if it wins.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-25: "I would love to work on some effects like fire in general, since it's ass. we wanna make sure that weather states and etc. will work well... this should be a self contained module as much as possible." On the review desk he chose "Rebuild the flame body" over more card tuning.

About forty rounds of card tuning left the judges calling every kind "painted opaque cards"; a better Mantaflow flipbook read as haze (research/2026-10-24-campfire-one-sim.md) and a professional flipbook failed our gate (research/2026-10-19-fire-step-back.md). The marched breath is the one fire holding its sheet rows at every hour. A field is the same flame from every side, changes in minutes rather than a bake of hours, and takes wind, rain and going out as changes of the same field. Shipped games mostly bake flipbooks (research/2026-10-19-how-games-draw-fire.md); we depart on our own measured evidence.
