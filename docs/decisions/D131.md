# D131: Building on uneven ground

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A structure set into the ground cuts the ground away only inside its footprint, down to its floor, no deeper than its own height; under the floor stays, for the foundation, and outside a building the ground is not changed (narrowed by D193, 2026-10-12). Building below ground is D143. Trees and rocks where it would stand are not cleared by what is built: the player clears them first, and until then it cannot be placed. Small plants and grass are cleared as before.

## Why

Recommended on 2026-10-11 and agreed by the developers as reasonable ("placing a room could let it cut away"); "we should prevent you from placing buildings where there are props like trees/rocks and make you clear those first". Built in the game. The ground cut away gives nothing yet.
