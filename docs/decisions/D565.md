# D565: Weapons and tools are assembled from parts by rules

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every ordinary weapon and tool is assembled from a few parts per kind (a handle, a head, a binding…), each made once with named connection points, put together by rules from the maker's people, realm and material; the item's grip, second grip and working edge are its parts' points, exact by construction, and a kind owns how it is held, carried, stowed and animated. Uniques stay whole models checked by the fitter (D552). It is checked by parts and kinds, never per item, on the pose and renderer the game uses. The design, its open questions and how it is verified: [assembled-items.md](../assembled-items.md).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-28: "we keep making minor incremental tweaks to make attachments work. I want a deterministic system as best as possible ... that seems unscaleable if we have 1000s of items", then "maybe we need to reconsider our approach and look more at procedural approaches but im still worried about how they attach", and "how do we make sure they render correctly". Whole models fitted after the fact need their grip and facing guessed from their shape and their leftovers tuned, per item; shipped games with many items (Borderlands, No Man's Sky, Veloren's modular weapons) attach by points authored on parts instead. It is D182 applied to equipment. The developers on 2026-10-28, on the review desk, approved the design's recommended answers: "Approve as recommended". On the size envelope: "im not sure on the envelope as i dont know technical design for this kind of system", so it is the agent's technical limit; on icons: "icons can use credits thats fine and we probably wont have a unique icon for every unique combination of parts". They also chose to make one approved style reference picture before any part is generated: "Yes, make one first".
