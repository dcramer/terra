# D565: Weapons and tools are assembled from parts by rules

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Proposed: every ordinary weapon and tool is assembled from a few parts per kind (a handle, a head, a binding…), each made once with named connection points, put together by rules from the maker's people, realm and material; the item's grip, second grip and working edge are its parts' points, exact by construction, and a kind owns how it is held, carried, stowed and animated. Uniques stay whole models checked by the fitter (D552). It is checked by parts and kinds, never per item, on the pose and renderer the game uses. The design, its open questions and how it is verified: [assembled-items.md](../assembled-items.md).

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-28: "we keep making minor incremental tweaks to make attachments work. I want a deterministic system as best as possible ... that seems unscaleable if we have 1000s of items", then "maybe we need to reconsider our approach and look more at procedural approaches but im still worried about how they attach", and "how do we make sure they render correctly". Whole models fitted after the fact need their grip and facing guessed from their shape and their leftovers tuned, per item; shipped games with many items (Borderlands, No Man's Sky, Veloren's modular weapons) attach by points authored on parts instead. It is D182 applied to equipment. Open until the developers answer the questions in the doc.
