# D518: Seeing the weather coming

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: the sky draws the climate's weather out to the horizon, not only the weather where the eye stands, so a front's cirrus shows a day ahead, its deck rolls in over hours, and a storm's tower and rain shafts are seen tens of kilometres off before it arrives. Options: to the horizon, as recommended; a few kilometres round the eye (cheaper, less to read); or the weather at the eye alone, as today (D418). See [sky.md](../sky.md#clouds).

## Why

D78 (the developers): players forecast the weather "mostly by looking". A sky of the eye's weather alone cannot be read ahead; the climate already knows where the fronts are (D315), so drawing them far is a matter of how far the weather map reaches.
