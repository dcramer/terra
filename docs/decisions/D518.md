# D518: Seeing the weather coming

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The sky draws the climate's weather out to the horizon, not only the weather where the eye stands, so a front's cirrus shows a day ahead, its deck rolls in over hours, and a storm's tower and rain shafts are seen tens of kilometres off before it arrives. The other options were a few kilometres round the eye, and the weather at the eye alone, as today (D418). See [sky.md](../sky.md#clouds).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-26, on the review desk: "To the horizon". This settles the question in the recommendation's favour.

D78 (the developers): players forecast the weather "mostly by looking". A sky of the eye's weather alone cannot be read ahead; the climate already knows where the fronts are (D315), so drawing them far is a matter of how far the weather map reaches.
