# D593: How to fix the base body's proportions

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Open

The base body's arms are 11 cm short shoulder to wrist, and the fault is in every template. Open: fix it by stretching the arms in the character pipeline, by scaling bones in the game, or by remaking the base from a new sheet; and whether the very wide chest and shoulders and the low crotch are to change too. Recommended: stretch in the pipeline, with a proportion check that fails today's base, and the idle's shoulders lowered in the clip. It costs no credits and holds for every body made after it. The stretch leaves the chest and crotch as they are.

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-01, on the idle's stills: "whys our models torso look weird af", then "ok lets figure it out". Measured against 4,082 men in ANSUR II, reach is below the 1st percentile and fingertips hang at the crotch, 15 cm above a real man's. Nothing measures proportions today, and all six design sheets draw the arm short. Measures, causes and costs: [the note](../../research/2026-11-01-why-the-torso-looks-wrong.md).
