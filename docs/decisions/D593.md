# D593: The base body's arms and legs are stretched in the character pipeline

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The base body's arms are lengthened to a 1.86 m reach and its legs made 10% longer in one stretch in the character pipeline (`convert_rig.py`), the whole body then scaled back to 1.80 m, on every template, designs and dressed models alike, with a proportion check that fails today's base. The chest and shoulders stay as they are until the developers answer that separately; the idle's pose is remade after, on the new body.

Leaning, built: chosen by the agent on the developer's word, 2026-11-01, and held Leaning until the developers say it is settled (D412), since their words delegate the method rather than settle it: on the test copy, "He looks fine but his pose is atupid there", then, asked how to build it, "Idk you are the expert". Stretching keeps the same man, costs no credits and rebuilds the clips from stored takes; a remake costs about 250 credits and may change his face, and every sheet so far drew short arms. It lands only once gear, every template, the sword in the guard and the fight's checks pass on it.

Built 2026-11-02 and landed with known faults, the agent's call on the developer's "you are the expert". Every body passes the proportion check, the rig check, the combat scorecard (nothing worse than its baseline) and `moves.ts` (only the two `Keyed_Guard` rows that fail on main too, held). Fixing the hung pieces' scale on the way made every skirt and apron about 9% smaller, their waistband at the waist, and all of them pass. The gear gate has no allow-list, so `build_bodies.sh` ends `BUILD fail` on 7 looks against main's 8, the 2% bar left alone:

- **The smith's apron**, base-male 3.0% (`Jump_Land`), heavy 5.5% (`Sitting_Exit`), strong 3.2%, female 2.5%; strong and female failed before the stretch too. The chest folds down onto the lap, which the thighs lift and which hangs 9 to 13 cm off them; two changes to its weights in `hung_garment.py` were worse. Fix: a joint of its own for the lap, pivoting at the waist and driven by the thighs, as Unreal's skirt bones on a pose driver; it touches the skeleton and the game's skinning, a job of its own.
- **Thin's shoes**, 2.2% swimming: one point at the back of the left heel, counted four times by its seam copies, 1.0 to 1.4 cm through with the foot pointed. Fix: the gate counting a welded point once, then a push at the heel.
- **The quiver** on base-male and heavy in a swim dive, as on main.

What was open before:

The base body's arms are 11 cm short shoulder to wrist, and the fault is in every template. Open: fix it by stretching the arms in the character pipeline, by scaling bones in the game, or by remaking the base from a new sheet; and whether the very wide chest and shoulders and the low crotch are to change too. Recommended: stretch in the pipeline, with a proportion check that fails today's base, and the idle's shoulders lowered in the clip. It costs no credits and holds for every body made after it. The arm stretch leaves the chest and crotch as they are.

Also open, tested on a scratch copy: legs 10% longer in the same stretch, the whole body then scaled back to 1.80 m. Recommended if the developer likes the look: reach 1.86 m, crotch 0.85 m, 7.7 heads tall, the chest at the edge of normal, and the walk's feet nearer the ground than today's. Legs 17% longer reach the real 0.87 m crotch but look stilted and put the hips 7 cm too high, since the crotch is low in the model's shape; a local lift would close the last 2 cm.

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-01, on the idle's stills: "whys our models torso look weird af", then "ok lets figure it out", and on trying longer legs in the same step: "Yes, try both". Measured against 4,082 men in ANSUR II, reach is below the 1st percentile and fingertips hang at the crotch, 15 cm above a real man's. Nothing measures proportions today, and all six design sheets draw the arm short. Measures, causes and costs: [the note](../../research/2026-11-01-why-the-torso-looks-wrong.md).
