# D484: A dungeon is stamped as one building of cubes in a dug box, and run in from its graph

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

`terra_dungeon::stamp` turns a dressed dungeon into what the world lays, as plain data in a local frame (metres, x east, y up, z south; the origin the roof's top at the north-west corner of the entrance cell; level L's floor top at -4(L+1) m) ([dungeons.md](../dungeons.md)):

- **Digs**: one terrain box a level (its cells' bounds and a cube round them), one under each level's basins and shores, one up the entrance well: the terrain editor scans its edits linearly, so few boxes. Each goes to 0.03 m under the floor's top, as creatures stand on the ground, not on cubes.
- **The shell** in building cubes (D181) of the theme's wall, floor and trim kinds: a floor and ceiling slab a cell, a wall a cube thick where placement put one, doorways and stair ways as `Fill::Open` cubes so each room and corridor stays a closed room to building's light (an empty doorway would join every space to the open entrance well), stairs of `Stair` cubes, 8 risers of 0.5 m over a 6 m run, the well open above them.
- **Modules to cubes and props** by words in their names (pillar, alcove, rubble, basin, shore, runner, decor, sarcophagus, urns, stores, rack, table, timber, vein); an unknown name is plain floor, so a new module is data.
- **The run-in**: the graph's mobs at free spots away from doors, a chest a room with loot holding it, the goal object and a key object at each key room's feature (dungeon objects, not items), a studded door leaf at each locked and one-way door, up to 30 torches and braziers, the start on the surface and a tour of floor points from it to the goal.

The sandbox's experiments `dungeon`, `dungeon-mine` and `dungeon-keep` (theme and seed in `assets/data/dungeon_demo.ron`) dig a level site in the lush forest, lay the ops as one building with automatic footings off, and spawn the rest. Tests check, over 200 seeds a theme, that a walker stepping up one cube and down two reaches every object, chest, mob and fire from the start (so the stairs climb), that air from inside never leaves its own space but up the entrance well, and that every model exists.

## Why

Building's cubes already carry the kits, damage, light probes and drawing a dungeon needs, as the castle uses them (D181); a dungeon in cubes is one more base, not a new mesher. Plain data from a plain crate keeps the stamp testable without Bevy (D28) and the same for the server's instance (D468). Three experiments reading one data file keep a theme and seed data, not a flag for one scene (D249).
