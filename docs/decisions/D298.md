# D298: A change of material within a pattern is a repaint at once

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D232. Change material between two kinds of one family (one pattern, framed or not) is a repaint at once: the change's every step, the old taken out and given back, the swap and the new put in, done as one act and checked as one (`plans::change`), so what stands changes its material where it is, as Satisfactory's Customizer and V Rising's wallpaper repaint; across families, where the structure itself changes, the cubes are marked and built anew a step at a time as D232 has it. Drawn as a lookup (D290): a change of material and nothing else rewrites the layers of the changed cubes' corners in the meshes drawn, on screen the next frame, the sections laid again behind it with the new kind's weathering and joints; the material in a hammer is kept resident so the lookup never waits for it.

## Why

Construction 002's third question, left to the agent with every open call on 2026-10-14 ("Like get it done"); the developers' "they need to be efficient when placing materials to quickly swap to the right visual". Measured on the Windows build: the lookup 0.5 ms of work and on screen the frame after the change, the sections laid again three frames later.
