# D586: High's sun shadows in three cascades

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The high preset draws the sun's shadows in three cascades of 2048², not four, over the same reach: the first to 24 m, the last to 300 m, Bevy splitting them geometrically at about 24 and 85 m (four split at 24, 56 and 129 m). Shadows from 24 to about 130 m are a little coarser; nothing nearer or farther changes (`quality::shadows`). Medium keeps three to 200 m, Ultra four to 1,200 m, as Unreal's scalability levels set `r.Shadow.CSM.MaxCascades` a level.

Revisit when something other than the ground, trunks and props casts past 100 m, or when a place's middle distance shows the coarser shadows.

## Why

Forest perf round 6 timed each cascade on `forest-run` (a probe span a cascade, RTX 4090, one run each): 0.27, 0.30, 0.41 and 0.49 ms, 1.84, 4.64, 7.98 and 9.84 million triangles. Of the cuts tried, the GPU part went 8.73 ms (base, 8.79 again) to 8.16 with no crown stand-ins casting (the canopy's shade gone: not kept), 8.52 with shadows to 150 m, 8.24 with three cascades and 8.08 with both; three cascades kept the full reach and saved half of what the stand-ins cost. Fewer cascades also shrink the shadow views' uniform allocation and bin unpacking (0.63 to 0.55 and 0.54 to 0.47 ms).

The A/B (forest perf round 7), `cargo xtask bench forest-run --runs 2` on the worker's RTX 4090, main at eda521310 against the change: median 9.14 and 9.18 ms to 8.58 and 8.60, the GPU part 8.77 and 8.82 to 8.24 and 8.25, `per_view_shadow_pass` 1.54 to 1.18 ms; the 99th percentile 12.04 and 12.09 to 12.35 and 12.42, within what it moves run to run (the probe's two base runs 13.18 and 12.61). The lush place's six views at summer noon and 17 h and at 01-15 noon and 16.5 h, main against the change: at most 2.2% of pixels moved more than 24 of 255 (winter noon, the edge; main against itself 0.14%), the mean change at most 2.2 of 255, the shade under the canopy and the far trees alike to the eye. The shadow maps hold 48 MiB at High, not 64 (D420).
