# D496: A fire's glow is marched short, by its own lights

Area: Engine and code · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

Bevy's volumetric fog shader marches a fire's glow volume (`terra_world::fire::fog`) in 16 steps, not the fog's 48, and takes no directional light into it; every other fog volume, the haze among them, is marched as before. The shader tells a glow by its absorption: a volume absorbing over `GLOW_ABSORPTION`, 100 a metre, is a glow, since `fire::fog` carries a glow's air 1e5 times thicker (`AIR_SCALE`, absorption 2,000) and no real fog comes near it (the haze's is 0.05). The change is a patch of `bevy_pbr` 0.20.0 (`patches/bevy_pbr/src/volumetric_fog/volumetric_fog.wesl`, `patches/README.md`, as D184 allows), marked `Terra's patch (D496)`; `fire::fog`'s test reads the mark from the shader and holds a glow's air well over it.

## Why

A near campfire's volumetric pass was most of its night cost (docs/fire.md, What the light costs): its glow box, the eye inside it, is marched over the whole screen, with the moon's shadow cascades sampled at every one of 48 steps. A fire's glow is warmed by its fire's lights alone and the moon in it is not seen, and a soft halo needs few steps. Bevy's `FogVolume` has no per-volume step count or light mask to extend, and the shader is Bevy's own, so the change is made in its source (D196, D184). Shipped engines keep a fire's halo in a froxel grid at a fixed cost (Unreal's volumetric fog); Bevy's fog is per pixel, so the march is cut short instead. Telling a glow by its absorption keeps the patch to the shader, with no new uniform.

## Measured

Fire round g32, RTX 3080, 1080p, the bench view of a campfire 4.4 m off on the yard, direct bench-profile runs interleaved (10 of main, 5 of this): by night the median frame 16.40 to 13.06 ms (volumetric pass 7.28 to 4.07), by dusk 12.23 to 12.13 (3.50 to 3.42). The stills at 18:24 and 21:30 match main's (pit, ground ring and the air above the pit within 0.2 of 255 in mean grey; the halo and haze the same to the eye), and the fire sheet passes its gate. Both builds burst (10-35 frames of +6 to +17 ms GPU time, a whole-GPU stall at the card's power cap, fire round g31): main in 9 of 10 runs by dusk and 8 of 10 by night, this in 5 of 5 at each.
