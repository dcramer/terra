# D132: How damage shows

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Leaning

Damage shows on what was hit, as wounds and how it moves, not as numbers.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "damage should be visible on targets ideally, not numerical. like you should see the target get wounded", perhaps as a future thing. How it could work on every model is proposed in [combat.md](../combat.md). The tech demo has no damage numbers; a blow sprays and the body jerks.
