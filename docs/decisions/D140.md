# D140: Large materials, carried

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Large structures need large materials, such as logs, which are carried to the plan (D139). Better ways to transport them are probably unlocked later (Leaning).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12 ("probably"), then plainly with D139: "for all large construction it'd originally be placed as a blueprint, and you'd have to then bring the materials to it to build it (kind of like the forest or grounded 2)". See [building.md](../building.md#large-materials-and-carrying).
