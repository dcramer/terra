# D477: Climbing is free climbing on stamina

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A body can climb any surface it can hold, on stamina, as in Enshrouded and Breath of the Wild; climbing is a skill by doing (D88). How it plays in detail (speed, what can't be climbed, stamina cost, mantling at the top) is designed before it is built.

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-25, on the review desk, choosing among ledge grab and mantle (recommended), free climb and none for now: "Free climb".
