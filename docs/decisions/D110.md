# D110: A life-sized world

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The world feels life-sized: things are at real-world scale, so trees stand as tall as real trees, rocks range up to boulders bigger than a person, and hills and mountains are as large as real ones. The player is small against the land.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11 as a design requirement, after scene tests where trees and hills read as miniatures. It applies to every model's size (the pipeline sets each one's height in metres) and to terrain generation, and it fits the world's scale (D13). See [art-style.md](../art-style.md#life-sized).
