# D144: Building controls

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The building controls and snapping in [building.md](../building.md#controls): a click lays a plan for free and work spends materials; height on Shift and the wheel, kind of construction on Ctrl and the wheel, undo on Y (Enshrouded's; Ctrl and Z would roll), drawing walls and rooms by dragging; right mouse the build menu, Q take apart, G move, F to use and open doors; snapping to joins, then the grid, then surfaces, sticky and gliding. How a part is put into a plan is still being worked through (D139).

## Why

Asked for by the developers on 2026-10-12 ("make sure you've got the controls locked down"), then: "generally speaking your plans feel good".
