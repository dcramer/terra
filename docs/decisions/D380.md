# D380: Small animals in every biome

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every biome has a few small animals that belong there, found where their climate and cover put them: in the lush forest red squirrels, chaffinches, brown hares and red foxes; at the mountain lake mallards on the water and a grey heron at the shore, the forest's kinds in its shore woods; on the tundra mountain hares, ptarmigan and arctic foxes; in the town house sparrows and feral pigeons on its streets and dogs at their owners' heels, besides its hens. Each lives its day (rests, grazes or pecks, wanders) and flees as its kind does: a squirrel up a trunk, a bird on the wing, a duck swimming off, a hare or fox running. The kinds were chosen by the agent from the developers' suggestions ([creatures.md](../creatures.md#small-animals-about-the-land)).

## Why

The developers, 2026-10-15: "we should add some small animals to all biomes, we have already some, others we can generate." They suggested for the forest red squirrels on trunks and the ground, songbirds flitting, a hare or rabbit, perhaps a fox; for the lake ducks on the water and a heron at the shore; for the tundra arctic hares, ptarmigan, lemmings and a fox; for the town pigeons or sparrows and a cat or dog.
