# D385: Building's debug views

Area: Tools and workflow · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

One key, F4 (Shift+F4 back), and the remote's `debug.view <name>` cycle what every building is coloured by, each with a legend on screen saying what it shows and how to read it (`build::views`, [development.md](../development.md#buildings-debug-views)), as Unreal's view modes and Unity's draw modes do: **texels** (screen pixels a texel covers from a metre off against a texel a pixel, D362; hatched where blurred as seen now), **roles** (each cube's edges by role, roof lines dashed by part, faces by what is beyond them, within 40 m), **detail** (each mesh by the detail it draws, sections' and groups' boxes by the detail they are laid for, the base being built's parts), **damage** (health lost, where the drawing breaks a surface, damaged cubes boxed), **shade** (light kept after texture occlusion, SSAO and corners' baked shade, tinted by what takes most), **normals** (mapped normal in world axes, tangent frames as ticks), **plan** (built, ghosts, next), **light** (the light alone, as Unreal's Lighting Only: every surface an 18% grey lit as it is, normal map and occlusion kept, colours and baked corner shade left out, to read against a grey card). The construction materials' arrays draw a view themselves, its code compiled in only while one is on (`BUILD_VIEW`, a specialisation key); every other mesh of a building wears a copy of its own material with the view's shader (`build_view_piece.wesl`) and gets its own back; the colours come from one shader module (`build_views.wesl`). Off, nothing runs and nothing is compiled in; every preset draws them.

## Why

[research/building-workflow-retro-2026-10-16.md](../../research/building-workflow-retro-2026-10-16.md), item 1: most look and fault questions that week took a round of measuring where one look at a debug view would have answered them; D71 asks that tools explain themselves.
