# D223: Crowns that thin card by card

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A crown near the camera is one mesh with all its cards; each card carries its offset from its middle and its rank in its clump (in the tangent, which leaves have no other use for), and the foliage vertex shader shrinks the next card to go to its middle while those that stay grow to cover as much (by the count's ratio to the power 0.55), from all of them at 18 m to two a clump at 110 m (a conifer's to its least at 70 m, D318), measured from the camera in the shadow pass too. Past 110 m the crown is those two cards at the same size, then lumps. This is SpeedTree's continuous leaf LOD, which the Witcher 3 builds on, in place of crossfading between versions. Trunks and branches still crossfade, and are next.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: "how does it work in witcher that this doesn't pop in the distance". [research/forest-lod-and-mood.md](../../research/forest-lod-and-mood.md). The walk bench is 11.6 ms on the RTX 3080, within budget.
