# D589: The v0 milestone

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: the next milestone is v0, a session two players play on two machines from a seed through two procedurally generated realms joined by a gate, as [docs/v0-goals.md](../v0-goals.md) says, with building and villages on hold.
- **The tracks.** The developers' eight areas (the title screen, two realms and a portal, terrain and a living world, the character controller, animation, multiplayer, sound, inventory and action RPG play), divided so they run side by side, with three gaps added: what creatures choose to do, a second realm's content, and a way to prove the session.
- **What it asks of others.** D105's MVP left out portals and many players; v0 takes that loop as built and adds them. D579 offers no seed, for a reason that lapses here, and is the developers' to amend. The doc's questions (who it is for, what the second realm is, how large the worlds are, why a player crosses) are theirs.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-01: "heres our major TODO, these are the things we think we need for a v0 (but its not the entire game)", and "we want these to be separated out into what can be done in parallel". Their eight areas are in the doc; the division into tracks, the gaps, the numbers and the order are the agent's proposal, for them to edit.
