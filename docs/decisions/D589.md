# D589: The v0 milestone

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: the next milestone is v0, the foundations of the engine with one thin demo on top, proved by a session two players play on two machines from a seed through two procedurally generated realms, beating a boss whose mark (D590) opens the gate between them, as [docs/v0-goals.md](../v0-goals.md) says, with building and villages on hold.
- **The tracks.** The developers' eight areas (the title screen, two realms and a portal, terrain and a living world, the character controller, animation, multiplayer, sound, inventory and action RPG play), divided so they run side by side, with three gaps added: what creatures choose to do, a second realm's content, and a way to prove the session.
- **What it asks of others.** D105's MVP asks whether the loop is fun and leaves out portals and many players; v0 asks whether the engine is sound, and needs both. D579 offers no seed, for a reason that lapses here, and is the developers' to amend. The doc's questions (who it is for, what the second realm is, how large the worlds are, how a player reaches the boss, how its fight works) are theirs.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-01: "heres our major TODO, these are the things we think we need for a v0 (but its not the entire game)", and "we want these to be separated out into what can be done in parallel". Then: "we're not really solving the reason to cross or actual gameplay yet, just trying to get foundations of an engine in place". Then, on the demo: "we have some kind of boss in the main game. to get to the boss you probably have to do some stuff (maybe level up or something, progress around? find clues?) when you beat the boss he drops an artifact, that artifact allows you to open the portal, and go to the other world, and thats basically the end of the demo (the other world is playable, but no more progression etc)". Their eight areas are in the doc; the division into tracks, the gaps, the numbers and the order are the agent's proposal, for them to edit.
