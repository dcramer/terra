# D589: The v0 milestone

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The next milestone is v0, the foundations of the engine with one thin demo on top, proved by a session two players play on two machines from a seed through two procedurally generated realms, beating a boss whose mark (D590) opens the gate between them, as [docs/v0-goals.md](../v0-goals.md) says, with building and villages on hold.
- **The tracks.** The developers' eight areas (the title screen, two realms and a portal, terrain and a living world, the character controller, animation, multiplayer, sound, inventory and action RPG play), divided so they run side by side, with three gaps added: what creatures choose to do, a second realm's content, and a way to prove the session.
- **What it asks of others.** D105's MVP asks whether the loop is fun and leaves out portals and many players; v0 asks whether the engine is sound, and needs both. D579 now gives a world a seed. The second realm's theme, the way to the boss and the fight's rules are left for later.

## Why

The developers, 2026-11-01: "heres our major TODO, these are the things we think we need for a v0 (but its not the entire game)", and "we want these to be separated out into what can be done in parallel". Then: "we're not really solving the reason to cross or actual gameplay yet, just trying to get foundations of an engine in place". Then, on the demo: "we have some kind of boss in the main game. to get to the boss you probably have to do some stuff (maybe level up or something, progress around? find clues?) when you beat the boss he drops an artifact, that artifact allows you to open the portal, and go to the other world, and thats basically the end of the demo (the other world is playable, but no more progression etc)". Their eight areas are in the doc; the division into tracks, the gaps, the numbers and the order are the agent's, and stay changeable as the work teaches. Then, the same day, asked to settle it: "Yes, mark Decided".
