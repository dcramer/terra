# D238: Laying a base once a change, and making only the detail that is seen

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

A base is laid whole once, on a worker (`sections::Whole`), and a change lays again only as far as it reaches (D281); each section waiting is cut from it rather than laid alone: what a section holds depends on its whole building (a row of logs' joins, a deck's boards), so laying it alone laid most of the building again for every section. A section is made at the detail it is drawn at and the next coarser it fades into, not every coarser one: within 32 m (36 kept) full and middle detail, to 70 m (76 kept) middle and far, beyond far and proxy, since the coarser is made again before it is seen. Past the middle detail a deck's plank, and at the proxy's distance an undressed log, is a plain box, and far off a band of dress is its profile's three furthest corners: they were most of a far building's triangles. Masks shade a corner's dirt only below its reach, and baking reads sRGB from a table. Measured by `cargo xtask sections` (D185's village, on one thread).

## Why

Building step 3's bar (docs/building-plan.md, checking it): a section laid and merged in under 2 ms on a worker; it took 3.5 to 5 ms, about 1 ms of it laying, mostly its building's roof and rows again. Now the median section is cut in 0.01 ms and merged in 1 to 2 ms near, 0.3 ms far, after its base is laid whole in about 2 ms; the village's far details went from 2.9 to 1.1 million triangles. Roofs laid as cubes (D226) will be laid by their neighbourhood and may make the whole-base laying moot; D281 lays a change again only as far as it reaches.
