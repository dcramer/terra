# D436: Creature body plans, and what is made another way

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: every creature with a skeleton is one of six body plans, biped, quadruped, avian, serpent, many-legged and swimmer, or a composite of one with chains grafted at a socket (the Worman a serpent with arms, the Seawish a swimmer stood on its tail with arms, the Drillhog a quadruped with spinning parts); slimes, swarms, masses of tentacles and shapeless monsters are made another way ([creature-rigs.md](../creature-rigs.md#1-body-plans-as-data-d436-d439)). Open: which plans the monsters need first, serpent or many-legged after the quadruped.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-22: "i want to have stable, very good predictrable pipeline that if you start generating some animals/monsters, we know they will generally be good." A pipeline is predictable only over bodies it knows; Spore made its creatures from a few labelled plans for the same reason. Which bodies the game's monsters take is a design call.

The developer did not object to this recommendation when he settled D437 on 2026-10-23; it stays Open until he says it is settled.
