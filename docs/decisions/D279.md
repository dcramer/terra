# D279: No open sky through the leaves of a forest, and its haze green in a glade

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Leaves take 0.05 of Bevy's own diffuse transmission (it was 0.15 to 0.35): it lights each leaf's far side from the open sky's environment map, unshaded by the crowns, and the light through a leaf is the foliage shader's own, from the sun and from the sky occlusion volume (D247, D272). The forest's haze stays its own until the sky over the eye is more than half open (it gave way from 0.4, D273), and under the trees is two thirds the green of light come through leaves rather than a third, the rest the horizon's.

## Why

Review of 2026-10-14: the middle distance among the veteran oaks "turns teal-grey", blue above green. Measured at the walk's 30 s: blue 0.334 against green 0.351 there, 0.310 against 0.322 after, red down more; with the volume's own light zeroed it was bluer still, so the blue came from what ignores the canopy.
