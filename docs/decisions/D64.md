# D64: Equipment

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

An equipment grid, a character sheet: slots for what the player wears and carries as gear. The standard slots: head, chest (which covers the arms), gloves, legs, feet, back and belt; more may be added later. Not every slot shows on the body: some belt items do, such as a quiver, and some don't. No layers for now: one piece per slot.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10; the slots and no layers on 2026-10-12. Jewellery is open: perhaps two rings and a necklace, or instead a few trinket slots, probably not shown. See [items.md](../items.md).
