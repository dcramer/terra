# D251: One material system for walls, floors and roofs, of hand-made pieces

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every kind, wall, floor or roof, is drawn by one system, as Enshrouded's materials are: its cubes' surface in its material's textures (D197), dressed by role (D215) with hand-made pieces modelled in Blender, or from Tripo and cleaned up there, not geometry generated by code. Roof coverings (their courses, ridges, hips, valleys, eaves and verges) and frames (wattle and daub's posts, plates and braces) become kits on that system, in place of the code that generates them now (D236, D241). A floor is the top of its kind's cubes, dressed by its role, so a floor of stone is stone flags and one of timber boards, in the same kits as walls. Generated logs (D200) are compared with hand-made log pieces on the same system and replaced where those look as good. Whether the terrain shares these materials is D252. Amended by D290: geometry the rules shape wearing a hand-made model's bake counts, beside pieces placed whole.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13, told roof coverings and frames were generated and never compared with hand-made pieces: "i think we want hand made models? enshrouded does this, we are doing what enshrouded does. ideally the same system used for roof materials (and terrain? idk) is whats also the wall material (and floor materials, which might be same as wall materials?)".
