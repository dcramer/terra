# D165: Monster cores

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Monsters leave cores. Absorbing them hurries your least-advanced powers, at the books' price: each makes practice count for less, and leaning on them makes crossing past gold harder. Cores are also crafts' components, a way to calm pools, and a trade good.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-12: "keep the cores". See [magic.md](../magic.md#raising-powers).
