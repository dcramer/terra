# D637: A player's body leaves the world when they disconnect

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

When a player disconnects, by choice or by a lost connection, their body leaves the world at once and their character is saved where it stood (D592); rejoining puts it back there. No sleeping body is left behind. For now: it may be revisited with the rest of the game's design.

## Why

The developer, 2026-11-06, asked by [netcode.md](../netcode.md) whether a body leaves or stays asleep: "Yes, players disappear when they disconnect for now."

## Log

- **2026-11-06**: D637 decided (by the developers).
