# D579: The game's title screen and its worlds

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The game opens on a title screen of its own, as shipped survival games do (Minecraft, Valheim, Enshrouded), and the launcher (D575) keeps only the build's choices. The title screen offers Continue (the world played last), Worlds (the list, each world played or deleted from it), New world, The yard, Settings and Quit. A player keeps **several worlds**: New world makes another, with a name and its mode, Survival or Creative (D494), chosen when it is made and kept with it, as Minecraft's game mode and Valheim's world modifiers are; it never replaces one. Deleting a world is its own action and asks first. No seed is offered: the land is one generated world for now, so a seed would choose nothing. [launcher.md](../launcher.md#the-title-screen-d579).

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-29, on the review desk, chose the option reading "Build a title screen with Continue / New game / Creative now; the launcher keeps only build choices." ("Now"). Then, 2026-10-30, on several worlds and on Creative: "a list is fine, creative would be per world i guess? how about you just consider how other games work". Minecraft's singleplayer list, Valheim's world list and Enshrouded's keep several worlds, each made with a name and its mode and deleted only after a confirmation; so does this.
