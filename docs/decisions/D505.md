# D505: What can be climbed

Area: The player and survival · Owner: Design · By: — · Status: Open

Recommended: every surface can be climbed, with a grip by material that scales the climb's cost and a few materials that cannot be held (ice, glass, plaster), so a base keeps climbers out by its materials and an overhang. Options: everything alike (Breath of the Wild, Conan Exiles, Palworld); by material, as recommended; or only marked faces, as Enshrouded's lattices ([controller.md](../controller.md#climbing), question 1).

## Why

D477 (the developers, 2026-10-25: "Free climb") leaves how climbing plays to be designed before it is built. The climbing section of [controller.md](../controller.md#climbing) works it out from shipped games (Breath of the Wild, Conan Exiles, Genshin, Palworld, Enshrouded) and asks this on the review desk, 2026-10-25; nothing is built on it until the developers answer.
