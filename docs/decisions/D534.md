# D534: The Cetri, a tree that walks

Area: Lore, magic, combat and growth · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A monster drawn by a developer's child ([creatures.md](../creatures.md#the-cetri)): the Cetri, a towering living oak, 6 to 8 m tall, that stands among the forest's trees as one of them, wakes and lunges at a player who comes close, then stalks after them slowly and heavily. Realistic and scary, keeping the drawing's features: a trunk of real oak bark and wood, a round crown of real oak foliage, two long branch arms ending in twig claws, narrow deep-set eyes that glow a dim yellow-amber from inside the bark only while it is awake, and a split in the trunk lined with jagged splintered-wood teeth. Asleep it is an ordinary tree: arms up as boughs, eyes dark, mouth shut.

## Why

The developer, 2026-10-26: "my kid invented ents from first principle, i want them to be real monster, slow and towering in the game. realistic and scary. model them and give me a small video of them". The drawing, titled "Cetri", says: "It disguises itself as a normal tree." and "If you come close it jumps at you."
