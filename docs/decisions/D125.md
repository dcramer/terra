# D125: Our own design, from many sources

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

The core mechanics of *He Who Fights With Monsters* and its approach to world-building can be used as they are (D87, D98). The specifics, such as which styles, disciplines, essences and peoples there are, we invent ourselves, drawing heavily on literature and game design for inspiration. The lore is written out in our own words in [lore.md](../lore.md): the books' fundamental systems of how the universe works, and their names for those systems (ranks, essences, confluences, awakening stones, spirit coins, quintessence, auras, the astral), but never their content: the actual lists of essences, abilities, stones, confluences, monsters, peoples, places and gods are ours.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11, of combat styles and the books; restated on 2026-10-12: "the core foundational lore will be from He Who Fights With Monsters, but we won't literally do item for item from those books. We'll just use its world building for the fundamental systems that exist in the universe", to be very clear in the design docs and written out in our own words. Clarified the same day: "what I mean by don't copy the book is the actual essences etc.; we will come up with our own lists".
