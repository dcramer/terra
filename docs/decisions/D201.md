# D201: Plans and building in the game

Area: Building · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

As step 4 of [building-plan.md](../building-plan.md) builds it: every change the hammer, a script or the remote makes to the player's base is a `terra_build` operation through one checked path (`build::act`), with the claim's square of columns and its budget of planned cubes (`builds.ron`), the cubes bodies stand in and items stand on, and what the builder carries; openings are planned cubes made open (`Op::Open`, `Op::Close`), so a doorway is in the plan and a door or window, a prop, is fitted into it once the wall round it is built; what goes in next is the lowest of a part not yet in, a course across the part for long members and the nearest cube that can go in for blocks, then roofs a stage at a time; fractions of an item are cut from whole ones and the rest kept as the claim's offcuts, given back as they make whole items; only walls built whole close a room in; the ground is sampled again after a room cuts it (`Op::Ground`), refused where it would undermine what is built; a base is saved as a snapshot and the operations since; a save of another version is refused (D353). The room shortcut lays its own parts only where nothing is, and no longer opens walls between rooms.

## Why

Mine, building step 4 on 2026-10-13: the choices the drawing and networking steps build on, so they are not left in code. Exact refunds need fractions kept somewhere; the claim's is the least a player sees.
