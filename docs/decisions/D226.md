# D226: Building by hand, roofs too

Area: Building · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Players build by hand as in Enshrouded: brushes of 1, 2 and 4 m, carving any box, taking out the cube aimed at, and roofs built by hand, cube by cube, so a roof can take any shape and storeys can be added over it; ridges, eaves, verges, hips and valleys form from the roof cubes' neighbours as walls' trims do (D215). The game's quick ways stay as conveniences: laying a room, a wall, and "add a roof with this material" over what is enclosed, which lays roof cubes like any other. All unlocked from the start for now, for testing. Amends D154, which chose the Sims' way, and changes how D209 lays roofs. Amended by D343: a storey may go up over a roof left on, or with it taken off, the roof whole round whatever stands on it.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-13: on brushes, "it doesnt matter, but for the sake of testing we'll just have it unlocked"; on roofs, "we want you to be able to customize the roof, or add more stories, which means you gotta be able to manually build the roof. the auto roof thing was just from the sims its not our goal (though we could have it as a behavior.. like you could 'auto add a roof with [x material]')".
