# D656: Today's pickaxe in hand is a one-handed pick, 0.55 m, its head 0.36 m across, until the two-handed pickaxe's takes are built

Area: Items and crafting · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

The stone and iron pickaxes' looks are a one-handed pick: 0.55 m overall, the head no more than about 0.36 m across, held at the butt (`art/held/stone_pickaxe.json`, `iron_pickaxe.json`). They are still the free pack's model, fitted: scaled to that length, and its head, 0.86 of its length across in the source, narrowed across the handle by the data's `head_scale` (0.76; `fit_held.py`, `haft.py`), the handle below it untouched so the grip is the same. D567 stands: the pickaxe as a kind is two-handed, and `assets/data/kinds/pickaxe.json` keeps the 0.9 m reference and envelope for it, with no item of that size in the game until its two-handed takes and the off hand's second grip are built. This is the size of the look while it is swung in one hand, not a change of the kind.

## Why

The developer, with a screenshot of the pickaxe in hand: "the pickaxe is still comicly large? why arent you fixing this". The looks were fitted to 0.90 m with a head 0.77 m across, a two-handed railway pick's size, and the game holds and swings every tool in the main hand alone (`pickaxe.json`'s `hands.open`), so a tool as long as the leg hung from one fist. Sized against one-handed picks (recalled, not looked up): a geologist's rock pick is about 0.33 m with a head near 0.18 m; a hand pick-mattock has a 0.38 to 0.45 m handle and a head of 0.28 to 0.35 m; a miner's hand pick 0.45 to 0.60 m; a full pick's head is about 0.65 m on a 0.9 m haft, 0.7 of its length, which at 0.55 m is 0.36 to 0.39 m. The same call as D653 made for the axe. Decided by the agent (the coordinator's technical stand-in, held-pickaxe j1); whether the pickaxe stays two-handed, and at what size once it is, is the developers' (D567).
