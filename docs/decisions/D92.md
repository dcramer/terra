# D92: No hand-holding

Area: The game as a whole · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

A principle of the game: it does not hold your hand. There may be quests, but not a tracked quest log that points you where to go, and no journal: what to remember is up to the player.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11 as "one principle of the game". Proposed: quests are what people tell you, directions come from landmarks, and the map shows what you have seen and marked; see [gameplay.md](../gameplay.md#no-hand-holding). Bears on the map (D76).
