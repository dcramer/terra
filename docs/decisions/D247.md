# D247: The lush forest's trees: a closed canopy, leaves lit through, bark in proportion

Area: Art, characters and sound · Owner: Tech · By: Agent · Status: Decided

Amends D235. **Canopy:** grown oaks stouter (trunks 3.8 to 4.6% of their height through) with crowns as wide as they are tall or wider, starting lower; each leans toward and reaches farther to a side it found light on, its great limbs unevenly up the trunk, the lowest one or two the greatest, near level and long; leaves that sag below 70% of the bole are shed. Young oaks are a sub-canopy of 8 to 12 m trees with wide low crowns, half as many again. Trees stand in clumps with gaps (spacing 0.65 to 1.45 times its own by broad noise), not evenly. **Leaves:** lit by the foliage shader's own light through them (`leaf` in the foliage uniform: brightest looking toward the sun, only where the sun reaches the leaf, less deep in the crown), Bevy's diffuse transmission cut from 0.55 to 0.25 so front-lit leaves keep their colour; their pictures greyed 15% and darkened 15%; the inside of a crown sees down to 0.3 of the sky and is darkened toward 0.3 in the sun's light too. The card atlas's colour is grown out over each card's edge and everything clear (alpha dilation from a pixel inside the edge, `textures.ts cards`), so filtering at a card's edge reads leaf rather than black or a pale rim. **Bark:** along a limb its picture repeats as often as round it at each ring, so it keeps its proportions as the limb thins (it was up to seven times stretched up thin branches); branches run out to a point rather than an open tube; relief deeper (0.05) and the normal map read 1.8 times as strong; moss grows on what faces up, in the bark's own furrows and toward the foot on its damp side, not in patches or a band; oaks in three barks (Bark Willow 02, Bark Willow at its true 1 m, Bark Brown 02), pines in two shades, spruces in two. **Conifers:** a Norway spruce's card is a cluster of dark needles on hidden twigs (a new picture), tinted to about sRGB 0.15 blue-green; its whorls apart in tiers, sagging along their length and turned up at their tips; its lowest fifth to third bare dead twigs. A pine leans and sweeps, its bole nearly a cylinder tapering in the crown, its crown fuller.

## Why

An adversarial review against Sons of the Forest (3/10): the canopy open, ordinary oaks spindly lollipops, leaves flat-lit stickers with a pale fringe, bark reading as fur, spruces teal ferns with orange midribs, pines even orange tubes; the developers: the trees "look mid even from close distance". Measured on the RTX 3080 on a shared machine: sky looking up from eye height 17.5% to 5.1% (D245); the walk bench 11.8 ms median (99th percentile 13.1) before, 12.0 (12.7) after; `lodcheck` and `collide` pass.
