# D94: Claims

Area: People and the tribe · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

You build within areas you claim, so the world is not destroyed everywhere and nobody has to manage it. A claim belongs to a tribe and is one of its outposts (D166); nothing regrows inside one by default (D150). How claims are placed, limited and kept up is proposed in [building.md](../building.md#claims); their size is D104.

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-11 ("will likely need"), and assumed by every later decision on outposts, regrowth and buildings (D150, D155, D166, D171). Technically, it bounds the world's stored changes to what players hold (D47, D52).
