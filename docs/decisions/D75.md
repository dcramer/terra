# D75: Sea level

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Developers · Status: Decided

Every world with water has a sea level, and the sea behaves as physics says it should: its surface is level, the same height everywhere on the world under that world's gravity, and water fills the ground below it wherever the sea can reach. How deep it is depends only on the ground beneath (D74).

## Why

Said by the developers on 2026-10-10. My reading of "as physics says": a hollow below sea level that the sea cannot reach stays dry, and buoyancy and pressure follow from depth. Flowing water is not planned. Replaces D24. See [worlds.md](../worlds.md#what-the-developers-want).
