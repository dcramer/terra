# D321: Biome demos: a mountain lake, a tundra, a living town

Area: Worlds and realms · Owner: Design · By: Agent · Status: Leaning

The next demos after the lush forest, as [demos.md](../demos.md) says, each made by the world's rules on the 30 km world in the start's climate, the sandbox choosing only the place, the date, the hour and the camera. **A mountain lake:** a lake in a valley whose forest gives out with height where the growing season is too cold for its trees, the tree line emerging from species' niches (D313), each tree shorter and sparser toward its limit and then krummholz, above it alpine meadow, scree, rock and snow by the date. **A tundra:** where the world's climate puts it, about 11 km north of the start, its ground cover five communities by exposure and wetness, the same cover as the alpine meadow. **A living town:** a market town of 30 to 40 buildings round a square, about ten roles of people keeping their days, and domestic animals. The open choices (heights, seasons, trees, the town, animals, voices) are demos.md's questions.

## Why

The developers on 2026-10-15: "I would like to see a forest with a lake in the mountains, i want mountains to have both foresty part and the high peaks. i would like to see more of a tundra effect, as a final effect i would like to see a town that is bustling with life - has NPCs, maybe some animals, etc." The specifics are the agent's recommendation, Leaning until they answer.
