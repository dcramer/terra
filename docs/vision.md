# Vision

## Pitch

Draft, rewritten on 2026-10-10 when the developers dropped space and, probably, terraforming.

Players survive and build across a dozen or so very different worlds, realms reached through portals or something like them (D72, D87), starting on one that is easy to live on. Each world has its own land, life, cultures and dangers (D59, D86). You grow as you go: many skills that you gain by doing things and improve by doing them more (D88). Worlds are in [worlds.md](worlds.md); surviving them is in [survival.md](survival.md).

The earlier pitch, of dead planets terraformed into living ones, is parked with terraforming (D60).

## Pillars

Draft. A feature that serves none of these is cut.

1. **The world is the main character.** Persistent worlds, each unlike the others, that visibly change because of you.
2. **Always getting better.** Many skills, gained by doing and improved by doing more, so there is always a sense of progression (D88). (Replaces "dead to alive", parked with terraforming, D60.)
3. **Automate survival.** Every chore starts manual and ends as infrastructure.
4. **Local action, global consequence.** You change a place; places add up to a planet.
5. **Seamless, and together.** No loading screens within a world. Played through a persistent server that holds up to a thousand players (D51, D52), with a few friends as the small case.

## What we take from each inspiration

| Game | We take | We leave |
|---|---|---|
| Planet Crafter | Phased terraforming; the world visibly transforming (parked, D60) | Handcrafted map; numbers that only go up; life as decoration |
| No Man's Sky | Procedural everything; a palette per planet | Infinite universe; shallow planets; space |
| Minecraft | Dig and build anywhere | Blocky cubes; flat world |
| Vintage Story | Survival systems with some depth | The punishing difficulty |
| Space Engineers | Voxel planets with separately built structures | Engineering-grade physics; space |
| Green Hell | The environment as the enemy | The intensity |
| SimEarth | A coupled planetary simulation | Abstract god-game view |
| Spore | Life arriving in stages, simple to complex | Creature editor |
| Factorio | Automation as the answer to threats; your own output provokes the enemy | 2D factory focus |
| Enshrouded | Probably one of our two biggest inspirations, with Subnautica (D85). Voxels done really well: ground you dig and reshape, and buildings of small voxel pieces that join and blend seamlessly. Its look and art. Its building, "top notch". Its combat and its movement. See [research/enshrouded.md](../research/enshrouded.md). | Not yet said. Its combat is fought by reflexes, where our threats are answered by building (D3) |
| *He Who Fights With Monsters* (books) | Realms rather than a solar system (D87); many skills that grow with use (D88); cultures to find (D86) | Not yet said |
| Subnautica | Probably one of our two biggest inspirations, with Enshrouded (D85). Base building (D48), the build menu (D67), the inventory (D84), tools you carry | Not yet said |

Astroneer is deliberately not on this list (D15). It shows the tech is achievable by a small team; its gameplay and art are not models for us.

## Tone

Slightly harder than Planet Crafter, well short of Vintage Story. Threats are real but are solved by building, not by reflexes. No fail state.

## Non-goals

- PvP.
- Photorealism.
- Handcrafted maps.
- A creature editor.
- Thousands of planets. About a dozen, each worth knowing (D17).

## Constraints

- Two developers.
- Every asset is free, bought or LLM-generated, and code is LLM-written. This pushes us to procedural content and a code-first stack.
