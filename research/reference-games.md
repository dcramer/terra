# Games to base game design 001 on

2026-10-11. Question: which existing games could [game design 001](../docs/proposals/game-design-001/README.md) be based on, so that its core mechanics are ones we already know are fun?

The developers want a base game: something to point at and say "that, but". No one game has everything in the proposal, so this compares the candidates against what the developers have asked for.

## What we are matching against

From the proposal and the decisions: a living world with NPCs, monsters and wildlife (D89); bases and outposts held with recruited defenders (D90), recruits found mostly by rescue or for helping a settlement; reasons to explore and settle more than one place (D91); skills gained and improved by doing (D88); no hand-holding and little on screen (D92, D93); building and combat like Enshrouded's (D85); a persistent world for many players (D52).

## The candidates

From what I know of each game, with a little checked against guides and wikis (sources below); nothing here was played for this note.

| Game | What it has that we want | What it lacks |
|---|---|---|
| **Enshrouded** | The 30-second loop the developers already like: movement, combat, voxel building and digging, the look. Survivors you rescue come to live at your base as crafters. Building only inside an altar's radius, and fast travel between places. A spreading threat, the Shroud. | As far as I know, bases are never attacked and the world does not go on without you: camps respawn, nothing moves. Skills come from a points tree, not from doing. Small groups, not a persistent world. |
| **Soulmask** | Closest to the hold-your-ground loop. You recruit people (by knocking them down and winning them over), they work and fight for you, and raids come when your activity at a base builds a "fever" meter. Towers need a garrison of recruits to fire. Work outposts at resources draw raiders. A lit bonfire keeps structures from decaying. | Recruiting by force rather than rescue. A tribal setting. Not known for its building or combat feel. |
| **Conan Exiles** | Recruits who defend (thralls), and attacks on your base (purges) that test what you have built. | Recruiting by capture. Purges are tied to wealth deposited, not to a world going on. |
| **Kenshi** | The most living world of the lot: factions, caravans and raids happen without you; you recruit people and found outposts that get attacked; skills rise by doing; nothing holds your hand; no fail state. | Combat and building have little feel; it is single-player. |
| **Valheim** | Skills that rise by doing. Biomes whose materials gate progress, which is what makes you explore. Player-built portals. Raids on your base. Vague guidance and nothing marked. | No NPCs at all. |
| **Outward** | No hand-holding taken furthest: no quest markers, your position not shown on the map, a backpack you carry. Defeat is a setback, not an end. | Little building; small, hand-made world. |
| **Wurm Online** and **Ultima Online** | A persistent world for many players, skills by doing (Wurm has well over a hundred), settlements with land you hold. Ultima Online also tried a living ecology at launch, which reportedly did not survive players killing everything: a warning for D89 at a thousand players. | Dated feel; little of Enshrouded's moment-to-moment. |

## A suggested base

**Enshrouded for how it plays, Soulmask for what you are doing.** Enshrouded gives the 30-second loop and the 10-minute loop: move, fight, gather, build, rescue people who come home with you. Soulmask gives the session loop the proposal is missing a model for: recruits who work and defend, outposts at resources, and attacks provoked by what you do. Then take one thing each from the others:

- **Kenshi**: the world going on without you, which is the part none of the others has and the riskiest to build.
- **Valheim**: skills by doing, biomes that gate progress, player-built portals.
- **Outward**: how far no hand-holding can go and still be fun.
- **Ultima Online**: what happens to a living world when many players hunt it.

The one-line pitch that would follow: *Enshrouded's world and fighting, where the people you rescue help you hold outposts against a world that is going on without you.*

## Limits

None of these games was played for this note; the summaries are from memory, with the Enshrouded, Soulmask and Conan Exiles details checked against community guides and wikis, which disagree in places and describe versions that change. Whether a game's mechanics are fun for us is something only playing it shows: the developers playing a few hours of Soulmask and watching some Kenshi would test the suggestion faster than any amount of reading.
