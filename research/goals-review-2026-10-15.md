# Building against the developers' goals: the third review

2026-10-15. Question: does what landed in building since the second review ([goals-review-2026-10-14.md](goals-review-2026-10-14.md), at 256eea5) match what the developers asked for: Enshrouded's voxel approach (D211), building by hand (D226), the cube as the truth (D192), the art guide (D14), one material system of hand-made pieces (D251), and buildings by the hundred at a playable frame (D185)? Whether it works is secondary.

The developers' standing rule: "you should always have an agent review your work against our original goals because i know we've gotten off track sometimes and moved away from the enshrouded-style voxel based approach".

How it was done: the 18 commits from 256eea5 to d7561be touching `terra_build`, `terra_game/src/build` and `play`, the kits, `art/kits`, `tools/kits`, `tools/textures`, `xtask`, `docs` and `research` (D258's modelled pieces, D262, D277, D278, D280, D281, brick's cost, the mesh checker's fixes, the scale and edit numbers); the code itself (`modules::lay` and `fit`, `Base::in_wall`, `put_up.rs`, `village::ByHand`, `sections::reach`, `Base::raycast_where`, `build/plans.rs`, `inspect.rs`, `layout/frames.rs`), each kit's `pieces.json` and the eleven Tripo models under `art/kits/`; `research/building-scale.md` and `benchmarks.csv`; the latest shots (the modelled pieces street, gable, dormer and wattle views, before and after; the logs after D262; the town300 street, rise and far views; the cabin and thatch apex renders), and the mesh checker (`terra_build`'s `artifacts` example, `--all`, what `cargo xtask artifacts` runs) afresh at d7561be. The Enshrouded reference folder named in the brief was not in the scratchpad, so Enshrouded is judged from [voxel-building-look.md](voxel-building-look.md) and the first two reviews. No fresh pictures were taken: two other agents held the Windows sandbox throughout, and the newest shots (09:03) postdate the last building commit. Load on the machine was 15 on 32 cores. Ranks: **drift** (away from a goal, or a developers' call taken by an agent), **defect** (meant to work, does not), **polish**.

## The short answer

The second review was answered fast, and mostly at the root. D251 has started to be what the developers asked: every roof's visible edges and wattle's frame are now Tripo models baked into each kit's atlas, read by `tools/kits` in place of numbers, and the docs say plainly which pieces are still made by script. The village, town and castle go up through the checked operations, two buildings are built a cube at a time, an edit lays only what it reaches, a walkway draws alike at any layer, and the logs and earth read better. What is left is in how the modelled pieces are laid (squeezed, never varied, the frame buried in its daub until the relief was turned back down), in a checker that finds thirty thousand faults and gates none, in a street walk whose worst frames doubled while its median improved, and in looks the developers have not seen: the concepts every new piece was made from were judged by an agent, and the whole modelled log D200 waits on is made but not shown.

## Answers to the questions asked

1. **The second review's findings.** Fixed properly: 3 (the demo through the operations, two built by hand), 6 (wall or floor from the cubes), and 7's logs, earth and strays. Fixed in scope but short of measured: 4 and 5. Fixed in part: 1 and 2 (the pieces are modelled, the developers have not seen them), 8 and 9. Papered over: the wattle frame's dashes (finding 3) and the thatch apex's ring.
2. **D251 as built.** Closer to what the developers asked than at the last review: a roof's covering is its cubes' surface in its material, and its ridges, hips, verges, eaves, valleys and flashing are Tripo models cleaned and baked in Blender, as D251 allows; wattle's frame likewise. That is Enshrouded's way. What is still scripted is acceptable where it is hidden or plain (rafters, battens, ridge beams under the covering) and where the developers chose it (D200's logs, with the comparison pending). It is drift if it stays for what is seen at every corner: stone's and earth's lintels, sills, copings, plinths, quoins and jambs, whose next step should be models. At the bar: better than what they replaced close up (the verge is a real bound bundle, the bargeboards real boards, the frame adzed oak), worse along a line (identical copies, squeezed members, a ridge in segments) and, on shakes and slate, too pale.
3. **New drift from Enshrouded's approach.** None in the store: what is wall, rooms, door steps and far hulls are all worked out from the cubes, D278's hull is the cubes' own surface, and the village is now what the operations built. Building by hand is shown in the demo. The one new gap is a course laid by hand on the ground drawn as a floor (finding 7), and blocks by hand get no footings where the quick tools do.
4. **Looks against D14.** Finding 8. From the street the village reads as a real place; what reads cheap is close up along lines (repeats), the pale trim on shakes and slate, and earth's flat walls.
5. **Performance.** Measured with before and after: D278 far off (`far` 859 to 253 MiB of meshes, 20.9 to 1.8 s to all on screen); the town from the rise on the Radeon (80.9 to 36.2 ms); the castle edit's reach (342 to 198 sections, 337 to 270 ms on a worker); `terrain::choose` moved off the main thread; `going-up` edit to screen 17.3 ms. Over the bar or unmeasured: the street walk (72.5 ms at the 99th percentile on the 5090, 64.3 ms median on the Radeon), the castle on the Radeon (67 ms), the castle edit to the screen, `act` at 0.13 to 0.19 ms, the hammer's aim, and stone's 300,000 triangles a house. Every number was taken on a shared machine; the quietest (load 8 to 13) is the one whose worst frames doubled.
6. **Decisions and docs.** Findings 1, 9 and 10. No decision cited is missing from the table; D277 to D281 are well formed and correctly Tech, except brick's cost.

## What is on track, and should stay

- The cube store, checked operations with inverses, parts that merge, and now the village, town and castle put up through them, with a test.
- Wall or floor, rooms, doorways and door steps from the cubes alone; the walkway drawn alike at any layer.
- One material system as Enshrouded's: a covering as the cubes' displaced surface, its edges modelled pieces baked into one atlas a kit; a pipeline (`tools/kits`) that takes a model in place of numbers role by role.
- An edit laying only what it reaches, tested equal to laying whole, cut off the main thread.
- Far bases as their cubes' hull, merged by cell to the horizon and streamed by distance, with memory counted per base: the far view went from 859 to 253 MiB.
- Tools that measure instead of eyes: `cargo xtask artifacts`, `lodcheck` with a town view, `going-up` and `town300` in `benchmarks.csv` on both GPUs, a system-by-system trace of the street walk.
- Brick as a kit file and a material, and the first two buildings in the demo built a cube at a time.
