# Building against the developers' goals: the second review

2026-10-14. Question: does what landed in building since the first review ([goals-review-2026-10-13.md](goals-review-2026-10-13.md), at dca0111) match what the developers asked for: Enshrouded's voxel approach (D211), building by hand (D226), the cube as the truth (D192), the art guide (D14), one material system of hand-made pieces (D251), and buildings by the hundred (D185)? Whether it works is secondary.

The developers' standing rule: "you should always have an agent review your work against our original goals because i know we've gotten off track sometimes and moved away from the enshrouded-style voxel based approach".

How it was done: every commit from dca0111 to 4171373 touching `terra_build`, `terra_game/src/build`, the kits, `tools/kits`, `tools/textures`, `xtask`, `docs` and `research` (52, D251 to D267); the code itself (`sections::affected` and `Whole::lay_again`, `Base::work_out` and `in_wall`, `layout::cell`, `parts`, `rooms`, `ground::door_steps`, `roofing`, `village`, `build/layout.rs`, `tools/kits/pieces.py`, `art/kits/*/pieces.json`, the kit files); the audit and the scale note; the latest shots (gable ends in every wall and roof kind, logs before and after D262 and the hand-made log module beside them, earth before and after its relief), and fresh ones of the thatch rows of the kit sheet on the Windows build. The machine was at a load of 35 to 60 throughout, so no frame time here, ours or the notes', is a clean measurement. Severity: **drift** (away from a goal), **defect** (meant to work, does not), **polish**.

## The short answer

The first review's defects were mostly fixed properly, at the cubes and with tests: parts merge (D260), rooms and doorways from the cubes in 3D and door steps as stair cubes (D261), thick log walls as skins (D263), Block first in the hammer (D253), claims kept apart (D265), and brick came in as data only. The new drift is in D251 itself. The developers asked for "hand made models" in place of geometry made by code. What was built is the same idea moved into Blender: `tools/kits/pieces.py` makes every ridge, verge, eave, frame member and the "hand-made" log from numbers in a JSON file. Nothing is modelled by hand, and the log comparison D262 settled was between two generators. Meanwhile the village is still put up by the shortcuts, an edit still lays its whole connected building again, and none of the performance work has numbers taken after it, except the flora fix.

## Answers to the questions asked

1. **The first review's findings.** Defects 3, 4 and 6 and drift 8 were fixed at the root, with tests. Drift 1 was fixed only for separate buildings. Defect 5 was fixed for rooms but not for drawing. Drift 2 was not touched. Drift 7 has its before numbers only.
2. **D251 as built.** One system, yes: walls, floors and roofs are each a kind's cubes' surface in its material, dressed by role, with roof coverings as the roof cubes' displaced surface and their edges and frames as modules. The generated courses, caps and boards are gone (928093f), and every dressing piece is of the kind's own raw materials, which `cargo xtask kits` checks (5489fc6). Hand-made, no (findings 1 and 2). Covering and frame geometry is still generated by code: in Python (`pieces.py`) rather than in Rust, and stone pillars as drums and decks, treads and planks in `terra_build::timber`, which D200 covers. Worse than what it replaced: no worse in the shots seen. The gable ends are better, cut straight with no stubs (e301f58). The thatch covering reads flatter from a distance than its old courses.
3. **New drift from Enshrouded's approach.** No new state outside the cubes: door steps came back into them, and claims and rooms are worked out from them. The whole-building reach of an edit is the remaining whole-thing special case (finding 4). Storey bands in drawing (finding 6) and the shortcuts on the demo's path (finding 3) are the old ones.
4. **A new material as data.** Yes. Brick is `assets/data/kits/brick.ron` and a fetched CC0 material. The one code change in a86677d made the kit sheet list every kit file instead of naming earth and wattle, which is a correction toward D136. Two caveats: it borrows stone's pieces (realistic for brick with stone dressings) and costs Stone, since there is no brick item yet.
5. **Looks.** Finding 7. The logs' pale patches do read as blotchy camouflage from the street, but not close up.
6. **Performance.** The flora freeze on make_way was fixed with a measurement (208 to 231 ms down to 58.9 ms worst, under load). The edit hitch was cut in scope but not measured since. The CPU time per frame in the village has not been explained or worked on (findings 4 and 5).
7. **Decisions.** Finding 9, and finding 8 for the stale rows. No missing decision numbers.

## What is on track, and should stay

- The cube store, operations with inverses, and property tests, now with parts that merge and split as cubes are laid by hand (D260). Building by hand no longer runs into a ceiling.
- Rooms, doorways and door steps worked out from the cubes alone (D261). Nothing outside the cubes collides.
- One material system in the sense Enshrouded has one: a surface in the material's textures, dressing by role, and roof coverings displaced by their height maps with modelled edges and frames along the lines. Each piece is of its kind's raw materials, checked by `cargo xtask kits`.
- New materials as data: brick, and lime-washed earth.
- Gables cut straight to the roof's deck, and lintels and sills bedded into the wall.
- `cargo xtask rules` and `cargo xtask artifacts`: the rules, the drawing's junk and an edit's reach checked by tool, not by eye (D71, D257).
- Scale benches for the 300 buildings the developers asked for, a castle and far bases, with before numbers on both GPUs.
- The flora fix: found by timing system by system, and measured after.

## Limits

Judged from code, commit messages, the notes' own numbers and stills, by an agent. Nothing was played, and every live number was taken on a heavily loaded machine. The fresh shots are of the kit sheet's thatch rows on the yard, not the village. The pale boards and the apex ring were seen in pictures and not traced to their pieces. D258's roofs and frames are still in progress, so finding 7's thatch may change.
