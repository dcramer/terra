# Subnautica's inventory

2026-10-10. Question: how do items and containers work in Subnautica, which the developers named as the baseline for ours (D84)?

## What it does

From memory of the first game and community sources; the fan wikis would not load, so the details are second-hand. Subnautica 2 is in early access and still changing.

- **The inventory is a grid, and items take up shape in it.** In the first game it is six wide and eight tall. Most things fill one cell; some tools, tanks and large fish fill more. Picking something up puts it in the first place it fits.
- **Nothing stacks.** Ten titanium is ten cells.
- **No weight.** The only limit is room in the grid.
- **Equipment is a set of slots by kind**: head, body, hands, feet, tank, and chips. Gear in a slot does its job while worn.
- **The quick slots point into the inventory.** There are five. Putting a tool there does not take it out of the inventory; drop the tool and the slot empties.
- **Storage is more grids**, in lockers of several sizes, built in a base or a vehicle. Moving between them is the same as moving within the inventory.
- **Dying costs what you picked up since you were last safe.** In the first game, items gathered since you last entered your base, lifepod or a vehicle are dropped, and the rest is kept; you respawn at the last safe place. A guide to Subnautica 2 says you drop what you were carrying where you died and swim back for it, and that the starting inventory is small (about 20 cells), grown by upgrades.

## Limits

Nothing here was checked in the game. Item sizes are not listed because I could not confirm them. Subnautica 2's rules may differ from the first game's in ways not covered by the guides.
