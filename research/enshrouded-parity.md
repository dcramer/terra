# Building against Enshrouded's, feature by feature

2026-10-13. Question: feature by feature, how does Enshrouded's building compare with ours as built today, and what closes each gap on our cube store, operations and layouts without rebuilding them?

The developers: "im pretty confident that our voxel system isnt good yet like enshroudeds tho?" The bar is D211: an approach to voxels like Enshrouded's, on par with it in how buildings blend, not a copy of its look. This note audits the whole system against that, not only the look, which [voxel-building-look.md](voxel-building-look.md) and [role-pieces.md](role-pieces.md) covered.

How it was done: Enshrouded from the official wiki, Keen's patch notes (all 119 Steam news posts from May 2023 to today, read through [Steam's news API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=1203620&count=400&maxlength=0); cited below as "Steam news" with their date, all listed at [Enshrouded's news page](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1203620)), Keen's talks, and guides, each marked **Keen** (Keen said it, or the official wiki lists it), **guides** (community guides and players, not checked in the game) or **inferred**. Ours read from the code on `main` at 8d0768d (`terra_build`, `terra_game::build`, `terra_game::play::tool`, `assets/data/builds.ron`, the kit files), not from the docs, where the two differ. Nothing was played or measured for this note. Enshrouded leaves early access on 15 October 2026; its 1.0 notes are not out, so this is the early-access game.

## The short answer

The look is closer than the developers think, and the tools are further. Since D215, a stone or log wall's corners, tops, feet and openings are dressed by their role, which is Enshrouded's method; what is missing there is variety (three kits against Enshrouded's 53 block materials), per-voxel decoration, and cost on the integrated GPU. What makes ours *feel* unlike Enshrouded's voxels is what the player can do with them: the hammer lays only logs, with no material picker; it places one cube or draws walls, floors and rooms, but has no brushes, no carving by hand, no taking out of the cube aimed at, and no terrain tools; and there are five shapes against Enshrouded's thirty-odd. Most of that is a shortfall, not a design choice, and all of it but the shape set sits on the cube store and operations as they are.

## Since (2026-10-14)

The tables below are as the code was at 8d0768d. Since, from the ranked gaps: 1, the material in hand on Ctrl and the wheel and picked from a cube, and earth, wattle and daub and brick beside logs, stone and hewn timber (D227, D232, D236); 2, brushes of 0.5 to 4 m, carving a box and taking out the cube aimed at (D226, D233), with *Block* first in the hammer and in hand (D253), and parts that merge as cubes are laid by hand, so a section never runs out of them (D260); 4, 32 shapes, the corner slopes, upper half and inverted ones among them (D231); 5, changing a material in place (D232); 6, a cube's edit timed to the screen (D257), and a change laying again only the buildings it reached; 8, roofs as cubes built by hand, "add a roof" laying the same cubes (D226, D241). Walls, floors and roofs are drawn on one material system of hand-made pieces (D251, D258); rooms and door steps come from the cubes alone (D261), as does what is wall and what floor (D277). Still open: terrain tools (3), painted decoration (7), saved designs (9) and a building camera (10). The last line of the ranking is reversed: roofs are no longer made by the game but built (D226).

## Shapes

| | Enshrouded | Ours today | Choice or shortfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| The set | Per material: single block, cube, walls of three widths, columns, ceilings, stairs, stepped and inverted stepped walls, door and window frames, roof side, inner and outer corner, peak, ridge and ridge end, flat roof (**Keen**, [wiki: Building](https://enshrouded.wiki.gg/wiki/Building)). | Arches, cylinders, round | Not as voxels: round doors and windows (Hollow Halls) and spiral stairs (Forging the Path) are props (**Keen**, Steam news); an arch and a Greek column came with Wake of the Water, whether as props is not said (**guides**, [GamesFuze](https://gamesfuze.com/locations/enshrouded-how-to-get-the-new-arch-and-greek-column-water-update/)). Players ask for arches and curves, "you end with some sawtooth things full of corners" (**guides**, [feature board](https://enshrouded.featureupvote.com/suggestions/524026/add-arches-curved-building-pieces-ornate-arches-etc)). Stone window heads read as round from the stones round them (**guides**, [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1203620/discussions/0/4339851902697489714)) | None; diagonal and round walls deferred (D146). Our lintels are straight | Even. An arched head as a dressing of the head role would pass Enshrouded at no cost to the cube |
| Decorative pieces | Free-placed props without collision, thinner than a voxel (**Keen**, water talk) | Doors, windows, shutters fitted into holes (D201); furniture and props as items | Even in kind; fewer in number |

**Where the cube changes.** A shape is 4 bits of the 32-bit cube, codes 0 to 10, 14 and 15 in use (`cubes.rs`, `pack`), so three are free. Inner and outer corner slopes need eight codes, an upper half one, inverted slopes four. Two ways, both a repack, neither a rebuild: take a bit from `pending`, which has 3 bits for 3 values, making the shape 5 bits (32 codes); or give each section its own palette of shapes, as it has of parts. Either way the snapshot format gains a version and `save.rs` converts older saves, as it already does, and `Shape::top` (walking), the surface's cut (`surface::draw_cut`), the roles and the stair and shape layouts each learn the new shapes. The operations do not change: `SetShape` already takes any `Shape`.

| | Enshrouded | Ours today | Choice or shortfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| How placed | By hand, per voxel (a 0.5 m roof block) or as 2 and 4 m roof pieces: side, inner and outer corners, peak, ridge, ridge end, flat (**Keen**, wiki) | A section is a rectangle with a style (gable, hip, lean-to, flat) and a pitch, laid over walls or drawn; a storey's roof is the highest of its sections at each point (D209, `layout/roofs`) | A **choice** (D154): the game makes the roof |
| Ridges and eaves | No automatic roof: the player places ridge and corner pieces (**Keen**, wiki). Roof is a voxel material: a wall's top course of roof blocks merges into the roof, and eaves form where a wall's top meets it (**guides**, [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1203620/discussions/0/4206994473254518044/)); the single roof block rounds into a mound and hips and valleys follow (**inferred**, [roof gallery](https://enshrouded.wiki.gg/images/RoofTilesGallery.png)). | What is missing | Free composition: dormers, turrets, mixed pitches, roofs on roofs | Mixed pitches in one roof, dormers, curved and conical roofs deferred (building-tech.md); no roof placed a cell at a time | **Shortfall** for advanced builders, deferred by design; placing details by hand comes "with progress" (D154). Roofs do not touch the cube: they are sections and stages stored beside it (`Op::AddRoof`, `Op::RoofStage`). A hand-placed roof would be new section kinds, or roof cubes with slope shapes feeding the same height field; both additive.

Filling is an existing terrain operation given a tool; flattening is one new `terra_core::Operation` (setting the density to a plane over a footprint, as Voxel Tools' flatten does, D196), followed by `Op::Ground` for any base it reaches, which already refuses a change that would leave something unjoined.

## Multiplayer and persistence

Enshrouded: up to 16 players a world; permission groups with passwords say who may take from containers, build and remove blocks or props in a base, terraform, and add or upgrade altars (**Keen**, Melodies of the Mire, Steam news 2024-06-05); the server is authoritative, terraforming predicted on the client (Steam news 2025-05-13); voxels are compressed on disc (**Keen**, Souls of the Frozen Frontier). Since Forging the Path a world can be published for others to visit, and 1.0 adds Sparkworks, wiring levers and plates to doors and traps (Steam news 2026-04-21, 2026-10-08). Ours: every change is an operation checked by one pure function, in a server's order (D21, D201), saved as a snapshot plus the operations since (`save.rs`); `terra_net` is not started, and nobody else can be allowed into a claim yet. Nothing in building waits on it, and the operations are the right shape to send. Not a gap in how building feels today.

## The gaps, ranked

By how much each moves the game toward the developers' sense of Enshrouded's voxels, most first. Sizes are rough, for one agent.

1. **Choosing the material, and more of them.** Shortfall. A Ctrl-and-wheel kind picker in the hammer, the kind held per builder rather than the one `BuildLook::laying`, roofs taking their kind from the walls as now; middle mouse, which today picks only the part aimed at, picking its kind too, as Enshrouded's material sampling does. Then kits as data: a refined stone (the module fill planned for 2a), a brick or plaster, a planked timber, wattle and daub, each a kit file, its materials from the library and its dress from `tools/kits`. Picker: two or three days. Each kit: two to five days. No cube change. Which kinds, in what order, is **design, Open** (building.md question 1; recommended there: wattle and daub, logs, planks, stone).
2. **Brushes, carving and taking out what you aim at.** Partly a choice (D154: draw the shape) and partly a shortfall (brushes and carving are already in the order, step 8). A brush mode in the hammer: a box of 1, 2 or 4 m (2, 4 or 8 cubes) or a dragged box, laid as one `LayPlan` in the kind in hand; a carve mode marking any box of built cubes with `Op::Carve`, so the roles dress the hole; and, in take-apart mode, the cube aimed at when nothing rests on it (`support` already answers that), else the last-in order as now. About a week. No cube or operation change. Recommend **Open**: brushes and carving from the start alongside drawing, rather than as a later unlock, since they are what makes building feel like voxels.
3. **Terrain tools.** Shortfall. Fill from a terrain tab in the hammer with the existing `Operation::Fill` (shapes as Enshrouded's: cube, ramp, disc, box); a flatten tool as a new `terra_core` operation ported from Voxel Tools' (D196); each followed by `Op::Ground` for the base it reaches. Then grass allowed up against a building's foot (`terra_world::cover` reading the claim's buildings). About a week and a half. No cube change. Whether ground changed outside a claim heals is **design, Open**.
4. **More shapes.** Shortfall. Inner and outer corner slopes, an upper half and inverted slopes, then a stepped wall; each walked (`Shape::top`), cut in the surface, given roles and dressed in every kit. The one item that touches the stored cube: the shape widened to 5 bits from `pending`'s spare bit, with a snapshot version and conversion. One to two weeks. An arched head over openings, as a dressing of the head role, is a day or two and needs no shape.
5. **Mixing and replacing materials in place.** Shortfall. A repaint mode that sends `Op::Replace` over the cubes aimed at or brushed, built through the existing mark, swap and fill order; a general rule for which of two kinds runs through at a joint (a weight per kit, generalising D179's order), and meet trims for each pair from the kits rather than for logs on stone only. Three to five days. No cube change.
6. **Edit latency and the integrated GPU.** Shortfall against D211's "optimised cubes" and D185. Measure first: a bench step timing a click to its mesh on screen, for a cabin and a large hall. Then lay again only the sections a change's layout reaches, rather than the whole building, where the kit allows (runs and roofs are what reach across), and the steps building-drawing.md lists for the integrated GPU (fewer far shadow casters, impostors past 180 m, a cheaper near surface). One to three weeks. No cube change.
7. **Painted decoration.** Shortfall against Enshrouded; ours already weathers by rule. A sparse table per section and a `Decorate` operation, read by the masks in `dress` and `surface` as a weight beside the kit's own. About a week. Not the 32-bit cube. Whether players paint wear and overgrowth is **design, Open**.
8. **Roofs by hand.** A choice for now (D154, D209), deferred; not a shortfall for most players. Sections of their own pitch and height, and dormers as a section standing on a wall, are additive to the height field. One to two weeks. **Open**, recommended after 1 to 5.
9. **Saved designs and copy.** Not a gap: Enshrouded has none, so this is where ours can pass it. A design is the operations of a selection, moved, turned and mirrored (facings are four, so a mirror is a remap), laid again as plans that cost nothing (D139).
