# The lighting system: is the way we are building it correct

2026-11-09. The question, the developer's, today, after a den's light volume in the camera's picture put out the sky's light on the land (D485's amendment): "step back and make sure the way we're implementing the lighting system is correct". It informs D270, D273, D310, D336, D338, D485 and a lighting design doc that does not exist yet.

How it was done: three read-only surveys of the code (the sun, shadows and exposure; the sky's light and the probes; the air, fog, local lights and checks), each reporting mechanisms with their lines, then judged against shipped practice. The practice is from memory, not searched today: Unreal's sky light, reflection captures, volumetric lightmap and auto exposure; Frostbite's "Moving Frostbite to PBR" (Lagarde, de Rousiers 2014); Valve's ambient cube (Mitchell 2006); Hillaire's atmosphere (2020). Not read: `terra_iso`'s own lights, the sky's shaders, the deferred and SSR paths, Bevy's clustering of probes. Not run: nothing here was measured except what the fix's commit measured.

## The answer

1. **The parts are the industry's.** One shadowed directional light, a painted sky as an environment map (harmonics for diffuse, filtered for reflection), irradiance volumes of ambient cubes where the sky is hidden, a reflection probe a room with the smaller probe winning (D485), a physical atmosphere, a clamped metered eye. That is Unreal's sky light, captures and volumetric lightmap in small. Nothing here needs replacing with another technique.
2. **The game the developers play is not the one the light was tuned in.** The forest's sky volume, and with it `OpenOverhead` and every rule that reads it (the wooded night's 3.5 stops, the open air's haze, the reflection's dimming), exists only in the `lush` experiment (`lush.rs` inserts `SkyOccluders`; nothing in `play` does). In play the woods hide no sky, and the only volume is a den's. D270, D273, D286, D310 and D336 were measured in a scene play does not run. This is the largest fault, and why today's bug was found by playing and by no check.
3. **Three things are worked out once for the camera that belong to each fragment.** That is the family today's bug was in: what lights a surface changes with where the camera is or faces.
   - The environment map's brightness, diffuse and reflection, is scaled by how much sky is open round the *eye* (`skylight.rs` `sky_reflected(around²)`): with the camera under the crowns, open land outside the volume dims with it. Its comment says so.
   - Which shader runs flips with the frustum (`IRRADIANCE_VOLUME` is a view's, set when a volume is in its picture). Today's patch made the two agree for diffuse light; `sky_through` and the snowed trunks still differ.
   - A volume's edge is a hard step between two sources that do not match (the volume at full brightness, the map at brightness × around²), with no blend (`falloff` 0).
4. **The reflection is not occluded where the diffuse light is**, except on foliage. In a den the diffuse light is the volume's and the reflection is the whole open sky: the lilac sheen that greyed the player today is what skin, leather and stone get in a cave. Shipped engines derive the reflection's occlusion from the diffuse light's, per fragment (Frostbite's specular occlusion; Unreal's sky light occlusion). `foliage_fragment.wesl` `sky_specular` already does it; it belongs in the patch, for every material.
5. **The exposure is two systems doing one job, joined by hand.** A metered eye held to +0.75/-1 stops (D214, D286), and under it a scripted eye of rules by place (canopy, open air, snow, deck, room) written to the camera every frame. The canopy's rule, up to 3.5 stops, is not eased: it is instant as the camera crosses a cell, and a step when the bake arrives. Shipped games give the meter a range by context (Unreal's min and max EV, a compensation curve) and one speed, or script the target and ease it in one place. Ours should be the second, since the rules exist: one target, one filter.
6. **Thirteen constants exist to cancel another system**, most because Bevy mixes fog and the painted sky in after the exposure: the haze by `exp2(wooded)`, the dusk sky by `exp2(key - set)`, the deck's veil by `1/2^ev`, the fire by its stops, the held sun against the air's sun gain. The haze's cancels only one of the five things that move the eye. Frostbite's rule is that everything is scene-referred and exposed once; the fog's colour multiplied by `view.exposure` in our patch of `bevy_pbr` removes the pairs rather than adding the next.
7. **The sun hands over to the moon by a jump.** One directional light: above -1 degree the sun, at about 11,700 lux; below, the moon, at 0 until -8 degrees. The direction and the illuminance step in one frame, hidden only if the air's transmittance has already put the sun out there. Not verified. Fade to nothing, then turn.
8. **The keys are not physical, and that is fine.** Day is EV 11.8 with 32,000 lux, night EV 9 with 210: a compressed range tuned to reference frames, as shipped games compress it. What costs is that a key carries both a light and an exposure for one result, so each tuning moves two numbers; and "night" is five different ramps of the sun's height (`-12..0`, `-12..-4`, `-3..3`, `-1..6`, `-11..-3`).
9. **The checks hold stills at 13:00.** Looks hold lush, lake, tundra and town at 13 and two far views at 8; no place is held at night, none through a day, none across a forest's edge, none while moving. `turnlight` (today) is the only check that a surface's light does not depend on the camera, at one place and hour. The measuring itself (numbers against reference frames, bars proven by a planted fault) is ahead of practice; it is aimed at how a still looks, not at whether the light is continuous.
10. **There is no lighting design.** About sixty decisions and the comments in `daylight.rs`, `eye.rs` and `keys.rs` are the design; `docs/engine.md` has one row. Nobody can read what the rules are meant to be, so nobody can say which is broken.

## What to change, in order

Each is technical (D389) and within the decisions unless said.

1. **Sky visibility that follows the camera, in play** (changes D270's one volume over a known forest). A clipmap of the same ambient cubes round the camera, one or two levels (say 4 m cells to 150 m, 16 m to 600 m), filled from the world's own trees and ground as they stream, so every near fragment is inside it, there is no edge near the eye, and play has what lush has. Unreal streams its volumetric lightmap by bricks; a camera clipmap is what dynamic worlds use (DDGI's cascades). The den's and rooms' probes stay as they are, smaller and winning. Until it exists, the forest rules are untested in play.
2. **Per fragment, not per camera**: the reflection's occlusion from the volume's light against the open sky's, in `pbr_functions.wesl` for every material (generalising `sky_specular`); then `sky_reflected` goes, and the map's brightness is the open sky's everywhere. Volumes take a falloff, and the patch blends the remaining weight with the map, as it already does for reflection probes.
3. **One eased eye**: `shine` computes a target EV and one filter eases it (an eye's seconds, faster to darken), covering canopy, snow and the bake's arrival; the metered band stays on top.
4. **Fog and the painted sky exposed with the scene**, in the patch; the compensating constants then deleted one at a time against the looks.
5. **The handover**: the sun faded to nothing before the light turns to the moon; a test over the heights -3 to 0 that the light's illuminance times the air's transmittance has no step.
6. **Checks for continuity and for invariance**, each proven by a planted fault (D632): a fixed patch of ground and the player, metered through a day in steps, no step between neighbours over a bar; the same patch from eight directions and from two camera distances, at noon, dusk and night; a walk across a forest's edge and into a den's mouth, the patch's light and the camera's EV without a step. GPU stills, so continuous checks.
7. **Small**: a volume is added to the world only once it is lit (it is black for a frame or more now); probes are ranked by distance to their box, not their centre, so a large volume is not dropped for far rooms; the string-swapped shader patches (`grading.rs`, `hable.rs`, `haze_start.rs`, `air_start.rs`) are listed in `patches/README.md` and fail loudly when their text is not found; one `night` and one `open` ramp, named, in `eye.rs`; `shine` stops writing `Tweaks` every frame.
8. **`docs/lighting.md`**: the sources of light and their precedence per fragment, the eye, what is exposed where, what each check holds; a page, with the decisions cited. Written after 1 to 3, so it describes what is meant.

## What was right and stays

The smaller probe winning (D485, as Unreal's captures and Godot's probes); room probes a box each with their own dimming (D486); the atmosphere and its far air; shadows by quality with stand-ins for trees and temporal filtering; fire's light held in screen terms (D377); lights kept when their meshes are culled (`bounds.rs`); looks judged in numbers against references (D356).
