# Enshrouded's combat

2026-10-12. Question: how does Enshrouded's combat play, now that the developers have made it the baseline for ours (D180)?

## What it does

Gathered from guides, players and previews, not from playing it; checked where sources agree.

- **Action RPG, real time.** Light attacks for steady pressure, heavy and charged attacks for damage and for breaking guard. Some weapons have charged abilities of their own.
- **A dodge roll** that costs a lot of stamina. Players call learning it early the thing that keeps you alive.
- **Block and parry.** Blocking needs a shield or a two-handed weapon and takes damage off for stamina. A parry is a block raised just as the blow starts; enemies tell their attacks (a skeleton's sword glows before it lands), so you learn each enemy's pattern.
- **Stagger.** A bar under an enemy's health fills with parries and heavy blows; full, the enemy is stunned for a moment, open to a finishing blow (the Merciless Attack, a skill unlock, several times normal damage). Then the bar empties again. The loop is read, parry, stagger, punish.
- **Bonus from behind**: a backstab hits harder.
- **Targeting.** A soft lock and a hard lock; the bow aims freely with a crosshair. Wands and staffs aim by the lock, and players have complained it picks the wrong target. Version 1.0 (out 2026-10-15) promises targeting that favours the centre of the screen and the nearest enemies.
- **Ranged and magic**: bows, the safest family, weak once something closes; wands for quick elemental shots; staffs for heavy area spells; spells and skills that reward mixing weapons (a wand critical boosts the next staff blow).
- **Builds from a skill tree**: warrior with shield, battlemage with ward and parry, archer, mage.

Not found: a list of its combos, or how its animations are made. Its smoothness is the developers' judgement from playing it.

## What it means for us

- The baseline is weapon play with timing and footwork, with abilities on top. Combat styles (D124) and essence abilities (D66) add moves and options to that loop rather than replacing it with cooldowns.
- Most of what it does fits what is decided: stamina pays for attacks, dodges and blocks (D61); a critical by aim and timing rather than dice (D119) is its parry and backstab in other words; hit zones (D119) add aim to melee, which it has less of.
- It shows numbers and bars. We show damage on the body instead (D132), so a stagger would need to read from the creature: it reels, drops its guard.
- Its lock-on complaints are a warning for question 3 in [combat.md](../docs/combat.md).
- Feel lives in animation: moves that start at once, chain, cancel into a dodge, and a hit that lands with weight. That is engine and art work, and with a server that decides (D51) the client must play it at once.
