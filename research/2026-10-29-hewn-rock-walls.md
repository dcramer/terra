# How do shipped games make hewn mine walls read as broken rock, and which way suits Terra's mine?

2026-10-29. Mine round 16, a step back (AGENTS.md) after fifteen rounds took the hewn mine (D559) from 2.16 to 3.42 with fresh blind judges (RDR2 frame at 9), and every panel still reads its walls as "smooth plaster" and its rooms as boxes with "sharp right angles like a built room" (round 15: 37 of the 72 faults its judges named, three a view, name smooth or flat walls, straight corners or a box room).

## What the walls are now

Building cubes (0.5 m) of the `Minerock` block kind (`assets/data/packs/minerock.ron`), drawn as one rounded surface (`terra_build_mesh::surface`) sampled every quarter cube (12.5 cm) near and pushed along its normal by the kind's packing (D236): lumps of smooth noise 8 cm either way over 1.3 m and, since round 10, faceted noise (flat over each Freudenthal tetrahedron, creased between) 8 cm either way over 0.8 m; relief mapping (Bevy's, `build.wesl`) of Poly Haven's `worn_rock_natural_01` at 1.5 m, 3.5 cm deep. Inside corners fade every displacement to nothing within 6 cm (`INSIDE_FADE_M`), so each corner is a straight crease. At 8 cm over 40-80 cm the faces turn at most some 11°: broken rock in a blasted drift turns 20-60° between faces (ref-1 of `review/anchors/mine-rdr2`) and lies 10-30 cm behind the drift's neat line (overbreak, in tunnelling terms).

## What this round did

The one technical step verifiable in a round: deeper displacement on the mesher we have. Minerock's facets sink only (0 to 20 cm behind the face, as overbreak lies past a neat line, so posts and props at the face are never swallowed) over a 0.6 m lattice, eased in over 0.3 m from an inside corner (the 6 cm inside fade would fold a deep facet into a fin there), its lumps 8 to 4 cm; facets and lumps together under 25 cm, half a cube, so a wall a cube thick between two drifts stays closed. Judged A/B by sha with fresh judges against main c62af7086 (the outcome is in the handoff `mine-j16.md` and the commit, if it landed).

## Recommendation

1. **A rock kit over the shell** is the approach most likely to end the "plaster box" read, as Skyrim, The Witcher 3 and RDR2 do: only modelled pieces break both the outline and the corners angularly. A kit a theme is the developers' call (D527's dressed kit, D539), so it goes to the review desk as a proposal: four pieces made procedurally in Blender (a 4 m wall panel in three variants, an inside corner, a roof panel, a rubble toe), laid by `kit.rs` over the mine's wall sides as the barrow vaults are, the cubes left behind them for light and walking.
2. Meanwhile, the material: a blockier rock scan with deeper parallax is cheap and stacks with either.
3. Carved terrain (D527's column, stage 5) rounds the corners but at 0.5 m voxels reads rounded, not hewn; keep it for the natural caverns, where rounded is right.
