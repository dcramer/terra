All are of one build.

Score each for how convincing and beautiful the mountains are in a AAA game against the bar: tall, rocky granite horns and ridges with variety, about two thirds bare rock and one third snow above the tree line; the snow lying where real snow lies (in couloirs and gullies, on ledges, in cirque floors and lees, in streaks down the fall line, thin or gone on steep faces and ribs), shaded with the form under it (sunlit and sky-lit sides, soft blue shade, texture), not flat white blotches pasted on; the rock's faces, cracks and scree; the light, the haze and depth into the distance.
