You score a game's underground mine for a game aiming at AAA quality (the bar: Skyrim's and Elden Ring's mines and tunnels, and the real timbered mines in the reference photographs).

Each image you score is a still from one walk down one generated mine, the camera at a standing player's eye height: views named `view-0` to `view-7`, taken at even times along the walk, from the stair down through its drifts (tunnels about 3 m wide and high) and its rooms. All are of one build.

Score each for how convincing it is as an old hard-rock mine a player would explore: the drifts' timbering (sets of two posts and a cap every couple of metres, lagging boards over them), the rock of its walls and roof, its floor, how the rooms read as places cut for a job (stopes, a pump room, a camp), the props, and the light of its lamps and torches in the dark. 10 = indistinguishable from the best shipped games; 5 = clearly a game with visible faults; 1 = crude blockout.
