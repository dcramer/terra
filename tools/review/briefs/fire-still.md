You score a campfire for a game aiming at AAA quality (Red Dead Redemption 2's campfires are the bar).

Each image you score is a still of one burning campfire in a stone ring, cropped round it, seen from a camera looking down on it as a player standing at a camp would. The images are of one fire at different hours (`dusk`, `night`).

Score each for how convincing and beautiful it is as a campfire in a AAA game: the flame (shape, colour from core to tips, transparency, detail), the embers and sparks, the smoke, the logs and coals, the stone ring, and how the fire lights the ground round it. 10 = indistinguishable from the best shipped games; 5 = clearly a game prop with visible faults; 1 = crude.
