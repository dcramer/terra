# Fire

The fire module's automatic check (docs/fire.md, "The automatic check"; D415): every fire measured against photographs of real fire, so a bad one fails before a person sees it. Node 22.18 or later and ffmpeg; no dependencies.

```bash
node tools/fire/measure.ts <still.png> 800x500+0+420                    # a fire's numbers in a crop
node tools/fire/measure.ts <still.png> 800x500+0+420 --class structure  # held to a class: ✗ where one falls out, exit 1
node tools/fire/motion.ts <clip>... [--matte <matte>]... [--box WxH+X+Y] # how a filmed or rendered fire moves (research/2026-10-25-fire-motion.md); several clips (one build at several placements, a matte each) gated by each measure's middle-half mean
node tools/fire/film.ts [out=shots/motion] [--base <sha> | --alone] [--films] # the motion gate (D472): the campfire at 7 placements x 2 takes x dusk, night, its hearth light held at main's 6741693a and the sky as the sheet's, the base filmed beside the build; exit 1 on a miss
node tools/fire/film.ts --judge <out> [--alone] | --aa <side dir>...       # judge kept films again; pool A/A films into gate-aa.json and print the tolerances
node tools/fire/aa.ts [out=shots/aa] [--at 0.9] [--hour dusk] [--runs 2] [--from 120] # the motion gate's A/A: one placement filmed twice by one build, frames compared (which differ, where, how far; the sporadic frames, every picture differing over half its pixels; diff pictures where they differ much), each film's gate values
node tools/fire/references.ts [--fetch]                                 # measure the photographs into references.json
cargo xtask fire [--time noon,dusk,night] [--gate] [--hold]              # the fire sheet: every fire the game draws, held to its class
node tools/fire/bake/bake.ts <set>... | --all [--draft] [--pack]        # bake flipbooks in Blender (5.2, CPU) into assets/textures/fire/
node tools/fire/bake/bake.ts campfire-sim [--pack]                      # a sim's cache to a looping 3D volume, its seam checked (D493)
node tools/fire/bake/march.ts <set> --out <film.mkv> [--plays 2] [--raw N] # film the packed loop (or the sim as read) for motion.ts
```

- **The motion gate is paired** (film.ts, paired.ts, D472): the base (`--base <sha>`; else where HEAD leaves origin/main, HEAD on main) is checked out in `target/gate/base`, built with the same held light into the build's target and filmed beside the build in the same run, both at once. A measure passes when the build meets its bar from the real clips (motion.ts `GATE`), or when it is no further outside the bar than the base plus the hour's tolerance: `~` and the base's value follow it in the line. The tolerance is 2 x sqrt(2) times the run-to-run sd of the gate's value, from repeat films of one flame (`gate-aa.json`, drawn as the gate draws two takes a placement), so a build no worse than its base fails a measure it misses about one run in 44; film.test holds `TOLERANCE` to the data. Main's night splits sit on the 1.2 bar, so alone the gate failed main about one run in three (g85). Give `--base` as a sha on the worker (its origin/main may be behind or absent): a `--dirty` job's patch is HEAD there, so the default base would be the build itself, and film.ts refuses a base that falls to the build (HEAD with nothing uncommitted) unless it is named (`--base HEAD`, an A/A run of one build filmed twice). Each side keeps only `<out>/{build,base}/<hour>.{json,txt}` (all `--judge` reads): the lossless films, about 12 GB a run that a worker job would collect, are deleted once measured unless `--films`. `--alone` films the build only and holds it to the bars.
- **games.ts** (`node tools/fire/games.ts [--dir <frames>]`) measures the AAA game frames `games.json` lists (each frame's file, game, class or `"excluded"` with why, light and box) that a fire class's colour answers to (D423, D433): where a class's bands from the photographs and from the frames differ, the frames win.
- **sheet.ts** (`cargo xtask fire`) runs the yard at each hour on one of the game's shared GPU slots (D319), not `gpu.queue`, being a small run: a campfire, a log cabin set alight and a dragon breathing on a stone wall, under a held sky (`HELD_SKY`: `--tune sky.cloud.volume=0`, the dome's 2D clouds and never the marched ones, so sky work cannot move a fire's numbers), each shot from a fixed camera five times 0.5 s apart (`SHOTS`, `SPACING`; the campfire's last shot and the view above's first are frames 330 and 420, as before). On each shot's frame `fire.seen` tells the box each kind of fire projects to on the picture (`terra_world::fire::seen`), which is its crop; a row's numbers are the mean of its shots', each shot's kept in `table.json` (D433). It prints a line a fire and hour, writes `shots/fire/sheet.jpg` (a row a fire, a column an hour), `sheet.txt` and `table.json`. `--gate` prints only where a fire is worse than `held.json` (what is out today); `--hold` rewrites that file once a stage brings numbers in.
- **The proof** (`references.test.ts`): every reference photograph holds to its own class, and the castle film r12's wall fires (`r12-walls.json`, their numbers only) fail the structure class.

- **measure.ts** finds the flame (max channel at least 110 with red the largest, blue under green and saturated past a fifth, so a lit pink stone is not flame; or near white) and measures its colour bands (white, pale, yellow, orange, red, red plus orange), B/R and G/R and dim share at the picture's own size, with the run's flame matte only where the renderer drew flame (`--flame-matte`, `<shot>.matte.png`, D433; the sheet's shots have one, so lit ground is not counted), then resamples the crop so the flame is 256 px tall and measures the soot round it, the soot at its edge, the dark pockets in it (per flame-height square) and its detail by octave (fine, mid, coarse). The method is MEASURED.md's, from the castle film's rounds; the tests know their answers by construction.
- **tips.ts** (`node tools/fire/tips.ts <label>=<file>@<crop>...`, `@strip:<crop>` for a motion strip's 8 frames) measures a flame's colour, fill and runs by third of its height, image only, so ours and another game's stills compare; its top row is the first with 3 px of a blob of at least 3% of the crop's flame (`tipstop.ts`, tested in `tipstop.test.ts`), so a stray wisp does not set it.
- **tongues.ts** tracks a flame's tongues through a clip for `motion.ts`, as storm-cell trackers do: how long each lives, how often a tip sheds or one splits, how far each leans (tested in `tongues.test.ts`).
- **references.ts** measures every photograph in `photos.json` (file, class, crop round its flame, light, and its Commons credit) into `references.json`: per class each number's median, least and most. A fire is held to least to most, widened by each number's tolerance (`METRICS` in measure.ts), never to the median: real fires vary.
- **The photographs stay out of the repository**: their licences (CC BY, CC BY-SA) ask for attribution and share-alike terms. They live in `~/.cache/terra-review/refs/fire-real`; `--fetch` downloads any missing from Wikimedia Commons as the same 2000 px rendition their crops were chosen on.
- **bake/, volumes** (D493, docs/fire-module.md's last section): a set of kind `"volume"` names a Mantaflow cache already baked (`volume.cache`, today g23's F, made by that round's sim.py) and a box of its cells; `volume.py` reads it through Blender's OpenVDB module, each adaptive frame placed by its config's `res_min`; `volume.ts` crossfades the `crossfade` frames after the loop into its start, tiles the frames into one 3D texture (`columns` across), encodes it BC5 (`bc5.ts`, R flame, G temperature) as a DDS volume with its sidecar in `assets/textures/fire/`, an Rg8 copy in `shots/` only, and fails the bake if the seam's step (by cells and by 8-cell blocks) is over 1.25 times the mean step or the 95th percentile. The sidecar lists each frame's tongue tips (up to three local tops above three quarters of the flame's height, with the gas's velocity there from volume.py's `vel.bin`, Mantaflow's cells a time unit at the recipe's `time_scale`), where shed wisps would leave from (docs/fire-module.md, "Shed wisps"). `march.ts` films a volume offline from g23's camera, shaded as g23's films but with no smoke or logs, so a loop is compared with its own sim filmed the same way (`--raw`), not with Cycles' films.
- **bake/** bakes the flipbooks (docs/fire.md, "The baker"): `bake.py` simulates a recipe of `bake/sets/` in Mantaflow and renders heat, motion and depth passes in Cycles; `billow.ts` bakes a smoke set with `billow` (blaze-smoke) without a sim, lumps of pyroclastic noise voxelised a frame at a time and lit from six axis suns, writing bake.py's raw passes for `smoke.ts` (docs/fire.md, "The smoke"); `pack.ts` packs them into the flame map, motion map and sidecar; `smoke.ts` packs a six-way smoke set (`"six_way": true`) into Unity's six-way lightmap layout, two maps and a sidecar, with a maps preview; `sheet.ts` draws `shots/fire/bake/<set>/sheet.jpg` and measures the ramp preview against the set's class. `--draft` tries a recipe coarse and short in shots/ in under a minute. **ramp.ts** is the blackbody ramp the preview colours heat with.
