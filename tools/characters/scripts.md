# Characters: every script

A row a script of [tools/characters](README.md): its arguments, what it reads and writes and what it says. Read the row of the script you run (grep its name), never the whole file. Run from the repository root; Blender scripts through `tools/blender/run.sh`.

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `status.ts` (`cargo xtask characters`) | Where character work stands, in 40 lines from recorded results, running no check (D625): each criterion of `criteria.json` against its bar, how stale each check's record is and the command that refreshes it, the tasks in order with the files that own them (`path#symbol` resolved to its line), the next thing to run; `--json`. Tested in `status.test.ts` |
| `record.ts` | A check's result recorded for the status: what it said a stage, its commit and the files uncommitted then, in `~/.cache/terra-characters/<check>.jsonl`, the last 20 runs; `wardrobe.ts` and `build.ts` record theirs |
| `vendor.ts --list [PREFIX]` · `vendor.ts NAME\|PREFIX… [--colourways] [--dry]` | Their pieces copied byte for byte from the private repository's CC0 packs into `art/characters/quaternius/<pack>/` (D599): each glTF piece (a name, or a prefix such as `Male_Knight_`, the Modular Parts and rigged hair alone, never a whole outfit's file), the `.bin` and textures it names from beside it, `--colourways` its outfit's other BaseColor maps; their `SHA256SUMS` lines added after its own; refuses other bytes under a vendored name and two pieces of one name. `--list` names what the packs ship, `*` vendored. Tested in `vendor.test.ts` |
| `make_character.sh NAME SHEET HEIGHT` | The whole body pipeline on Studio credits: keeps the sheet, its front view, Tripo's model and rig in `art/characters/NAME/`, then runs `finish_character.sh` |
| `finish_character.sh NAME HEIGHT [RIGGED]` | The free part, rerun for every body when it improves: convert, check, render, export. Given one of Quaternius's bodies (`art/characters/quaternius/`), it skips the conversion and proves the export theirs with `intact.ts` (D599). Given a dressed figure of theirs, `HEAD.gltf+PIECE.gltf+...` (their head-only body and outfit pieces), it renders and exports it joined on the head's skeleton and proves every piece theirs; the rig check and proportions judge their full body instead |
| `convert_rig.py` | Tripo's V3 rig (Unreal skeleton) onto ours, keeping its twist bones and weights |
| `clips.py` | Cuts the clips in `art/animation/clips.txt` out of Quaternius's animation libraries into the game's mannequin file |
| `made_clips.py` | The clips we made rather than took from the libraries (`art/animation/made.json`), each put into the mannequin file in place of the library's: its best looping stride, carried onto our skeleton by world rotation from the T-pose, run in place, the upper limbs' roll handed to the forearm and shin |
| `keyed_walk.py BODY.glb CLIPS.glb SPEC.json` | A walk keyed on a body's own rest from measured targets (`art/animation/keyed/giant_walk.json`), for a body no take fits: feet placed, legs solved by two-bone IK with the knee toward where the toes point, hips sunk after each contact, crown nodding late; written into CLIPS as an action after `made_clips.py`, so `export_character.py --clips=` carries it |
| `stride.ts BODY.glb CLIP... [--falls] [--trace]` | A walk clip's weight measured on the exported body: footfalls, clearance at mid-swing, the hips' sink after contact and when it bottoms, the crown's lag, the pace; `--falls` the footfalls as a scene keys them |
| `phases.ts FILE.glb CLIP... [--tip 0.83]` | Measures a blow's active frames: the sword's tip, followed through the skeleton from `socket.hand.R`, while it is ahead of the hips within a sweep at pace of 0.1 s at least (the sweep timed whole, so a fast blow passing the front in 2-4 frames strikes there), its speed paced over five frames, so a frame's jump (a pop in a made take) is listed apart and is no strike; the moves data takes its spans (D405) |
| `moves.ts [--set FILE.ron] [--template male,...] [--fps 15] [--carry] [--legs]` | The move check (D447): every move of a set (`--set none`: only the `--also` clips), and what a drawn fighter shows besides, on every body template (a `--template` that is none of `templates.json`'s is an error naming them; each held look's own depth also as `held_cm.<look>`, into the torso, head and thighs, and `held_legs_cm.<look>`, into the calves and feet, read and said in a line, over the bar only under `--legs` until the looks that fail it are refitted; `--carry`, in the clips the game carries in (`CARRIED_IN`), lays a look's carry pose (`carry_hold`, D560) on its arm where no stance holds the arms, held in the body by `carry_arm.ts`, with `held_up_deg.<look>` its handle off up; `--arms` keeps the hanging hands off the hips after the clip as `terra_walker::arms` does, D535, by its twin `arms.ts`, tested in `arms.test.ts` on the Rust test's case): how deep hands, arms and held looks go into the body, how deep the sword goes through its own shield's board and the board through the sword arm (`clash.ts`, D481; scored by `tools/combat`, no bar here), where a blow's tip crosses (bearing and reach) and which way it sweeps, a raised shield's facing, for a clip with a spec a held guard's drift and a block's shield arm up in front, and how far a move's last pose is from the guard's and how near it comes, from its cancel to its end, to the next one's first; ✗ over a bar, exit 1. `glb.ts` is the reader it and `phases.ts` share |
| `held.ts` | A held look hung on a posed body as the game hangs it (D504): from the hand socket it is held in or, strapped, the bone its `strap` names; its sampled points, tip and board in that frame. Shared by `moves.ts` and `shield_place.ts` |
| `glb_clips.ts` | Puts named clips from one binary glTF into another and changes nothing else (D563), so remaking a clip moves no float of the body; `made_clips.py` writes its clips in with it. `NEW=A+B` joins two clips into one (a blow and its recovery). `--only OUT.glb PACK.glb CLIP...` cuts a bought pack to the skeleton and the clips the game plays, the file committed (`art/animation/ual2_fight.glb`) |
| `mesh.ts <file.glb[:Clip[@rate]]>...` | Does a take made elsewhere mesh with our fight, as it stands in its file: how near its start is to the guard (`--guard`, feet and hips, best held frame before the wind-up), its feet (slip, dragged and swept steps), the body in it (sword hand's fastest 100 ms, hips, shoulders) and its shape (hands apart, where it lands, the rate its hit needs against the first clip named); tested in `mesh.test.ts` |
| `root_drive.ts RM.glb CLIP[+CLIP]` | A move's `drive` keys from a clip's root motion (a pack's `_RM` file): the root's speed ahead each 1/30 s, going as far as the root does, so a driven body steps as its clip does |
| `glb_diff.ts A B` | Where two binary glTF files differ, read at a glance (D71): the first differing JSON paths, each accessor whose values differ and by how much, each image whose bytes differ; for finding why a build step's output changed |
| `intact.ts OUTPUT.glb\|.gltf... [--source FILE\|DIR]` | The intact-parts check (D599): every piece of Quaternius's in an output (matched by node or mesh name to `art/characters/quaternius/`) is theirs as made: each vertex's position, normal, UVs and weights by joint name within float noise, every triangle theirs with its winding, each weighted joint's bind matrix kept; vertex order and the split into primitives are free, since an export changes both. A piece whose every vertex, triangle and bind is theirs but with some of their triangles left out is named as an exact cut of theirs, with how many it keeps (their UpperBody is one of their FullBody; the D600 trial's bare slot pieces are such cuts). A mesh of ours is listed, unless most of its vertices sit on one of theirs without being an exact cut (theirs renamed, merged or changed). The ✓ line names every piece of theirs. ✗ and exit 1 on a change. Run it on every output a step builds from their pieces. Blender's exporter keeps only four influences unless told `export_all_influences`, which this catches on the woman's Ranger body |
| `vendored.ts` | The guard on Quaternius's parts (D599), three of `wardrobe.ts`'s stages: every file under `art/characters/quaternius/` listed in its SHA256SUMS and matching it, only the CC0 packs vendored; each the private repository's file of the same name and bytes (said, not failed, without it); and no step able to reshape their files; tested in `vendored.test.ts` |
| `theirs.py` | `theirs.refuse(script)`, called first by every script that reshapes a mesh and by `tools/blender/run.sh` before Blender starts: stops when given one of Quaternius's files or a file made from them, in any spelling, saying what to do instead (D599, D556); standard library only, so it runs inside Blender and out |
| `cut_slots.py [male\|female]` | D600's trial pieces: a bare torso, legs and feet cut from a copy of their full body by face height (feet under 0.44 m, legs under 1.03 m, their UpperBody's faces left to it), each an exact cut with their normals set back corner by corner, so the four with UpperBody make the body again; and their full body with its skin patches, for the hidden way. Writes `out/quaternius-SEX-bare-{torso,legs,feet}.glb` and `out/quaternius-SEX-patched.glb`; `intact.ts` names each as a cut |
| `layers.ts OUTER.gltf INNER.gltf [--clips FILE.glb] [--frames N]` | One piece worn over another through the clips (D599: their outfits overlap at the seams on a head-only body), a measurement that changes nothing: both posed on the outer's skeleton by the mannequin's clips (each bone's turn in the world from rest, the pelvis moved; a short form of `retarget.py`), skinned, then a ray from the bone each inner vertex follows out through it: the outer met before the vertex and not after is a poke-through, by the distance between; and whether the outer still covers the inner's top edge, so the empty inside never shows. A line of totals, then the clips past 5 mm or opening the edge; tested in `layers.test.ts` on tubes |
| `slots.ts SEX OUTFIT [SLOT=OUTFIT ...] [--frames N]` | D600's two ways measured on `cut_slots.py`'s pieces, state by state (dressed, coat off, legs off, feet off, bare), through the mannequin's clips played in place: the swap (a bare cut in each empty slot) and the hidden body (their full body, the patches a worn piece covers at rest collapsed, `fit_gear.covers`' rule). Per state and way: draw cost, poke past 2 mm, open borders (a cut edge or hidden patch not covered from outside) and the see-through ones, open rims (a worn piece's rim with nothing inside or over it), and cracks or creases between twinned cut borders. `feet=Peasant` mixes outfits. Writes `out/slots-SEX-OUTFIT.json` |
| `slot_stills.py out/slots-SEX-OUTFIT.json [STATE ...]` | Blender: per state, the swap figure's row above the hidden figure's, Sword_Idle and Sprint_Loop at two moments then the worst poke and open-border moments, into `out/d600/`; copy them to the desk from WSL |
| `haft.py` | A tool's haft thinned to a real thickness in Blender (`"haft"` in an item's or prop's data), shared by `fit_held.py` and `refine.py` so the two agree |
| `grip.ts` | The hands closed on what they hold, the twin of `terra_walker::grip`: each finger joint closed from rest about the handle's line until it touches the handle (GraspIt!'s auto-grasp), a power grip in hand, a hook grip hung, none strapped. Shared by `moves.ts` (its `fist` row, the scorecard's `held.fist.*`) |
| `carry_arm.ts` | The upright carried arm held in the body's frame (D560), the twin of `terra_walker::carry_arm`: an arm in a look's carry pose marked upright (`carry_upright`, from the pose's `upright` in `fight.json`) keeps its turn against the body, not the chest, as ALS's mesh-space arm curves lay an overlay's arm, so a torch stays flame up through a sprint, a crouch and a jump; any other pose's arm (`Carry_Tool`) leans with the chest. `moves.ts --carry` lays it |
| `shield_arm.ts` | The shield arm fitted to each body after the clips (D554), the twin of `terra_walker::shield_arm`: the shoulder blade, upper arm and forearm turned the least way that puts the strapped board back on its pose's target (`art/animation/shield_targets.json`). Shared by `moves.ts` |
| `surface.ts` | How deep a point is inside a skinned mesh that may cut through itself: the distance to the nearest triangle, signed by the winding number (libigl's signed distance), so a hip crease's self-crossing skin reads right. Shared by `skin.ts` |
| `shield_place.ts [--at COMMIT] [--capture POSE] [--json FILE]` | The shield's board place (D554): where each shield pose puts the board (centre, face, up) in the chest's frame on every template, from any commit's assets, against its target in `art/animation/shield_targets.json`; `--capture` writes a pose's target; tested in `shield_place.test.ts` |
| `shield_path.ts` | Where a fighter's shield goes through a blow and between chained blows (its round trip, how far it faces and grips off), pure numbers on the posed body's points for `moves.ts`, tested in `shield_path.test.ts` |
| `skin.ts` | The body's skin for `moves.ts` and the duel's composed-pose checks (`tools/sandbox/duel/pose.ts`): each body template's shape, how deep a point is inside the torso, head or thighs at a pose (`TRUNK`; `BODY` adds the calves and feet, the move check's), and the arm points (hand, fingers, forearm, elbow, upper arm) both measure an arm in the body by |
| `motion.ts CLIP [--keep N]` | Makes a clip's takes with Kimodo from its entry in `made.json`, measures each on the mannequin (`gait_check.py`), and keeps one as its take (below) |
| `clip_spec.ts` | A move's clip spec (D450), an entry of a set's list with its guard (`"guard"`), right- and left-hand keys (`"hand"`, `"left"`) and blow (`"blow"`: its move, sweep and contact bearing, the move it chains into, a held guard, a block's raised span) and timing (`"timing"`: seconds to the hit and back on guard, met by the playback speed): reading it, the sweep and contact check `moves.ts --spec` fails a blow on, the take kept, and phases written into a move set |
| `clip.ts CLIP... [--list FILE.json] [--into SET.ron] [--measure]` | The clip step (D450), one command a spec: the guard and hand keys (`kimodo_keys.py`), Kimodo's takes, every take onto the mannequin side by side (`made_clips.py --try=CLIP#N`), the base exported with the list's clips carried by aim (`export_character.py --list`), each take's phases, `moves.ts` on every take against its spec, the first that passes kept, its playback speed for its timing (the hit its active span's start, as the combat scorecard reads it), its phases into a scratch set (`out/clip/<set>/moves.ron`) checked as a set with its chains or, with `--into`, the take kept, `fight.glb` rebuilt and the game's set written: each blow as the clip the game plays of the take (`Open_Slash`, the entry made `of` it), and every other move playing that clip (the finisher, the partner's) given its new active span and end, its speed, cancel and drive left to tune; a line a stage, the rest in `out/clip/<set>/clip.log` |
| `kimodo_keys.py GUARD.npz KEYS.json OUT.json` | Run in Kimodo's own Python: a spec's Kimodo constraints, the guard pose as a full-body key at the first and last frames and each hand key a pose solved from the guard as a person reaches (the elbow's swing that keeps the wrist straightest, the forearm carrying the hand's twist); a sword key is turned by its blade and `edge`, a shield key by its forearm's line and `back`; a `KEY` line a key says where its fist and blade ended up and its wrist, and a key no wrist reaches is refused (D565 step 3); `--reach GUARD.npz KEYS.json` writes no constraints and says each key's reach as one `REACH` line of JSON for `keys.ts` |
| `keys.ts check CLIP` · `search CLIP AT [...]` · `between CLIP A B` | A fight clip's keys found and judged before a take (D565 step 3), each solved by `kimodo_keys.py --reach`: `check` says every key's wrist, forearm twist and elbow swing, how far each moved from the key before, and refuses a key past the keys' bar or sooner than 0.25 s after the guard (a caution for a last key twisted far from the guard's); `search` tries one key over blades in a cone and edges about its tip's way, with named targets (`--edge-toward`, `--swing`, `--twist`, `--flex`, `--dev`), printing keys to paste; `between` makes keys between two or toward the guard. Says first that the guard take's own wrist reads past the bar, so a key near it reads past too; tested in `keys.test.ts` |
| `clip_check.py` | Checks every clip is one our skinning can follow: no roll of the upper arm or thigh, no step too big after the twist step |
| `twist.py` | The twist step: a limb's roll moved to the hand and foot and halved on the twist bones, baked into each clip as it is sampled for a body |
| `proportions.py BODY.glb...` | The proportion check (D593): reach, fingertips and wrist (the arm hung straight from the shoulder joint) and crotch, in the T-pose at rest, against ANSUR II's men's 1st to 99th percentile as shares of the body's height (`art/characters/proportions.json`); a `PROPORTIONS` line a body and `PROPORTIONS fail` with what to do; run on every person's body and morph by the build. A body of Quaternius's out of band is `PROPORTIONS as made`, reported, not failed (D599) |
| `stretch.py` | The stretch every person's body gets as it is converted (D593): arms to a 1.86 m reach and legs 10% longer, scaled back to its height, as a warp of space planned on the rigged body (`plan`) and laid on its skin, joints, designs and dressed models alike (`apply`, `line_up`) |
| `test_checks.py` | Planted faults the checks must catch (D632), from the mannequin, in Blender's Python (`tools/blender/run.sh tools/characters/test_checks.py`, three minutes, seven Blenders at once): `rig_check.py` passes the mannequin re-exported and fails each of its checks on a copy with its fault (weights: every twentieth vertex unweighted; skeleton: hand_l on upperarm_l; rest_pose: spine_02 turned 10°, on a rig with twist bones, every bone at its child the control; reach, seams, symmetry, motion: a weight moved; motion's standing feet: the skin 6 cm up; its sink: the soles through the ground on the root), its docstring lists them; `gear_check.py` passes a vest of the torso's skin 2 cm out and fails it bound to the root alone |
| `draft_rows.ts` | The catalogue's rows drafted for an outfit just vendored (`wardrobe.ts --build`): a row a new piece in its neighbours' shape, a look for an outfit drafted whole with its items to name; accessories and bodies the catalogue lacks left out, each said |
| `test_stretch.py` | Tests of the stretch and the proportion check on a stick figure whose measures are known, in Blender's Python (`tools/blender/run.sh tools/characters/test_stretch.py`) |
| `test_bands.py` | Tests of laying straps over a figure dressed in pieces: a ray from outside lands on the whole figure's first face, not through a slit of the kept one, and a face turned in is turned out from the nearest bone (`tools/blender/run.sh tools/characters/test_bands.py`) |
| `test_hold.py` | Tests of `hold.py`'s aim and roll along a bone's joint line, not its +Y: a clavicle skewed 12° as Quaternius's are aimed and rolled on its line, and every hold of the fight's list on our base exactly as before (`tools/blender/run.sh tools/characters/test_hold.py`) |
| `test_twist.py` | Tests of the twist step's roll (`twist.unwrapped`) on rolls whose answer is known another way, in Blender's Python (`tools/blender/run.sh tools/characters/test_twist.py`) |
| `retarget.py` | Clips carried from the mannequin's skeleton onto a body's by each bone's rotation in the world from a retarget pose (D542), as Unreal's IK Retargeter does: the body's limb segments pointed as the mannequin's at rest, the rest kept |
| `tint.ts FILE.glb R,G,B` | Lays a base colour factor on every material of an exported creature (`bodies.json` `creatures`' `colour`), the last of its build step `creature:<name>` |
| `wake.py NAME.glb [--sheet=PNG]` | A creature that wakes gets its face, found on the body: a mouth that splits open (a cut, a throat behind it, splinters on its lips, a blend shape) and eyes that can glow; run after `convert_rig.py`, before `export_character.py` |
| `crown.py NAME.glb [--sheet=PNG]` | A tree creature's bare crown dressed with the forest's oak leaf cards (D536), placed and shaded as the forest's oaks are, each skinned to the limb it grows on; run after `wake.py`, before `export_character.py` |
| `deform_check.py` | Rings of skin along every limb through the clips: how much each squeezes, flattens and turns |

| `clip_sheet.ts OUT BODY.glb [--root=RM.glb] ENTRY...` | Clips as made on our body for the eye (a bought pack's, a take's): a film of each played once at its own speed from two sides, carried by its root motion over a marked ground, sword and shield in hand, and a sheet of six moments each (`clip_sheet.py` renders; run through `cargo xtask gpu film`) |
