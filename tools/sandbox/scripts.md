# Sandbox: every script

An entry a script of [tools/sandbox](README.md), each one line: how it is run, what it does, what it reads and writes and what it says. Read the entry of the script you run, `grep -n "^- \[duel.ts\]" tools/sandbox/scripts.md`, never the whole file. The client's commands as a module are [client.ts](client.ts), in the README.

- [loop.ts](loop.ts), run by `cargo xtask loop`: the play experiment's loop end to end in one running game, restarted once to load what it saved. Each step sets itself up, aims at what it needs by asking the game where it is, acts, and waits for the game to say what happened. A failing step says what the game said instead and leaves `shots/loop-<step>.png`.
- [cabin.ts](cabin.ts), run by `cargo xtask cabin`: a log cabin on a stone floor from plan to doorway: laid, a doorway and window cut, a roof laid, built step by step from the pack, a cube taken out and the taking undone, a refusal, a plank door and two pairs of shutters fitted, the shut door stopping the player, opened with the interact key, walked through and shut again from inside, a deck a storey up on four posts beside it built and walked up onto by its stairs, stone picked in the hammer for a wall with a cube in it changed to logs and a corner slope on its end, a chest set down and a wall of logs built from the wood stored in it with none carried (D141), saved and loaded the same. Each step checked by `build.state`; screenshots in `shots/cabin-*.png`.
- [rules.ts](rules.ts), run by `cargo xtask rules` after terra_build's rules tests and drawing audit (D257, [research/building-audit.md](../../research/building-audit.md)): every building rule checked live by state in an optimised build: nothing built in the air, off an unbuilt post or off an overhang into nothing; nothing taken from under a cube or an eave, carved, changed or taken out from under what it holds; nothing outside the claim, where the player stands or through the claim stone; a post stops the player at its width and an underside at head height stops them under it; a slate hip built and pictured half up and done (`shots/rules-*.png`); a cube's edit timed to the screen (`Build: change on screen`), and the worst frames while building and walking the village's street (`sandbox.frames`).
- [demo.ts](demo.ts), run by `cargo xtask demo [--skip-cabin] [--hour 15]`: the construction demo's pictures, the same every time. It runs the cabin script, loads the game it saved, sets the player at the end of the village's street and takes the camera round fixed views: the street at eye height, the green, the street from its east end, the village from a rise and from above, every building of the village close up, a stone house going up beside the cabin with the rest of it ghosts, and the cabin. The village logs where to stand for its views (`Settlement vantage: …`, `Settlement: …: its door …`), so they follow it wherever its seed sets it down. The hour is set before each picture with the clock running. Pictures in `shots/demo/NN-<view>.png`, and a sheet of them where ImageMagick is installed.
- [places.ts](places.ts): the table of places (D326), each with the sandbox options that put the camera there, its fixed views, the reference it is held to and its route: `lush`, the lush forest, and `lake`, the mountain lake (docs/demos.md §1). `vantage.ts` and `film.ts` take `--place <name>`, `lush` by default.
- [vantage.ts](vantage.ts), run by `cargo xtask vantage [--place lake] [--time 13,17] [--date 10-15]` (`--date` for a day of the year, docs/climate.md): the vantage sheet ([docs/biomes.md](../../docs/biomes.md#how-wed-know-d69)), the lush forest from fixed views (its floor at eye height, its edge, the clearing, an overlook) at each hour asked for, side by side in `shots/vantage/sheet.jpg`, so a change to how the world looks is judged on the same views; and, at the first hour, how closed the canopy is: the share of sky looking straight up from eye height at twelve places through the forest, its mean and median (D245). For the views inside the forest it prints how each looks against Sons of the Forest's own daytime forest frames (the reference `looks/sotf-forest-day.json`; any frames or film can be measured against any reference with `node tools/sandbox/look.ts measure [--against <name>] [--player] <files>`, and a reference made from frames with `node tools/sandbox/look.ts reference <name> --about "…" [--open] <files>`, its pictures kept outside the repository; a reference of open views made `--open` is measured by band too, the sky, the far land under the skyline, the middle distance and the near ground, with how far the far land stands under the sky and how much bluer it is than the middle (D326), and `--draw-bands <dir>` draws where the bands fell), measured as [research/2026-10-14-sotf-forest-look.md](../../research/2026-10-14-sotf-forest-look.md) measured them: mean brightness, the darkest twentieth, the share below 0.08, red and blue against green, local contrast and lit litter in the lower third ([look.ts](look.ts), checked by [look.test.ts](look.test.ts); D286). One scripted run an hour, about ten seconds each, moving its camera from view to view (`--vantage …@frame`) and saving each with `--shots`.
- [looks.ts](looks.ts), run by `cargo xtask looks [--place lush|lake|tundra|town|all] [--time 13] [--from <dir>]`: the looks check (D356), each place's measured views shot (vantage.ts's `shootViews`) and held to the art style's rules for places ([docs/art-style.md](../../docs/art-style.md#places)): a table a place of each number [style.json](style.json) holds, the views' mean, the reference's number and its tolerance, ok or FAIL, and the view furthest out, then what is furthest out across places; it exits 1 when a number does not hold. `--from` measures `<hour>-<view>.png` already shot. Each view is shot again six frames on with the lakes drawn flat magenta (`--water-mask`), saved beside it as `<hour>-<view>.water.png`; the debug-tint rule leaves the water it marks out of its fills, by what draws it rather than its colour (D429). Its measures beyond D286's, debug tint and lilac, hard shade edges, facet edges, flat colour and flat foliage (leaf colour spread and leaves catching the light, D368), are look.ts's, tested on made-up pictures; the judging and the style file's consistency with the places, references and guide by [looks.test.ts](looks.test.ts).
- [flightplot.ts](flightplot.ts), run by `node tools/sandbox/flightplot.ts <scene> <out.png>`: a trailer scene's flier course plotted before it is filmed (D382), as `terra_creatures::fliers` flies it ([trailer/flight.ts](trailer/flight.ts) works its plan out the same way): from above coloured by speed with a dot a second, and through the film its height, speed, the bank its turns ask, its climb and how far it is off the camera's aim, orange where it breathes fire; each breath cast from the mouth into the scene's building (`cargo run -p terra_build_scenes --example rays`), red where it strikes and grey where it misses, the building's footprint under the course, and each breath said on the terminal: how many of its aims strike, how far from the mouth, and how far off the way. The `flight` scene (`--cut tools/sandbox/trailer/flight.json`) films a dragon's moves over the meadow, to judge how it flies.
- [collide.ts](collide.ts), run by `cargo xtask collide [--count 20]`: walks the player into what stands in the lush forest and checks, against the meshes themselves rather than the shapes fitted to them, that what stops them is what they see (D267). Every thing with a footing is listed (`lush.solids`), so a kind added later is tried too, and the seven kinds the forest must have (`Rock`, `Boulder`, `Stump`, `Log`, `Oak`, `Pine`, `Spruce`) must be there and fitted. Of each kind, low and tall ones, and logs' low ends walked across; for each the player is set down 4 m off with nothing else in the way to it nor, for a low thing, past it where they step down ([ways.ts](ways.ts)), and must stay where they were put (not pushed out of a spruce's skirt), at a different turn round it each time, and walks at it for 110 frames, every frame recorded; one with no such way gives its place to the next nearest of its kind. It fails when the body stood more than 0.1 m into a mesh taller than a step, was stopped more than 0.35 m short of a tall thing as drawn (an invisible wall), did not step over a thing under 0.35 m, or was lifted more than a step onto something taller. Pictures in `shots/collide/`. About a minute and a half, on a release build. `world.obstacles {at, within}` answers what stops a walker near a spot (`world.solids {at, within}`, each one's pieces in the world's frame, for a fixture), `world.fell {at, within, taller_than?}` fells the trees within so far of a spot (those taller than so many metres), as a scene clears woods round a set piece, `lush.footprint {points, thing?, lifts?}` how far spots are from the meshes (of anything, and of anything but `thing`; given each spot's feet over the ground, only what of a mesh stands over those feet), and where the player has been since it was last asked.
- [artifacts.ts](artifacts.ts), run by `cargo xtask artifacts --frames [--quality high,low] [--checks still,sun,glide,going-up,undamaged,damaged]`: building's junk that only frames show, on the GPU at each preset ([docs/building-drawing.md](../../docs/building-drawing.md)): views held still with the clock stopped (anything changing is noise: acne, crawl, mip shimmer), the sun moving over a still view (acne, cascades), a camera gliding down the town's street (a pixel flipping for a frame), a stone house going up from outside and inside (flashes while sections are laid again, TAA trailing a new piece, a light probe popping as its room closes), houses never harmed (no damage drawn on them) and houses harmed (their timber's and roofs' damage holding still and showing). Frames are read and compared in Node ([png.ts](png.ts), [frames.ts](frames.ts), whose measures [frames.test.ts](frames.test.ts) checks against planted faults); each check's worst frame is saved with what changed marked red in `shots/artifacts/<preset>/sheet/`. About ten minutes a preset.
- [lodcheck.ts](lodcheck.ts), run by `cargo xtask lodcheck [--views floor,path,clearing,village,town] [--time 17] [--limit 0.5]`: what pops as levels of detail change, without camera motion to confuse it, in the lush forest, the bench's fifty buildings on the yard (`village`) and down a street of forty buildings (`town`, D278); and a view whose last frame is over 1% black. Under WSL each view runs on the Windows build, on the real GPU, since WSL's own draws on the CPU too slowly to finish. Each view is held still with the clock and wind stopped while every switch distance in the world is swept from half to twice its own (`--detail-sweep`), so every crown, trunk, shadow and grass ring crosses its switch in front of a still camera. Each pair of frames is differenced at half size and opened morphologically, so a crossfade's scattered pixels vanish and a patch that appears or goes at once survives. Prints each view's worst frames with the share that popped and the scale it popped at, marks them red in `shots/lodcheck/sheet.jpg`, and fails if any frame pops more than the limit. About 40 s a view. Checked against crossfades turned off, which it fails three times over.
- [popin.ts](popin.ts), run by `node tools/sandbox/popin.ts <film.mp4 | frames directory> [--out dir] [--least 12]`: what appears whole inside the picture between frames of a film or still sequence already made, an animal put out in view most of all (docs/scene-rules.md, Appearance). A pixel is new when it is brighter or darker than anything within 5 px (at 480x270) of it in the two frames before, and stays the next two; a patch of new pixels not touching the picture's edge is a pop-in. Cuts and flames are passed over. Prints each with its time and place, marks the largest in `<out>/popin-<frame>.jpg`, and exits 1 if any. About a minute for 20 s at 30 fps. A 40x24 px patch planted into the lake film's r12 is caught; the same faded in over 0.6 s is not.
- [groundlight.ts](groundlight.ts), a continuous check (`tools/continuous/checks.json`): the yard at night from a fixed camera beside where a campfire will stand, shot before it is staged and once it burns, through the GPU scheduler on a release build; the ground within the hearth light's reach must brighten against ground beyond it, in linear light, and the ring's firelit stones must read orange (hue 20-40°, saturation at least 0.6), not salmon pink, or it exits 1. `--from <dir>` measures a run already made.
- [torchlight.ts](torchlight.ts), a reading (D71, not a gate): a held torch's light at midnight in play, `cargo xtask gpu still -- node tools/sandbox/torchlight.ts [--den] [--woods EAST,SOUTH] [--out <dir>]`; in the meadow at the start, by a tree and (`--den`) on a den's floor it says, a line a number, how far the pool reaches on the ground, the lit ground against the unlit night at 3, 6, 10 and 15 m, the holder against the ground, the light's hue, the holder's shadow, the flicker over 120 frames, the picture's steps while running, and its share of black and clipped pixels. `--from <dir>` reads a run already shot.
- [turnlight.ts](turnlight.ts), a continuous check (`tools/continuous/checks.json`): the play start at dusk (`--time 19.25`), the view aimed to the four quarters by `play.aim`, a still each through the GPU scheduler on a release build; the ground and the player in the lower middle of each must be at least 0.3 of the brightest quarter's, in linear light, or it exits 1: facing a light volume (the den's) once put out the sky's light on everything outside it. `--from <dir>` measures a folder a run wrote.
- [volumelight.ts](volumelight.ts), a continuous check (`tools/continuous/checks.json`, D485), `cargo xtask gpu still -- node tools/sandbox/volumelight.ts [--volume "Den light" --view x,y,z,x,y,z [--patch x0,y0,x1,y1] [-- <sandbox options>]] [--out target/check/volumelight]`, `--from <dir>` for a folder already shot: the light of a patch of a still with a light volume on against off, as a ratio, in one game: each view held once unshot, then shot with the volume, with it moved out of the world by `sandbox.place`, and with it back (`<view>-on.png`, `-off.png`, `-back.png`); one line, the mean of on and back over off, and back over on for how steady the run was. With no `--volume` it judges seed 3's den at 13:00: a trunk over the den within 0.1 of itself without the den's volume (the median of each pixel's ratio, as sunflecks move on the bark), the chamber toward the mouth dark (luma at most 0.02, blue share at most 0.25) and at least twice as bright without it; exits 1 otherwise. Read on 2026-11-09, two runs: trunk 1.054 and 1.058 (back over on 0.998 and 1.000), the chamber 0.012 and 0.011, 0.16 and 0.17, 5.1 and 5.6 times, the grass 1.034 both; with the open air's cells lit as the den's (planted, D632) the trunk 0.857, and it fails.
- [indoorlight.ts](indoorlight.ts), a continuous check (`tools/continuous/checks.json`; D485, D486): the crypt of `--experiment dungeon` from its first room and a torch-lit corridor at noon and at midnight, through the GPU scheduler on a release build; inside, each view's mean brightness must be the same by day and night within 10% and its darkest twentieth under 0.02, or it exits 1; a forest view from the same run is reported beside them. `--from <dir>` measures a run already made (`noon-00090.png`, ...).
- [alone.ts](alone.ts), a continuous check (`tools/continuous/checks.json`; D623): every experiment the sandbox lists but the icon baker, each run alone offscreen on a release build through the GPU scheduler to frame 20; one that exits badly, panics or draws no frame fails, with its log and its panic named. `[experiment…]` runs only those; `--out <dir>` (`target/check/alone`) keeps the logs and stills.
- [saved.ts](saved.ts), a continuous check (row `saved`; D579, D622, D623): a saved game comes back as saved on a new 30 km world (`--seed`, j2's first by default) behind the loading screen: a claim stone, a chest and a wall laid, the clock run on (`daylight.pace`), the trees round the woods' nearest grown one felled (`world.fell`) and the player moved; closed, which saves it, and opened again; the base, the storage, the hour and day (`play.state`'s `clock`), the felled trees (none stands again) and the player's place must each be the same. A line a check; `--out <dir>` (`target/check/saved`) holds the world.
- [pops.ts](pops.ts), run by `cargo xtask pops [--frames 1200-1320] [-- sandbox options]`: what pops in or out, or flickers for a frame, in a stretch of a scripted run, by default the bench's walk through the lush forest at 17:00. It saves consecutive frames with `--shots` and compares each with the ones either side, small and blurred so the camera's smooth motion cancels: a pop changes a frame from the one before but not into the one after, a flicker from both neighbours, which agree. Prints each frame's share that popped and flickered, and puts the worst beside where it happened, marked red, in `shots/pops/pops.jpg`. A planted 20-pixel patch scores about 1%; a smooth walk under 0.2%. Leaves brushing the camera score high too.
- [trailer.ts](trailer.ts), run by `cargo xtask trailer [--only lake,forest] [--again] [--stills] [--no-render] [--size 1920x1080]`: the trailer ([docs/trailer.md](../../docs/trailer.md), D357). Each run in [trailer/runs/](trailer/runs) (a place of `places.ts`, its hour and date, the player's route, its sound) is filmed in the play experiment with the game's own camera following, the route smoothed into a spot every 4 m ([timeline.ts](timeline.ts), checked by [timeline.test.ts](timeline.test.ts)), with the look `--cine` gives it (the exposure and clock held, motion blur), every second frame of a run stepped at 60 kept for 30 fps; letterboxed and left with its own sound bed (`terra_sandbox --sound-bed`, the game's code-made sounds and music) as `~/.cache/terra-review/trailer/<name>.mp4`; then [trailer/cut.json](trailer/cut.json)'s runs are crossfaded between a title and an end card into `cut.mp4`, with a contact sheet. It builds the release sandbox first (a no-op when it is fresh, a line when it was rebuilt), so a film of new code never runs an old binary. A run newer than its shot file, its scene and the sandbox is kept. `--stills` saves only each run's first, middle and last frame on one sheet, to judge a route in a minute. A run may be a scene instead (`scene`): a timeline of remote calls and camera moves in [trailer/scenes/](trailer/scenes), written with [qa/script.ts](qa/script.ts), made by `--calls` in the same run, the player hidden: [dragon.ts](trailer/scenes/dragon.ts), a dragon burning the burg on the valley floor, cut by itself with `--cut trailer/dragon.json`; [troll.ts](trailer/scenes/troll.ts), a troll fighting a man with sword and shield on the lake's south shore, its fighters staged figures (`terra_creatures::staged`, D402), cut with `--cut trailer/troll.json`. Needs ffmpeg. Two more scenes: [lakesunset.ts](trailer/scenes/lakesunset.ts), the dusk lake facing the set sun with the troll by its campfire, and [settlement.ts](trailer/scenes/settlement.ts), the settlement's time-lapse rising into dusk. [cetri.ts](trailer/scenes/cetri.ts) (D534): walking up the lush forest's path in the morning, an oak towering over its neighbours wakes, its eyes lit and its trunk split into a mouth, lunges at the walker and comes on in slow, heavy steps.
- [progress.ts](progress.ts), used by trailer.ts: runs a long command that writes frames and says how far it is as it goes, a line each 60 frames and a line each minute it has said nothing, loading or between the far-apart frames of `--stills` (D388), so a scheduler that stops a silent run sees a long shot is alive.
- [forest-walk.ts](forest-walk.ts), run by `node tools/sandbox/forest-walk.ts [--film <out.mp4>]`: the forest walk's check (D467, `--experiment forest-walk`, [its keys](../../crates/terra_sandbox/README.md#the-forest-walk)): one scripted run walking the forest film's path at summer noon, then winter, dusk, night, the weather key round to snowfall and the feedback key, held to what the walk logs (`forest-walk: …`, kept in `shots/forest-walk/said.txt`), the snow lying after the flip and the feedback note and picture it wrote (to `shots/forest-walk/feedback/` by `TERRA_FEEDBACK_DIR`). With `--film`, every second frame as a phone-safe video and three stills beside it.
- [film.ts](film.ts), run by `cargo xtask film [--place lake] [--frames 300-2700] [--time 15] [--date 10-15] [-- sandbox options]`: a video of a scripted run, by default the bench's 40-second walk through the lush forest, with `--place` that place's route (sandbox options after `--` add to the place's, as `-- --weather snowfall`), to see how the world looks and moves. Every second frame is saved with `--shots …/2` and put together by ffmpeg at 30 frames a second, so it plays at the game's speed however slowly it was drawn; `shots/film.mp4`, about 4 MB a second at 1080p. Needs ffmpeg.
- [dungeon.ts](dungeon.ts), run as `node tools/sandbox/dungeon.ts [--experiment dungeon-mine] [--views 8] [--out shots/<experiment>]`: a dungeon demo's fixed views and film (docs/dungeons.md), the camera dollied down the stamp's tour as the run logs it (`Dungeon view`, `look`, `pace`) in a `--cine` shot, the player hidden and the eye adapting, filmed at the game's speed, with `--views` stills taken at even times along it (`view-<k>.png`), so each round of a theme is judged on the same views and walk.
- [wind.ts](wind.ts), `node tools/sandbox/wind.ts [--view crowns|all] [--time 15] [--date 08-25] [--out shots/wind] [-- <sandbox options>]`: the still-camera wind measure (D597). Films each view with film.ts, 12 s from a camera standing still looking up under a crown in the lush forest (on main 2026-11-02: shared 0.85, neighbour r 0.91), and block-matches the upper 60% of the picture (the crowns) in 32-pixel tiles at 640x360, each tile's shift over a tenth of a second found to a tenth of a pixel. A line a view: the amplitude (median and 90th percentile, pixels), *shared*, the share of the motion one shift common to the whole view explains, and *neighbour r*, how alike side-by-side tiles move (and 4 tiles apart), and *second against step*, how much further the median moving tile goes in a second than in a tenth (a sway near 10, a buzz under 2), and *over a second*, shared and *regional r* again on how far each tile went in a sliding second (tiles up to 3 apart for regional), where a slow wave the crowns sway in together shows though each tenth is mostly their own stirring (Enshrouded 0.19 to 0.26 and 0.52 to 0.55). It fails when shared is over 0.3, neighbour r over 0.5 or second against step under 5, Enshrouded's still-camera Springlands measuring 0.11 to 0.24, 0.36 to 0.43 and 7.2 to 7.3 ([research/2026-11-02-why-our-trees-read-fake.md](../../research/2026-11-02-why-our-trees-read-fake.md)), or when nothing moved. `wind.ts <clip.mp4…>` measures clips already filmed, a reference's. Checked by [wind.test.ts](wind.test.ts) on made-up films: one sliding as a block, tiles moving each on their own, one still, a slow sway and a buzz, and one slow wave under each tile's own jitter against the same on phases of their own. Needs ffmpeg.
- [qa.ts](qa.ts), run by `cargo xtask qa clips|<scenario> [--set <kit>|mixes|all] [--part <stage>] [--no-trace] [--runs 3]`: QA clips for people to watch, the same every time, of building and destruction in a set of kits, each 10 to 30 s of one stage from one or two fixed cameras, its caption saying what to look for. `clips` makes every clip: [`build-walls`](qa/build-walls.ts) (footings and walls rising), [`build-openings`](qa/build-openings.ts) (a window cut and shuttered, a door fitted and opened), [`build-roof`](qa/build-roof.ts) (the roof through its four stages), [`build-swap`](qa/build-swap.ts) (a wall's material changed in place), [`build-carve`](qa/build-carve.ts) (a window carved), [`build-storey`](qa/build-storey.ts) (the roof off, a storey on, a roof again: nothing but a roof is built on a roof's cubes), [`build-deck`](qa/build-deck.ts) (a deck on posts and its stairs) and [`destroy`](qa/destroy.ts) (a finished house brought down by knocking out its ground course, fragments falling, settling and fading). Each puts its cabin up as far as it needs at once and unfilmed ([qa/lib/cabin.ts](qa/lib/cabin.ts)), then films its stage. Other scenarios run by name: [`film`](qa/film.ts) (the whole cabin as one long film), [`harm`](qa/harm.ts) (cracks, a wall's foot, a roof losing its walls, a tower, the ground giving way, mending), [`materials`](qa/materials.ts), [`shapes`](qa/shapes.ts) and [`castle`](qa/castle.ts) (edits to the 140,000-cube castle, put up as the base with `build.example`, timed to the screen). A scenario is a file of sandbox options and a timeline of remote calls on given frames and camera moves, written against a set ([qa/script.ts](qa/script.ts)); a set is a wall kit with its roof, `all` the building baseline's (D333: the materials `assets/data/settlement.ron` builds in) and `mixes`, every baseline kit meeting; a frozen kit is filmed by name. Building costs nothing (`--free-build`). Each clip runs three times on the Windows GPU: a first pass live over the remote, to learn how many steps each fill takes and to fail on any refusal the scenario does not expect (`allow`); the film, a scripted run making the calls on their frames (`--calls`) with every section laid before each frame is drawn, every second frame joined by ffmpeg at the game's speed with its caption, in `shots/qa/<scenario>/<set>.mp4` and a 720p copy (`<set>-720p.mp4`), and a contact sheet, `<set>.jpg`; and the trace, the same calls in a benchmark run laying on the workers as play does, every frame's time in `<set>-frames.csv` and, in `<set>.txt`, the frames against the 13.3 ms budget (D25), each edit's time to the screen, changes shown torn, and the worst frames beside what was done on them. The trace runs three times (`--runs N`), each after waiting a bounded time for a quiet machine with the load sampled while it runs ([load.ts](load.ts), as `cargo xtask bench` does, D185); the middle run by its 99th percentile is the one written, beside every run's numbers, which were busy, their spread and a verdict against D25 (or, on an integrated GPU, D239's median). A camera move within 40 cm of a building's box (`solid`) is said before it is filmed. `--first` makes only the first pass, with a picture at the end of each stage in `first-<set>/` and, with `--answers`, every call's answer: what a scenario is written by. `shots/qa/index.html` has a row a set, each clip's sheet linking to its film beside its p99 against the budget. About three minutes a clip.
- [duel.ts](duel.ts), run by `cargo xtask duel [--out <dir>] | --check <fight.jsonl>…`: the yard's duel filmed and checked (docs/movement.md's Look row). The player is driven by the fight script `assets/data/duels/yard.ron` (`--duel`) against the sparring partner, in four runs one after another (one game copy a machine, D319), each with a `--cine` shot of its own (written beside its frames: a fixed camera from the side, square to the fighters' line, one behind the player's right shoulder and raised, one high above, and a keyless shot, the game's camera behind the player), so the HUD is hidden and the exposure and clock held; every second frame from 20 to 1450 is kept. Each run writes its fight record (`--fight-log`, every frame's fighters, asks and contacts) as `fight-<camera>.jsonl`. [duel/checks.ts](duel/checks.ts) reads them, a line a check, ✗ on a failure: the four records the same fight (move starts and contacts on the same frames); start latency (a move asked starts that frame or the next, a chained one the frame its window opens or the next); no idle frame between a chained press and its move; feet sliding (a ball of the foot within 2 cm of its lowest for 4 frames or more travels no more than 3 cm; rolls and a body down left out); pushboxes overlapping by no more than 5 cm; a blow with its target in reach (`Move::in_reach`) during its active frames lands, unless rolled through; each contact holds the striker for its hit-stop to within 1.5 frames (64 Hz ticks against 60 frames), a hit's as the move set gives it; the fighters' middle stays within 1.5 m of where the fixed cameras look (`framed`); and the game's own camera, from the player run's record (which carries the camera's place, look, up, field of view and aspect), frames the player, 1.8 m feet to crown, at a median share of the frame's height inside `targets.camera_player_share` of tools/combat/refs/enshrouded.json (D180), the player's middle within 5% of the frame's width of its middle and the partner whole in 90% of the drawn frames (`player framed`, D451). [duel/moments.ts](duel/moments.ts) picks the contact sheet's moments, a row a step: the move it made (or the partner's blow a guard met) at its wind-up, contact and recovery, from the side and three-quarter cameras (`sheet.jpg`). The four films go in a 2x2 grid with each camera named and the step along the foot (`duel.mp4`) and through the review desk's ffmpeg line under 15 MB (`duel-desk.mp4`). All in `~/.cache/terra-review/duel/` (`--out DIR` elsewhere, so two films do not overwrite each other) with `checks.txt` and each run's log; the frames are removed. Each run's output goes to its log as it comes, a run's newest line passes on every 30 s on stderr and a run silent for 8 min is stopped ([streamed.ts](streamed.ts), yard.ts's too: under WSL `cargo xtask gpu` sees a Windows run's work only as output); a run that did not finish leaves its own rows not measured, naming it and its log (the same fight; the player's framing and pose), the rest judged, and its sheet or film not made. `--check` with no records re-judges the runs in `--out`'s dir by their logs. Exits 1 when a check fails or a run did not finish. `node --test tools/sandbox/duel/checks.test.ts` plants each fault in a made-up fight. Start it as `cargo xtask gpu film -- cargo xtask duel`; a few minutes. [duel/shield.ts](duel/shield.ts), which duel.ts runs, measures how far the shield jumps in the fighter's own frame while a parried blow staggers it. [duel/pose.ts](duel/pose.ts) measures the pose the game shows (each body's bones and held looks after every pose writer, the record's `pose` and `sheathed`, D530) with the move check's geometry: the player's head off the game camera's aim through its blows, the shield's middle over the hips with the block not held, and the heater against the forearm it rides (`node --test tools/sandbox/duel/pose.test.ts`).
- [diverge.ts](diverge.ts): where two runs of the game part ways. The sandbox writes the play experiment's whole state every frame with `--trace FILE.jsonl`; `diverge.ts A.jsonl B.jsonl` names the first frame that differs and the values that differ in it. `diverge.ts --twice -- <options for a scripted run>` runs the same run twice and compares, which says whether the game does the same thing from the same start and keys.

- [whycheck.ts](whycheck.ts), run by `cargo xtask gpu still -- node tools/sandbox/whycheck.ts --windows`: `why` names what moved a body drawn between ticks (D548). The play sandbox's dev build on Windows (WSL's CPU-rendered dev build had not left the loading screen after 300 s), its clock held: the player warped, `why Player Transform` must say drawn between ticks and last moved outside the ticks by terra_walker's `warp`, on the frame the game said it warped; ten frames run, it must name a move in a tick on one of them, by a line not in `tick.rs`, or still the warp. - [portal.ts](portal.ts), run by `cargo xtask gpu still -- node tools/sandbox/portal.ts [--trips 5] [--settle 5] [--debug]`: the portal's census, the test that proves nothing leaks ([docs/instances.md](../../docs/instances.md), D469). One run of `--experiment portal` (release unless `--debug`) crosses to the placeholder realm and back five times; after trips 2 to 5 the forest's entities by archetype, assets by type and resources equal trip 1's exactly, the GPU's and the process's memory are within 2% of it, each kind of creature living before the first trip lives after every return, and the forest's state differs only in the player's place. It lists what of the forest is still there in the realm and says each crossing's time as the game measured it.
- [frametimes.ts](frametimes.ts), run by `node tools/sandbox/frametimes.ts --old a.csv … --new b.csv …`: two builds' benchmark runs (`--frame-times`, a CSV a run, a cold first run left out) against each other, both walking the same path (the same route spots reached), on four bars for a change to the game's own work on a render-bound frame, where a whole frame's p99 is mostly the view: the median of new's runs' p99 game time within old's runs' range; every new frame over 25 ms long in the render world or the GPU, not the game; every new frame over 25 ms with one in an old run within 60 frames (the same ground's first sight); the median of new's frame p99 within old's run-to-run range. A line a run, a line a bar, PASS or FAIL; its bars are checked in [frametimes.test.ts](frametimes.test.ts).
- [farlook.ts](farlook.ts), run by `node tools/sandbox/farlook.ts [--hours 13,22] [--look "Cap tree"] [--views 2] [--stage a.glb,b.glb] [--realm industrial] [--out shots/far.png]`: a realm looked at in one step (D642): begins a world in it (`--realm`), takes stills of the nearest things of a name, or of models stood before the player (`--stage`), at each hour (a run an hour, about a minute and a half), lays them on one sheet and says its frames a second and what it sounds of; the stills are deleted, the sheet is the caller's to delete.
